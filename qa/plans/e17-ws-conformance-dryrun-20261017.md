**Coverage caveats, stated rather than left implicit in "PASS".**

- _In-process, transport only._ The rows drive the real router, hub and emitters over a Starlette `TestClient`, with a fake clock, fake
  identity and a fake snapshot source. They prove the wire contract, not payload content (the producing epics') and not behaviour behind
  a real network (E17-Q02 chaos, E17-Q03 load).
- _Time._ A04, A08 and A09 move a fake clock, so they prove the deadline logic, not wall-clock luck. B08's "within 1 s" is the
  exception: a real-time budget (`REVOKE_BUDGET_S`) in its own `perf` row, which a revocation delayed by 3 s now fails. Measured over 400 runs, the budgeted span takes 1.4 ms at the median and 6.6 ms at worst.
- _Both wires._ A row that sent a JSON text frame now sends the same thing in the negotiated codec (a non-object, a 40-deep nest and a
  256 KiB body as MessagePack; undecodable text as a binary frame). One row cannot: the 65 526-name `unsub` fills the 256 KiB cap as
  JSON text, and MessagePack caps an array at 128 entries (SR-185), so it is JSON only. A cross-wire row compares every frame of one
  scenario on the two wires.
- _Strict xfail and the S6 rows._ One row fails today for its stated reason (plan §13): replay finish flips when E26-T05 (#628)
  lands. It is not a Gherkin scenario or a DoD item of #409. The two S6 rows that were strict xfails (#2409, #2410) were flipped by
  #3102 and pass on a flag-on `World`; with the flag off (the default until #2900) the gateway still closes at 1 024 frames, which
  `test_backpressure_flag::test_router_without_backpressure_keeps_the_1024_frame_close` pins, so #2409 (P1) stays open. The owner's
  `approved` comment records the deferral, and waives `03-testing-strategy.md` §16's zero-open-P0/P1 rule for #2409 (or the owner
  closes it as resolved behind the flag).
