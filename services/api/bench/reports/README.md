# Benchmark reports

Generated by `python -m bench.ingestion.runner` (E08-Q04), one Markdown + JSON per run. Runs are
gitignored; `e08-sample.md` is the committed laptop smoke sample (machine profile
`laptop-i7-8550U`). The nightly `perf-ingestion-nightly` uploads its reports as an artifact.

How the soak reads memory (06-performance §2, budget #8): `rss_mb_per_symbol` is the RSS the
symbol lanes add, per symbol (the RSS sampled before the lanes are built is subtracted, never
below 0). The soak lanes run the trade stream and the book state. In `soak_bars` they also run
the bars path at its cap (#399 D2-G4, `bench/ingestion/bars_lane.py`): bar builders for 32 specs a
symbol and the WS series store holding each with 1 000 bars, hydrated through the production tail
read from the recorded golden bars. `bars_setup_mb_per_symbol` is the RSS that building and
hydrating it added. `bars_store_at_cap`, `bars_closes_folded` and `bars_builders_healthy` fail a
run in which a series fell below that cap, a lane's builders folded no close onto it, or a lane's
bar set reported any sticky degraded reason (`BarsHealthReason`: a quarantined builder, a dead lane
or ticker task, a cold-started state blob, a restore gap, an emit-sink timeout). The bar writer and
the WS publisher are not in the lanes (`bars_lane.py` gives what each leaves out). Aggregation
enters this figure when it enters the lanes. The whole-process RSS at each sample, imports
included, is reported apart as `rss_mb_process`: it warns at +10 % and, once a same-profile
baseline whose gate is not warn-only records it, fails at +50 % (`gate.record` writes warn-only,
so the #2825 PR sets it). The smoke profile takes its first sample after an unsampled warm-up
(`warmup_s`, `warmup_wall_s` in `scenarios/soak_steady.json`, which says why 120 s).

The full `soak_bars` profile also holds the figure to a plateau (R4 of #399 D2-G4, registered on
#399 before its 24 h reading): `rss_plateau_slope`, the Theil-Sen slope of `rss_mb_per_symbol` over
the last 12 h of scenario time (`plateau_window_s`, 721 samples at 60 s), must be at most 0.18 MB/h,
over at least 100 samples, else the check fails with a note. "MB" is the harness's own unit, a MiB,
as in `rss_mb_per_symbol`. The limit is budget #8's headroom over a 30-day horizon, (300 - 170) MB /
720 h; the 5 % flatness band alone admits about 0.42. R4 reads growth that is sustained across the
window: a single step registers only within about 13 minutes of the window's middle, and growth
that starts in the last 3.5 h reads 0 at any rate, so the flatness band (5 % of the process RSS)
stays the guard for a large step, and a late leak is not seen. The slope is also reported as
`rss_slope_mb_per_h_per_symbol`. It is a hard check, so it guards a full run that has no baseline;
a baseline recorded from a full run should leave that metric out, as a relative band means little
for a slope near zero. The smoke has no 12 h window and so neither the check nor the metric. In the
lane the five `delta` series never close: the feed alternates buy and sell prints of 0.001, below
the smallest threshold the wire takes, so their hydrated bars are in the level and their fold path
is not exercised.

`HARNESS_VERSION` is `e08-q04/2` and the gate compares no baseline recorded by another version.
The six E08 baselines are `e08-q04/1`, and `rss_mb_per_symbol` in
`baselines/e08/soak_steady.json` and `e08-sample.md` is whole-process RSS divided by the symbol
count, so for them the gate shows no rows (the hard checks still judge each run) until a baseline
is recorded again (#2825). `baselines/e08/soak_bars.json` is `e08-q04/2`, recorded on a second
laptop (`laptop-i7-13850HX`) and warn-only like the rest: the gate compares it and can only WARN.
Record a baseline on a fresh checkout through the nightly's own entry point
(`python -m bench.ingestion.runner`): the lane-added figure is a small difference of two large
numbers, and warm bytecode caches read about 10 % higher than cold ones (paired runs, 5 each:
3.4 to 3.5 MB per symbol warm against 3.14 to 3.21 cold).
