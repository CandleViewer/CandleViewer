## 2. SCR-079 — the paper reset (US-PAPER-006; BQ-06 … BQ-10)

### 2.1 SC-8 settled: hidden in LIVE (BQ-06; PROPOSED to the owner)

On a paper account, in a DEMO session, the drawer's "Paper account" group ends with **Reset paper balance**, a secondary CMP-001.

**In a LIVE session the control is absent:** there is no slot, no placeholder and no disabled control. The frame carries the note "Reset paper balance must not render here, not even disabled (US-PAPER-006)". The ticket's AC and the story both require this (`11`:4043).

**Why this is not a breach of `14` §0.5 #5** (`14`:85), which never hides a control inside a visible surface:

- A paper reset is not a permission the live session lacks. It is a paper-only control with no object in a live session: the live drawer shows a live account, which has no paper balance.
- §0.5 #5 governs the legs of a control that exists in the session's environment.
- The exception is A-3, against `14` §0.5 #5, PROPOSED to the owner.

The T2-disabled variant D01's Probe 5b tests is drawn once, as an inset marked "not adopted" (`SCR-079/reset-alt-t2`; `D01`:108, :334), so the owner can rule after the run.

### 2.2 The confirm (CMP-044; BQ-07, BQ-08)

- **Which variant:**
  - **Typed** (`RESET`) when the paper account holds open simulated positions or working orders. The reset discards them, and that is irreversible.
  - **Standard** (plain) when it holds none. The reset then changes only the balance.

  This follows E42-D03's ladder, a gate per blast radius (`E42D03`:698–699): the plain variant takes rung 2's form (Confirm once the counts are read) and the typed one rung 3's (a token). Which rung the paper reset sits on is PROPOSED (Q-3), since the rungs are assigned upstream.

- **Focus:** it starts on Cancel in both variants (CMP-044 as built, `15`:525); the typed field is the next stop. Confirm is a CMP-001 `danger` and never the default focus.
- **Copy (PROPOSED, `D01`:302–303):**
  - title: "Reset the paper balance?";
  - the counts: "This clears 3 open simulated positions and 2 working orders and sets the balance to 10,000 USDT.";
  - "Your simulated history stays in the journal. Live and demo exchange accounts are not affected.";
  - the typed instruction: "Type RESET to confirm".

  The counts come from the paper account's state at open, and the dialog re-reads them on Confirm.

- **Starting balance:** a numeric field in USDT, prefilled with the current starting balance. The range is PROPOSED with no contract (G-2), and its error is text: "Enter a whole number of USDT from 100 to 10,000,000." (PROPOSED).

## 4. SCR-122 — live eligibility (US-PAPER-007; BQ-11 … BQ-15)

### 4.1 The control

The control sits in E42-D03 §8's Accounts fieldset (`E42D03`:511). Each account row (`AccountAccessGrant`, `22`:19195) gains a **Live** switch after Trade.

- **On a LIVE account:** the switch reads "Live: on" / "Live: off — demo only", in words.
- **On a DEMO account:** it is T2, "This is a demo account."
- **Data:** `22`'s grant carries `level` and the account's `environment`, but no live flag (G-1; E42-D03's CG-19, `E42D03`:532). The switch names the data it needs and proposes no shape.

### 4.2 Grant, revoke and acknowledge

- **Grant:** rung 1 (`E42D03`:697): CMP-044 standard plus step-up (`users.access_change`, step-up, `14`:2587), with the restatement "alex can trade LIVE on sub_001 after acknowledging at next sign-in."
- **Acknowledge (B12):** at alex's next sign-in, a one-time full-page notice comes before the workspace, `role="alertdialog"`: "You can now trade LIVE on sub_001. Granted by the owner at 14:02 UTC." with "I understand", which is audited. It cannot be skipped, and until it is acknowledged live order entry stays T2 (G-3; the copy and the user names are PROPOSED fixtures).
- **Revoke with open live positions (B10):** rung 2, as removing account access is (`E42D03`:698); the confirm names them, "alex holds 2 open LIVE positions on sub_001. They stay manageable in reduce-only mode; new entries are refused."
  - **Effect (B13):** the reduce-only state shows in alex's workspace as a CMP-028 `warning` panel notice with the same sentence.
  - **Entry controls:** each is T2 "Live access was revoked: you can reduce or close positions; new entries are refused."
  - **Exits:** they stay enabled.
