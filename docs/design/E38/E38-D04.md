## 2. The SIMULATED badge (DS-A SimulatedBadge, PROPOSED)

### 2.1 Anatomy

A CMP-011 neutral composition (`15`:157): the word **SIMULATED** in `type.ui.label` capitals, then a PROPOSED registry glyph `simulated` at `size.icon.sm`. The glyph is decorative beside the word; the registry has none today (`16`:181), so the word alone carries until the glyph lands. Two sizes: `md` (24 px, in a heading) and `sm` (20 px, in a row or chip). The radius is `radius.control`, unlike the env pill's full round.

### 2.2 Tokens

| T   | Part     | Token                                                                                            |
| --- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| T-1 | fill     | `color.neutral.subtle`                                                                           |
| T-2 | hairline | 1 px `color.border.strong` (decorative: the word carries the meaning)                            |
| T-3 | word     | `color.text.primary`, `type.ui.label`, weight semibold                                           |
| T-4 | glyph    | `color.text.primary`, `size.icon.sm` (PROPOSED glyph `simulated`)                                |
| T-5 | spacing  | `space.inset.xs` gap, `space.inset.sm` horizontal padding, `size.control.compact` height at `md` |

The word measures 12.22<!--=CR:badge.word.dark--> / 14.37<!--=CR:badge.word.light--> / 13.83<!--=CR:badge.word.hc--> on its fill. The hairline measures 1.91<!--=CR:badge.hair.canvas.dark--> / 1.57<!--=CR:badge.hair.canvas.light--> / 1.91<!--=CR:badge.hair.canvas.hc--> on the canvas and 1.80<!--=CR:badge.hair.raised.dark--> / 1.46<!--=CR:badge.hair.raised.light--> / 1.80<!--=CR:badge.hair.raised.hc--> on the raised surface. The hairline is reported, never gated: it is decorative, and the badge is not a control.

### 2.3 States

- default;
- in a heading (`md`);
- in a row (`sm`, the `SIM` column's cell);
- on a stale row (the row's own stale treatment, the badge unchanged);
- forced colours (`CanvasText` word and border; the glyph kept).

It has no hover, focus or disabled state, because it is never a control.

### 2.4 Rules

- **R-1** Wherever a figure comes from the paper matcher, its region heading carries the badge (`md`) and its region name begins with "Simulated" (`14`:2272). A chip alone never suffices.
- **R-2** Never on the env badge, never with an env colour, never shortened below the word (memo CA-5).
- **R-3** In a table the carrier is a column, so a sorted or exported table keeps it (`E26D02`:359).

## 4. DEMO and SIMULATED together (`T` AC 2)

The env badge (CMP-074 / CMP-075, `color.env.*`, `15`:843) sits on the chrome and names **where the account is**. The SIMULATED badge sits on the data region's heading and names **who filled it**. A demo-environment simulated fill shows both, in those two places, and never merges them into one chip.

What tells them apart:

- they share no word;
- the badge is never blue, while DEMO is (§2.2 T-1);
- the glyph (outline dot vs `simulated`);
- the shape (full round vs `radius.control`);
- the place (chrome vs heading).

Under memo §8's rule no pair is told apart by hue alone (`MEMO`:250). The note drawn on board 04, PROPOSED for the copy deck: "DEMO is where the account is. SIMULATED is who filled it. A trade can be both."

## 5. The fill-model disclosure block (DS-B FillModelDisclosure, PROPOSED; `T` AC 3)

### 5.1 Rows

The block opens with CA-6 and shows six rows, **always expanded**; nothing hides behind a disclosure. Each row reads "plain words · the configured value", from the session's `FillModelConfig` (`24`:3986), never a typed default.

| B   | Label          | Plain words (PROPOSED)                                     | Field                                                              | Value shown                                                           |
| --- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| B-1 | Fill model     | how orders are matched against the order book              | `mode`                                                             | "Book walk", "Probabilistic" or "Conservative"                        |
| B-2 | Queue position | a resting order waits behind the size already at its price | `use_queue_position`, `prob_fill_on_touch`                         | "On" or "Off — 35 % chance of a fill on touch"                        |
| B-3 | Latency        | delays applied to your orders and to market data           | `latency_submit_ms`, `latency_cancel_ms`, `latency_market_data_ms` | "Submit 60 ms · Cancel 60 ms · Market data 30 ms"                     |
| B-4 | Slippage       | extra price movement against you                           | `prob_slippage`, `slippage_floor_ticks`                            | "None beyond the book" or "{n} ticks minimum"                         |
| B-5 | Fees           | maker and taker fees                                       | `maker_fee_bps`, `taker_fee_bps`                                   | "From your Bybit fee rate" or "Estimated: maker 2 bps, taker 5.5 bps" |
| B-6 | Funding        | funding payments on open positions                         | `apply_funding`                                                    | "Applied" or "Not applied"                                            |

The example values are the `24` defaults (`24`:3991–3993, `24`:3999), shown only as specimens; the block renders the session's values. When the fee-rate call failed, B-5 reads "Estimated" with the fallback rates, as `24`:4032 marks `fee_estimated`.

## 6. Analytics: one data set at a time (`T` Scope; D-5; BQ-04)

- The journal analytics (SCR-095, E41's) open on **Live**.
- A view switch, CMP-003 semantics, reads **Live · Simulated**. It changes the whole dashboard's data set and its heading (P-6). It never adds simulated rows into a Live total.
- A comparison (opt-in, off by default) draws the two sets as two directly labelled series, "Live" and "Simulated", distinguished by line style as well as colour (`16`:191), each with its own total. It never draws one combined total.
- Replay's simulated fills stay in their own replay journal (`11`:4359), as #511's ruling BQ-8 holds.
- `T`'s "include-toggle" is read as this view switch (C-1).
