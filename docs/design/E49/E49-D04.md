## 1. Purpose, method, locked inputs and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this pack fixes

One graded pass over every animated surface: a verdict per inventory row on E49-D01's scale, a normative
reduced-motion mapping per motion class, the hold ring's record (reduced, not exempt), the numeric-readout rule, the
flash and pause checks cited per surface, the token-conformance list, E49-D02's G-1 answered, and PR 3's plan. It
adds no animation and re-decides no merged motion row (BQ-2).

### 1.2 Method (honest)

A heuristic evaluation (Nielsen H1–H10, WCAG 2.3.1, 2.3.3 and 2.2.2, and `05` §7's rules) of each owner motion table
against D08's tiers, a persona walkthrough (a trader in a fast market with vestibular sensitivity, a keyboard-only
trader, the owner on a shared machine) and a public-knowledge competitive review (§15). The completeness argument is
the union of the owner tables, the motion lint and the register (§11); no build ran and no session was logged.

## 15. Competitive review (public knowledge only; no third-party screenshots)

- **Reduce, not remove.** Public platform guidance (Apple's reduce-motion setting, the CSS media query's own
  description) replaces large or parallax motion with dissolves or none and keeps information intact; §3's three
  words are the stricter reading, a cross-fade only where already ruled.
- **Essential motion.** WCAG 2.3.3's exception (motion may stay when "essential to the functionality or the information being
  conveyed") and the common practice of keeping a
  progress indication while dropping its animation match §5: the hold stays, the sweep goes, and no exemption is
  needed.
