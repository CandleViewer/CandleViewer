## 1. Purpose, scope and the safety framing

The two _monitoring_ surfaces of E39 — **SCR-071** the risk dashboard (R-150 `/risk`, the owner's supervision page; R-151 `/risk/:accountId`, the manager's "what are my limits" page) and **SCR-073** the lockout notice (the interruption a manager sees when the server locks their account, B20) — plus the two components they contribute, **CMP-117 RiskLockoutBanner** and **CMP-138 RiskCapMeter**, taken from E39-D01's wireframes (its 7 `SCR-071 / *` and 9 `SCR-073 / *` boards) to hi-fi, with the a11y review and the handoff package E39-S04 consumes. **The UI mirrors server truth and server enforcement:** the server enforces risk and RBAC (C-2.12), statecharts record while synchronous code enforces (C-2.21; B20 INV-B20-a `28` :7943 "`halt_new_orders` sets a synchronous flag consulted by the `Validator`"), and the UI is a renderer (E39-D01 D3 :311 — caps, blocks, `until`, the warning band and every count come from the server; the client never computes `until`). **A lockout notice never implies that closing or reducing is blocked when it is not:** under a B20 lockout reduce-only closes stay available everywhere (D10 :317; `14` SCR-073 :1544 "Read-only access to everything else remains"; every §8.2 row "closing and reducing still work"; §9.1 :563), and only the **frozen** (B18) manager banner promises nothing beyond "ask the owner" (OD-11 :687, §8.1 :488). **Native-SL protection (C-2.6) is never implied removed:** R4 widened to lockouts (SR-E39-06 :244) — a lockout cancels only exposure-adding working orders, position TP / SL and reduce-only stops are never cancelled; the `positions_without_native_stop` banner says protection is being re-attached (B8 `position_protection`, E40-D01 CO-6), never that the invariant was waived. **Meters are never colour-only** and carry the number, the cap and the as-of time (D8 :315; US-ADMIN-004 NFR `11` :4860 "every figure carries a freshness timestamp"); **LIVE vs DEMO is text** (`14` §0.5 item 1 :81; `05` §4 item 1 :116–117; D11 :318; CMP-075 on every row); **staleness is explicit** (D9 :316; `14` §0.5 item 4 :84; E42-D02 D4 :130 "old" / "down" — never a zero or a blank for a value not read); **the override is owner-only with a drawn step-up** (SR-025 `04` :806; `22` :11934 `killswitch:write`, scope none; shipped `services/api/candleviewer/api/step_up.py:527` gates `POST /risk/lockouts/{id}/override` under class `risk_caps`, 5-minute grace — drawn as shipped, the no-grace `killswitch` class labelled as G-3's target :274) and **never offered to a role that cannot use it** (a manager sees T2 "Ask the owner for an override", D4 :312; a viewer T2 "Your role can't clear a lockout." PROPOSED; RBAC server-side C-12.4, the UI mirrors it); **live values announce at throttled polite rates** (`05` §6.2 :147–148; D13 :320; CMP-035 `aria-live="off"` with threshold announcements, `15` :364).

**Out of scope:** SCR-072, SCR-134 and CMP-211's anatomy → E39-D03 (#879; cited, never redrawn); implementation and Storybook stories → E39-S04 (the `15` entries name the stories, nothing here writes one); the request-override endpoint → E39-S02 (#955, OD-4); the lockout facts in the client contract → E39-S01 / E39-S02 (OD-12); the runbook → E39-T03 (#957). **The bar** `(heuristic)`: a best-in-class terminal's risk page (E39-D01 §6: TopstepX's countdown "at the top of the platform", ATAS "Reset In", the three breach tiers of F16 — alert · block new risk · flatten-and-lock; nothing refetched here): the state of every account is readable in one glance under stress, the lock says what it did and did not do, the countdown is legible but silent, and nothing on the page lies about freshness.
