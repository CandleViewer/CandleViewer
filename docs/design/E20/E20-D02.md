## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this spec settles

The structure of the strip and its modal before their surface: which rows show by default, where each word, glyph and number sits, how a narrow bar keeps its information, how a row is reordered without a drag, what a breach looks like in greyscale, how the strip hides and comes back, and where SCR-045 gains the Deep Stats columns and the export. The hi-fi stage (E20-D03) may not substitute colour for any word or glyph named here.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk work at the base under the agent-delivery adaptation: no participant, prototype or engineer session exists; every judgement is heuristic (§16.1). The audit tool checks every cite at its pinned revision, the row vocabulary against `24` §7, the MEMO defaults at its head, the frame register and the board count.

### 1.3 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 C-2.11.** The strip reads the live public trade stream on demo and live alike: demo's public feed is “same as live (demo has no separate public feed)” `20`:871. It shows no account data, no DEMO / LIVE badge and no environment control; nothing here differs by environment.
- **R-2 Words carry meaning.** A breach, the forming bar, a partial history and an unavailable source each carry a word; no state rests on shade, line style, opacity or motion alone (`11`:1915, MEMO AD-5). A missing value is a stated absence, never a zero.
- **R-3 One label per row.** Every frame names a row by its `24` §7 label (`24`:1211–1232); a short label appears only in the compact strip, mapped 1:1 (MEMO AD-7).
- **R-4 Announcements.** The forming bar carries no live region; the read shortcut is the on-demand path. Every other announcement here is polite, once, by label, at most one a second per region, a newer one replacing a queued one (E47-D04:123, :146): a reorder through the dialog's one coalesced region (§6.3), `Apply` (W-22, §6.6), the strip shown or hidden (W-23, W-24, §5) and the export (W-25 … W-27, §7.2). E20-D04 owns the policy and may reword (MEMO §7.2).
- **R-5 No field invented.** A control whose contract is missing is drawn with its gap named (G-row) and never wired to an invented parameter.

No new contrast ratio is stated here: wire greys are validated with the repo's contrast matrix at E20-D03. The T2 label and reason reuse E09-D09's measured pairs (tertiary label 5.62 / 5.92 / 11.21 on raised and 5.96 / 6.36 / 11.87 on canvas, dark / light / high-contrast: E09-D09:292, :293). ## 3. CMP-225 strip under SCR-030 (boards 01, 02, 04, 05)

### 3.1 Anatomy

The strip sits between the price pane and the volume sub-pane, where SCR-030's sketch places it (`14`:730). Left to right: a fixed label column (the row labels, right-aligned, and a header cell carrying W-1, the threshold set), then one column per visible bar whose x is the bar's own column (§8). ## 4. Narrow bars: the collapse and the readout (boards 02, 03; MEMO AD-3, AD-4)

### 4.1 The collapse (`SCR-030/strip-NarrowBars`)

Below the minimum legible bar width each cell drops its digits and keeps its row class's glyph: a signed row keeps `▲` / `▼`, an unsigned row keeps the presence mark `·` (PROPOSED), Delta Divergence keeps its flag when true, and a breached cell adds the breach-glyph slot. Row labels stay. No number is clipped, ellipsised or partial. Each cell's accessible text keeps the full value, its unit and the sign word (W-5). The floor's number is E20-K01's, seeded from E18-D02's planning floors (E18-D02:124), never invented here (MEMO G5). ### 4.2 The readout (`SCR-030/strip-NarrowBars-readout`, `SCR-030/strip-readout-overflow`)

CMP-197 lists, for the bar under the crosshair, every enabled row's §7 label, value and unit in strip order; the forming bar's values carry W-7. Past 12 rows the readout shows W-6, its path to SCR-045 (§7). ## 11. Focus order and keyboard

- **Strip (workspace archetype, E47-D04:174).** Inside the chart region: the gear, then the table as one stop. The table is a grid (E47-D04:219): `role="grid"` named "Deep Stats", a roving tabindex, `↑` / `↓` by row and `←` / `→` by bar (`15`:2934). Each row's label is its row header; each column header is the bar's time, the last one with the word "forming" (W-7). On entry focus lands on the cell last focused by its row and bar time, else the first row's newest closed bar, and the grid announces its extent once per entry (E47-D04:223, its W-3 pattern). `Tab` leaves in one press (E47-D04:219). The focused cell is kept by row and bar time when a bar closes or the range pans or zooms; if its bar leaves the visible range, focus moves to the nearest visible bar's cell in the same row (PROPOSED). The read shortcut is E20-D04's (MEMO G7).
- **SCR-033 (dialog archetype, E47-D04:198).** On open, focus lands on the first enabled row's checkbox. Order: the toolbar (Compact mode, Sparkline N, Thresholds on, Set), the list, then the footer (Hide Deep Stats, Cancel, Apply). The list is one Tab stop with a roving row (E47-D04:117): `↑` / `↓` move between rows, `←` / `→` between the focused row's controls (checkbox, Format, Sparkline, Threshold, and when expanded the comparison select and the bound field, then "⋯"; the handle is not a stop); `Tab` leaves it in one press (E47-D04:219). A select or field keeps its own keys while it is being edited, entered with `Enter` and left with `Esc` or `Enter` (PROPOSED); `Esc` outside an edit cancels the dialog. `Esc`, `Cancel` and `Apply` return focus to the gear (E47-D04:293, §9); after "Hide Deep Stats" the gear is gone and focus goes to the chart pane's menu button (§5).
- **SCR-045.** Unchanged grid model (E47-D04:211); the export button is in the header's Tab order; on completion or cancel, focus returns to it.
- **Reflow and zoom.** At 200 % text and the 400 % reflow width no row and no value is lost: a value a collapsed cell drops is in its accessible text, the readout and SCR-045 (MEMO §7.5). SCR-033 fills the viewport at 320 CSS px with its body scrolling, and each row's controls wrap without two-dimensional scrolling (E47-D04:127); the hint chip wraps under the label.
