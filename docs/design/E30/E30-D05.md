### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity (RULED BQ-04, confirmed).** **Blocking:** a clause or a WCAG 2.2 A / AA criterion with no compliant path in the merged designs, a state carried by colour alone, an announcement with no string or politeness, **or an R5 or G-T2 miss on a money path** (this review's widening: `DS`:15 already makes a G-T2 miss Major in a spec, and here the control moves money). **Major:** an at-risk clause, a key collision without a declared shadow, a T2 over- or under-conversion. **Minor:** wording, cadence or order, or an at-risk clause whose interim path the merged designs already give. **Info:** recorded for a consumer's charter (`E29-D05`:64).
- **R-2 Findings and their closing (RULED BQ-05).** An F row names the merged sentence, the replacement, one owner (the spec and its area, never an agent) and one target: E30-D06 (#919), which lifts the finding's version into the pack, plus the consuming story (Info rows: E30-D06 only). "Fixed in the design file" is read as "fixed in the pack E30-D06 lifts" (C-n). Every F row carries an `R5` cell (`submit — arm-gated` · `carve-out — never arm-gated` · `n/a`) and a `G-T2` cell (`T2 · <rendering>` · `validity` · `busy` · `n/a`); the tool fails a row with neither filled. **AC 4:** a Blocking F row stamps E30-D06 (#919) "cannot be Done until fixed or an exception is recorded on #918" and holds nothing on D02, D03 or D04's approvals.
- **R-3 Verdicts.** `compliant` · `at risk` · `non-compliant` · `out of scope — <owner>` · `not yet decidable — routed to E30-Q05` (`E29-D05`:66). A `non-compliant` clause carries a Blocking F row; an `at risk` clause carries a Major F row or names its interim path.
- **R-4 R5 (arm carve-out, `DS`:74–78).** Written so to keep it apart from the risk-register class "R5 Scope" in `T`'s header (`E30.json`:473) and from E29-D05's "R-5" ("never colour alone", `E29-D05`:68). Submits need the arm on a LIVE account; a cancel, a close, a quantity-reducing amend, a take-profit change, attaching a missing native stop, tightening a stop and re-arming a protective trigger never do (`22`:15696–15699). A control refused on another leg (permission, view-as, kill switch) is T2 on that leg (`DS`:77–78), and attaching or tightening a stop is never refused on any leg (`22`:16185).
- **R-5 One motion definition.** D04 §19–§21 is the ticket's motion owner (`D04`:553); every motion row here cites its M row.
- **R-6 No scope in a string.** No announcement names an account, a balance or a count outside the caller's scope (`T` §Security; `D04`:228). The example strings in this review use placeholders (`{account}`, `{qty}`, `{symbol}`); the tool fails a quoted example that carries a fixture account or a decimal amount (G11).

### 7.2 The three points ruled, and one found on the read

- **(a) WCAG 2.4.3 on the `×`.** The `×` is first in the DOM (the header) and third in the Tab order. D03's reading is accepted (RULED BQ-08 (a)): the order is the DOM order read from the initial focus on Cancel, so the sequence still follows the visual order of header, body and footer (`D03`:156).
- **(b) SC 2.4.11.** The modal is compliant (CF-11). The SCR-077 drawer is non-modal, so `Tab` leaves it for the ticket while it stays open (`D03`:430: "non-modal: then the ticket"); a right-hand drawer of 420 px compact or 480 px comfortable (`D03`:200) over a 1280 × 800 workspace can sit over the ticket column, and nothing in D03 keeps it off the ticket's focused control. That is F-02 (Major, to D03: `Esc` closes the drawer and returns focus to [Details], the interim path). SC 2.4.11 itself fails only when the focused control is entirely hidden (`05`:99, "never fully hidden"); F-02's "never covers" is the stricter design target (the 2.4.12 reading), not the 2.4.11 floor.
- **(c) The time-out speech.** The deck string is the source (RULED BQ-08 (c)). D03's `confirm.timeout.announce` reads "Confirmation cancelled — nothing was sent" (`D03`:473); the shipped 0:00 speech reads "Confirmation timed out — nothing was sent" (`CD`:154). They differ: C-m, fixed in E30-S04's wiring of the shipped component.
- **(d) WCAG 2.2.1, found on the read — RULED (orchestrator, 2026-10-30, #2701 ledger addendum A row A33).** D03 argues that a time-out which resolves to the safe state needs no extend control (`D03`:110), and asks this review to confirm the argument (`D03`:733, Q10). On the criterion's letter the argument falls short: SCR-114's adjust range is 10–60 s around the 20 s default (`D03`:296), three times the default where 2.2.1's "Adjust" asks for ten, and the warning at 0:10 leaves 10 s to extend where "Extend" asks for at least 20. A33 records the limit as 2.2.1's essential exception: it is a safety control, because confirming an order against a market state older than the window would authorise a stale-priced submit, so an extend on demand would defeat it. The cross-walk records the exception (§14.2), E30-D06 lifts it (F-06, Minor; Q-03), and the owner's post-Done walkthrough may revisit it. Whatever the window, the guarantee is the server's: the arm token, live trading, the B18 kill switch and the order-entry gate are decided on the request, and risk checks the sized order after them (`24`:1674–1676); a leg lost mid-dialog, the arm lapsing included (LR-07), makes Confirm T2 before it is pressed (AR-04).

## 9. The error summary (RULED BQ-12)

`D02`:141 and `D02`:277 make the summary a CMP-027 `role="alert"` box that also takes focus on insertion (`D02`:345; `D02`:356). An alert role and a focus move read the same summary twice. The pattern, as ruled:

1. **Heading** "Can't submit — {n} problems" (`D02`:141), the box's first child, `tabindex="-1"`.
2. **Order.** One entry per blocking error, in DOM field order (the Tab order of `D02`:345), never in the order the errors arose.
3. **Link targets.** Each entry is a link naming the field and the problem; activating it moves focus to the field and opens a collapsed section first (the TP row, the limits disclosure).
4. **Focus on a submit failure.** On a press of the `aria-disabled` submit (it stays pressable, `D02`:141), focus moves to the heading: the one programmatic focus move on the panel (`D02`:661). **No `role="alert"` on the box while focus moves to it** — F-09 (Minor; the merged text reads twice, the interim path is that both readings say the same thing).
5. **Server refusals never move focus** (`05`:96). They are announced once by their banner's role (LR-17 to LR-23), and every input is retained (SC 3.3.7; `T` §Tech; `D02`:277; `D02`:281).
6. **Field messages** show on blur, as merged (`D02`:276), and are read with the field (LR-25).
