### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity** (#818's settled ruling). **Blocking:** a `05` clause or a WCAG 2.2 A / AA criterion with no compliant path in the parents, a state carried by colour alone, or a destructive step not perceivable non-visually. **Major:** any G-T2 miss (`DS`:15), and an at-risk clause on a trading or destructive step with no interim path. **Minor:** wording, cadence or order, or an at-risk clause whose interim path the parents already give. **Info:** recorded for a consumer.
- **R-2 Findings, dispositions and their closing** (BQ-10).
