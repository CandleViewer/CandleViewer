**Flash audit (the tool's §5):** the 250 ms `rules` coalescing is 4 updates / s **as data, 0 flashes** — row 8 swaps a word and a 10 px glyph, far under WCAG 2.3.1's area threshold (a flash counts only when it covers ≥25 % of a 10° field, ≈341 × 256 px at a typical viewing distance), and the one case that could alternate at that rate — a rule whose `last_run_status` flips ok / error — is **latched**: ERROR holds until the next `ok` run plus ≥1 s, so the word cannot flicker; the 300 ms validation debounce is 3.33 / s, **above the 3 / s bar, which is why row 5 has no flash by design**; the skeleton shimmer runs at 0.83 cycles / s on a 1,200 ms period (3 × `slow`, E11-D06 D16; 2.5 / s even if `slow` itself were the period) with a two-tone **relative-luminance change of 0.011** (`color.surface.hover` #22292F against `color.surface.raised` #161B22 — the tool prints it; WCAG 2.3.1's flash threshold is a change of 0.10; the earlier "1.17:1" was a contrast ratio, the wrong metric) and stops on data or at 5 s; nothing else loops; no motion lasts more than 5 s without a stop condition (`16` §7). **Reduced-motion statement (AC 4):** every animated row names its non-animated equivalent and the carrier that conveys the information without movement (row 4 animates nothing: the group never collapses); the tool asserts both cells non-empty for all fourteen rows.
