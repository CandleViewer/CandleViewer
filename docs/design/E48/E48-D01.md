## 1. Locked inputs (built on, never re-decided)

1. **Offline first.** SCR-119 makes no network call except the explicit update check and the bundle job (`14`:2523). The guide, the shortcuts, the glossary and the licences are bundled with the app. Nothing here assumes fetched content.
2. **Server truth.** Backend build data comes from `getBuildInfo`, available to every signed-in role, "no infrastructure detail beyond version and commit" (`22`:15436). The UI derives no version and invents no field; a field `BuildInfo` lacks is a G-row (§16).
3. **Security baseline (C-2.7, C-12.6).** No secret, API key, token, signature, password or credential appears on SCR-119, in the version table, in copied text or in a diagnostics bundle. Redaction is always on and is never a user option: no control, setting or copy offers to turn it off. Redaction is an allow-list that drops unknown fields (`TM48`:49), and the pre-write scan fails closed (`TM48`:50). A hit fails the job and leaves no archive (`22`:12459–12460). Bundles stay on this computer and are never uploaded automatically (`11`:5362). Generation is audited as `health.diagnostics_exported` (`22`:12461). §5 and §7 cite this item.
4. **Roles (BQ-03, AMEND).** The bundle job is Owner-only as served: `admin:read` (`22`:12464). Its control sits inside SCR-119, a surface every role sees, and E09-D09 never hides a control inside such a surface (`E09D09`:38, :44); its admin-domain row renders no verb (`E09D09`:116). So for a Manager or Viewer "Create diagnostics bundle…" is **T2**: `aria-disabled`, CMP-079 with the CMP-019 `lock` glyph, `rbac.owner_only` "Only the owner can {verb}.", rendering 2a, or the section's sentence form (DG-08, B15). The verb "create a diagnostics bundle" is PROPOSED and routed to E09-D09's owner as an amendment to its §3.2 verb table (G-T2.4; A-4). While the Owner views as another user, the control is T2 `rbac.view_as` (DG-12). The rest of SCR-119 is the same for every role. `TM48`'s Manager-scoped bundle is G-2 and C-5.
5. **No step-up on SCR-119.** No operation here names a step-up class; D8's per-action rule is not exercised (R-1).
6. **T2** (`E09D09`:49). A control the session may not use is disabled with a visible reason. It keeps its native role and its full label, led by the CMP-019 `lock` glyph ("Not available"), and its row names its rendering (G-T2.1c). G-T2.1b, verbatim (`DS`:28–29): `aria-disabled="true"`, never the `disabled` attribute — it stays in the Tab order and a menu item stays arrow-reachable. G-T2.2, verbatim (`DS`:40–42): hi-fi label `color.text.tertiary`, reason `color.text.secondary`; for primary / buy / sell actions the fill becomes `color.surface.raised` with a `color.border.default` hairline; **never `color.text.disabled`, never `opacity.disabled`** on the label, glyph or reason. G-T2.3, verbatim (`DS`:47–48): `Enter`, `Space` or a bound hotkey on a T2 control changes nothing and re-shows the reason inline with one polite announcement. The T2 controls here are Check for updates while offline (UC-08; its reason is a PROPOSED state key, OQ-1) and Create diagnostics bundle… for a Manager, a Viewer or a view-as session (DG-08, DG-12).
7. **Shell-only items are absent in a browser** (`11`:5223): never drawn disabled, always with one explanatory line (UC-07).

## 11. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- **Headings.** `h1` "Help & about"; one `h2` per block; accordion headers are buttons inside `h3`. No level is skipped.
- **The version table** is a real `table` with a caption and row headers (§5).
- **Licences.** A named region ("Third-party licences", `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby` its heading), keyboard-scrollable (`tabindex="0"`), collapsed by default and listed by licence.
- **Names.** Every link names its destination ("Read the accessibility statement", "Open keyboard shortcuts", "How detectors work"); never "click here". The copy control names exactly what it copies (§7.1).
- **Focus (R3).** Initial focus on the `h1`. Tab order follows the reading order: the copy control, the update control, the three links, then each accordion header. A dialog opens on Cancel and returns focus to its trigger on close. `Enter` in the window fields does not submit.
- **Live regions.** One polite region: "Version details copied.", "Up to date.", "Version {v} is ready.", "Bundle created." Only DG-05 is assertive, once.
- **Text resize (R1).** At 200 % zoom and the in-app text scale at 1280 × 800 the settings nav collapses to its menu button, the version table's values wrap under their headers, and the bundle dialog scrolls its body with its buttons in view.
- **Forced colours (R2).** Every state is a word; the callouts keep a `CanvasText` border; the focus ring uses `Highlight`.
