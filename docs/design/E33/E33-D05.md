### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity.** **Blocking:** a clause or a WCAG 2.2 A / AA criterion non-compliant with no compliant path in the merged designs, a status carried by colour alone, or an announcement with no string or politeness. **Major:** an at-risk clause, a name that discloses what a role may not see, a T2 over- or under-conversion, a contrast pair below its bar. **Minor:** wording, cadence or order, or an at-risk clause whose interim path the merged designs already give. **Info:** recorded for a consumer's charter. `05`:238's P0/P1 defect bar is E33-Q05's and is not used for design findings.
- **R-2 Findings and their closing (RULED BQ-7, confirmed).** An F row is a defect in a merged design against `05`, WCAG 2.2 or `T Sec`; it names one owner (a spec and its area, never an agent), one target (E33-D06 (#990), or the consuming story E33-S05 (#1105), E33-S06 (#1106) or E33-Q05 (#1100); a contrast row below its bar targets E05; a D04 entry row targets #988's owner) and a close state. A finding closes when it is routed (owner and target named, the consumer's acknowledgement at merge) or accepted with a written rationale, both under the owner's `approved`; "complete" in T DoD is read as "complete in the pack E33-D06 lifts" (C-21).
- **R-3 Verdicts.** `compliant` · `at risk` · `non-compliant` · `out of scope — <epic>` · `not yet decidable — routed to E33-Q05`. A `non-compliant` clause carries a Blocking F row; an `at risk` clause carries a Major F row or names its interim path.
- **R-4 T2.** D02 §1 and D03 §1.3 state the one T2 rule (D02:111; D03:101); every gated control here is read against it, and an over- or under-conversion is Major.
- **R-5 Never colour alone.** Every colour-carrying signal has a word, glyph, sign or `aria-*` state recorded in §9 (T AC3; `05`:107; D04:141); a status carried by colour alone is Blocking.
- **R-6 Motion.** `15`'s CMP-043 lines bind (`05`:209); D04 §7 is the epic's motion spec (D04:268–309); E10-D04's R-1..R-8 (E10-D04:83–90) are applied as PROPOSED, as E35-D04 did (E35-D04:71).

### 8.2 Algo-child fills and same-sample aggregation (RULED BQ-17: PROPOSED, owner approval pending (#2701))
