### 1.2 Method

Heuristic design over the memo's defaults and the merged specs; no session, no participant, no measured time; every evaluative statement `(heuristic)`; every number from the audit tool; every merged spec at its merge commit; every PROPOSED item stamped; the ticket's `C-x.y` ids verified verbatim (the tool's gate). The memo's restatement keys K1–K5 (:249–254) are the checklist for every paragraph written here (§4).

### 1.3 The two rules, stated once (every later section with a gated control cites "§1.3")

**T2 — disabled with a visible reason** (E09-D09 §2.2 :49, §3.1 :83–97, §3.2 :99–111, §9.1 :224–233, §9.3 :238–247; CMP-079 RbacGate `15` :799; CMP-019 Icon `15` :224). A control gated by a role, grant, environment, session or **registered state** keeps its native role and its full label; `aria-disabled="true"`, never the `disabled` attribute, so it stays a Tab stop and a menu item stays arrow-reachable; a CMP-019 `lock` glyph (`decorative=false`, name "Not available") leads the label in the same colour; the reason sits under it (2a inline; 2b tooltip echo only for icon-only controls and toolbar segments), in the DOM at mount and linked by `aria-describedby`; role legs are wrapped in CMP-079. Hi-fi: label `color.text.tertiary`, reason `color.text.secondary`, fill `color.surface.raised` with a `color.border.default` hairline; never `color.text.disabled`, never `opacity.disabled`. Keys: `Enter` / `Space` / a bound hotkey / an arrow onto a T2 radio changes nothing (a press stages nothing) and re-shows the reason once, politely. Reasons are `rbac.*` keys (the verb from §3.2: `orders:write` → "place orders", `killswitch:write` → "use the kill switch") or registered state keys; a screen never writes its own string, a reason never names data, ≤60 characters, one sentence, full stop. A read-only panel carries one notice every gated control points at. **The view-as leg comes first** on every write (`rbac.view_as` "Read-only while viewing as {user}."), one notice per frame; the controls that stay enabled under view-as are the protective writes: the account-scoped kill switch where the viewed user holds `killswitch:write` on that account (`22` :291–294, `24` §8.10 — the C-2.6 carve-out), and the exits — Cancel…, Cancel and flatten… and Cancel all… — which the standing ruling keeps enabled as protective writes where the target's own legs allow them (`22`'s carve-out list names no algo cancel yet: D03-C16, D02-C15's twin); every other algo verb is a write the server refuses while viewing as (`403 view_as_read_only`). Board layer `t2-reason`.

**The validity look is not T2** (E09-D09 §9.1 :230; E30-D02 D3 :128): a control disabled by form validity keeps its own fill and label at `opacity.disabled` (0.45 <!-- audit:opacity_disabled=0.45 -->), the reason under or beside it at full opacity in `color.text.secondary`, `aria-disabled`, focusable, a press shows the error summary; no lock glyph, no CMP-079, no key; layer `validity-reason`. Busy, "Checking with Bybit…", "Cancelling…" and "Loading algos from the server…" are **status** states (`aria-busy`); a completed, cancelled, halted or failed row is an **outcome**; a deferred capability is an **absence** (T1 or hidden-by-plan with a note), never a lock. Over-conversion is a defect.
