**The shipping form (§6.1).** The prototype missed every budget on this host, so each moving row is read as
"inconclusive" and its Duration cell leads with its minimal form (R353 BQ-11), one property at most
`motion.duration.fast`. D9-03 already is one property at `motion.duration.fast`, so its minimal form is its ruled
form and it ships as written. The halo of D9-05 and the canvas halo of D9-09 play over `motion.duration.fast`; the
tree half of D9-09 is DOM and keeps its timing. Their full forms, written after the minimal form in the cell and
described below, are restored only if E14-T04's reference-machine B8 bench passes (G-2).

What the three moving rows mean, beyond the table (each in its full form):

- **D9-03, the snap latch (BQ-06).** The marker is D02's 10 px ring with the value text "High 65,400" beside it
  (`D02`:332), in TP-13 `color.drawing.snap` (`D08`:131; R352 BQ-05). The value text is part of the static marker: it appears as
  a cut at the latch, in the existing drawing-label batch (`26`:224), so T's "no new text" holds because the motion
  adds none. The motion is the ring alone. Starts are capped per HS-1: at least 500 ms between settle starts on the
  marker; when latches change faster, the latest latch wins, nothing queues, and the ring is drawn at rest (a cut).
  The same entry serves weak and strong magnet and the keyboard snap cycle (`Ctrl+↑` / `Ctrl+↓`). The magnet mode is
  carried by the magnet button's name, never by motion. Nudges never snap (`D02`:332). The polite announcement keeps
  D02's cadence (`D02`:525). Budget: inside B8's ≤ 12 ms (`26`:684) and SCR-037's ≥ 58 fps (`14`:954), because it is
  the one row that runs during a drag.
- **D9-05, the selection emphasis (BQ-07).** The end state, the `color.drawing.selected` outline and the handles, is
  drawn in frame 0, and the halo only emphasises it: its colour is the end state's own, so the motion adds no meaning
  and no token. TP-11 and TP-13's focus hue stays with the active handle and the snap ring, and R352 BQ-05, carried at `D08`:19,
  keeps those two apart: "the 10 px ring + value text vs the 8 px dot keeps the two apart by shape". A press that
  crosses the drag travel threshold (`D02`:291) cuts the halo to rest in that frame (DNA-4). A new start on the same
  element follows §6's re-start rule: the latest start wins and nothing queues. Multi-select (the tree,
  Select area) emphasises nothing on the canvas: the count chip "7 selected" carries it (`D02`:283). More than 20
  objects selected at once means no halo, by HS-1's per-frame cap. Selection from the tree uses the same entry; the
  tree's coupled row is D9-13, a cut.
- **D9-09, the undo restore (BQ-08).** The pair is kept. On the canvas, the D9-05 halo plays on the restored object
  over `motion.duration.slow`, started in the same frame as the tree row's. The restored object is selected and
  focused (`D02`:428), so the static end state carries the answer too. In the tree, the restored row is selected again
  (`aria-selected="true"`, `D07`:216, :317), so its fill is already the opaque `color.surface.selected` (`D07`:207)
  and D07's flash from that colour to the resting fill changes no pixel. **R353 BQ-08 precision (RULED, orchestrator
  lens round 1, 6512980157): "the tree overlay is tinted `color.drawing.selected`", the halo's colour, so both
  surfaces say which one returned in one hue, "at peak opacity 0.25 → 0 over `motion.duration.normal`, row 3's easing;
  never a fill tween".** Row 3's easing is its fade-out curve, `motion.easing.accelerate` (`E05-D03`:69), not D07's
  `motion.easing.standard` (`D07`:216): D07 hands the curve to this spec (`D07H`:104), and E05-D03 row 3 governs
  (C-3). **CR-1 is RULED: E05-D03 row 3, restored-row use (RULED #353 BQ-08)**, cited by R353's comment id until
  E05-D03's PR 2 lands it as one more A-row carrying exactly that overlay, not row 3's `motion.duration.fast` exit. A
  press that crosses D02's drag threshold (`D02`:291) cuts the canvas halo and the tree overlay to rest in that frame
  (DNA-4); the undo's announcement and focus are already applied. A bulk undo (`D02`:537's "497 drawings deleted")
  emphasises at most 20 objects per frame, at most 3 halo starts in any 341 × 256 px region per 1 000 ms and at most
  21 824 px² of halo in any such region (§6). The 20 are the restored objects on screen, in the tree's order among
  them; a restored object off screen (D07's off-screen test, `D07`:313) gets no halo, only D07's "restored" tag and
  the count announcement, as do on-screen objects past the caps. An undone move cuts the geometry back to where it was
  (DNA-1) and then emphasises it at its restored place. Redo uses the same entry. D07's word "decorative" gets a
  one-line delta (§11): the emphasis now carries which object returned.

The cuts that touch persistence (BQ-09): creation (D9-11) and delete (D9-10) never animate (DNA-5), so no motion
implies a server state. Writes are optimistic (`26`:262); persistence is carried by words, the "not saved" tag and
chip and the save-failed path (`D02`:500; `D04`:297-300), which this spec cites and does not change. Delete's undo
affordance is E05-D03 row 4a's toast, which governs (C-5). A rejected delete puts the drawing back as a cut with the
unsaved or conflict tag (`D02`:500-501). That is T's failure-restore path, and it uses no motion.

## 3. The do-not-animate list

**T's four clauses, quoted byte for byte from `E14.json`:675 (§Scope / Deliverables, third bullet).**

- DNA-1: "price-anchored geometry never eases to a new position (it would misrepresent where the object is)"
- DNA-2: "live VWAP values never count up"
- DNA-3: "drag never has inertia"
- DNA-4: "nothing in the kit animates during an active drag except the snap indicator"

**The additions, each from a parent or T's own text.** The tool pins the bold sentence of each.

- **DNA-5. No drawing animates into existence or away.** Creation and delete are cuts (D9-10, D9-11), because a
  fade would imply a server state the write has not reached (T §Security notes; BQ-09).
- **DNA-6. The tree never scrolls smoothly to a coupled row.** R353 BQ-03 precision (RULED, orchestrator lens round 1,
  6512980157): "the coupled tree row comes into view in one frame — a jump, never a smooth scroll", and "D9-13 is that

**HS-1 and MR-1 for the repeating row.** D9-03 is the one row that can start again and again: with at least 500 ms

covers 153.9 px², 0.17 % of a 341 × 256 px region.

**HS-1 and MR-1 for a re-start on one element (D9-05, D9-09).** The latest start wins and nothing queues: a new start on
an element cuts any running D9-05 or D9-09 entry on it to rest in that frame, and plays from the peak (0.6 for the halo,
0.25 for the overlay) only if at least 500 ms have passed since that element's last start; inside 500 ms the element is
drawn at rest. D9-05 lasts 200 ms and D9-09 400 ms, both under the 500 ms gap, so a start that lands on a running entry
is always drawn at rest. Any half-open 1 000 ms window holds at most 2 starts per element, under MR-1's 3. E05-D03
counts a user-action one-shot as no flash (`E05-D03`:121-122); the cap holds anyway.

**HS-1 and HS-1b for the bulk restore.** A bulk undo of 497 drawings emphasises at most 20 and leaves at least 477 to
the "restored" tag and the count announcement; the 20 are restored objects on screen, in the tree's order among them,
as HS-1 puts what the user sees first (`E05-D03`:105-108), and an off-screen one gets the tag and the announcement
only (§2). HS-1b bounds a 341 × 256 px region (87 296 px²) to 21 824 px² of emphasised area (25 %). Its words are "In
the light theme" (`E05-D03`:109); this spec applies the same cap in every theme (C-4). A halo is modelled as 4 px ×
the length of the outline it widens. The longest line in one region is its diagonal (426.4 px): a line halo covers at
most 1 705.6 px², so 12 fit (20 467.2 px²) and a 13th does not (22 172.8 px²); 20 would cover 34 112.0 px². A
rectangle the size of the region has a 1 194.0 px outline: its halo covers 4 776.0 px², so 4 fit (19 104.0 px²) and a
5th does not (23 880.0 px²). The ellipse inscribed in it (Ramanujan's approximation) has a 942.5 px outline: its halo
covers 3 770.0 px², so 5 fit (18 850.4 px²) and a 6th does not (22 620.5 px²). The area cap therefore binds before
HS-1's count of 20 in a dense region, and sooner for an outline than for a line. HS-1b's start clause binds first. The

starts at most once per undo. The tree overlay is bounded by the same 20: at most 20 rows carry the 0.25 → 0

overlay with no layout on the chart container; a restored row beyond the 20 gets the "restored" tag only (`D07`:317).

## 8. The in-motion review, the protocol and the walkthrough (A28)

**BQ-13, RULED (orchestrator, 2026-10-30, #2701 ledger addendum A row A28).** For Done, "reviewed in motion" is met
by the orchestrator's delegated in-motion review on PR 2: the Opus lens plays the three prototypes through the runner

evaluation. The owner's own run is a walkthrough item after Done, not a gate: **owner walkthrough: post-Done
(#2701)**. No participant, timing or quote is invented.

**Heuristic evaluation (heuristic; no participants).**

- H-1 (heuristic). Visibility of system status: the snap settle (D9-03) shows the latch while the pointer moves, and
  the value text names it, so the user never has to infer the latch from the pointer's position.
- H-2 (heuristic). Recognition rather than recall: after an undo, the halo and the row overlay (D9-09) answer "which
  one returned" without the user recalling what they deleted; the "restored" tag keeps the answer for 10 s.
- H-3 (heuristic). Error prevention: no geometry eases (DNA-1, DNA-7), so no frame shows a level at a price it does
  not have.
- H-4 (heuristic). Aesthetic and minimal design: ten of thirteen moments are cuts, and the three moving rows each
  animate one property, once.

**Session protocol (3 participants; for the owner to run, not run here).** Setup: the PR 2 prototype at 200
drawings, normal motion, then `?motion=reduce`. Task 1: "select the drawing under the pointer where three overlap;
say which one you picked." Task 2: the facilitator deletes two drawings and presses `Ctrl+Z`; "after an undo, name
which object returned." Task 3: "drag an anchor until it snaps to a High; say the value." Record per participant:
the answer, whether it was right, and the mode. Pass: each participant names the restored object in both modes.

**Owner walkthrough checklist (≤ 10 items; posted on #353 with PR 2).** W1 `?play=snap`: of about 90 latches at the 240
px/s sweep, 6 start a settle (at most 2 in any 1 s), and each is cut to rest by the next latch within 1–2 frames; a held
latch shows the full 100 ms settle; the ring and its value text go together when the drag ends; W2 the value text
appears with no motion; W3 `?play=select`: one halo fade on the picked object; W4 a drag that starts mid-halo or
mid-restore cuts the halo and the row overlay; W5 `?play=undo`: the halo and the row overlay start together; W6 a bulk
undo emphasises at most 20; W7 every entry again with `?motion=reduce`, the carrier present; W8 no drawing fades in or
out on create or delete.

## 12. The audit tool

`python3 -I -B docs/design/_tools/e14_d09_motion_audit.py --check` (also `--json` and `--self-test`; `--source
NAME=FILE` re-checks the pinned ruling words against a saved copy of R352, R353, A28 or L1). Stdlib only and offline; it
reads every cite at `57348a8ea3` with `git show`, the token names from the token JSON there, and
`proto-results.json` from the working tree. Ten gates:

- G1-TABLE: §2 holds D9-01 to D9-13 in order, every cell filled and no Carrier or reduce cell a dash; the moving rows
  are exactly D9-03, D9-05 and D9-09; each row's layer is the ruled one; a cut reads the instant pair, Property
  `none`, "identical (a cut)" and an AC 1 cell that starts "static is not worse:"; a moving row's AC 1 cell starts
  "carries:"; only D9-03 runs during a drag, and D9-05 and D9-09 state the cut to rest at the drag threshold.
- G2-TOKEN: every backticked token name exists at the base, or is one of D08's two proposed names; no motion token but
  the seven; each moving row names its ruled duration and easing, led by its minimal form when §6.1's verdict,
  recomputed from `proto-results.json`, is "inconclusive".
- G3-PROPERTY: a canvas half animates one instance alpha or one per-instance scale; a DOM half only `opacity` or
  `transform`; every moving canvas row costs "+0 draw calls"; every row with a DOM layer says "no layout on the chart
  container".
- G4-DNA: DNA-1 to DNA-4 byte for byte against `E14.json`:675; DNA-5 to DNA-8 with their pinned sentences; the DNA
  ids applied on their rows.
- G5-ARITH: §6's frames, bytes, starts, counts and areas recomputed from the tokens and HS-1, HS-1b and MR-1.
- G6-RULED: A28's stamp (in §1 and in §8) and "owner walkthrough: post-Done (#2701)", CR-1's cite, the §7.5 title, the
  BQ-11 sentence, `Partial: PR 2 of 3`, the R352 words and the three R353 precisions, each exact; D9-09, D9-12 and D9-13
  carry their precision's words and none of the words it removed.
- G7-CITE: every cite in range at the base and every cited span pinned to its words, so a moved cite fails; each cited
  file identical at `3bac78e344` (`26` bar its +2 at :350) and E05-D03 identical at `29fe3d5b2e`; no cite into `16` §7's recipe lines.
- G8-HYGIENE: the public-text list (in this file and the tool), local paths, hosts, closing keywords, ids and any
  wording that leaves a ruled item open.
- G9-BOARDS: §10's six boards, five dark and the light twin of 03, PENDING.
- G10-RESULTS: `proto-results.json` says "the prototype is not the engine", ran 200 drawings for 5 repetitions on

  without it (+0), uploads at most §6's bytes per frame, and emphasises at most 20 in the bulk restore; the eight play
