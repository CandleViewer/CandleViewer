**Gesture collisions with SCR-030.** The register (D02 §10, 27<!--=COUNT:d02.collisions--> rows, lifted by hash) is the

drawing gesture uses a modifier-click on the plot while chart trading is armed (`D02`:266), and where a handle's
target overlaps an order, SL / TP or position line's tolerance the press goes to the trading layer (`D08`:176). The
E31 modifier-click reconciliation stays where D02 routed it.
