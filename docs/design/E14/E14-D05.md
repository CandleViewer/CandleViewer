### 1.2 What these tools touch (normative)

A Fibonacci ladder, an anchored VWAP and a measure ribbon are analysis ink: none places, amends or cancels an order, and no number on them is an order quantity, a risk or a balance. The only server writes are workspace writes — `createDrawing` / `updateDrawing` under `workspaces:write` (`22:8834`, `22:8951`) when a fib or a VWAP is committed or edited, and the measure's **Pin**, which is a `createDrawing` (D04 §4.4, in flight). Every trading question on the same plot — the position tool, the order and SL / TP lines underneath — is D06's and D02 §5.7's and is routed there by citation. Reads are never gated: a Viewer and a view-as session see every level, band, badge and ribbon number as D02 §5.1 `:266` fixes (the locked behaviour, a read, T1).

### 1.3 The three budgets and the four kinds of "no"

**Budgets (carried forward verbatim, never loosened):** (1) **all drawing labels in a pane share one text draw call** — `26` §3.7 `26:224` ("drawing labels a third. ## 3. The label-collision algorithm (D2; DoD 2; AC 1; Q9, RULED — 6371750538) — the tool's section 3 runs every step

**Inputs:** the level list sorted by price; each level's pixel `y` at the current scale; the label box height **h = 16 px** (the `type.ui.caption` line box 14 + the 1 px pane-colour outline on both sides); the gap **g = 2 px**; the side from §2.4 and the room on it; the span between the anchors in px. **Priority classes (deterministic):** **P0** {0, 1} · **P1** {0.5} · **P2** {0.618, 0.382} · **P3** {0.786, 0.236} · **P4** every other level, ordered by distance from 0.5 descending, ties by value ascending — for the scenario's eight levels the order is **0, 1, 0.5, 0.382, 0.618, 0.786, 0.236, 0.886**. **Status (the Q9 ruling, 6371750538):** the algorithm is a **PROPOSED engine rule** routed to E14-S04's implementer, who may change the mechanism as long as the frames' outcomes reproduce — P0 / P1 survive at any span, the cap of step 6, the budget of step 7; the five fixed spans of the table below (400 / 120 / 60 / 30 / 12 px) are the spec's **test vectors**, and the tool's `collide()` is the reference implementation.

1. **The side and the form (pass 1).** The side is §2.4's: right → left in the full form, else the wider side in the short form, else inside. The form is **full** when the chosen side's room (inside: the ladder's own width) holds the widest full label plus the 8 px inset (98 px in the scenario), else **every** label drops to its short form (the percentage alone); the price stays in the tooltip and in D04's level row. A ladder is never mixed-form.
2. **Place P0 (pass 2, first).** Centre the boxes of 0 and 1 on their lines (inside: the box sits above the line). If the two boxes meet (the lower top < the upper top + h + g) **both move outward**: 1's box sits above its line, 0's below (flipped in the inverted orientation), so the two never collide at any span. A P0 box the pane clamp moves (step 8) **flips inward** instead — 1's below its line, 0's above — and, if still blocked, takes the next free column inward; P0 never drops.
3. **Walk the rest by priority.** Keep the occupied intervals `[top − g, top + h + g]` of column 1; a level's label is drawn iff its box meets no kept interval.
4. **0.5 never drops.** When 0.5's box is blocked in column 1 it moves to the **first free column inward** — column 2 at x = column 1 − (`w_max` + g) on the right and inside sides, + (`w_max` + g) on the left side (§2.4; 92 px in the scenario) — so 0, 0.5 and 1 survive at every span; no P2–P4 level ever leaves column 1 (the pane-edge case of step 8 is the only one with a third column).
5. **Dropped levels keep their lines** (the ladder stays complete; only the label is suppressed).
6. **The cap** of one column between the anchors is `floor((span + g) / (h + g))` labelled levels — the "maximum simultaneous labelled levels before auto-thinning" the ticket asks for; the two outward P0 boxes and the column-2 box lie outside it, which is why three labels survive when the cap is 0.
7. **The per-pane glyph budget (PROPOSED fraction).** From B3's measured case (`26:229`: 12,500 glyphs in one draw call at ≤ 2.0 ms) the drawing-label batch may hold **0.25 × 12,500 = 3,125 glyphs** per pane. **The budget is additive**, not a share of the measured case: the cell batch and the drawing-label batch together are 125 % of the measured 12,500, which no measured case covers — stated here and routed as an open engine question in §13.2 (DoD 6). One full eight-level ladder is 106 glyphs (29 ladders fit; 9 at 24 levels); when the pane exceeds the budget, **unselected** ladders lose their labels in the order P4 → P3 → P2 → full P0 / P1 → the short form, top-most z first kept; at SCR-037's 200 drawings as 200 ladders the stages measure 21,200 → 18,400 → 12,800 → 7,200 → **1,800 glyphs**, where the budget holds — every unselected ladder shows 0 / 0.5 / 1 in the short form, the selected one its full set. The tail chip (19 glyphs), the marker's caption (11) and the ribbon chip (25) are counted in the same batch. Four of these glyphs — `(`, `)`, `·` and the minus U+2212 — lie outside `26:220`'s atlas set (the tool's glyph gate names them): §16.2 A9 asks the chart-engine owner for them; until the atlas has them the **interim** is the hyphen-minus for U+2212, a space for `·` and the word without its parentheses — stated, never a silent substitution.
8. **The pane clamp.** Every label box is clamped to the plot's inner margin (8 px inside the top and bottom edges) before it is tested; a clamped outward P0 box flips inward (step 2). At the scenario's 12 px span with the ladder's top anchor on the plot's top edge, 0 keeps column 1, 1 takes column 2 and 0.5 column 3 — the only case with a third column (`SCR-037/fib-edge-clamp`).
9. **Several ladders in one gutter (pane-level).** Ladders whose label columns overlap share one interval list per column; the walk runs over them in order — the selected ladder first, then descending `zIndex` — in two passes: **A** places every ladder's P0 and P1, **B** every ladder's P2 → P4. A label of one ladder is therefore never drawn over another ladder's, and the selected ladder's 0 / 0.5 / 1 are placed before any other label (`SCR-037/fib-two-ladders`: the selected ladder loses only 0.786 to the second ladder's 1; the second keeps 5 of 8 with its 0.5 in column 2).
10. **Complexity.** The walk is O(n log n) per ladder per viewport change with n ≤ 24 levels (a sorted interval list per column; O(n²) naive, bounded either way), run on zoom, pan, resize and a level edit — never per frame; a live tail never re-runs it.
11. **Time zones** run the same walk on the x axis with the label width in place of h (§4).

### 5.4 The distinguishability rule (AC 2; Q8, RULED — 6371750538; the halo's extra draw PROPOSED and bench-gated)

The indicator VWAP is tier 0 (solid 2 px, a slot hue) with B0 bands and its value in the legend row (E13-D03 `:219`, `:189`); a user may give the drawing a slot colour (D04 CR-1 makes slot hues shared), so **hue is never the carrier**. The anchored VWAP **drawing** differs by **two normative carriers, present in every viewport that shows a segment**: (1) **the anchor marker**, always drawn and **clamped** to the pane's inner left margin when the anchor is off-screen (an indicator has no anchor on the plot); (2) **the on-plot value chip** at the tail, **clamped** to the inner right margin when the tail is off-screen (indicators carry values in the legend, never on the plot) — so the both-ends-off-screen viewport still shows both (`SCR-037/vwap-panned`). The third mark, (3) **the 1 px pane-colour halo** under the curve and the band edges (indicators have none), is a **contrast device, not an identity carrier on its own**: it is the pane colour, so it shows only where the curve crosses candles or cells and vanishes on empty canvas; where it shows it survives a same-hue pick, greyscale, forced-colors and hc. **The rule as overlays grow:** clamped anchor marker + clamped on-plot chip (+ the halo over data ink) = a drawing; legend row + no marker + no chip = an indicator; drawings never take E13-D03's dash tiers (identity) or `dash.4-3` (estimated) except the approximated prefix of §6.3; two anchored VWAP drawings differ by colour, anchor and chip. **Consistency with US-IND-007** (`11:1459`, `11:1475`): the same kernel, the same σ naming "1σ / 2σ / 3σ", the same off-by-default bands; the one intended difference is where the value lives (chip vs legend) `(heuristic)`. Engine cost: one extra instanced line draw per polyline batch for the halo — **PROPOSED and bench-gated** (the Q8 rider, 6371750538): the §13.2 card and A7 state the per-frame cost in E11-D06 §10's register shape and name the fallback if the engine bench refuses it — **no halo; the clamped marker and the clamped chip alone carry the identity**, and the label outline (`26:222`) stays. The both-ends-off-screen case (`SCR-037/vwap-panned`) was ruled with Q8; the alternative — a 3 px weight tier 0 does not use — is recorded as rejected for ink.

## 8. The density study and the ink budget (D7; AC 4; DoD 3) — the tool's sections 7 and 8

**The composite** (`SCR-030/density-composite`, dark; its light twin on board 09): B3's footprint at benchmark density — 100 bars × 25 rows of 12 px cells = 2,500 cells (`26:661`) — with the 8-level ladder over 400 px, the anchored VWAP with three band pairs **with fills on at 0.05 as the worst case**, and the ribbon. The board draws a representative 12 × 10 cell block with the density stated and the rest as a hatched "2,500 cells" region (never 2,500 shapes).

**Coverage of the 360,000 px² cell field (measured):** opaque drawing ink **10.58 %** in total — fib lines 0.92 %, fib labels 3.06 %, the VWAP curve with its halo 1.33 %, the six band edges 2.00 %, the ribbon outline 0.39 %, the ribbon chip 1.62 %, the tail chip 0.93 %, the marker's "(estimated)" caption 0.33 %; **tinted** area — the ±3σ zone (6σ = 587 px) is taller than the 300 px field, so with fills on the band fills tint **100 %** of the field and the ribbon fill a further 33.33 % inside it (the union is 100 %). The labels are the largest opaque item, which is why §3 bounds them.

**Every pair, measured (the full matrix is §10.1; the fill composited over the ink and over the canvas — the layer order the spec uses, `26` §3.1):** the candle bodies and the wick keep ≥ 3:1 (`05:128`, item 4) over the three-stack band fill at 0.05 (worst **3.54**, the down body, dark / hc; 4.10 light), over the flattened fill at the 0.20 ceiling (worst **3.13** dark / hc; 3.76 light) and over the ribbon fill (worst 4.27); **the footprint cell text** — the element AC 4 names — keeps ≥ 4.5:1 against its outline under every default fill case (rows 37–39: the three-stack at 0.05 **7.75 / 12.15 / 8.65**, the ribbon fill 9.79 / 15.11 / 11.01, the flattened 0.20 fill 6.70 / 10.45 / 7.48 — the outline is E06-D01 §5's `palette.neutral.0` at 0.8 over the cell fill in dark and hc, the worst of the three fills, and the pane colour in light where E06-D01 measures no pair, stated as the assumption) and falls to **3.22 / 4.62 / 3.45** under a three-stack at 0.20 (row 40, reported) — so the cell text is the second element behind D04's flatten switch; a **three-stack at 0.20** (a user raising `fill_opacity` to the ceiling with three bands on) measures **1.76 dark / 2.39 light** — under the bar — which, with the cell text under the same stack (row 40), is the element **placed behind D04's flatten switch** (in flight, G-T4): the switch flattens the stack to one fill at the ceiling, and the ceiling itself is this hue's 0.20. The drawing colour **over a cell fill** measures 1.14 / 1.34 / 1.14 (bid), 1.82 / 1.30 / 1.82 (ask), 1.16 / 1.18 / 1.16 (imbalance) and 1.14–1.82 over the candle bodies — adjacent pairs, reported not gated, and the reason for **the halo rule**: every drawing line on these three tools (the level lines, the curve, the band edges, the time-zone verticals, the ribbon outline) carries the 1 px pane-colour halo, so the pair the eye reads is line-on-canvas (8.50 / 3.74 / 8.50) wherever the line crosses data ink; a label's pane-colour SDF outline does the same for text. The cell text's own pair (`color.text.primary` on the cell fills, 1.54–3.80) is E06-D01's, drawn with the mandatory outline (`26:222`), and is reported, never gated here.

**The ink-budget rules (stated once):** (1) labelled levels per column ≤ the §3 cap, 0 / 0.5 / 1 always; (2) the drawing-label batch ≤ 3,125 glyphs per pane (PROPOSED fraction), the cross-drawing thinning order of §3 step 7; (3) lines 1–2 px (`style.width` ≤ 8 is the contract's; the default ladder uses 2 and 1), every line with its halo (bench-gated — Q8; if the engine bench refuses the halo the adjacent pairs above are reported, not answered, and the label outline stays); (4) chips never over cell text — the gutter and flip rules of §2.4 / §5.1 / §7.1 keep every chip on the canvas or at the pane edge; (5) fills ≤ 0.05 by default and off by default on the VWAP; a stack above the ceiling is flattened; (6) the hatch under the approximated prefix costs +1 draw call per pane only while fills are on. `SCR-030/density-flattened` shows the same composite with the flatten switch on; `SCR-030/density-composite-light` and `SCR-030/density-flattened-light` are their light twins on board 09 (the light pairs of §10.1 — the worst gated pair there is 3.22, the selected outline). Nothing in the composite is waived: the one failing element (the three-stack at 0.20 — the candle inks and the cell text under it) is behind the switch, the adjacent pairs are solved by the halo, and the labels are bounded by the cap and the budget `(heuristic: at 10.58 % opaque ink the candle bodies and the cell numbers of the sample block stay readable on the board; the measured pairs are the evidence, the eye is the check the owner makes)`.
