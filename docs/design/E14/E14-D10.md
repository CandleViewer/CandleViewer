### 8.2 The combined rule (PROPOSED default 1)

`E06-D03`:105 caps the cursor region at two writes a second while a key is held. Read for the drawing band: **the chart panel's polite writes — the cursor region (RG-02) and the drawing announcements (RG-03) together — stay at or below two in any one second** (C-v, to E06-D03's owner, applied through E14-D12; not an edit). The shell pair, the tree and the toast host keep their own numbers (BG-09, BG-10, BG-12). Without the rule a held nudge key (2 per s) plus one commit puts three writes in one second: that is F-01. While a tool is armed or placing, the arrows move the pending anchor and RG-03 speaks its value; RG-02 stays silent (C-j), so the two never double one key.

### 8.3 The collision order (`T AC4`)

When two writes would fall inside one second, in this order: (1) an assertive row is never coalesced or dropped (`05`:148); (2) a user-action confirmation is never dropped and is serialised at least 500 ms after the write before it; (3) a state entry waits behind class 2 and is dropped only by a newer write of the same fact; (4) a coalesced value: **latest wins, the older one is dropped, the trailing flush is kept.** So the one dropped is always an older value. **A pointer drag writes nothing per move** (BG-06), so a drag cannot overrun the reader.

### 8.4 The worst case, simulated by the tool

The tool runs 60 s of: a nudge key held throughout, a snap-cycle press at 40.2 s, a commit every 5 s from 2.5 s, a delete at 21 s and its undo at 23 s, an unsaved episode at 30.5 s, and the key's release at 60 s. It asserts: the maximum writes in any 1 s window across RG-02 and RG-03 = 2 <!-- audit:sim.max-per-1s=2 --> (≤ 2); every dropped write is class 4 (0 <!-- audit:sim.dropped-non-class4=0 --> non-class-4 drops); every class-2 write is spoken (14 <!-- audit:sim.class2-spoken=14 --> of 14 <!-- audit:sim.class2-total=14 -->); the trailing flush after release is spoken (kept <!-- audit:sim.flush-kept=kept -->).
