## Method note (agent-delivery adaptation, owner decision 2026-09-25)

The ticket calls for 4–6 moderated sessions on clickable prototypes or competitor tools. Delivery is a solo agent with one human owner, so — as `e16-recorder-mental-model.md`, `e22-tape-reading.md` and `e30-d01-session-protocol.md` did — the sessions are replaced by (1) the heuristic evaluation of §2 against the contracts, the shipped vocabulary and the upstream design decisions, (2) the tool's audit of what the documents actually say (§3.1) and the vendor documentation fetched 2026-10-21 (§3.2), and (3) the protocol of §7 and the checklist of §8 for the owner. No prototype exists for SCR-055..059 (E25-D02 is next sprint), so the protocol's stimuli are the boards and the named vendor pages until D02's frames exist. The two new problems the ticket names — an estimate neither ignored nor over-trusted, and a regime label that must not read as advice — are treated as mechanisms to design against (§2 F21, F26–F29), not as preferences to poll.

## 5. The `methodology.opened{detector}` baseline (heuristic until there is real usage)

- **Event definition (PROPOSED):** one event per drawer open, `detector` = the section the drawer opened at (`iceberg | stop_run | absorption | exhaustion | big_trade | regime`, the `DetectorMethodology.detector` enum), plus a `source` dimension the contract lacks (`scr055 | scr056 | scr057 | scr059 | cmp134 | scr050 | footprint`, G6); a regime open from SCR-057's "why this classification?" emits **both** `regime.explain_opened` and `methodology.opened{detector: regime}` and is counted once as an open (the former is the click, the latter the read).
- **Denominator:** with ≤6 users (owner + ≤5 managers) rates are counts, so the yardstick is **coverage per (user, detector)**, not a percentage of impressions (none are tracked). The audit log supplies the decision-point denominator: `detector.enabled_changed`, rule arming with "Uses estimated detectors? yes" (SCR-083), `*.alert_created`.
- **Targets (PROPOSED):** (1) **first contact** — within 7 days of a detector first being visible to a user, ≥1 open per (user, detector): 100 % coverage; (2) **before consequence** — 100 % of estimated-detector enablings and rule armings by a user are preceded by ≥1 open of that detector's section by that user, ever; (3) **steady state** — ≥1 open per user per 4 weeks across all detectors while any estimated badge is visible; (4) **after a parameter change** — ≥50 % of `detector.threshold_changed` are followed within 10 minutes by an open of that section (the thresholds in force are in the drawer).
- **What a low rate means:** target 1 or 3 at zero = badge blindness or an undiscoverable affordance (the F26 counter-measures failed, or the chip is on exact data and was learned away); target 2 below 100 % = over-trust at a decision point, the callout variant is not doing its job. **What a high rate means:** many re-opens of the same section by the same user within a session = the inline tooltip does not pre-answer "what does estimated mean" (F29), or the drawer is being used as the parameter display SCR-059 should be.
- **Review:** read at the end of S13 and again four weeks after R2 ships; the first real numbers replace this section as a dated addendum.

## 6. The stop-run comprehension test (protocol; nothing here has been run)

Stimulus: one still of SCR-030 with a single stop-run marker at a swept low (hollow chevron, direction, magnitude text) and **no legend, no chip tooltip open, no explanation**; a second still, counterbalanced, with the breakout twin (US-DET-007 sc. 3). Think-aloud throughout; the moderator never asks "is this clear?".

1. **Meaning, unprompted:** "Tell me what happened in the market here, in your own words." Code the answer: sweep-and-revert (correct) / breakout / liquidation / a signal to trade / unknown.
2. **Trust and use:** "If this appeared live, what — if anything — would you do with it?" then "How much would you trust it, 1 to 5, and what makes you say that?" Flag any answer that treats the marker as an instruction or a prediction.
3. **The word:** "There is a chip that says estimated. Say in your own words what that means for this marker." Code the belief: precision / provenance / reliability / other (the AC2 **before** field).
4. **The drawer:** open SCR-058 at the stop-run section (plain-language summary first; the thresholds in force; the failure modes; "this is an estimate, not exchange-provided fact"). Let the participant read; no commentary.
5. **Re-ask 1–3 verbatim.** Record the shift in meaning code, trust 1–5 and belief category (the AC2 **after** field); then "Explain the marker back to me as if I had never seen it."
6. **Discrimination:** show the breakout twin: "Same thing or different? How do you know?"

Pass / decision: the marker is kept if ≥3 of 4 participants code it as sweep-and-revert **after** the drawer and nobody reads it as an instruction after the drawer; if any participant reads it as an instruction **after** the drawer the copy (not the glyph) is reworked and re-tested; a trust shift of ≥2 points in either direction is reported per participant, never averaged.
