## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

**Purpose.** SCR-054 has three moving things and a pane that can go stale (`T` Context): a per-second countdown, low-frequency OI and funding values, and bursty liquidation prints. For each, this spec fixes the property animated (if any), the tokens, the reduced-motion equivalent with its carrier, and the cadence it may not exceed.

**Method.** A desk spec from merged text, every cite re-read at the base. Nothing was run, and every evaluative sentence is `(heuristic)`. The default is less: a transition is kept only where it helps the eye find a change, and never where the static design already carries it.

- **R-1 The cadence rule.** No motion lasts longer than the period of the data that starts it, and no motion starts faster than that data arrives (`T` Scope; §3).
- **R-2 Suppressed, never queued.** A transition that would overlap the previous one, start inside a cascade or start in an over-budget frame is skipped. Its end state is applied in that frame. It is never queued, stretched or replayed (`T` Scope; §5, §8).
- **R-3 The loop's clock.** Canvas motion (the liquidation emphasis, the stale ramp, the collapse clip) integrates real `dt`, never a frame count, as `E11D06`:99 D23 requires. No CSS transition touches canvas content.
- **R-4 No information in motion.** Every moment's end state carries its meaning in text, a glyph or geometry, identical in both motion modes (`E05D03`:118, MR-3).
- **R-5 Announcements never wait for motion,** and no transition moves focus.
- **R-6 Safety and staleness first.** A stale chip, an error or the < 5 min warning appears in the frame it is known, in both modes; no transition delays it. The < 5 min warning is a cut, like E11-D06's countdown rows 15 and 17 and D15 (`E11D06`:317, :319, :91) and E47-D08's I-18 (`E47D08`:158).

## 3. The cadence rule in numbers (R-1; G-CADENCE)

- **M-01.** The countdown changes once per 1000<!--=BUD:countdown.ms--> ms, on one shared 1 s timer (`D02`:497), and swaps its text in one frame, so it has no motion to outrun.
- **M-02.** OI and funding arrive at ≤ 1 Hz and render on change only (`14`:1335), so at most one change per 1000<!--=BUD:data.ms--> ms. The emphasis lasts `motion.duration.fast`, inside `T`'s 120<!--=BUD:flash.max.ms--> ms with 20<!--=BUD:flash.room.ms--> ms to spare. HS-1 asks for at least 500<!--=BUD:hs1.ms--> ms between starts per cell (`E05D03`:105). The data's period is twice that, so a cell starts at most 1<!--=BUD:flash.per.s--> flash per second, inside MR-1's 3 (`E05D03`:100).
- **M-03.** The liquidation emphasis starts at most 1<!--=BUD:fr5.starts--> time per panel per 1000<!--=BUD:fr5.ms--> ms (E47-D08 FR-5), and the region admits at most 2<!--=BUD:fr3.starts--> starts from every source together in any 1 000 ms window (FR-3; `E47D08`:191, :193). A burst inside the window updates the size text and the count only, never re-starts the overlay (`E47D08`:247). The emphasis lasts `motion.duration.fast`, inside its 1 000 ms window.
- **M-04.** Buckets are 250<!--=BUD:bucket.ms--> ms (`14`:1335). D02 §6's "one insertion per side per bucket" is an insertion cap, not an animation licence (BQ-09, RULED (orchestrator, 2026-11-02, issue comment 6573059855)): as an animation it would allow 8<!--=BUD:bucket.cap.starts--> starts per second in one region (two sides × four buckets), four times FR-3. So the row and the bar are cuts, like I-23.
- **M-05 to M-07** start on an event or a user action, never on a data clock.

## 4. The value-change emphasis (M-02; BQ-08)

E05-D03's value-flash recipe applies with one change of tint, its figures unchanged: peak 0.25, `fast`, `accelerate` (BQ-08, RULED (orchestrator, 2026-11-02, issue comment 6573059855)). The shape is unchanged: an opacity-only overlay behind the digits, entering as a cut at the peak and decaying over `motion.duration.fast` · `motion.easing.accelerate`, at most 20 emphasised cells per frame, the latest wins and nothing queues (`E05D03`:69; `16`:213). Row 3's two further clauses hold too (`E05D03`:105, :109; `16`:234): a cell that has changed at ≥ 1 Hz for 10 s stops emphasising until it is quiet, so an OI or funding feed at its 1 Hz ceiling stops M-02 after 10 s, while its digits keep updating; and in the light theme HS-1b caps the emphasised area and the starts in any 341 × 256 px rectangle.

- **The tint.** The overlay uses `color.surface.selected`, not `color.buy.default` or `color.sell.default`. An OI rise is not a buy, and D03 gives the funding rate no side hue: its sign is text (`D03`:150). A side hue here would invent a direction the data does not carry. The neutral hue departs from row 3, so it is C-4, **PROPOSED to the owner** and unmeasured. The emphasis never plays on a stale or non-live value (`16`:213, :234).
- **What carries the change.** The new digits and their sign, in both modes. Under reduce the overlay is removed, with no momentary text tint (`E05D03`:91), and nothing is lost.
- **Flash counts.** At most 1 start per second per cell (§3). The neutral overlay's luminance step on `color.surface.raised` is small, so `05`'s flash audit treats it as an emphasis. The flash-rate budget itself is E47-D08's (#1307; #5444): cited by FR-n id, not duplicated, and each emphasis counts toward FR-3's region total (BQ-07).

## 5. Liquidation prints and the cascade (M-03, M-04; BQ-09 overridden, BQ-10)

**The rule (BQ-09, RULED (orchestrator, 2026-11-02, issue comment 6573059855)).** A bucket is a feed row: its row and its bar enter as cuts, like E47-D08's I-23 tape row. The one liquidation animation is E47-D08 I-24's emphasis, and this spec places it on the bar of the bucket that holds the print. It is an opacity overlay in `color.liquidation.flash` (PROPOSED by E47-D08 §6; its value E47-D10's), at most one start per panel per 1 000 ms (FR-5), within the region's two (FR-3), with the arbiter ranking it first (`E47D08`:247). A burst inside the window updates text only; nothing queues (R-2). The feed inserts one row per side per bucket, at the top, with no slide, frozen under focus, the pointer or `Pause` (`D02`:243), in both modes. Sources: E47-D08 §3 / §4.1 / §6 at #5444 `e60fe7baa`, if #5444 merges as is; E24-D02 §6 / §13; E05-D03 MR-1 and HS-1b (`E05D03`:100, :109).

**The cascade threshold (PROPOSED to the owner, G-1).** No source names a value for when the rate counter shows: the entry and exit rates are E24-S05's (`D02`:225). This spec proposes 10<!--=BUD:cascade.threshold--> prints/s over the last 1 000 ms, to the owner. At or above it the counter shows and LR-4 summaries run. The state ends only after D02's minimum dwell of ≥ 5 s below the exit rate (`D02`:225); the exit rate, like the entry value, is E24-S05's (G-1).

**Budget.** At `14`'s target of 500<!--=BUD:cascade.rate--> prints/s for 10<!--=BUD:cascade.s--> s, the emphasis starts at most once per second, whatever the rate. The panel keeps its 2<!--=BUD:script.ms--> ms per frame of scripting for the counter, the bars and one insertion per side per bucket (`14`:1335; `D02`:492). The emphasis costs one alpha uniform on one bar (R-3), measured in the owner's run (O-2).

## 7. Reduced motion (`05` §7)

**Which source wins.** The in-app override (SCR-117, `Ctrl+Shift+M`) wins over the OS `prefers-reduced-motion` in both directions; `system` defers to the OS. The resolved value is E05-D03's precedence table, cited and not restated (`E05D03`:206–217; `16`:218).

**The two reduce forms** (BQ-06, read as E12-D10 C-3 does): _remove_, the end state at frame 0 (M-02, M-03, M-05, M-06), and _already a cut_ (M-01, M-04, M-07) (`16`:207). `05` §7 item 2's allowance of "a single low-amplitude cross-fade" is not used; reduce is always a cut (E05-D03 §3: every reduce cell `none`; BQ-15).

**Canvas calm** (E47-D08's third tier: `disable_canvas_animation` on with `data-motion="safe"`; `E47D08`:123–133, if #5444 merges as is). The canvas moments behave as under reduce: M-03 is none (`E47D08`:247), and M-05 and M-06 are instant (I-31, `E47D08`:171). M-02 is a DOM recipe, so it follows `data-motion` only and runs. Canvas calm also turns the engine's `heatmapFade` off; what that does to the per-redraw decay is A-4's question.

**The walk** (`T` AC, animation disabled): a new value is read from its digits and sign; a new print from its glyph and size text, its bar, the `Liq` readout and the feed row; staleness from the chip and the 0.85 dim; a collapse from the stub and its words; zones from the hatch and the labels. Nothing is discoverable only by having seen an animation (R-4). Auto-moving content keeps `05` §7's rule that auto-moving content lasting more than 5 s has an accessible pause control: the feed's `Pause` is the control.

## 8. Budgets and the drop rule (R-2)

The panel's frame budget for scripting is 2 ms during a cascade (`14`:1335). Every canvas moment is one uniform per frame (R-3). M-02 is a DOM overlay on the compositor. **The drop rule:** a transition that would start in a frame already over budget is skipped and its end state applied (R-2). Under the `06` §4.4 degradation ladder the panel stops M-03 and M-02 first, then M-05's ramp, before any data path degrades. The end state is identical whether or not anything played.

### 9.3 The cascade throttle in numbers (`T` AC)

At 500 prints/s, a summary is announced at the leading edge and then at most once per 5000<!--=BUD:throttle.ms--> ms.
