The formats are D03 §2 (`D03`:89). The contrast matrix is D03 §11.1 (`D03`:310), 53 pairs in three themes. This pack restates only the rules a builder needs first:

- **Hue only where it carries a class.** The quadrant bars take `color.candle.up` / `color.candle.down` by price direction; liquidations take `color.status.danger.default`, both sides; the funding series takes `color.chart.line`, both signs (`D03`:68–70). Words and figures stay `color.text.primary`.
- **Densities** (`D03`:261). Compact: 24 px rows, `space.2` gaps, `space.inset.sm` padding (the default). Comfortable: 32 px rows, `space.3`, `space.inset.md`. `dense` is chart-only. There is no "spacious" (D03-C1).
- **Fixed sizes** (`D03`:68). The quadrant lane is 16 px. The pattern floor is 6 px per bar (half width 3 px) and the tips start at 10 px per bar; the floor numbers stay PROPOSED for E24-S02's measurement.
- **Focus** (`D03`:302). Every stop takes the double ring in `color.focus.ring`. Targets are at least 24 × 24 px, by padding.
- **Forced colours** (`D03`:306). Text, glyph and geometry carry every state; the fills and hues disappear.
- **Missing tokens.** The heatmap ramp `color.chart.liquidation.1…5` (D03-G1) and the five PROPOSED engine aliases (`color.chart.line`, `.rule`, `.boundary`, `.axis`, `.hatch.texture`) resolve through D03 §1's fallbacks until the design-system owner adds them (§14).

## 17. The tool and the mutation list

`docs/design/_tools/e24_d05_handoff_audit.py` (`--check`, `--json`, `--self-test`, `--repin-ruled`), stdlib only, run with `python3 -I`, default-deny. Its gates:

- **G-DECK:** the deck parses; the header line is first; every row has every field, a unique key, a known kind, status and provenance; a `merged` row cites a merged file, a `RULED` row names its ruling; every row's text, or its `quote`, is on its cited line at that line's ref.
- **G-PROV:** §8.3's rule over every row.
- **G-NAMED:** every key of §8.2 is a deck row.
- **G-COMP:** every component of `14`:1330 has a §3.2 row, and every `15` cite there is that component's heading.
- **G-PROPS:** every §4 prop is in D03's Props line, and every Wire cite's line carries its field name.
- **G-STATE:** §6 has D02 §9's rows in order, with the same frames.
- **G-MOTION:** §7's cells equal D04 §2's.
- **G-SETTINGS:** §10's defaults equal D02 §10's.
- **G-EVENT:** §12's events are `14`:1333's.
- **G-BUDGET:** every BUD and COUNT anchor is computed from its source.
- **G-AHEAD:** every DATE anchor is computed from `CAL`'s constants.
- **G-FILLED:** no placeholder in the pack, the hand-off or the deck.
- **G-AROW:** every hand-off §C row's Old text is on its cited line at the base.
- **G-CITE:** every cite in range, on a non-blank line, and equal to the pins file's hash.
- **G-HYGIENE:** no closing keyword, local path, URL, agent name or public-text deny word in the three texts, the tool or the pins.
- **G-SCOPE:** the diff touches only the five files of this ticket.

Self-test mutations, each failing only its gate, then the texts restored byte for byte: MU-1 blanks a deck row's owner (G-DECK); MU-2 drops "predicted" from a predicted row (G-PROV); MU-3 names a missing key in §8.2 (G-NAMED); MU-4 points CMP-126's heading cite at CMP-127's line (G-COMP); MU-5 adds a prop D03 does not have (G-PROPS); MU-6 drops a frame from §6 (G-STATE); MU-7 gives M-05 the fast token (G-MOTION); MU-8 changes a default (G-SETTINGS); MU-9 renames an event (G-EVENT); MU-10 types a wrong bucket budget (G-BUDGET); MU-11 types a wrong date (G-AHEAD); MU-12 writes a placeholder (G-FILLED); MU-13 changes an A-row's Old text (G-AROW); MU-14 re-points a cite to an unpinned line (G-CITE); MU-15 writes a closing keyword (G-HYGIENE); MU-16 touches `14` in the diff (G-SCOPE); MU-17 gives a deck row a text not on its line (G-DECK); MU-18 makes an approximate row claim publication (G-PROV); MU-19 puts a deny word into the tool's own text (G-HYGIENE).
