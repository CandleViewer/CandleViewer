### 8.3 Provenance (`T` AC; the Security note)

## 17. The tool and the mutation list

`docs/design/_tools/e24_d05_handoff_audit.py` (`--stage deck|full`, required; `--check`, `--json`, `--self-test`, `--repin-ruled`), stdlib only, run with `python3 -I`, default-deny; clean under `ruff check`, `ruff format --check` (the formatter gate; `AGENTS.md`'s black line is stale, #4229) and `mypy --strict`. The deck stage (PR 1a) reads the deck and the tool alone and runs G-DECK, G-PROV, G-NAMED's deck part, G-HYGIENE and G-SCOPE; the full stage (this PR) runs all 18 gates and fails G-SCOPE when any of the five files is missing. `--repin-ruled` refuses in the deck stage or with the pack missing.

- **G-DECK:** the deck parses; the header line is first, names the `frozen` flag and pins the row and status counts; every row has every field and `frozen`, no field outside the closed set, a unique key, a known kind, status and provenance; a `merged` row cites a merged file, a `RULED` row names its ruling; every row's text, or its `quote`, is on its cited line, a text as a whole delimited span, never a cut prefix, and a merged row's `quote` is checked as the quoted prefix plus the line's own tail; every owner is a ticket key (`server` only on a merged row).
- **G-PROV:** §8.3's rule over every row, the charge lines and the cross-check of the text against `none` / `recorded` included; every displayed charge line has its `words` row and its `symbol` twin, both PROPOSED, the twin holding the words row's `quote`.
- **G-NAMED:** the deck header names T-1 … T-7 and each group has a row, T-5 its `last_success_at` row; in the full stage §8.2's keys per group are the deck's rows of that group, and every key of §9.7's table is a deck row.
- **G-COMP:** every component of `14`:1335 has a §3.2 row, and every `15` cite there is that component's heading.
- **G-PROPS:** every §4 prop is in D03's Props line, and every `23` or `22` Wire cite's line carries its field name.
- **G-STATE:** §6 has D02 §9's rows in order, with the same frames.
- **G-MOTION:** §7's cells equal D04 §2's.
- **G-SETTINGS:** §10's defaults equal D02 §10's.
- **G-EVENT:** §12's events are `14`:1338's.
- **G-BUDGET:** every BUD and COUNT anchor is computed from its source.
- **G-AHEAD:** every DATE and SPRINT anchor is computed from `CAL`'s constants and the ticket JSON.
- **G-FILLED:** no placeholder in the pack, the hand-off or the deck.
- **G-AROW:** every hand-off §C row's Old text is on its cited line at the base; pack A-1 sits on the last pointer line of `14` SCR-054, found by content; CMP-126's `15` heading carries exactly two pending rows, E08-D06's and E24-D03's.

- **G-HYGIENE:** no closing keyword, local path, URL, agent name or public-text deny word in the stage's texts, the tool or the pins.
