## 5. Tokens and redlines (D03 by reference; nothing measured here)

The token per element and state is D03's state table, row by row: §3 S54-1 … S54-9, §4 S54-10 … S54-18, §5 S54-19 … S54-39, §6 S54-40 … S54-51, §7 S54-52 … S54-57, §8 S54-58 … S54-62, §9 S54-63 … S54-69, §10 S54-70 … S54-76, §13 S54-77 … S54-78. The formats are D03 §2 (`D03`:89). The contrast matrix is D03 §11.1 (`D03`:310), 53 pairs in three themes. This pack restates only the rules a builder needs first:

- **Hue only where it carries a class.** The quadrant bars take `color.candle.up` / `color.candle.down` by price direction; liquidations take `color.status.danger.default`, both sides; the funding series takes `color.chart.line`, both signs (`D03`:68–70). ### 8.3 Provenance (`T` AC; the Security note)

Every row carries `provenance`: `predicted`, `estimated`, `approximate`, `recorded` or `none`. The rule (G-PROV): a value, label, name, chip, dialog, header, message or announcement row that is `predicted` holds the word "predicted"; one that is `estimated` holds "estimated" or "estimate"; one that is `approximate` holds "about". "≈" is a symbol, not a word, and never counts. Descriptions and tooltips beside such a value may rely on its label. A row whose text says "predicted", "estimate", "about" or "≈" is never `none` or `recorded`. No `predicted`, `estimated` or `approximate` row says the exchange published the value: "exchange-published", "published by Bybit" and "Bybit's predicted" are refused.

**The charge lines.** A charge ("you pay" / "you receive") is computed from the predicted rate, so it is a predicted value. ## 17. The tool and the mutation list

`docs/design/_tools/e24_d05_handoff_audit.py` (`--check`, `--json`, `--self-test`, `--repin-ruled`), stdlib only, run with `python3 -I`, default-deny. Its 17 gates:

- **G-DECK:** the deck parses; the header line is first; every row has every field, a unique key, a known kind, status and provenance; a `merged` row cites a merged file, a `RULED` row names its ruling; every row's text, or its `quote`, is on its cited line at that line's ref, a text as a whole delimited span, never a cut prefix; every owner is a ticket key (`server` only on a merged row).
- **G-PROV:** §8.3's rule over every row, the charge lines and the cross-check of the text against `none` / `recorded` included.
- **G-NAMED:** every key of §8.2 and of §9.7's table is a deck row; D04 A-16, A-19 and A-20 are listed.
- **G-COMP:** every component of `14`:1330 has a §3.2 row, and every `15` cite there is that component's heading.
- **G-PROPS:** every §4 prop is in D03's Props line, and every `23` or `22` Wire cite's line carries its field name.
- **G-STATE:** §6 has D02 §9's rows in order, with the same frames.
- **G-MOTION:** §7's cells equal D04 §2's.
- **G-SETTINGS:** §10's defaults equal D02 §10's.
- **G-EVENT:** §12's events are `14`:1333's.
- **G-BUDGET:** every BUD and COUNT anchor is computed from its source.
- **G-AHEAD:** every DATE and SPRINT anchor is computed from `CAL`'s constants and the ticket JSON.
- **G-FILLED:** no placeholder in the pack, the hand-off or the deck.
- **G-AROW:** every hand-off §C row's Old text is on its cited line at the base.
- **G-CITE:** every cite in range, on a non-blank line, and equal to the pins file's hash.
- **G-HYGIENE:** no closing keyword, local path, URL, agent name or public-text deny word in the three texts, the tool or the pins.

- **G-HANDOFF:** hand-off §G has one block per changed component (CMP-186, CMP-126, CMP-127, CMP-227), headed by its `15` name, with every line of `16` §11.4's template (`16`:543) in order, each `[x]` with evidence or `[ ]` with an `N/A` or `PENDING` reason.
