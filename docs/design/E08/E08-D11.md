## 1. Purpose, scope, method, locked inputs

### 1.1 Purpose and scope

Fix the motion of every E08 state change on the DOM surfaces — the SCR-152 banner, the per-panel scrims of SCR-050 / SCR-053, the rows and cells of SCR-100 (CMP-161), the live fields of CMP-036, the SCR-151 backfill variant, the CMP-076 connection chip and CMP-078's per-panel Stale treatment (the dim, the T2 header strip, the tape header chip, the speed widget's "—") — so that a change is **noticed once, never constantly**, a flapping feed cannot strobe, 200 rows can go stale in one frame within budget, and nothing ever implies a freshness the system does not have. **Boundary:** the chart canvas (SCR-030) is E11-D06's; the two specs agree on the rows that touch both (the disconnected scrim at 0 ms and its cut in the snapshot frame, the stale chip instant, the resync scrim lifting only when the `snap` applies, the 85 % stale dim); the 15 % "verifying" hold is proposed to it as C-14 and the stale exit's cut as C-18. CMP-077's overlay is re-specified by E17-D01 A1 (unruled extension) and its motion is not defined here. CMP-092 / SCR-153 backend-health banners, trading-action motion and SCR-147's admin connectivity rows (E08-D02 §2's last column; E42-D02, #3926) are out of scope. Every heuristic choice is labelled (heuristic); every duration, easing and hysteresis constant not fixed by the plan is **PROPOSED** until the owner's run (§11). No one has felt this motion.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk specification over the merged E08 chain, E17-D01, the plan documents and the shipped code (§1.4), with the arithmetic in the audit tool and the two prototypes measured once on this host. No participants, no sessions, no reference-machine run. The sign-off scenario's motion designer, accessibility specialist, CDO and front-end engineer are the owner (adaptations, 2026-09-25); "front-end feasibility confirmed" is the written §10 held against shipped code, "performance assertion agreed with QA" is the written §6.5 case — both confirmations stay **pending** and are not claimed.

### 1.3 Locked inputs (built on, not re-decided)

- The eight states and their precedence; a state change is a **single flip on enter/exit, no pulsing motif**; the stale chip is the primary signal and opacity is reinforcing only (E08-D01 §2, §4, §6; CMP-239 contract). Precedence order itself is open (E17-D01 A7, Q-G): every row here is defined per state entry/exit and works under either order.
- The banner anatomy and copy, the 0–96 px slot, `recovered` dismissible and auto-collapsing only after dismissal or 10 s, whichever is later, the per-panel scrims and the SCR-100 row treatment (E08-D03 §2, §3, §5); the reserved slot and "a panel that closes and reopens inside the 30 s grace shows no transition" (E08-D02 §3, §4; US-MKT-005).
- T1 per topic (book 2 s, ticker 5 s, trades 10 s), T2 at 3× (6 / 15 / 30 s), T3 (Delayed, chip minimum 5 s), T4 (10 s, 30 s, 5 min), T5 (≥ 3 state changes in 5 s → one "Connection unstable"), T6; "fast to warn, slow to clear — except that a block never outlives its cause" (E17-D01 §4.1, D10, D15; A2 ruled).
- The reconnect ladder 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 5 / 10 / 30 s with full jitter (`23` §9.2); every topic re-snapshots in priority order after a reconnect (`23` §9.3); a `snap` with an incident `reason` — E17-D01 F7's closed set `upstream_desync`, `backpressure` — vs a routine one (`initial`, `client_resync`, `upstream_reconnect`, `reconfigure`, `instrument_revision`, `replay_seek`, `generation_swap`, `history_repair`, and any reason the client does not know: F7 makes an unknown reason routine; C-12) (`23` §7.4; E17-D01 F7, D15); 5 resyncs per stream per minute (`23` §7.5; `resyncGuard.ts`).
- SCR-100: ticker coalesced to 4 Hz, virtualised beyond 60 rows (`14`); the renderer runs off `requestAnimationFrame`, never off frame arrival (`23` §8.5; `06` §5.1); hidden windows `ctl` visual topics to ≥ 1 000 ms (`23` §8.5).
- The motion mode has one source: `data-motion` `safe|reduce` on the document root; the in-app override wins over `prefers-reduced-motion` in both directions (`16` §4; E06-D03 D5; E11-D06 D21). `05` §7 items 1–4 and §6.2's live-region rules; the ≤ 1 per 5 s ambient budget (E40-D01). The seven motion tokens of `16` §2.6 — no semantic motion token exists.

## 5. Reduced motion, designed per element

One source: the resolved `16` §4 mode (`data-motion`, the in-app `system|on|off` override winning over `prefers-reduced-motion` in both directions; entry points `Ctrl+Shift+M`, Settings → Appearance → Reduce Motion and the accessibility preferences all write the one store). Every row of §4 names its twin; the rule is **instant style change plus text — never removal of the signal** (AC 3). Under reduce: the banner, scrims, chips, tags, strike-through and overlay all flip exactly as in motion-safe mode (they already do); the five transitions that exist (M4, M7, M9, the entry settle M13 / M16 / M17 / M32, M19) become cuts — the dismissal cuts to the empty slot, the bar steps, the scrim lifts in the `snap` frame as a cut, the dim lands at 0.85 (or 0.15 on a panel) in one frame with the chip, and the emphasis is simply absent; the exits (M14, M33) are cuts in both modes (the arrow glyph and sign carry the direction; `16` §7's "colour-only text tint" is not used — `05` §7 item 2 wins, C-5). No state becomes invisible: every non-live state keeps its mandatory text label (E08-D04 §5). The mechanism is shipped: `theme.css` neutralises every CSS transition and animation under `:root[data-motion="reduce"]`, so no row needs its own media query; the engine flags (`disable_canvas_animation`, `flashOnTick`) do not reach these DOM rows and are not consulted. The keyboard path and the reduce path coincide where one exists: dismissal by `Esc` or the close control, Retry now, Details. The owner's T7 (§11) runs both directions of the override.

**Focus (PROPOSED, heuristic; owner E17-D02).** No motion here moves focus on its own (`05` §3.4 item 3 — a WS push never steals focus): the banner's 0 ms appearance never takes focus (`role="alert"` must not); `[ Retry now ]` keeps focus and swaps its label; `[ Details ]` opens as a flip and moves focus to the panel's first control, and `Esc` or its close returns focus to `[ Details ]`; dismissing `recovered` returns focus to the control that dismissed it or, when focus was inside the banner, to the slot's next tab stop; a scrim that blocks the ladder never makes the grid `inert` and never moves focus — the block is disabled-with-reason (`14` §0.5 item 5, the CMP-079 pattern) with the lock cursor, the focused cell keeps focus through every transport change (under a 2 / s flap an `inert` grid would have moved a keyboard trader's focus four times a second), a keystroke gets inline text at the point of action plus one polite announcement (E17-D01 D13), and the scrim is `aria-hidden` with its text exposed via `aria-describedby` on the panel region (E08-D03 §5); the header's words block nothing and move nothing. T8 (§11) records it; QA's E08-TC-C05 already observes keyboard focus on the watchlist grid through a drop.

## 6. The mass transition (AC 4)

### 6.1 Technique

One class change on the container — the watchlist `<table>` / panel — never 200 per-row toggles: the row background (`stale.surface`) flips by inheritance (one style recalculation, no animation), the per-cell marker is a CSS class on the existing cell (CMP-161 / CMP-239 contract, no extra node), the chip text is appended only to the ≤ 60 rendered rows (SCR-100 virtualises beyond 60; the container class covers the other 140 when they scroll in, with no transition), and the **one** settle is the panel's `::after` pseudo-element — a single compositor layer whose `opacity` goes 0 → 0.15 over `normal` / `standard`. The end colour is identical to the per-row path's: text at 85 % over the stale surface = c·0.85 + s·0.15 in both constructions (the tool's `dim:` composite), so a row that went stale alone and a row that went stale with the panel look the same. **No colour animates at N = 200**; colour animation exists only in the ≤ 20 pre-tinted emphasis overlays (D15, D18) — and those never play on a non-live cell. The prototype's "container 200" arm keeps all 200 rows in the DOM and restyles every one through the descendant selector — an upper bound, not the production case, which is the 60-row arm.

The only prior measurement is E06-D03 F10 / F11 / F14 (a 200-row DOM table costs 0.3 ms static and 1.4–8.5 ms under a 30 bars/s pan depending on structure) — a different workload, cited for scale.

### 6.3 Degradation

Under `06` §4.4 pressure the settle and the emphasis go instant before any data cadence is touched (D24); never desync the DOM-mirror layer (`06` §4.4 "never acceptable"); the chips, labels and flips are never shed — they are the information.

### 6.4 Frame-time arithmetic

A 200 ms settle spans 6 / 12 / 28.8 frames at 30 / 60 / 144 Hz; a `fast` fade 3 / 6 / 14.4. With `standard` (0.2, 0, 0, 1) the first frame covers 40.6 % / 10 % / 1.2 % of the travel at 30 / 60 / 144 Hz — the 30 Hz floor makes the settle a two-step; acceptable because the chip, not the dim, is the carrier (D11). The container flip's one-off cost scales with the rendered rows (4.0 ms at 60, 12.8 ms at 200 here, the max of five); the settle itself is one compositor layer at zero main-thread cost per frame.

### 6.5 The performance assertion for QA (PROPOSED; routed as G-6, agreement pending)

> **Case E08-D11-P1 (forced mass-stale under load).** Given the watchlist renders 200 symbols (60 rows in the window) with ticker updates coalesced at 4 Hz and the value-change emphasis firing on 20 cells per frame, when the ticker feed is forced stale for every symbol in one frame (the panel-level flag), then on the `06` §3.1 reference machine the **toggle cost** (the class change to the end of forced style + layout) is ≤ 16.6 ms, **no rAF delta** over the 400 ms from and including the toggle frame exceeds 20 ms (1.2 × the 60 Hz interval) and **none reaches 33 ms** (2 ×, a dropped frame), exactly one `opacity` transition runs (the container overlay), and no `background-color` transition is observed in the animation inspector. **Case E08-D11-P2a (flash gate).** Given the AC 1 flap (five drops in ten seconds), when the `A11Y-G005` collector captures the banner slot and the SCR-050 scrim (A-6), then it records < 3 flashes / s on each (its one output). **Case E08-D11-P2b (hysteresis reducer).** Given the client hysteresis reducer (E17-T04) on a fake clock, when it replays the tool's scenario timelines — AC 1, the 2 / s flap, the slow flap, the quiet symbol — then its appearances, fill steps, word swaps, scrim flips and announcement list equal the tool's `--json` `scenarios` and `quiet` blocks (the golden fixture): for AC 1 one appearance, 2 fill changes, 4 title swaps, 2 scrim flips and three announcements; for the slow flap 2 alerts and one polite "Connection unstable"; for the quiet symbol one chip flip per minute; nothing above 1 swap / s except the block.
