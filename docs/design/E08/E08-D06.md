## 1. Surface, personas and openers

- **SCR-103** (modal drawer, CMP-046) explains one symbol's exchange rules beside its live market and CandleViewer's
  recording of it, and keeps the three apart, because they differ in trust: exchange-enforced rules, live values that
  can go stale, and CandleViewer's own facts (ticket Context). CMP-125 is its compact popover (§9).
- **Personas** (`10` §0): P1 Owner, P2 Manager, P3 Viewer. Every role holds `instruments:read`, `marketdata:read` and
  `recording:read` (`22` `x-permissions`), so all three bands load for everyone; §8 decides what each role is shown.
- **Openers** (each consumer owns its trigger; the contract is `open(symbol, {source, section?})`): "View symbol info"
  in SCR-102 and SCR-041, delisted rows included (E08-D05 §6.3); "Open symbol info" (SCR-047 reason 3) and "Show
  coverage" (SCR-047 reason 2 and the before-history band row, `section: "coverage"`) in E11-D04; the SCR-100 row menu
  (E08-D07); the palette command "Symbol info…" (SCR-012). No new hotkey (§10.1). Deep link `?panel=symbol-info&symbol=`
  proposed for `12` (A-9).

## 5. Number formatting and precision rules (AC 5)

- **F1 Strings, not floats.** Every contract value is a decimal string (`22` `Decimal`, `^-?[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?$`); the
  client never makes a JavaScript `Number` of it, never calls `toFixed`, and never lets `Intl` cap the fraction digits.
  Arithmetic for examples runs in the shared policy port (`packages/protocol/src/rules/policy.ts`), a hand-written
  BigInt port of `exchange/policy.py` proven equal by the shared corpus in values and violation codes, not in message
  strings (CR-2); only its violation codes (`FilterViolationCode`, `FILTER_VIOLATION_CODES`) and rounding modes
  (`PriceRoundMode`, `QtyRoundMode`) are generated. Its `parseDecimal` throws on `1E-7` (`BigInt` rejects the
  exponent; there is no explicit guard, `policy.ts:45-53`), so F2's expansion must run first.
- **F2 Positional notation only.** The shipped serialiser uses `str(Decimal)` (`api/instruments.py:109-116`), which
  prints a tick of 0.0000001 as `1E-7`, against `22`'s pattern; the client expands an exponent form exactly and logs it
  once (`symbol.info_value_malformed`, `kind: exponent`); anything else unparsable shows "—" and logs; never a guess
  (CR-3).
- **F3 Rule values in minimal exact form:** tick, step, minimum and maximum quantity, minimum value and leverage drop
  trailing fractional zeros ("0.10" → "0.1", "100.000" → "100", "100.00" → "100×") and never lose a significant digit.
  Trailing zeros carry no rule (0.10 and 0.1 admit the same prices); Bybit's `price_scale` is a display scale, not a
  rule, and stays out of the rows (TradingView keeps its `pricescale` apart from the tick in the same way [1]).
- **F4 Prices** (examples, last, mark, index, high, low) show the larger of the tick's decimals and the value's own
  significant decimals: 82,751.2; the index 82,782.37 keeps its second decimal and is never rounded to the tick
  (E30-D02 §5.4: "64,010.0").
- **F5 Quantities** show the larger of the step's decimals and the value's own: 57,911.602 BTC; 0.250 BTC. Until the
  tick or step is known (the contract band still loading, or failed), F4 and F5 show a value's own decimals as
  delivered, never a default precision.
- **F6 Rates and changes:** percent by an exact decimal shift, with a sign; funding keeps at least 4 decimals (0.0001 →
  "+0.0100 %", 0.0000771 → "+0.00771 %"); the 24 h change is a statistic, rounded half-even to 2 decimals in decimal
  arithmetic ("+1.23 %", as E08-D05 shows it). A value that is zero or rounds to zero takes no sign and no arrow
  ("0.00 %", "0.0000 %"); the margin rates of §4.4 carry no sign.
- **F7 Magnitudes** (turnover, open-interest value, disk) show 3 significant figures, rounded half-even in decimal
  arithmetic, and a result that reaches 1,000 moves to the next unit (999.6 M → 1.00 B): values with K, M or B (10^3,
  10^6, 10^9), disk in decimal kB, MB, GB or TB ("812 GB" for `22`'s 812,340,221,100 bytes); the exact value is in the
  row's accessible description; never for a rule value.
- **F8 Grouping, units, one value per row:** a comma groups the integer part only, the decimal point is `.`, independent
  of locale (CMP-020's test); the unit is a word in its own column: prices and the tick in the quote coin, quantities in
  the base coin, values in USDT, leverage "×", interval "h". A row carries one value and one unit: last, mark and index
  are three rows, and 24 h high and low are two, so their digits compare down one tabular column (side by side they
  would not fit the 11-character column, §3), and a screen reader hears one term and one definition per value (§10.2).
  A value wider than the column (a 1000× contract's 24 h volume in base units can run to 14 characters,
  "50,000,000,000") stacks under its label, as CMP-036 `Stacked`; digits are never cut or ellipsised (board 18).
- **F9 One formatter.** The drawer, the order ticket's messages and `policy.py`'s `user_message` strings render through
  one shared function (CR-2), so "Price must be a multiple of 0.1." reads alike everywhere. Today `policy.py` prints
  `str(tick_size)`: "0.10" for BTCUSDT as delivered, and its `round_price` returns "50000.00" for that tick (computed),
  where the corpus (tick "0.1") shows 50,000.0 and E30-D02 one decimal (64,010.0).
- **F10 Times** are UTC. A gap shows minutes, its start rounded down and its end rounded up (`22`'s 02:11:00–02:14:20
  reads 02:11–02:15), so the band never understates a gap; the exact `from` and `to` with seconds are in the row's
  accessible description, as F7 does for magnitudes (§10.2); the as-of and Updated lines keep their seconds.

## 11. Data lifecycle and performance

1. **Open.** The contract band paints from the client's instrument index when the symbol is in it (warm: within
   100 ms, no request, the SCR-079 precedent); `GET /instruments/{symbol}` runs once per open for tiers and
   `stale_since`, never blocking the first paint. A symbol not in the Trading-only index (not trading, or newly listed)
   waits on that request (cold, §6). A detail whose `revision` differs from the painted one marks the changed rows as
   D-13 does, never a silent swap, and refreshes the client's index.
2. **Live.** The drawer is one consumer of `ticker.{symbol}` in the app's ticker store, which subscribes only if no
   other panel has (US-MKT-005, fan-out); values repaint text in fixed columns at the topic's 250 ms cadence (`23` §6.1),
   with no layout shift. On close the consumer is released and the store keeps the topic for 30 s, so a reopen within
   30 s paints at once with no transition (E08-D02 §4). Until H-7 ships a producer for the `ticker` family, a
   subscription stays `snapshot_pending` (`23` §7.7) and never gets a `snap` or a `d`; the shipped bootstrap is
   `GET /instruments/{symbol}/ticker` (`marketdata:read`; `high_24h` and `low_24h` always null, `api/ticker.py:71-72`),
   read at open and after a reconnect, and retried on E08-D05's ladder for up to 60 s while it answers 503 "warming up"
   (`:110-111`), which lasts, for a symbol no other panel streams, until the drawer's own subscription warms the stream
   (`ws_wiring.py:130`). Its `ts` is the Updated line, its ISO `next_funding_time` reaches CMP-126 as epoch ms, and
   `stale: true` is T1 at once (this spec's reading, as D-4's per-item `stale`; H-9). A band fed only by it turns stale
   at 5 s (T1) and stays there, as Awaiting the first tick does: it never escalates to T2 or returns to live.
3. **Recorder.** The E16-S02 store, no subscription of the drawer's own (ticket: "only the live band subscribes").
   **Coverage:** `GET /market/data-coverage?symbol=` once per open (`06` row 13: reads under 150 ms p95); it answers
   the last 90 days (#2216), and 422 for a symbol that is not Trading (CR-13, §6). It also answers 422 for every symbol
   until the server holds its first catalogue snapshot (`_catalogue_listed` fails closed, `app.py:2745-2751`), so the
   drawer reads a 422 by the contract band: a status other than Trading, or a 404, gives §6's not-trading line; a
   Trading status gives Coverage failed; no status yet (Catalogue loading, or Contract load failed) gives "Recorded
   coverage waits for the instrument list.", and coverage is asked once more when the contract band loads.
4. **Revision and reconnect** (D-13): one re-read of the detail; a status change re-renders the bands (§6).
5. **Close** (`Esc`, ×, the scrim, a navigation): release the consumer, cancel requests in flight, restore focus.
