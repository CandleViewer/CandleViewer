## 3. Trail advance: column step, sampling offset, crossover (BQ-4; board 02)

### 3.1 The rule

The trail is a texture-space operation (`26`:297): each column write uploads one 1 × rows column (`26`:308) and advances the ring cursor; the time axis is mapped through `colTime`, so a shift costs nothing extra (`26`:309). Two renderings, both on that mechanism:

- **Step (default; the reduce mode).** The image moves left by exactly one column in the frame the write lands. At 100 ms buckets that is ≤ 10 steps per second (`26`:23).
- **Continuous (safe mode only).** The shader adds a sampling offset `o = min(1, (now − lastColTime) / bucket_ms)` columns, a uniform written once per frame. When the next write lands, the cursor advances and `o` returns to 0 in the same frame, so the image never jumps back; a late column holds `o` at 1 and never extrapolates. It runs only when **all** hold: safe mode, the column wider than 3 device px (the `23`:811 target), the trail not paused (D02:156), and no load shedding (§8). Otherwise the step.

A bucket change (`23`:834) is a re-snapshot, not a motion: the trail re-lays in one frame.

## 4. Re-centring (AC 1; BQ-6, BQ-7; boards 03, 04)

### 4.1 The numbers (PROPOSED engine constants, §9)

- **Trigger band:** the outer 20 % of the visible rows at each edge, rounded down, at least 3 and at most 10 rows (§4.2).
- **Target:** the mark on the centre row (row ⌊visible / 2⌋ from the top).
- **Minimum interval:** 1 000 ms between two automatic re-centres. A user re-centre (`C`, `Space`; D02:368, `14`:1214) bypasses it and restarts it.
- **Deferral:** if the mark is in the band inside the interval, nothing moves; if it leaves the view, the edge indicator W-1 or W-2 shows at that edge, and the re-centre runs when the interval ends if the mark is still in the band or out of view.

Applied on each book update and once at the interval's end (auto-follow only; M AD-3, M:167):

```text
if mode != auto-follow or paused or pointer down on the ladder or a row menu open: defer
row = markRow - topRow
inBand = row < band or row > visibleRows - 1 - band
if inBand and now - lastAuto >= 1000 ms: re-centre (target row), lastAuto = now
else if mark outside view: show W-1 / W-2
```

### 4.3 The oscillation replay (AC 1)

Forty visible rows (band 8), a book applying every 5 ms for 10 s, the mark alternating between two prices every half period. "Edge ± 1 row" crosses the band's inner edge; "edge to edge" swings one view height, so every swing enters a band. "Band rule alone" is the same band without the interval: the jitter `11`:1982 forbids. This spec never re-centres twice within 1 000 ms.

### 4.4 The motion, the modes and click safety (BQ-7)

Safe mode: the ladder's row container takes one `translateY` from the old to the target offset over `motion.duration.fast` with `motion.easing.decelerate`; the pane's `priceOrigin` offset follows the same eased value from the same frame clock (R-4), uploading only the exposed rows (`26`:312). Reduce, and centre lock in both modes: a cut. Centre lock re-centres on every update (W-16, D03:461), so its tween would never settle; it is always a cut. Free-scroll never moves on its own (D02:223). An automatic re-centre never starts while a pointer is down on the ladder or a row menu is open; it waits for release (`dom.recentre_deferred {reason}`). Focus stays on its price row: `↑` / `↓` already suspend follow (D02:363), so an automatic re-centre never moves the focused row out from under the keyboard. Click-trading is E31's and off in E21; hit-testing during a tween is a G-row (G-3).

## 5. Rescale (AC 3; BQ-8; board 05)

### 5.1 The interpolation

The adaptive bound (`11`:2017–2019) is a **shader uniform**: the ring keeps raw quantised size and the shader divides by the bound before the LUT (`26`:305), so a rescale never re-uploads the ring (`26`:301 normalises at upload; C-3, G-1). On a new target the bound moves in ln space along `motion.easing.standard` over `motion.duration.slow`, and each frame's step is clamped to δ, the largest step in ln(bound) that moves no LUT position's relative luminance by `heatmap.rescale.lumaStep` (0.09) or more. δ is derived once per LUT build, which happens only on a theme or palette change (`26`:311), so it costs nothing per frame; the 0.01 margin under 0.10 covers the 8-bit LUT's rounding. A clamped ramp that is still behind at 400 ms keeps stepping by δ until it lands. A reversal (a target on the other side of the current bound) waits until 1 000 ms after the last ramp began; a same-direction retarget continues from the current value. The CMP-113 anchors (D04:167) stay static during the ramp and cut once at its end; the legend is never announced. Under reduce the same ramp runs: it changes luminance only and moves nothing, `05`:233's "single low-amplitude cross-fade" (C-2).

### 5.2 Per-frame luminance on the shipped ramps

Model (PROPOSED; G-1): the LUT is `color.heatmap.zero` and the five stops of a set, evenly spaced and linear in 8-bit sRGB; the cells are 102 resting sizes (zero and 0.001 to 100 × the bound on a twentieth-of-a-decade grid); linear and log mappings (`26`:305); a collapse and a rebound by 10× (`11`:2018) and by 100×; the standard and CVD sets, bid and ask; 60 Hz. "Cut" is the instant rescale the ticket's Scope names: a full-panel jump on more than half the cells.

| Theme         | Cut: worst ΔL | Cut: cells ≥ 0.10 | `slow` eased, no cap | Smallest δ | This spec: worst ΔL | 10× lands | 100× lands |
| ------------- | ------------- | ----------------- | -------------------- | ---------- | ------------------- | --------- | ---------- |
| dark          | 0.650         | 62%               | 0.347                | 0.112      | 0.091               | 400 ms    | 700 ms     |
| light         | 0.852         | 69%               | 0.297                | 0.177      | 0.094               | 400 ms    | 450 ms     |
| high-contrast | 0.650         | 62%               | 0.347                | 0.112      | 0.091               | 400 ms    | 700 ms     |
