## 10. Targets, densities, zoom and forced colours

- **Targets (2.5.8; `05`:104).** A row is 24 px at compact and cosy (cosy = compact rows over a `dense` pane) and 32 px at comfortable (D02:194); every header and footer control takes its density's control height; the ring sits outside the 24 px box, so it never shrinks the target (D02:388). Compliant at all three densities (AD-10). SCR-051's 18 px row-height setting is D02 C-2, E21-D03's to hold at 24 (D02:486).
- **Focus not obscured (2.4.11; `05`:103).** The grid's `scroll-padding` keeps the first and last rows' rings whole under the sticky header and footer (D02:388). Compliant.
- **200 % and the text scale (1.4.4; `05`:165).** Rows grow and are never clipped; the pane narrows, then hides behind a `Heatmap` toggle with the reveal still reachable; Prints and Cum. depth move to the row reveal (D02:392). Compliant.
- **400 % (1.4.10; `05`:120 R-12).** The header, notice and footer reflow to one column; the grid keeps its columns and scrolls inside itself under the two-dimensional data-table exception; the `Table` view stacks per row (D02:392). `05` R-12 has no data-table exception: C-06, A-06.
- **Forced colours (`DS` R2).** D02 §7.4 gives every carrier its system-colour rendering (D02:322); §8's last column lifts it per CR row. Compliant.
- **Pause (2.2.2).** The follow slot's `Pause` / `Follow` toggle stops the trail and auto-follow (D02:156). Compliant.
