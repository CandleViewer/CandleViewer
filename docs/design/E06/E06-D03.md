## 1. Purpose and scope

Decide the cost and shape of the DOM-mirror layer: nodes, update rate, screen-reader (SR) behaviour, structure, roles, live-region policy, keyboard model, the focus-visible treatment of the focused bar, and the engine-side contract that E06-T02 and E11 (M8) consume. Out of scope: the production layer, hi-fi SCR-045, per-cell footprint surrogates beyond affordability (ticket "Out of scope"; the affordability result is F15).

## 2. Method (honest)

- **Desk research** of the cited plan docs.
- **Measured microbenchmark, not the ticket's prototype scene.** The E06-K04 findings (`docs/plan/spikes/E06-K04.md`, "Honest limits") state no browser/Electron/GPU exists in the environment; there is no mirror stub in the K04 prototype. A standalone throwaway page builds a hidden table (200 or 60 rows × 7 columns), patches it per `requestAnimationFrame`, and forces style/layout (`offsetHeight`) inside the measured span. 2026-10-14 run: headless Chromium 156 shell on WSL2 Linux, 16 cores, software rendering, no worker/GL running concurrently; 20 s per run, 3 reps, 100k synthetic bars; scenarios static, pan at 30 bars/s, update storm at 20 msg/s (06 §7.4: median of the three p95s).
- **Re-measurement with bar-keyed recycling (2026-10-20, review F-01).** Same page, dataset, method and scenarios, plus the `recycle` strategy the D4 structure implies ("pooled, identity preserved", row = logical bar): a shift of k bars moves k `<tr>` nodes and rewrites 7k cells and their absolute `aria-rowindex`; unmoved rows are diff-scanned so live updates land; a shift ≥ the window rewrites every row in place. Added scenarios: pans at 120 and 600 bars/s, a `table-layout: fixed` variant, storm, and a Home/End-class hard jump once per second — two invocations: the 200-row matrix with its controls (`?set=recycle`), then the default 60-row window under the same fast pans, storm and jump (`?set=recycle60`). Binary: Chrome for Testing 155.0.8059.12 headless shell (Playwright build 1247 — the axe run's binary; the 2026-10-14 binary is no longer on this host), so the slot-keyed controls were **re-run on the new binary** and every before/after comparison in F14 is same-binary.
- **axe-core run (done, §7.1)** against the benchmark page and a static fixture of the D4/D6 structure, in headless Chromium on this host; re-run after the fixture's round-2 change.
- **Heuristic evaluation (done, §7.2)** of D4–D6 against Nielsen's heuristics and the trading-specific rules in `05-accessibility-standard.md` (§2 POUR table, §3.1, §3.4, §6, §7). Every finding is labelled `(heuristic)`; no participants, timings or quotes exist.
- **Not done, and not claimed:** Electron and the other two runtimes (ADR-0011: Chromium, Electron, Tauri/WebView2), the reference machine, measurement alongside a rendering worker, the NVDA/VoiceOver walkthrough. No SR testing was done by anyone; §7.3 is the protocol for the owner to run. Cadence results in §5 are a design proposal, not validated. See §8.

## 4. Decisions

**D1 Window and nodes.** Cap stays 200 rows (26 §12) as the _maximum_; the default window is **60 rows centred on the cursor**, configurable up to the cap (Settings → Accessibility); it does **not** change when the data table opens. **SCR-045 is a separate component**: a Panel built from CMP-049 Table + CMP-053 Pagination + CMP-056 ColumnPicker, virtualised over 100k rows with its own budget (`14`: ≤8 ms scripting per scroll frame), bound to the same `getA11yWindow()`/series surface and patched at 05 §6.3's cadence (default 1 update/2 s, stale banner); mirror and table share **only the cursor bar index** (D6/D9), never DOM. Rows are a bar-keyed pool with identity preserved (D2, F14). Hard cap 1,800 mirror nodes per chart pane (200 × 8 + ~40 detail + ~30 chrome), CI-asserted. Recorded in 26 §12. _(F1, F2, F10, F11, F14; review F-02)_

Field allocation per surface (what each exposes; this is how D7's "never less than the canvas" is checked — the canvas draws OHLC, volume, delta, footprint cells, imbalance outline, POC/value area, and every one of those is reachable below; POC and imbalance, which the mirror row does not carry, live in the announcement and the `Enter` detail):

**D2 Update policy (re-decided on F14; review F-01).** Rows are **bar-keyed** (a `<tr>` pool, identity preserved, row = logical bar): a shift of k bars moves k rows to the other end and rewrites 7k cells plus their absolute `aria-rowindex`; the cursor row and rows receiving live data are patched every frame; untouched rows cost nothing (the per-frame diff scan that lets live updates land without dirty tracking costs 0.1 ms and is kept). Measured p95 on pan (F14): **0.8 ms for the default 60-row window at 30 bars/s** (0.9 ms at 120 bars/s; 1.4 ms during a 600 bars/s fling), **1.4 ms for the 200-row cap** (1.3 ms with `table-layout: fixed`; 2.8 ms, 2.0 ms fixed, at 600 bars/s). **The mirror is exact every frame at every size — same-frame parity with the canvas — bounded only by the one-frame skew the worker protocol implies** (F16: the mirror is patched from the `viewport`/`a11y` message the worker posts after rendering, the same message the live region, the canvas `aria-label` and SCR-045 consume; this review names that frame explicitly and treats it as the floor, not as a desync). **06 §4.4 stays as written**: the round-1 proposal to read "never desync" as "eventually consistent within 8 frames" is **withdrawn**, and the owner decision it asked for is closed (§8 item 4). What the data moves is the **budget line, not the parity rule**: the default window meets 06 §4.3's 1 ms slice at every pan speed a user sustains (≤120 bars/s); a momentum fling at 10 bars per frame and the 200-row cap at 30 bars/s each exceed it by ~0.4 ms on this software-rendered host — a §4.3 allocation question, met as 06 §4.4 prescribes (shed update frequency elsewhere, heatmap cadence first; never mirror correctness) and by the cap being an explicit opt-in (Settings → Accessibility) whose cost is disclosed; E06-T02 confirms on the reference machine and, if the cap still exceeds 1 ms there, lowers it to the measured 1 ms size (≈100 rows by interpolation between 0.8 ms at 60 and 1.4 ms at 200). **Time-slicing over ≤8 frames (F11) is dropped, not kept as a fallback**: it is dominated — recycling is cheaper at both sizes and exact — so the review's suggested "fallback for shifts above N bars per frame" would trade exactness for nothing the data supports. A Home/End-class hard jump (or a fling of ≥ window bars) rewrites the whole window: a one-off of up to 14.3 ms for 200 rows (2.7 ms for 60) in one frame on this host, which E11 spreads over 06 §4.1's 3-frame settling allowance (≤5 ms per frame) — the only case where the mirror trails the canvas by more than the worker frame, and by no more than the allowance 06 §4.1 grants the canvas itself for the same jump. `table-layout: fixed` on the hidden table is recommended to E11 M8 (1.3/2.0 ms vs 1.4/2.8 ms). Written into 26 §12 (this PR). _(F1, F10, F11, F14, F16)_

**D3 Budget status.** 1 ms p95 is **met for static, storm and — with bar-keyed recycling at the default 60-row window — pan** in headless Chromium (F10, F14); at the 200-row cap the pan measures 1.4 ms (1.3 ms with fixed layout), over the slice by ~0.4 ms while exact every frame (D2); the slot-keyed 200-row pan of F10 (8.5 ms) was the cost of the wrong structure, not of the mirror. Production verdict is deferred to E06-T02 on real Electron + reference machine, with the worker rendering (pass criterion: p95 ≤1 ms on pan at the default window with D1+D2; the cap is sized from that run; §8 item 1). _(F10, F11, F14)_

**D4 Structure and roles.** As `E06-D03-mirror-structure.png` (to be rebuilt, §0) and `E06-D03-bench/mirror-structure-fixture.html`: wrapper `role="application"` + `aria-roledescription="trading chart"` + static aria-label (symbol, interval, range, last) + `aria-describedby` hint ("Arrow keys move the cursor. Alt+T opens the data table. Tab leaves the chart."); canvas `role="img"`, `tabindex="-1"`, not a tab stop; ONE pre-rendered `role="status" aria-live="polite" aria-atomic="true"` region; hidden table with `aria-label`, `th scope="col"` headers, the time cell as row header (`th scope="row"`, 05 §6.3 pattern), focused bar marked `aria-current="true"`; one tab stop for the whole chart (roving cursor, not `tabindex` per row). **Windowed-table semantics (WAI-ARIA 1.2; review F-06):** `aria-rowcount` = loaded bar count + 1 (the header row), or `-1` while paging; `aria-rowindex` = the bar's absolute position (header = 1, bar i = i + 2), so the SR says "row 2,912 of 4,321", not "row 31 of 61", and the index travels with the recycled row at zero cost on shift (F14). Focus-visible: the CMP-039 FocusRing on the root marks the component boundary; the focused bar itself is shown on the canvas by the keyboard data cursor (D10). The combination is axe-clean (F12). _(F2, F3, F4, F8, F12, F13)_

**D5 Live-region policy.** Keyboard-cursor announcements: 1 region, last-write-wins, with **three named parameters used identically here, in §5 and in 26 §12** (review F-07): **`leadDelay` = 0 ms** — the first press of a key announces at once; **`repeatWindow` = 500 ms** — while the key auto-repeats, at most one announcement per window, carrying the latest state; **`trailingFlush` ≤ 250 ms** — the final state is announced within 250 ms of the last key event and is never lost. Steady state while a key is held is 2/s (US-CHART-008's NFR); a lone press is one announcement. **Two text forms:** announcements inside a held key use the **short form** — time and close, ≤3 tokens ("14:12, 64,232") — because a ~10-word summary takes 2–3 s to speak at default rate and a new polite update every 500 ms would cut off its first words; the trailing flush and every discrete jump use the **full summary** (26 §12's example: time, close, delta, imbalance, plus D7's degradation phrase). Discrete jumps (`Home`/`End`, `Shift+←/→`, `Alt+T`, `Enter`, the loaded-data edge) announce immediately, never coalesce and reset the window. Ambient market values (bid/ask, mark, PnL) keep 05 §6.2's cadence in their own region; the cursor region never carries ambient ticks. Assertive is reserved per 05 §6.2 and is never used by the chart cursor. **Reduced motion** binds to the **resolved motion mode of the 16 §4 runtime contract** (`data-motion="reduce"`, delivered to the engine by `candleviewer:themechange`), which overrides `prefers-reduced-motion` in both directions (review F-09): announcements are unchanged, momentum is off, the cursor and the viewport snap (26 §12, 05 §7). **Recorded in 26 §12 (this PR)**; proposal until SR-validated (§7.3 T3 rates both forms). _(F4, F5, F6)_

**D6 Keyboard model.** Sources: **26 §3.11 (Keyboard row) and 05 §3.2 (Chart category)**, which D6 follows; CMP-181's "Home/End jump to range start/end" and the round-1 frame's "first/last visible bar" are superseded (F17a). Keys the chart root consumes: `←/→` one bar; `↑/↓` one price level of the cursor bar (footprint/profile; level summary announced); **`Shift+←/→` ten bars** (26 §3.11; `PgUp`/`PgDn` at most as an extra alias — no new hotkey-map entry); **`Home`/`End` = oldest loaded bar / latest bar and re-enable auto-follow** (05 §3.2), the viewport jumping to show the cursor inside 06 §4.1's 3-frame settling allowance; `Enter` expands the cursor bar's cells into the mirror detail (≤40 nodes; "Expanded, N levels"), a second `Enter` on a selected level opens the CMP-046 cell-detail drawer (CMP-109's `Enter`, F17d), `Esc` collapses or closes; `Alt+T` opens SCR-045 on the cursor bar's row (global binding, 05 §3.2); **`+`/`-` zoom and `Space` pan-drag modifier (26 §3.11) are consumed by the root, change the viewport and do not announce** (a window change surfaces only through `onA11yWindowChange`, D9); `Esc` with nothing to collapse is **not consumed** (universal cancel propagates, 05 §3.3; §7.2 H3). **The cursor is not confined to the visible window:** a step past the visible edge pans the viewport by one bar (ten for `Shift`) and re-centres the D1 window on the cursor, so a keyboard-only trader is never slower than a mouse user at the edge (TradingView and Sierra Chart crosshair stepping behave this way); a move that cannot happen fires only at the **ends of the loaded data** (and at the top/bottom level) and announces the edge immediately ("first bar", "last bar") as a discrete event (§7.2 H9). Application-role escape path: Tab always leaves; stated in the hint. Bidirectional sync: the engine owns a single cursor bar index; origin-tagged changes (pointer | keyboard | table | data) prevent double announcements (US-CHART-014 "Sync"). A "read current bar" key is proposed (§7.2 H5, shared with CMP-225). Focus-visible treatment of the stepped bar: D10. _(F2, F5, F8, F17; review F-03)_

**D7 Mirror under LOD.** Rule: **the mirror exposes the data the user can reach, not what the pixels show, but it must never expose less than the canvas and must state the degradation in text.** Concretely: L0/L1/L2/L3 all keep the bar rows (OHLCV, delta) in the mirror; cell values are available **only for the cursor bar via `Enter`** at every tier (not as 1,000s of nodes — F15), and at L2/L3 the cursor announcement states "footprint at low detail" so the user knows the mirror is richer than the canvas. The D1 field-allocation table is the parity check per surface. This makes the mirror deliberately _more informative_ than the canvas at L2/L3 (equal-status principle, 11 US-CHART-014), with the "never desync" rule of 06 §4.4 applying to values, not to glyph visibility. Consistent with CMP-182 "keep cell + DOM-mirror value"; supersedes CMP-109's per-cell surrogate contract (F15, §8 item 9). **Written into 26 §12 (this PR).** Whether the degradation is stated on every move or once per tier change is a walkthrough question (§7.2 H8). _(F1, F7, F15)_

**D8 degraded-2d.** Mirror content and behaviour are a function of bars + LOD tier only, never of `RenderProfile`; a contract test runs the same mirror snapshot under full/reduced/degraded-2d. **Written into 26 §12 (this PR).** _(F7, ticket AC4)_

**D9 Engine contract (consumed by E06-T02, E11 M8; CMP-180 ChartApi).**

```ts
interface A11yWindow {
  firstBar: number;
  lastBar: number;
  cursorBar: number;
  lodTier: "L0" | "L1" | "L2" | "L3";
}
type CursorOrigin = "pointer" | "keyboard" | "table" | "data";
interface ChartApi {
  // additions
  getA11yWindow(): A11yWindow; // 05 §6.1 query surface, served from the controller's last `a11y` message
  onA11yWindowChange(cb: (w: A11yWindow, origin: CursorOrigin) => void): () => void; // once per message, ≤1/frame

```

**Realisation across the worker boundary (26 §6; review F-08).** The engine renders in a worker, so the getters are synchronous only because they read the main-thread controller's cache of the last `viewport` and `a11y` messages. This PR proposes extending `a11y` from `{ cursorSummary }` to `{ cursorSummary, firstBar, lastBar, cursorBar, lodTier, origin }` (posted on change, ≤1/frame); `onA11yWindowChange` fires once per such message; `setCursor` travels as a `command`. The mirror, the live region and the canvas `aria-label` therefore trail the canvas by at most one frame by construction (F16, D2). `getBarSummary` is the single text source for the live region (full and short form, D5) and for the canvas `aria-label` (05 §6.4), so the two can never disagree. Mirror sync is a named Performance.measure stage `domMirrorSync` (exists in the E06-D02 harness JSON, `docs/design/E06/E06-D02.md` perStageMs). Mirror values carry the chart's data classification (F9). _(F1, F9, F16)_

**D10 Keyboard data cursor — the focused bar's focus-visible treatment (review F-04).** The focused bar has no DOM element of its own (D4), so its focus-visible treatment is drawn on the canvas: **while the chart root has focus, the cursor bar is marked in the foreground layer (26 §4) by a full-height column highlight ≥2 px wide in the `color.focus.ring` token** (≥3:1 against `color.chart.background` and every pane surface, validated per tier by the theme validator — 16 §2.1, 05 §3.4.6), with the crosshair's price and time labels. It is **the same single cursor as the pointer crosshair** (26 §3.11 "snaps to bar centre"; 05 §3.1.7 "arrow keys move crosshair by one bar"), not a second marker: the keyboard treatment applies to whichever bar the cursor is on while the root has focus, whatever the last origin, and the crosshair returns to its pointer treatment when focus leaves the root. It persists while the root has focus (no blink, no decay — never colour-only, the column is also the `aria-current` row and the announced bar); under the resolved `motion: reduce` mode (16 §4) it snaps, otherwise it may ease ≤ `motion.duration.fast`. The CMP-039 FocusRing on the root marks the component boundary — one ring, never two. Sighted-keyboard check: §7.3 T12, §7.4. _(F17; 05 §3.4.6)_

### 7.3 Screen-reader session protocol (05 §8.2 adaptation, for the owner to run)

Precondition: a build with the mirror and the D5 cadence (E06-T02's instrumented stub or E11 M8's first build). No such build exists today; only T1 can be dry-run now against `mirror-structure-fixture.html` (names and roles, no keyboard handling). Nothing below has been run; record only what is observed.

1. **Combos** (05 §8.1): NVDA 2024.x on Windows 11 with the Electron production build (row 1) or Chromium (row 3, fast gate); VoiceOver on macOS 14 with the Electron build (row 4). 2. **Data**: synthetic recorded fixture at 1 m with footprint, ≥300 bars loaded, LOD at L0 at the start zoom; a 20 msg/s update storm available (recorder replay).
3. **Tasks** — log per task: pass/fail, the verbatim announcement(s), count of announcements, a 1–5 rating for each of _flooding_ (1 = none) and _lag_ (1 = none), and anything that needed sight.
