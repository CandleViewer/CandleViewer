## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

**Purpose.** `15` calls the node canvas "the second-highest a11y risk after the chart engine" (`15`:1796). This review checks, before anything is built, that the one keyboard model D02 adopted can author every rule the pointer can, and that a screen-reader user hears a coherent account of it.

**Method.** Each `T` operation, each `05` clause and each WCAG criterion `T` names (2.1.1, 2.4.3, 2.4.7, 2.5.8, 1.4.11, 4.1.2, 4.1.3) is traced to the frame, table row or shipped string that answers it. Where nothing answers, that is a finding.

- **R-1 Severity.** `Blocking` · `Major` · `Minor` · `Info`. Information that exists only as a canvas position, colour or badge is Blocking (`T` AC 3); an A / AA failure on the authoring path is Major; a G-T2 miss is Major (`DS`:15).
- **R-2 Closing.** Every finding closes one way (BQ-05). A Blocking or Major closes by an A-row in §12 (Old / New, the Old found by content), applied by the A-row PR before E37-D05's hand-off (`T` Technical notes); where the parent already states the fix and only the shipped code differs, it closes by a route to the build ticket through a C-row of §13 (BQ-07). A Minor or Info may instead be routed to its owner, or accepted as an exception with its rationale.
- **R-3 Honesty.** `(heuristic)` everywhere; no session, timing or quote exists.
- **R-4 The boundary.** "The a11y review checks; it does not design." (memo §11, RULED). A finding names the gap, the criterion and the smallest fix. Every new string is PROPOSED for E37-D05's copy deck.
- **R-5 Shipped code.** E47-S03's strings and chords are DECIDED where `KEYMAP` registers them. Its PROPOSED copy is the core's own, routed here for confirmation (`COPY`:1–7).
