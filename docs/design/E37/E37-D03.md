## 8. Tiers — two axes: node count (D02's) × zoom band (visibility only) (D-9; BQ-09 RULED; the table E37-S01 implements verbatim)

#### CMP-146 NodeGraphCanvas

Use for one rule's graph on SCR-082 only; the canvas is `color.surface.canvas`, the grid decorative, the marquee the DS selection pair. **Never** draw a chart, a heatmap or any engine primitive on it (ADR-0029 decision 1: DOM nodes), never animate an edge along its length, never let zoom re-lay out text (§8), never hide the node list behind the canvas (D02 §8: co-equal). **Security:** the graph-only, unsupported-construct and error treatments are safety-relevant communication and are never de-emphasised, shrunk below the 24 px row or hidden by a zoom band (Z-2 and Z-3 keep the badge glyph + word and the error outline, glyph and word — §8; the banner and CMP-234 are never zoomed).

#### CMP-147 TriggerNode · CMP-148 ConditionNode · CMP-149 ActionNode · CMP-150 LogicGateNode · CMP-151 CommentNode

Use the shared card (§3.1) with the type's stripe, cap shape, glyph and word; the ten states of §3.2; the action's stop line and ratchet sentence are never collapsed away or dimmed (D02 §5). **Never** put the category colour inside the body (D-3), never bind a node-specific border or radius token (D03-C1, D03-C3), never dim a read-only node (`opacity.disabled` and `color.text.disabled` are not used on the canvas), never invent a disabled or running state (D-4), never show a trigger output edge (D02 §2). **Security** (CMP-148 / CMP-150): the `graph-only` and `mismatch` badges and the error outline are never de-emphasised for aesthetics — the badge keeps its glyph + word at every tier and band, the error keeps its 2 px outline, glyph and word; a design that reduces them is corrected before sign-off (ticket AC 4's gate applied to severity).

#### CMP-152 NodeGraphEdge

Use the ten states of §4.2; ED-3 and ED-5 are solid; the midpoint surrogate hosts the focus ring and the delete affordance. **Never** dash, hatch or "flow" an edge (E25-D01 §9.5; D02 352), never carry a port name on the edge (D02 E4), never colour an edge by the source node's type. **Security:** the invalid-preview and cycle treatments (ED-3, ED-9) are never thinned below 1.5 px, recoloured to a non-danger token or shown without the ✕ and the sentence.

Inherits CMP-046 (tokens, motion M-16, focus return); one story per node type is D02's row. **Never** re-specify the drawer's motion or elevation here; never let an inspector edit re-layout the graph (`14` 1907).

#### CMP-236 NodePalette

Use `color.surface.raised` entries at `size.row.height` 40 / 48 with the type glyph in `color.text.secondary` beside the name — the hue stays on the canvas. **Never** colour a palette entry by `color.node.*`, never hide a category behind a T2 lock the one notice already carries (D02 §3), never drag-only (the `Enter` insert is the primary path).

#### CMP-237 NodePort

Use the sixteen rows of §4.1; the type is glyph + name, the state is hollow / filled + the name's clause; the three state tokens of §2.1 and the fill rule. **Never** colour a port by type or by direction (D03-C2, BQ-03), never pulse the source port (M-11), never let the compatible halo and the focus ring share a radius (§6). **Security:** the incompatible mark (PT-3) and its sentence are never softened, delayed past the 0 ms flip or replaced by a tooltip-only hint.

#### CMP-238 Minimap
