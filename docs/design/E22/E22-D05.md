### 9.3 Reduced motion and flashes (D04 §5, D04:191–237, copied)

- **The carriers (MR-3).** Every row of §2 names one, present in both modes.
- **The tier-2 fills, classified (R577 BQ-08 (c)).** A tier-2 row scrolling past swaps `color.surface.raised` for

  it, needs a relative-luminance change of at least 0.1 with the darker state below 0.80 (`E05-D03`:168). The tool

**DNA-3 holds for these by HS-1b's area bound, not by the luminance test.** HS-1b's rectangle is 341 × 256 px =
87 296 px², and its bound is 25 % of it, 21 824 px² (`E05-D03`:109). The accent is one 2 px column of 24 px rows: at
most 11 rows × 48 px² = 528 px² in any rectangle (256 / 24 = 10.67 rows, rounded up; the 2 px column caps it at
2 × 256 = 512 px²), 2.4 % of the bound. The text is glyph ink inside its line boxes: 39 ch at `type.num.sm` 11 px set
258 px a row (`D02`:91), so at most 12 rows' line boxes, 12 × 258 × 11 = 34 056 px², meet a rectangle; with the
accent's 528 px² the bound holds while the ink covers under 62.5 % of those boxes ((21 824 − 528) / 34 056), and a
glyph inks well under that share of its 0.60 em cell (a heuristic, not measured; Q04-03 measures it). The tier-2 fill
would not fit the bound, which is why its classification above matters.

- **MR-1 and HS-1 apply to no mark (DNA-3).** A mark changes twice in its life on screen: it appears, and
  60 000 ms later it turns aged. In any half-open 1 000 ms window it changes at most once, so it makes no flash; a
  synthetic burst of 5 000 marks appearing in one frame is 5 000 single changes in that frame and 0 flashes, under
  MR-1's 3 (R577 BQ-08 (d)).
- **MR-2 and WCAG 2.2.2 (R577 BQ-08 (e)).** The tape advances by itself for longer than 5 s, and it has its pause
  control: `P`, the pause button and the soft pause (`05`:207). Recorded as compliant, not redesigned. A drawn mark
  does not move; the chart's own auto-follow is the chart's.
- **Announcements and focus never wait on motion.** There is no motion to wait on; each is applied in the frame its
  state is.

**The throttle (A38).** One window, half-open, from t up to but not including t + 5 000 ms: throttle 5 s, half-open,
leading + trailing. The first whale after a quiet of at least 5 s is spoken at once and opens a window; whales inside
the window are summed and spoken once at its close, and that close opens the next window; a window that closes with no
whale ends the run, and the next whale is a leading one again. The latest window replaces, nothing queues: a write
replaces the node's text and a summary never waits behind another. Whale-class writes are therefore at least 5 000 ms
apart; the user-action and feed sentences (AN-03 to AN-06) share the node, keep their own once-per rates and are not
spaced by the whale window.

- **Suppressed while a tape row holds focus and while `verbosity` is `low`.** A user on a row is reading rows, and the
  resume count covers the gap. After `Esc` no row holds focus (FP-05), so whales are spoken again. The whale gate does
  not go through the shipped `shouldAnnounce` (`model.ts`:87-95): that filter returns false for every update while
  `announce_prices` is off (`model.ts`:91) and true for a significant update before it reads `verbosity`
  (`model.ts`:92-93), the opposite of A38's gate on both inputs; Q04-17 fails a build that routes whales through it. A
  whale seen while suppressed is dropped, not held. When suppression begins, the open window's pending sum is dropped
  and the window keeps its clock: it closes at its end with only the whales counted after suppression ended, and a close
  with none ends the run, so the next whale is a leading one. A suppression therefore never speaks a whale that arrived
  while it held and never brings two whale-class writes closer than 5 000 ms (AN-08). `announce_prices` does not gate
  whales: a whale print is not a price update.
