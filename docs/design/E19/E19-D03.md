## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this spec settles

The surface of the two profile screens D02 structured: every colour, line, glyph, word and number that tells a trader where the volume sat, and the proof that each meaning survives without colour. It fixes the values of the profile and TPO tokens, the encoding a GPU renderer can draw cheaply, and the three component entries engineering builds against.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk work at the base under the agent-delivery adaptation: no participant, prototype, engineer or Design Review session exists; every judgement is heuristic (§17.1). Contrast is computed, never asserted: ratios with CM:48's `contrastRatio` over BT:92's `loadResolvedThemes` (the CI gate's own resolution), the PROPOSED aliases resolved from PRIM by hand; greyscale with CVD:39's Rec. 601 luma; deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia with CM:109's `simulateCvd` (the gate's transforms; `cvd_sim.py` has no tritanopia, D03-OQ4). The audit tool recomputes the normal and greyscale ratios, the alias resolution, the TPO arithmetic and the footprint-family identity.

### 1.3 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 C-2.11, a hard constraint.** D02's R-1 (D02:97) is kept: the profile reads public market data only and is environment-neutral, so the hi-fi carries **no DEMO / LIVE badge, no environment control and no account context**, and no per-account affordance (no "my fills", no account colour, no account filter) on this shared market-data panel (T Security); the global chrome band is the only environment carrier, and the panel is not a trading-capable surface, so no local badge repeats (`14`:81; BQ-11 ruling). Every board shows synthetic BTCUSDT data and no account identifier. Nothing here may read, mix or switch an environment (CONSTITUTION C-2.11).
