## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this spec settles

The structure of the two profile screens before their surface: where every control, level, word and state lives in both hosting modes; how a docked panel's rows line up with a chart's price scale; how SCR-039 stays reachable at compact; and which words carry meaning so the hi-fi stage (E19-D03) cannot substitute colour for them.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk work at the base under the agent-delivery adaptation: no participant, prototype or engineer session exists; every judgement is heuristic (§16.1). The audit tool checks every cite at its pinned revision, the PD trace, the state ↔ frame reconcile and the board count.

### 1.3 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 C-2.11, a hard constraint.** The profile reads public market data only (`22`:4884 `marketdata:read`, scope none); demo has no separate public feed (`20`:865), so the panel is environment-neutral: it carries **no DEMO / LIVE badge, no environment control and no account context** — the app's global chrome band (`14`:81) is the only environment carrier, and SCR-038 is not a trading-capable surface, so rule 1's local badge does not apply. Nothing on these screens may read, mix or switch an environment (CONSTITUTION C-2.11); an account context ("my fills on this profile") is out of scope and not drawn (T Security).
- **R-2 Words carry meaning.** POC, VAH, VAL and the developing state carry their word at rest (PD-2, PD-1); no state rests on fill, line style, opacity or motion alone; dashed, hollow and hatched marks are reserved (MEMO :163; F-3).
- **R-3 Density changes spacing only** (`16`:141): every density frame shows the same controls, words and levels; only row height, field height and gutters change.
- **R-4 Announcements ≤ 1 Hz.** Polite, once per change; level moves coalesced to at most one per 2 s per field (`05`:151); never per tick or per recompute; errors and state transitions once each.
- **R-5 No field invented.** A control whose contract is missing is drawn with its gap named (G-row) and never wired to an invented parameter.

## 4. Axis alignment (board 03; AC 3)

### 4.1 The rule

1. **Binding.** A docked panel is bound to one named chart: the chart it was docked beside, changeable from the alignment status (a menu of the layout's charts on the same symbol). The header says W-2 while bound and aligned.
2. **Row boundaries.** Row _k_ spans [`price_origin` + _k_ × `aggregation_ticks` × `tick_size`, `price_origin` + (_k_ + 1) × `aggregation_ticks` × `tick_size`) (`24`:987–989). Each boundary price is mapped through the bound chart's own price → y transform of the **same frame** (`26`:216–221): never a separate scale, never an interpolated copy.
3. **Linear scale:** equal row heights. **Log scale:** each boundary through `26`:221's log mapping, so rows at higher prices are drawn thinner, at their true log heights; rows are **never re-gridded** to equal heights and never re-bucketed to "look" linear.
4. **Auto-fit mid-animation:** the panel reads the chart's `priceLow` / `priceHigh` of the frame being drawn (auto-scale is recomputed at most once per frame, `26`:228; sync propagates once per frame, `26`:229); it never jumps to the animation's end state and never lags it by a frame. Motion choreography is E19-D05's.
5. **Under 2 px:** rows merge by summing adjacent levels (`26`:238) and the legend states the merged size (W-20; PD-8).
6. **When alignment cannot hold** — the bound chart shows another symbol, is in percent mode or inverted (`15`:2426), is closed or hidden, or is in log mode with a price ≤ 0 in range (`26`:772) — the panel draws its **own** CMP-188 on its left edge and the header says W-3; it never shows a misaligned histogram beside a chart. It returns to W-2 within one frame of the condition clearing.
