## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this spec settles

Which profile changes move, how, for how long, and when they must not. Profiles change more than any other analysis
surface; an unnoticed POC move is a trading error (T Context), and a 5 000-row histogram cannot ease per row inside the
frame budget (T Technical notes). Every row in §2 either earns its motion by what it communicates or is a cut.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Written from the contracts, the parents' states and the merged motion precedents (E05-D03, E08-D11, E11-D06, E14-D09,
E15-D14). Nothing is measured in PR 1: §4.2's threshold is arithmetic on PROPOSED planning figures, measured by the PR 2
prototype, which is not the engine. The flash figures in §8 are computed by the tool from the shipped tokens through
`docs/design/_tools/contrast_check.py`. No participant, timing or quote is invented.

### 1.3 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Truth first.** A level (POC, VAH, VAL, a ray, a bracket cap) is drawn at its new price in the frame its update
  lands; motion only follows the truth and never moves a level (T Context; E14-D09's DNA-1 by analogy).
- **R-2 Per update, never between updates.** An entry starts on a received update and ends before the next one can
  land; nothing interpolates between two updates (BQ-3). A per-update entry (M-1) runs only while the topic's
  `effective.throttle_ms` (`23`:404) is ≥ 500 ms, HS-1's start gap taken as a gate (PROPOSED, D-2); below it, a cut
  (§4.1).
- **R-3 Tokens.** The seven primitives only (`16`:119; PRIM:454–481); histogram ink takes `motion.duration.instant`,
  `motion.duration.fast` or `motion.duration.normal`, never `slow` (E05-D03:77); a cut is `motion.duration.instant` ·
  `—`. No token is added (T OOS).
- **R-4 Canvas entries.** One per-instance attribute or one uniform each, +0 draw calls, a TRANSFORM-dirty redraw
  (E11-D06:99), integrated on real `dt` so a dropped frame never changes the outcome (`26`:130), inside the 1 ms
  input-and-animations budget (`26`:139) and the ≤ 2 ms pane redraw (`06`:78).
- **R-5 HS-1 on every data-driven start** (E05-D03:105): ≥ 500 ms between starts per element, the latest update wins
  and nothing queues, an element changing at ≥ 1 Hz for 10 s stops emphasising until quiet; values and words always
  update. MR-1 (E05-D03:100) is the gate. **Combined cap (PROPOSED):** the emphasis entries M-2, M-4 and M-8
  together start at most 3 in any half-open 1 000 ms window per pane; the 4th and later in that window are cuts, so
  one pane region never sees more than MR-1's 3 starts whichever entries meet in it (§8.1).
- **R-6 Carriers (MR-3, E05-D03:118).** Every row names a non-motion carrier present in both modes, so the reduce
  tree equals the safe end state's; no meaning is carried by motion alone (`16`:119, :206).
- **R-7 Announcements.** D02 R-4 unchanged (D02:100): one polite region per panel, ≤ 1 per 2 s per field, latest wins,
  never per tick or per recompute; no new live region; no announcement waits on motion. This spec's strings use that
  region under the same rule (§8.2); focus never moves on a recompute, a cut or an entry, and the pane is not remounted.
- **R-8 One motion source.** Reduce is the resolved `16` §4 mode (`26`:788; E11-D06:97): the OS setting or the in-app
  override `Ctrl+Shift+M` (`05`:232); the engine reads it as `RenderFlags.animate` (flags.ts:3–8), and a flag that
  turns off, including the override toggled mid-entry, snaps a running entry to rest.
- **R-9 Forced colours (PROPOSED).** Under `forced-colors: active` the DOM parts of this spec follow the OS: the busy
  bar is drawn as a 2 px `Highlight` border-bottom of the header, the legend words (W-1, W-2, D03 W-10, D03 W-11, "{k}
  hidden") stay text in `CanvasText`, and none relies on a token colour. The canvas is not recoloured by the OS; its
  inks stay the in-app theme's, and the in-app high-contrast theme is a theme, not the OS mode. E19-D06 checks it.
- **R-10 Text scale.** At 200 % zoom or the in-app text scale (`05`:148) the legend words wrap or truncate with a
  tooltip equal to the full text, none is clipped, and the busy bar spans the header's width, never a px length.

## 4. The input cadence and the row threshold (BQ-3, BQ-4)

### 4.1 Cadence

T's "grows on every print" (T Context) meets a topic whose default throttle is 1 000 ms (`23`:753), raisable on
request to no less than 50 ms (`23`:404) and multiplied under `lagging` (`23`:1345) and `saturated` (`23`:1346). R-2
decides each row from `effective.throttle_ms`:

| `effective.throttle_ms` | Frames between updates at 60 Hz | M-1 growth | M-2 trace starts in any 1 s |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------- |
| 1 000 (the default)     | 60                              | runs       | 1                           |
| 250 (a request)         | 15                              | cut        | 2                           |
| 50 (the floor)          | 3                               | cut        | 2                           |

`motion.duration.fast` is 6 frames and `motion.duration.normal` 12 at 60 Hz, so every entry that starts ends inside the
60-frame default interval; under a request below 500 ms, HS-1 holds the trace to 2 starts a second and the growth is a
cut.

### 4.2 The threshold T_rows (AC 3)

Per-row motion costs one eased value per changed row per frame. The arithmetic below is PROPOSED planning and the PR 2
prototype measures it; a missed budget is "inconclusive" and the minimal form, a cut, ships (E14-D09:77, :113).

| Quantity                                     | Value                     | Source                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Input and animations per frame               | 1 ms                      | `26`:139                   |
| Profile motion share of it                   | 0.6 ms                    | PROPOSED                   |
| Planning cost per eased row                  | 5 µs                      | PROPOSED; measured in PR 2 |
| T_rows = ⌊share ÷ cost⌋                      | 120                       | computed                   |
| Bytes per frame at T_rows (4 B per instance) | 480 B                     | computed                   |
| Worst case visible rows                      | 5 000                     | `14`:1013                  |
| Worst case per-row motion                    | none (5 000 > 120: a cut) | computed                   |

Above T_rows the profile is already merging rows under 2 px (`26`:238), where an eased length would move several
levels as one; the worst case T names (5 000 rows, visible range, live tape) has no per-row animation at all: visible
range is M-7, a cut, and 5 000 is above T_rows.

## 5. POC relocation and developing → final (AC 2, AC 4)

### 5.1 The POC jump (M-2)

The update lands; in that frame the POC rail and its word are at the new row (R-1) and W-1 replaces the previous move
words. If the POC moved ≥ 2 rows, one band in the rail's lane (4 px wide, PROPOSED) spans the previous POC row to the
new one in `color.profile.poc` at 0.25 and fades to 0 over `motion.duration.normal` · `motion.easing.accelerate`; it
never covers a bar. In dark the peak is a hint (ΔL 0.035, §8.1): the rail, W-1 and the announcement carry the move
there, the band only adds where it came from. Only the previous received POC is held (one value, never a path), so the client keeps no history
the model does not give (T Technical notes; `24`:1002 is not on the wire, MEMO:249–250). A POC flipping between tied
levels (`24`:1009) is HS-1's case: ≥ 500 ms between starts, latest wins, and a POC that moved at ≥ 1 Hz for 10 s
shows no band until quiet; W-1 still updates.

**The announcement (PROPOSED; C-8).** A move of ≥ 2 rows appends W-1b to D02 W-33's text in the same polite update
("POC {price}, VAH {price}, VAL {price}, POC moved up 3 rows"), still ≤ 1 per 2 s, latest wins, so a screen-reader
user hears the jump and its size, not only the new level. HS-1's quiet rule applies to it too: a POC changing at
≥ 1 Hz for 10 s is not announced again until quiet; the legend words keep updating.

### 5.2 Developing → final (M-3, M-4)

While `complete` is false (`24`:1004) the band and the VAH / VAL lines are cut per update, and the developing value
recomputes by default once per 60 s (`developing_interval_ms`, `24`:1019). When `complete` turns true, in one frame: D03 W-10 becomes D03 W-11 and
the bracket gains its end caps (D03:267); then one `color.profile.valuearea` emphasis over the closed bracket's 8 px lane
(PROPOSED width), 0.25 → 0 over `motion.duration.normal` · `motion.easing.accelerate`, once; D03 W-11 is announced
once (D02:250). Neither `22`:20300 nor `23`:3136 carries `complete` yet: MEMO G-1 (MEMO:249) owns it and is carried by
cite (C-2).

### 5.3 Reduce forms (AC 2)

Under reduce, the POC relocation stays perceivable through the rail at its new row, W-1 with its direction glyph and
row count, and D02 W-33 at ≤ 1 per 2 s (D02:344) with W-1b appended on a ≥ 2-row move; the value area's finalisation through D03 W-11, the end caps and its
one announcement. Both are the same nodes the safe mode shows (R-6), so no meaning is lost and the accessibility trees
are equal.

## 6. Re-bucket, period change and visible range (AC 5)

### 6.1 Re-bucket and period change (M-5, M-6)

On a row-size, VA % or period change (`11`:1704; `14`:1026) the panel keeps the last frame un-dimmed and sets
`aria-busy`. If the recompute is still pending after 200 ms (PROPOSED), D03's 2 px busy bar in `color.focus.ring`
appears under the header as a cut, static, never a sweep (D03:314). When the result lands: one frame replaces the
histogram, the legend's row-size, VA % and period words swap in that frame, the busy bar leaves, and W-2 is announced
once. Announcements (PROPOSED; C-8): if the bar appears, W-2a is said once; W-2 and W-2a share the panel's one
polite region with D02 W-36, W-37 and W-38 under R-7, so W-2 follows the action's D02 word by ≥ 2 s and a later
recompute in that gap replaces it (latest wins): stepping the row-size spinner says W-2 once, for the last value. Focus
stays on the control that started it (the SCR-039 `Save`, a legend control); nothing moves it (R-7). It never shows the stale shade, D02 W-18 or E17's words, which mean the feed broke; board 06 draws the two side by
side. A composite look-back shows its progress (`14`:1026) as a determinate bar (PROPOSED), updated as cuts, with
`role="progressbar"`, the accessible name "Recalculating" and `aria-valuenow` updated at ≤ 1 per 2 s, not per cut.

### 6.2 Visible range (M-7)

Recompute is ≤ 10 Hz during a pan or zoom and debounced at its end (`11`:1730; `14`:1013; D02-C6 at D02:403); each
result is a cut, nothing eases during the gesture or at the settle, and only the plot and the legend re-render
(D02:319). Interaction keeps ≥ 55 fps (`11`:1724).

## 12. Heuristic evaluation (heuristic; no participants)
