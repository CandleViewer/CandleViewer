## 1. Locked inputs (built on, never re-decided)

1. **C-2.11 and WD-1.** Demo and live keep separate state and chrome. The list never sums the two: "never a combined total" (`D01`:130). The ticket's AC-2 Gherkin outranks interim WD-1 (BQ-01, ruled): both environments may be on, and WD-1 and D01's OQ-3 are recorded as amended for SCR-093 (C-1).
2. **The stories and research.** WD-5 orders the columns (`D01`:134); WD-7 makes tags free text with an optional category, ≤ 24 characters, counted in the manager (`D01`:136); WD-9 keeps one row per account and no aggregate row in a total (`D01`:138); WD-10 states the export's scope (`D01`:139); WD-12 opens SCR-094 from a row and SCR-096 as a modal from the header (`D01`:141); WD-13 keeps a Viewer's writes visible as T2 (`D01`:142).
3. **Server truth.** Totals are server-computed and may lag the rows (`14`:2181); tag operations are server-side, "a single transaction with progress rather than a client-side loop" (`14`:2228). The list is cursor-paginated and virtualised (`14`:2181): no sticky sub-header inside the virtualised body, no per-row image, sparkline or live subscription (`T` Performance).
4. **T2** (`14` §0.5 #5; `DS` G-T2). A journal write the session may not make is disabled with a visible reason, programmatically associated through CMP-079 RbacGate (`15`:898). Reasons are E09-D09 §3.1's keys, verbatim; a new reason is a PROPOSED key with its owner named. Each T2 control keeps its native role and full label, and its row names its rendering (G-T2.1c): 2a, the reason inline, the label led by the CMP-019 `lock` glyph ("Not available"; G-T2.1d); 2b, the CMP-016 Tooltip echo, for icon-only controls, the `lock` the badge on the icon; or the sentence form, where there is no control and no `lock`. G-T2.1b, verbatim (`DS`:28–29): `aria-disabled="true"`, never the `disabled` attribute — it stays in the Tab order and a menu item stays arrow-reachable. G-T2.2, verbatim (`DS`:40–42): hi-fi label `color.text.tertiary`, reason `color.text.secondary`; for primary / buy / sell actions the fill becomes `color.surface.raised` with a `color.border.default` hairline; never `color.text.disabled`, never `opacity.disabled` on the label, glyph or reason (the label pair is §8.1 P-04). G-T2.3, verbatim (`DS`:47–48): `Enter`, `Space` or a bound hotkey on a T2 control changes nothing and re-shows the reason inline with one polite announcement; arrow keys land on a disabled menu item without activating it. G-T2.5, verbatim (`DS`:57–58): a panel that is read-only for the session carries one panel-header notice; every gated control's `aria-describedby` points at that node. Two sessions make SCR-093 read-only for the session: a Viewer (every journal write needs `journal:write`) and view-as (every write); each gets one notice under the header (S93-18, S93-19), never a reason under each control.
5. **The C-2.6 carve-out.** No control on SCR-093 or SCR-096 places, amends, cancels or protects an order. Every gate in this spec covers journal writes only (tags, rating, exclusion, the tag taxonomy). Cancel, close, flatten and setting or tightening a stop live on the trading surfaces and are never gated by anything here.
6. **RBAC is server-side** (C-12.4). Reads are `journal:read` with `scope: granted_accounts` (`22`:9682); trade writes are `journal:write` on granted accounts (`22`:9746); creating a tag is `journal:write` with `scope: none` (`22`:9848). A Manager holds `journal:write` (`22`:1167), so a Viewer's reason is `rbac.permission` with the verb "write in the journal" (`E09D09`:113), not `rbac.owner_only`. **Tag rename, merge and delete are owner-only (BQ-24, ruled; C-12.4):** a tag is global, so one rename, merge or delete reaches trades outside a Manager's grant, and cross-manager reach is never the drawn default. For a Manager the three are T2 `rbac.owner_only` (T-07, S96-20); Create stays open to a Manager, and a Viewer never opens the manager (S96-13). The ruling is drawn ahead of the contract, as E09-D09 applies its recording ruling over the file (`E09D09`:97): `22` has no rename, merge or delete operation on a tag today, only list and create (`22`:9817; G-3), and G-11 asks E41-T04 to add the three owner-only, never on `journal:write` alone, which a Manager holds (`22`:1167). Every count a non-owner reads is grant-scoped, and `usage_count` counts every trade (`22`:9839), so a Manager's Usage column waits on G-11 (§3.1). If the contract makes tags per user (OQ-1), the T2 lifts by a change row. Opening replay creates a replay session (`replay:write`, `22`:6256), which a Viewer lacks (`22`:1179, `replay:read` only).
7. **The ladder.** AD-11 (`E42D02`:56) and D3's one anatomy per rung (`E42D02`:129): a merge and a delete destroy taxonomy no screen can rebuild, so both are rung 3, the typed token the source tag's own name (BQ-09); a rename is rung 1 with a counted sentence that restates the affected count (BQ-09, ruled; `14`:2227); a create needs no confirm.
8. **Focus (E47-D04, the focus owner).** A removed row moves focus to the next row in the current sort, else the previous, else the grid's empty-state stop (`E47D04`:285); a re-auth card puts focus on its first credential field (`E47D04`:302). A merge, a delete, and a tag or exclusion change that drops a row out of a filtered view all follow it.

### 2.1 Layout and columns (CL-01; WD-5)

- **Header:** the H1 "Journal", then on the right **Manage tags** (CMP-001 secondary; opens SCR-096) and **Export** (CMP-001 secondary; opens the export confirm). Below, the CMP-055 FilterBar (§2.3).
- **Columns, in this order** (BQ-06, ruled; the order PROPOSED on WD-5): select · Closed (UTC) · Symbol · Side · R · Net P&L (USDT) · Size · Entry · Exit · Fees (USDT) · Opened (UTC) · Account · Env · Tags · Rating. R and net P&L precede entry and exit (`D01`:134). "Net P&L" has no unambiguous field: `22` serves `realised_pnl` (`22`:22593) while `21` stores `net_pnl` net of fees and funding (`21`:3362–3365), so the column's source waits on G-13 (C-10). The Env cell repeats the group's word (LIVE or DEMO) so a copied or exported row is never misread.
- **Numbers.** Numerals `type.num.base` in `color.text.primary`, end-aligned. R and P&L always carry their sign and unit ("+2.10R", "−30.00 USDT"; `14`:2178); the header words "net" and "USDT" are PROPOSED (BQ-04). The hue is never the carrier: P&L numerals stay neutral, as E29-D03's CR-5 keeps them (`E29D03`:149; BQ-05), and CMP-116's accessible name says "profit" or "loss". Side is the word with a glyph: ▲ in `color.buy.default`, ▼ in `color.sell.default` (§8.1 P-08, P-09).
- **Symbol** is a CMP-021 link that opens SCR-094 (WD-12), named "Open trade BTCUSDT closed 13:58 UTC".
- **Tags** are CMP-011 neutral chips (§3.5): the name, a swatch, and on an auto-applied tag (`D01`:137) the word "auto" and no remove control (BQ-07); the cell shows the chips that fit, then "+N more" (K-49), and the cell's accessible name and its CMP-016 echo list every tag (BQ-25, ruled).
- **Rating** is "4 / 5" as text, or "Not rated" (K-57; BQ-17).
- **Divergent** (E41-T02): a trade whose P&L or fees differ from the exchange's closed P&L carries "P&L differs from the exchange — under review" (K-55) under its P&L, after the CMP-019 `warning` glyph in `color.status.warning.strong` (§8.1 P-19); it is shown, never hidden, and its figures stay (S93-22). No field carries the flag yet (G-14).
- **Row menu** (the actions column, CMP-041): Add tag…, Remove tag… (manual tags only: an auto tag is never offered, BQ-07), Set rating…, Exclude from statistics, Open in replay (K-48). On an excluded trade the fourth item reads "Include in statistics", and the row carries the text marker "Excluded from statistics" under the symbol (K-56; BQ-16). The first four are journal writes (T2 for a Viewer and under view-as, §6). Open in replay creates a replay session (§1 item 6): for a Viewer it is T2 on `replay:write` (T-08), and under view-as it points at the one notice (T-04). Every item stays arrow-reachable.

### 2.2 Environment groups (AC-2; CL-02; C-1)

- **The scope control** sits before the FilterBar, outside it (CMP-055 holds chips only): a `role="group"` "Environment" of two CMP-004 Toggle (Switch) controls, LIVE and DEMO, each `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`. LIVE is on by default (BQ-01, ruled). Both may be on; the last one on cannot be turned off, so a scope is always named.
- **One scope:** one group, headed by the CMP-075 grammar badge and the line "LIVE only — DEMO trades are not included" (K-01) or its DEMO twin (K-02).
- **Both scopes:** **two separated groups, one per environment, each individually totalled; no combined total is rendered across the two.** LIVE comes first. Each group is its own region (`role="region"`, named "LIVE trades" or "DEMO trades") holding its own CMP-049 table with its own caption, header row, totals row and cursor pager. A 24 px gap (`space.6`) and the group heading separate them; the heading sits **outside** each table, so it is never a sticky sub-header inside a virtualised body (`T` Technical notes). Up to four groups stack: LIVE, LIVE · paper, DEMO, DEMO · paper (BQ-02). Each table's body is its own bounded scroll region, at most half the viewport, virtualised independently, focusable (`tabindex="0"`) and named by its caption (BQ-03, ruled); at 200 % zoom each region takes CMP-049's stacked reflow inside its own cap, so the page scrolls down and no region scrolls sideways (R1).
- **Paper trades** (`21`:3349, `is_paper`) are orthogonal to the environment (`22`:19360), so a paper trade can be LIVE or DEMO. Paper is its own group inside each environment, "LIVE · paper trades" after LIVE and "DEMO · paper trades" after DEMO, each individually totalled and never summed with LIVE or DEMO, because live and paper results are never mixed (`11`:4697; BQ-02, ruled). SCR-093 shows paper as its own group by default; SCR-095 hides paper behind an explicit second scope row that is off on each visit (#1081 BQ-16). The asymmetry is deliberate and mirrors `14`'s DEMO default for analytics (`14`:2219): a list shows every trade in scope, grouped, while an analytics figure never takes paper in unasked.
- **Group borders:** a 2 px `border.thick` rule in `color.env.live` or `color.env.demo` at the group's start edge, with the word; the hue is never the only carrier (§8.2 V-01, V-02).

### 2.3 Filters (CL-03; `T` Security)

Environment is the switch group of §2.2; the other five are CMP-055 chips that name their field and value: Date range (CMP-047; default "Last 30 days"), Symbol, Account, Tag, Outcome (Win, Loss, Breakeven; `22`:9679). Session, rule source and setup (`14`:2176) sit behind "More filters" (K-43).

### 2.4 Totals and the recomputing affordance (`14`:2181)

- **The totals row** closes each group's table, outside the virtualised body: "42 trades · win 57% · expectancy +0.34R · net P&L +1,204.00 USDT" (K-12; the shape of `14`:2171, unit-labelled). It names its filter: "for LIVE · 1 Oct – 30 Oct 2026 · Accounts in your scope (3)".
- **Recomputing:** when a filter, a tag or a fill changes the set, the last totals stay, unchanged, with "Recomputing totals…" (K-13) as a CMP-038 line beside them and `aria-busy="true"` on the totals row. The new totals replace them in one cut when the server answers; a number never tweens or counts. "Totals as of 14:02:11 UTC" (K-14) follows the figures.
- **Server only:** the client never sums the rows (WD-9; G-2).

### 2.5 Bulk tagging (CL-04)

- Selecting rows (the select column; `Space` on a row) shows a bulk bar above the table: "3 selected" (K-21), **Add tag**, **Remove tag**, **Clear selection** (K-44).

- **Remove tag** lists manual tags only: an auto tag is never offered for removal here (BQ-07, ruled).
- Selection is per group: selecting in LIVE never selects a DEMO row.

### 2.6 Export (CL-06; WD-10)

## 7. Accessibility (`05`)

1. **Roles and names.** SCR-093's tables are CMP-049 grids (the select column makes them interactive), each with a caption ("LIVE trades, 1 Oct – 30 Oct 2026"), `aria-rowcount` the whole group and an absolute `aria-rowindex` under virtualisation; sortable headers are buttons with `aria-sort`. SCR-096 is `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, labelled by its title; its table is a grid.
2. **Keyboard (R3).** SCR-093: on route entry to `/journal` focus lands on the page `h1` (`tabindex="-1"`; `E47D04`:157); Tab order header actions → FilterBar → each group's grid (one stop each) → its pager; in a grid ↑/↓ by row, ←/→ by cell, `Space` selects, `Enter` on the symbol opens SCR-094, `Shift+F10` opens the row menu.
