## 1. Purpose, method and the rules stated once

**Purpose.** No E41 frontend story may start before this gate (`T` Context). The review checks D02's and D03's four screens against `05` and WCAG 2.2 AA, authors the speech and the keys they leave open, fixes the motion, and packs what engineering builds from.

**Method.** Each WCAG criterion `T` names is traced to the parent section that answers it (§2). Where nothing answers, that is a finding (§8) with a copy-ready A-row (§9).

- **R-1 Severity.** `Blocking`: a journal task a keyboard or screen-reader user cannot complete, or meaning carried by colour alone. `Major`: a WCAG A / AA failure, or a name, focus rule, announcement or pattern the ticket asks for and the parents leave unspecified; a G-T2 miss is Major (`DS`:15). `Minor`: a gap with a working path. `Info`: confirmed, accepted or routed.
- **R-2 Closing.** `fix: A-n` (its Old text or anchor found once in its target), `applied: <section>`, `routed: <owner>` or `accepted: <reason>`. A Major closes by an A-row, or is routed only when its fix needs a contract change the design cannot make (C-6.1).
- **R-3 Honesty.** `(heuristic)` everywhere; the screen-reader run is E41-Q03's.
- **R-4 The boundary.** The review checks and authors speech and keys; it does not redesign. Every new string is PROPOSED with its owner.
- **R-5 One vocabulary** (the deck's): `merged` (verbatim on a merged line at the base), `RULED` (an unmerged parent's text that its BQ ruling settled), `PROPOSED` (owner approval pending), plus a separate `frozen` group id for strings that ship verbatim (FZ-01 … FZ-05).

## 7. Motion (`T` Scope; `05` §7; G-MOTION)

### 7.1 Rules stated once

- **MR-1 No information in motion.** Every moment's end state carries its meaning in text, a glyph or geometry, identical in both modes (`05`:249; `16`:119).
- **MR-2 Tokens only.** Durations and easings are `motion.duration.instant`, `.fast`, `.normal`, `.slow` and `motion.easing.standard`, `.decelerate`, `.accelerate` (`16`:119), or a cut (one frame). E05-D03's spinner and shimmer use CSS `linear` as their recipe does (`E05D03`:74, `E05D03`:75); no bespoke curve is added.
- **MR-3 Reduce is a cut.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the in-app override every moment is its end state at frame 0; `05`'s low-amplitude cross-fade is not used.
- **MR-4 Numbers never tween.** A count, a total or a statistic changes in one frame.
- **MR-5 Motion never moves focus and never waits for speech.**
