### 1.2 Method

Heuristic design over the merged wireframes and the merged specs; no session, no participant, no measured time; every evaluative statement `(heuristic)`; every number from the audit tool (Decimal, tick 0.10); every merged spec at its merge commit; every PROPOSED item stamped; every `C-x.y` verified verbatim in `CONSTITUTION.md` and every `CMP-nnn` / `SCR-nnn` against its catalogue heading (the tool's gates). The ticket's "CDO", "accessibility specialist" and "engineering feasibility review" are the owner's `approved` under the adaptations, recorded as owner approval pending.

## 5. The translation the readout shows (every digit the tool's; Decimal, tick 0.10)

**The formula** (`22` :6680–6682: the server translates a percentage into a price distance "using the current mark price"; `24` :2906: the adapter converts percent to a distance "at arm time"; the `value` on the wire is `22`'s percent, "0.15" = 0.15 % — not `24` :2532's fraction, D05-C13): `distance = ref × pct`, `ref` = the mark the frame shows (64,012.5); for a long the first trigger = `activation − distance` once armed (`activation` = entry + R × 1 R, else the mark when it arms at once); for a short, `+`. **Rounding (BQ-9, PROPOSED to the `24` owner):** the distance is rounded **up** to the tick (a trail never sits closer than asked); the trigger then follows `24` :2532's own rule — "rounded conservatively (further from entry, never closer)" — and rounding the distance up moves the trigger at most one tick further from entry, which is that rule.
