## 12. The invariant: what the affordance may and may not say (C-2.6, C-4.14, C-2.12, C-12.4; T Sec)

The native exchange stop-loss is a **server-side invariant**: every position-opening order carries a native exchange-side SL, with no flag, environment, feature flag or "advanced mode" that disables it (`CONST`:128–134), flags never gate it (`CONST`:443–444), and the frontend's hiding or disabling is cosmetic — the backend behaves identically if the client is hostile (`CONST`:164–166, `CONST`:769–773). The control is E32-T01's single submission choke point (#931). Therefore the affordance **never implies the stop can be removed or disabled**: no field offers an empty held stop at rest (§5), no toggle exists ("There is no stop toggle", `15`:1426), no role or setting is drawn as a way around it (the loosen slot widens a stop, never removes it, D02:168), and every gated state names the stop Bybit holds (D02:167). The affordance **never claims more than the invariant gives**: the exchange holds the stop; CandleViewer's emulated layers are named as CandleViewer's and stop acting when it stops (§6); no sentence promises protection during an exchange outage or a fill at the stop price (§4). One server path places the stop after the order: E32-T01's `PASS_DEFERRED` outcome (#931, body line 25), in which the `trading-stop` write is scheduled with a 2 s deadline; until the stop lands — or if it never does — the `unprotected` banner shows it (`E30D04`:195 §2.3, `E30D04`:199: sustained while `native_stop_present` is false; `15`:336), never this affordance — a rejected attach cancels or flattens the leg and raises `SL_ATTACH_FAILED` (`CONST`:131–132), and no sentence here states a time by which the stop is attached. S-2 / S-6's "not after it" names the `PASS_ATTACHED` path (§4; C-15). The UI's `aria-disabled` and its validity look are a courtesy, not the control: a hostile client that bypasses them meets the same refusal from the server, surfaced as the refusal companion (§3). This is the statement the Fable light lens reads on PR 1 (RULED BQ-19).
