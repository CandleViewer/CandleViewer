## 1. Purpose, scope, method and locked inputs

### 1.1 What this spec fixes

Three modals are drawn together so that one native-stop treatment, one table grammar and one set of words hold across
them: SCR-064 (an open position's stop, targets, trail and breakeven), SCR-065 (a generated scaled or DCA entry
ladder, previewed and placed) and SCR-069 (the bracket half: entry, required stop, one target or a target ladder; the
OCO half is an annotated E33 extension point). Every state of the ticket's lists and of `14` is a named frame (§0)
with its data source, copy in place, live-region role, focus order and events. Out of scope (ticket): hi-fi visuals,
tokens and motion (E32-D03–D06); the OCO half (E33); the order ticket SCR-060 itself (E30; its two entry points are
proposed, §6.4); chart drag mechanics (E31; the keyboard twin is drawn, §4.5); alert carriers (E40).

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk work under the agent-delivery adaptations. The catalogue entries, the contracts at `e69a9200c` (`22`, `23`, `24`,
`28` B8), E32-D01's approved defaults and the merged E30-D02 were read, and every drawn state was checked against them
(§8). The engineering, QA and accessibility walkthrough the ticket asks for is that agent review (§8) and the heuristic
evaluation (§9); the owner's `approved` on #855 is the sign-off. No participant took part; every judgement is
`(heuristic)`.

### 3.7 Live regions and focus (`05` §6.2, §3.4)

Polite: read-outs, pending and progress lines and preview answers, each at most once per 2 s and always the latest
value (`05` §6.2). Assertive, once: a refusal, a rejection, `naked`, `stop-reattached`, the position closing, an arm
change. Nothing per tick; focus never moves on a push (`05`:96): a state that arrives mid-edit keeps focus where it is
(a control it gates turns T2 and stays focusable, §3.6) and is announced; the state tables' Focus column is the focus
when the modal opens in that state. State controls insert in the Tab order after the region they act on and before
the footer (§4.4, §5.5, §6.5). Under reduced motion progress bars step and highlights are instant (`05` §7).

### 6.5 Focus order and events

unit (ladder: each rung's % then R) → breakeven check → its R → the arm pill (LIVE) → Cancel → Place. The E33 slot holds
nothing focusable. `Enter` in a field commits it and never places, as in §5.5. State controls (§3.7): a banner's button
