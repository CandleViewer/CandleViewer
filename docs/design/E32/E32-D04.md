### 1.2 Method

### 1.3 The two rules, stated once (every later section with a gated control cites "§1.3")

**T2 — disabled with a visible reason** (E09-D09 §2.2 :46, §3.1 :83, §3.2 :99, §9.1 :224, §9.3 :238; CMP-079 RbacGate `15` :799; CMP-019 Icon `15` :224).
