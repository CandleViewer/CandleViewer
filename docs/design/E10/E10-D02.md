Penpot page **E10-D02 Shell Wireframes** · page id `a4fbc4c3-2c4d-80f4-8008-b28e21dc0945`. Frames are named `SCR-nnn / <state>` (`docs/design/README.md`); r2 replaces each 2026-09-26 frame of the same name in place and adds the ones that had no frame in a new row.

## Purpose

Low-fidelity structural wireframes for every E10 shell surface (app shell, nav rail, command palette, hotkey cheatsheet, toast host, floating panel window, system-state banners, dialogs and pages) so engineering can be estimated against fixed anatomy before the E10-D03 hi-fi pass. Greyscale: the neutral semantic tokens of the dark theme (`packages/ui/tokens`), named in each shape's plugin data, with **one exception**, the environment badge (ruling 2, below). The T2 lock glyph (CMP-019 `lock`, E09-D09) is a stand-in drawn from two shapes in its label's text token, never a font glyph that could fall back to a colour emoji. No colour, type, icon or elevation decision is made here (E10-D03); no motion (E10-D04).

## Deviations

- **State × role.** Four state frames are drawn for the owner, read-only for the viewer and normal for the manager (f31). A Viewer's session is read-only by role (14 SCR-010 names the state "read-only (viewer)"), so the Viewer's normal shell is f5, and the 2026-09-26 `normal-viewer` export, a byte copy of `normal-manager`, is removed. The role differences of the other states are drawn where their owners drew them: the Manager's NavRail and the Viewer's disabled submit on E09-D09 boards 06 and 02 (AC2, per the re-QA), the Manager's frozen and lockout banners on E39-D01's SCR-073 boards.
- **Manager FREEZE.** 14 SCR-010 (:292) says FREEZE is owner-only; E39-D01 D-1 (#2701, F12) lets a Manager freeze within their grants, never flatten, so f31 draws "Freeze my account…". Amending the catalogue line is E39's.
- **The kept normal frames are thin.** The three `normal-owner` frames are the 2026-09-26 skeletons: the 1280×800 export is a crop of the 1920×1080 frame and the 2560×1440 export is that frame parked in a larger board. The five redrawn state frames and f31 carry the full shell anatomy instead. f22–f26 and f28 draw a context TopBar without the HealthChips row, so they do not show the 80 px TopBar fixed below; the 1280×800 context shell of f24–f26 and f28 also has a compact TopBar (no account scope, workspace switcher, symbol and interval pickers or search trigger), so they are not the SCR-010 1280×800 layout: real 1280×800 and 2560×1440 layouts need the three `normal-owner` frames redrawn, an owner decision (Dependencies). The context frames draw less: f6–f9 draw the rail at its real height between a TopBar stub (wordmark, one ENV badge) and a StatusBar stub (the feed chip); f10–f14, f21 and f29 draw a context strip (wordmark and ENV badges; the user menu on f14, f21 and f29, where the strip is wide enough); f15–f20 draw the surface alone (the printable sheet, the toast corner, the OS windows with their own badges). These frames leave out the rest of the TopBar, including the Safety cluster (E39-D01 A0), and the StatusBar's ENV repeat: both are SCR-010's, drawn in full on f1–f5, f30 and f31.
- **125 % text scale.** Drawn for the cheatsheet (f14 inset); the TopBar and NavRail reflow stays a written behaviour (Accessibility notes), not a frame.
- **Superseded 2026-09-26 anatomy.** The canvas scrim for frozen, the shell-wide scrim for lockout, the centred reconnect overlay for disconnected and "Viewer role — read only" follow their owners now (E39-D01, E17-D01, E09-D09; States below).
- **Viewer read-only without E17-D01's banner.** E17-D01 §4 lists the viewer role among the Read-only overlay's reasons and its §5 puts a Read-only banner ("Order entry off — <message>. <what still works>") in the global chrome. For a standing role f5 follows E09-D09 §2.2 instead, "one notice, many pointers": the ticket's panel notice carries the reason and every gated control's `aria-describedby` points at it, so a Viewer's shell shows no banner. Both owners are cited; the convergence is E17-D01's owner's to record (Dependencies).

## SCR-010 App shell — structure

Regions, top to bottom and left to right, fixed at every breakpoint (columns resize; region order and presence do not change): **TopBar** h 80 (a 48 px first row and the 32 px HealthChips row) · banner slot (0 when nothing is shown) · **NavRail** w 240 (64 collapsed) · **WorkspaceCanvas** · **RightRail** w 240 · **StatusBar** h 48.

- **TopBar** (CMP-072): wordmark · ENV badge (CMP-074) · account scope (CMP-105) · workspace switcher (CMP-073) · symbol (CMP-219) and interval (CMP-220) · search trigger with Ctrl+K (CMP-084) · user menu with the role in words (CMP-061) · the **Safety cluster** (CMP-211) at the right. The second row is **HealthChips** (CMP-210): exchange WS, ingest lag, recorder and rate budget, each "label: state · value" with the state in a word; a warn or error chip is focusable and opens its admin screen (`15` CMP-210; E17-D01's global chrome for backend-health causes, D17). It is a row of its own because at 1920 px the first row has no room left beside the Safety cluster; 14 SCR-010's sketch also draws the TopBar on two rows.
- **NavRail** (CMP-071): the Terminal section with its workspaces, Rules, Journal, Replay, Alerts and Settings (counts in words: "Rules · 3 armed", "Alerts · 2 new"), and the **Admin entry for the owner only — structurally absent, with no gap or placeholder, for every other role** (AC2; E09-D09 §6).
- **WorkspaceCanvas**: hosts panels owned by other epics (SCR-030 chart, SCR-060 ticket, SCR-063 positions grid, drawn only to show what each state does to them); this ticket reserves the region and its isolation boundary.
- **RightRail** (CMP-209): the drawer stack — Notifications (SCR-014), Alerts, Recorder and detail drawers (14 SCR-010, `15` CMP-209); this replaces the 2026-09-26 "positions/orders/alerts/journal quick-view".
- **StatusBar** (CMP-208, a labelled `role="status"` region, ≤ 1 Hz): one feed chip (CMP-076; E17-D01 D1, one state per surface) · recorder · rules armed · clock offset · clock · bar close · **Kill switch…**, the kill-switch quick access (CMP-208 `onKillSwitch`; `12` §1.1): a named button that opens the same FREEZE confirmation as the Safety cluster and never engages by itself (E39-D01 D2, D12; the dialog is E39-D03's), T2 wherever FREEZE… is T2 · the ENV repeat (CMP-075) · version.

**Safety cluster (CMP-211) — ruling 1.** E39-D01, the kill-switch owner, places the Safety cluster at the right of the TopBar on every screen (its A0), with a kill-switch status line; the cluster holds CMP-211's three actions, FLATTEN ALL…, CANCEL ALL… and FREEZE… (`15` CMP-211). FREEZE… uses `confirmMode: "single"`: the control opens one blast-radius confirmation — focus on Cancel, Tab then Enter — and `Ctrl+Shift+K` opens the same dialog and never engages (E39-D01 D2, D12, R3-8). The confirmation dialogs, and the confirm treatment of FLATTEN ALL… and CANCEL ALL…, are E39-D03's (#879); this spec does not restate them. This replaces the 2026-09-26 RightRail-top placement and hold-to-confirm text, and the matching Study 2 decision in `docs/design/E10/research-findings.md`.

**Environment badge — ruling 2.** CMP-074 in the TopBar, CMP-075 in the StatusBar, on every trading panel, every grid row and every window: the word **DEMO** / **LIVE** in `color.text.primary` on `color.surface.raised`, and the shipped `color.env.demo` / `color.env.live` (`packages/ui/tokens/semantic-dark.tokens.json`, `semantic-light.tokens.json`) only on the 2 px border and the dot — an outline dot for DEMO, a filled dot and the bold weight for LIVE — so the word, the glyph and the weight carry it in greyscale and under colour-vision deficiency. Evidence: E30-D01's badge contrast matrix and legibility boards (`docs/design/research/e30-order-entry-under-time-pressure.md`, `docs/design/E30/img/E30-D01-02-legibility-matrix.png` with its `-greyscale` and `-deuteranopia` renders); the same anatomy as E11-D02's status-row badge; the final `live` treatment is routed to E30-D04. An owner session that spans both environments shows both badges (E39-D01's owner boards, rev. 4 · B; D-3), a session whose accounts sit in one environment shows that badge alone (f31; E39-D01's `SCR-071 / manager`), and every SCR-010 frame (f1–f5, f31) draws a DEMO-only and a LIVE-only TopBar side by side. The badge is never covered by a scrim or a modal (SCR-075 checklist): the modal frames scrim only below the TopBar.

### States (drawn for the owner unless named)

- **normal** — f31 for the manager; the owner's are the kept 2026-09-26 frames (Deviations), and the five state frames show the full normal anatomy around each state's change.
- **disconnected** (f1) — E17-D01 §5 "Disconnected": the SCR-152 banner in the banner slot ("Disconnected — reconnecting (3)", what is off, what still works, Retry now, Details); the StatusBar chip "Reconnecting (3) · last data 00:12" with no latency number; a scrim over every public panel with its last-good age; order entry off with its reason; cancel and close, the positions grid and the Safety cluster never covered (E17-D01 D8; FREEZE… confirms with the data age, E39-D01 D11). The kill-switch line reads "Kill switch: status unknown · last clear 00:12 ago": while the `system` stream is stale the state is unknown with its age, never "clear" (E17-D01 D11), and FREEZE… stays enabled because engaging is allowed while REST is reachable (E17-D01 §6); the HealthChips keep their last values, aged "as of 00:12 ago".
- **degraded** (f2) — backend healthy, exchange feed down = E17-D01's Stale with the upstream reason: the SCR-153 banner "Exchange feed down — the server is restoring the connection." with what still works, the chip "Stale — exchange feed down", public panels dimmed with their age; no scrim, no attempt counter; market and one-click orders on a symbol stop once its price is 5 s old, every new order at 15 s (E17-D01 §6). The HealthChips name the backend cause: "Exchange WS: error · public feed down" and "Ingest lag: warn · no data 7 s" (CMP-210).
- **frozen** (f3) — E39-D01 §7.1 and §8.1: the FROZEN banner (two bars and the word), the kill-switch line "engaged · 1 account (sub_001)", sub_001's ticket disabled with its reason, positions listed with their native stops (FREEZE "does not close positions unless you tick 'also flatten'", E39-D01; this engagement did not tick it), and Release… for the owner behind step-up.
- **lockout** (f4) — E39-D01 §7.2 and §8.2: a per-account B20 lockout shown by the CMP-117 lockout panel (reason, since, until, what still works, "I've read this", the owner's Override lockout… behind step-up); sub_001's ticket disabled with the chip text plus "closing and reducing still work", Close position (reduce-only) enabled; other accounts unchanged.
- **read-only (viewer)** (f5) — E09-D09's T2 treatment: the panel notice "Read-only — your role can't place orders." on the ticket, the Safety cluster's buttons with the lock glyph, the cluster's first line giving their reason ("Safety · your role can't use these") and each button's own reason echoed by tooltip on hover and focus ("Your role can't use the kill switch."), New workspace with "Your role can't save workspaces." (a Viewer holds `workspaces:read` only); never hidden, never dimmed with opacity. The StatusBar's Kill switch… is T2 as well, its reason beside it; the account scope reads "All (3)", the accounts the panels show.

### Role variants (structural, not a toggle)

- **Owner**: Admin entry present; every control enabled (f1–f4).
- **Manager** (f31): Admin entry absent from the tree; the account scope and the Safety cluster's reach follow the grants — one LIVE account in scope, FLATTEN ALL… and CANCEL ALL… acting on it, FREEZE… as "Freeze my account…" (E09-D09 board 06; E39-D01 D-1).
- **Viewer** (f5): Admin entry absent; every mutating control in T2 with its reason (E09-D09 board 02); read-only by role, so f5 is also the Viewer's normal shell.
- **HealthChips by role** (f5, f31): a Manager's and a Viewer's connection gets the role-scoped system view (23 §15.9, SR-188), so their chips show states only, no component detail values (no ingest lag, symbol count or rate-budget percentage).

## SCR-011 Nav rail & workspace switcher

- **Collapsed** (f6): 64 px, icons only; each item's tooltip opens on hover **and** keyboard focus and names the item, its count and its key ("Alerts · 2 new · Alt+5"); the active item has the selected fill, a bar, the weight and `aria-current` — never colour alone.

- **Empty** (f9): the SCR-151 pattern — "No workspaces yet", one sentence, Create a workspace, a learn-more link; a Viewer sees the action's reason instead.

## SCR-012 Command palette

- **Empty query** (f10): Recent (the last five invocations, newest first) and Suggested (role-aware, most used first).
- **Results** (f11): groups Navigate · Panel · Symbol · Action · Setting in that fixed order, a group with no match shows no header; destructive commands carry the ⚠ glyph and a "confirms" tag and open their confirmation, never firing from the palette (14 SCR-012).
- **No results** (f12): "No command or symbol matches “xyz”" with a suggestion (search the exchange's instruments); no group headers; the same answer for every query.
- **RBAC-disabled** (f13): rows stay in their group in E09-D09's T2 treatment — lock glyph, full label, the reason as the second line ("Your role can't change positions.") — replacing the 2026-09-26 `opacity.disabled` and "Owner only"; the SETTING row (Trading › Flatten confirmation) stays enabled, because a Viewer may open Settings (12 §2: R-200 is O, M, V) and only `/admin/*` entries are left out (14 §0.5 rule 5); no `admin.*` command exists for a session without `admin:read`.

- **Single** (f16): bottom-right above the StatusBar; glyph, severity word and text; a thin auto-dismiss bar, paused on hover and focus.

## System states

- **SCR-153 degraded banner** (f22): full width under the TopBar, non-blocking; one banner per cause (exchange feed down, database unavailable, recorder down, cold tier unavailable, rule engine down, GPU budget exceeded), each with what still works, what does not and a "Trading:" line; shed features named with Reduce quality further and Restore full quality; the restored state. Rate-limited is SCR-158's.
- **SCR-157 version mismatch**: the non-blocking banner per shell (f23: Restart and update in the desktop app, Reload in a browser, Later, What's new, the open positions named) and the blocking dialog (f24) listing what stops and what still works, with Continue read-only: "an incompatible backend blocks trading surfaces only" (14 SCR-157), replacing the 2026-09-26 "all shell interaction suspended". In the blocking variant the Safety cluster and the StatusBar's Kill switch… are T2 with the reason "unavailable until you update" beside them, and the kill-switch line reads "unknown — incompatible version": E17-D01 §6 (Read-only: protocol incompatible) blocks cancel, reduce-only, close and flatten and the in-app kill switch, whose fallback is the server console command `candleviewer kill`.
- **SCR-159 offline / tailnet-unreachable / backend-unreachable / GPU-unavailable** (f25–f28): one page template, four variants distinguished by icon, heading and retry wording; cached read-only content with its age where it exists; automatic recovery, announced. Offline, tailnet-unreachable and GPU-unavailable (f25, f26, f28) draw it in the canvas with the chrome and both ENV badges kept; in f25 and f26 the drawer lists only the events from before the connection was lost. Backend-unreachable (f27) is R-902 /offline, the full-screen takeover of 12 §2: the wordmark, the page and CMP-207 BuildFooter with the client's version and commit. Nothing has come from the server, so there is no TopBar, NavRail, RightRail or StatusBar and no user, role, Admin entry, count or notification; no ENV badge, because the environment is not shown until it is known (14 SCR-150 checklist); and no Safety cluster, because the in-app kill switch cannot reach the server (its fallback is the console command `candleviewer kill`, E17-D01 §6). GPU-unavailable names the software-rendering fallback and its limits (E11-D04 D13's degraded-2d: candles, lines, axes, crosshair and drawings stay; footprint cells show colour only; the heatmap is off); the 2026-09-26 claim that it also disables rail shortcuts had no source and is dropped.
- **403 (SCR-154, CMP-089)** and **404 (SCR-155, CMP-090)**: E09-D09 boards `E09-D09-12-forbidden-and-read-only` and `E09-D09-08-admin-404-dark` (ruling 3).
- **Error boundary (SCR-156, CMP-091)**: panel level (f29) — only the failed panel shows the fallback (Reload panel, Remove panel, Copy diagnostics, the error id, technical detail), the surviving workspace keeps running, and a panel that crashed three times in two minutes stops reloading itself and says why; route level (f30) — the whole canvas, shell chrome intact, Reload page, Reload app, Start in safe mode.

## Data / interactions / hotkeys

- Command palette: `Ctrl+K`; arrow keys move; `Enter` runs; `Ctrl+Enter` runs for every account in the active group, through the same confirmation; `Esc` closes.
- Hotkey cheatsheet: `Ctrl+/` (shipped keymap). `Ctrl+Shift+K` (opens the FREEZE confirmation) joins it once E39-D03 registers it (E39-D01 OD-16).
- Nav rail: `Alt+1..9`; collapse is persisted per user in the Zustand layout slice (no server round-trip).
- Toast host: one app-level queue, bounded; every toast is also written to SCR-014.
- Live values in the shell (StatusBar, HealthChips, the kill-switch line, rail counts) update at ≤ 1 Hz (Performance notes).

## Accessibility notes

- Focus order: SkipLink (CMP-085, the first stop, drawn focused on f2 on the empty left of the TopBar's HealthChips row, so it covers neither the ENV badge nor a NavRail label) → TopBar → NavRail → banner slot → WorkspaceCanvas → RightRail → StatusBar.
- Landmarks: `banner` (TopBar), `navigation` "Primary" (NavRail), `main` (WorkspaceCanvas), `complementary` (RightRail), a labelled `role="status"` region (StatusBar, CMP-208).
- Every chip and badge states its value in words ("Trading environment: Live"; "Connection: reconnecting, attempt 3, last data 12 seconds ago"; "Exchange WS: error, public feed down", CMP-210); disabled controls keep their full label and give their reason (E09-D09 T2).
- 125 % text scale (US-SET-007): the TopBar moves the workspace name behind an overflow menu before truncating; the NavRail grows by the scale factor up to a cap, then labels ellipsise with a tooltip; drawn for the cheatsheet only (Deviations).

## Security notes

- The ENV badge cannot be hidden by any theme, density or layout option: it is a structural element of the TopBar and the StatusBar in every shell frame (the context frames that draw less are listed under Deviations), repeated on every trading panel and window, and never under a scrim or modal. Before the environment is known, R-902's takeover (f27) shows no badge rather than a default one (14 SCR-150 checklist).
- The Safety cluster never fires on a single click: FREEZE… opens one confirmation (`confirmMode: "single"`, ruling 1); FLATTEN ALL… and CANCEL ALL… confirm as E39-D03 (#879) specifies.

## Performance notes

- The StatusBar and the TopBar's HealthChips row (dashed outlines on the shell frames), the kill-switch line and the rail counts are **1 Hz-updating regions**; they never re-render the WorkspaceCanvas subtree (the dashed isolation boundary), per the ticket's Performance notes.
