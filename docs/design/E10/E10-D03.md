### 1.2 The badge contract (normative; once)

### 1.4 What the shell touches (normative; the safety framing)

The shell informs; the server enforces (C-2.12, C-12.4, C-2.21; `14` §0.5 item 5 :85; `12` §4 :188; E10-X02 F7 :24; E09-D09 §14.3): every T2 drawn here is the UX mirror of a synchronous server check, and `/me` decides what renders, never what is allowed (D02 :91).
