### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Compositor only.** Only `opacity` and `transform` animate; nothing animates `width`, `height`, `top`, `left`, `box-shadow` or a `filter` (T AC1; E09-D08:213–215).
- **R-2 One layout per transition** (PROPOSED, §5): the WorkspaceCanvas takes its end geometry in exactly one frame and never lays out during a transition's frames.
- **R-3 Instant under reduce.** Every reduce twin is the instant end state with its carrier; a twin that only shortens a duration is a defect (T AC2).
- **R-4 Safety never waits.** A status change is applied in the frame it is known; a transition follows it, never precedes or delays it (E42-D04:576–580).
- **R-5 Focus never fades.** A focused control is never inside a fading element; focus never animates; nothing delays focus or an announcement (E09-D08:76, :216).
- **R-6 No motion oracle.** No duration, easing or transition differs by role, permission, environment or cause (E09-D08:83).
- **R-7 Nothing loops or flashes.** No chrome transition loops; the one loop is SCR-150's request-bound spinner (T-28), which E42-D04:581–582 allows past 5<!--=CITE:05.pause-s--> s; nothing flashes; nothing else moves on its own for more than 5<!--=CITE:05.pause-s--> s (`05`:207–208).
- **R-8 One source.** The resolved `data-motion` on the root is the only mode a component reads (`16`:138); no component reads the media query and no component adds its own reduce rule (E42-D04:584–585).

## 5. One layout per transition beside the canvas (BQ-6, PROPOSED)

### 5.1 The rule

PROPOSED (no merged text states it for the rail; the ruling confirms it as PROPOSED). The WorkspaceCanvas takes its end geometry in exactly one frame: at **frame 0** when a chrome region grows (rail expand, drawer open, banner in) and at the **last frame** when it shrinks (rail collapse, drawer close, a banner cleared). In between only the chrome element moves, by `transform` and `opacity`, inside the space it owns at that moment. The engine receives one `resize`, debounced one frame (`26`:333); the GL context is kept, nothing remounts and panels keep their state (`14`:519). Under reduce the motion and the layout both happen at frame 0. This generalises `15`:499 ("a docked drawer that reflows its host arrives in one frame"), E13-D04:328 (the dock "one LAYOUT frame, not animated") and E10-S01's grid-column rule (BL:239). E10-Q04's "no layout of the workspace subtree" (T Test) is read as **no layout during the transition's frames** (C-02).

## Part B — Reduced motion

## 9. The single source and the token-level switch

`data-motion` = `safe | reduce` on the root (`16`:138), written before paint (PRE:23) and kept by STORE:12–27; the override `system | on | off` wins over the OS in both directions (E09-D08:89; US-SET-007, `11`:5175–5185). No component reads the media query (R-8). The token-level switch is the shipped one: `--cv-motion-scale` 1 / 0 (CSS:43–53) multiplies every recipe duration once #3174 lands, and today CSS:55–80 already sets every transition and animation to `0.01ms` under reduce, so one provider switch satisfies the whole shell (T Tech). Builders add no per-component `transition: none` and never remount a layer on the flip (E42-D04:584–585). **Mid-flight:** a running transition completes on its own clock (at most 200<!--=TOK:duration.normal--> ms) and the next one obeys the new mode (E09-D08:178). `Ctrl+Shift+M` (`05`:78) is not in KM: G-03 routes it to E10-S02 (#305).

## 10. The substitution table

The register's Reduce and Carrier columns (§4) are the substitution table: every row's twin is its instant end state, and its carrier is the word, the glyph, the focus, the `aria-expanded` value or the announcement that tells a reduce user the state (`16`:103, :204). A reduce user misses a transition, never a state. Where `05`:206 allows "a single low-amplitude cross-fade", this spec takes T AC2's instant (C-10).
