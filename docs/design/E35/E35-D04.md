### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity.** **Blocking:** a clause or a WCAG 2.2 A / AA criterion non-compliant with no compliant path in the merged designs, a status carried by colour alone, or an announcement with no string or politeness. **Major:** an at-risk clause, a key collision without a declared shadow, a T2 over- or under-conversion, a contrast pair below its bar. **Minor:** wording, cadence or order, or an at-risk clause whose interim path the merged designs already give. **Info:** recorded for a consumer's charter (E11-D07:62; E13-D05:89). `05`:238's P0/P1 defect bar is E35-Q06's and is not used for design findings.
- **R-2 Findings and their closing (RULED BQ-7, confirmed).** An F row is a defect in a merged design against `05` or WCAG 2.2; it names the spec and its area, never an agent, plus the sentence, the replacement, one owner and one target (E35-D05 #940, or the consuming story E35-S08 #1048 / E35-S09 #1049; a contrast row below its bar targets E05). A finding closes when it is routed (owner and target named, the consumer's acknowledgement at merge) or accepted with a written rationale, both under the owner's `approved`; "fixed in the designs" is read as "fixed in the pack E35-D05 lifts" (C-18).
- **R-3 Verdicts.** `compliant` · `at risk` · `non-compliant` · `out of scope — <epic>` · `not yet decidable — routed to E35-Q06`. A `non-compliant` clause carries a Blocking F row; an `at risk` clause carries a Major F row or names its interim path.
- **R-4 T2.** D02 §1 and D03 §1 state the one T2 rule (D02:138; D03:134); every gated control here is read against it, and an over- or under-conversion is Major.
- **R-5 Never colour alone.** Every colour-carrying signal has a word, glyph, sign or `aria-*` state recorded in §9 (T AC2; `05`:107).
- **R-6 Motion rules.** `15`'s CMP-043 and CMP-046 lines bind (`05`:209); E10-D04's R-1..R-8, stated for the shell chrome (E10-D04:83–90), are applied here as PROPOSED, the product's only merged motion rules (RULED BQ-27).
