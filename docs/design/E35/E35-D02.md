**The one T2 rule (stated once; every later section with a gated control cites "§1").** A control the session may not use because of a role, a grant, an environment, the view-as context or a registered state is E09-D09's **T2** (§2.2): it keeps its native role and its **full label**; `aria-disabled="true"`, never the `disabled` attribute, so it stays in the Tab order and an arrow still lands on it; a CMP-019 `lock` glyph (`decorative=false`, name "Not available") leads the label; the reason sentence sits under or beside it (2a inline; the 2b tooltip echo only for icon-only controls), is in the DOM at mount and is linked by `aria-describedby`; role legs wrap in CMP-079 RbacGate. Visually: the fill goes to `color.surface.raised` with a `color.border.default` hairline, the label and the lock in `color.text.tertiary`, the reason in `color.text.secondary` — never `color.text.disabled`, never `opacity.disabled` on the label, glyph or reason (§10 measures 5.62 / 5.92 / 11.21 and 8.81 / 8.98 / 14.35). Keys (§9.3): `Enter`, `Space`, a bound hotkey or an arrow onto a T2 radio change nothing, stage nothing and re-show the reason inline with one polite announcement; a T2 control emits no audit and no analytics. Reasons are `rbac.*` keys (E09-D09 §3.1 with the §3.2 verb) or registered state keys (§8's table — the three `rule.*` keys E36-D02 registered and this spec inherits), never data, ≤ 60 characters. **The view-as leg comes first** on every write (`rbac.view_as` "Read-only while viewing as {user}."), one notice per frame, every write's `aria-describedby` → it. **Not T2:** a control disabled by **form validity** takes E30-D02 D3's look — its own fill and label at `opacity.disabled` (0.45), the reason as full-opacity `color.text.secondary` text under it, `aria-disabled`, focusable, a press shows the error summary; no lock, no CMP-079, no key; board layer `validity-reason`. **Loading and status** are E09-D09 §9.1's `aria-busy` states; **a completed state is an outcome**, not a gate; **a deferred capability is an absence** (T1, or hidden-by-plan with a note), never a lock. Over-conversion is as wrong as under-conversion (either is a Major in the lens); a control is role-, grant-, environment- or session-gated or it is not a T2. Layer names on the boards: `t2-reason` / `validity-reason`.

**SCR-085 (the dialog).** Initial focus **Cancel**; trapped; `Tab` order follows the footer's visual order and then the body from the top: Cancel → Arm on DEMO / Arm on LIVE… (the last footer stop; a T2 Arm stays in the order) → wraps to the body's first stop — "Change in the editor" (until D02-G5) or the radiogroup (after D02-G5: one roving stop on the checked radio; `↑` / `↓` move between DEMO and LIVE; an arrow onto a T2 LIVE lands on it, re-shows the reason once, politely, and changes nothing) → "show all" (when present) → the account rows' include boxes → "Open the simulation" → the CMP-227 chip → "How it is estimated" → "Choose which goes first" (CMP-159) → the flatten-all box / the override reason field (when present) → back to Cancel; `Shift+Tab` from Cancel lands on the last body stop, never on Arm (WCAG 2.4.3). **`Enter`** on a radio (after D02-G5) **never arms** (it selects); on "Change in the editor" it opens the editor and arms nothing; on the override reason field nothing (a textarea); on a button its action; **`Space`** toggles a box or a radio. **`Esc`**: before the arm — closes, nothing armed, focus returns to the Arm button in SCR-081 (E36-D04 §4.4 line 240) or the row menu's button on SCR-080; during pending — closes, the arm continues server-side and resolves in SCR-080's row (memo §9d lines 450–451); after the outcome — closes as Done; in `disarm-confirm` — cancels the disarm. **200 % and 1280 × 800 (`05` §5 item 7; `16` §3 rule 4).** The dialog fills the viewport (640 × 400 CSS px at 200 %): the title row stays, the band stays above the scrim, blocks 3–6 collapse into disclosures (open by default; "Will act on" and "Evidence" first), the footer stays one sticky 48 px row, the body scrolls; nothing clips.
