### 1.1 What this review fixes

The accessibility contract of the layouts and workspaces band: the one keyboard model with a scope and a conflict status per binding (§3), the focus destination per layout mutation (§4), the region map and the naming rule later epics follow (§5), the announcement script and its budget (§6, §7), the T2 table over every gated control (§8), the R1–R7 matrix (§9), the clause verdicts, the WCAG 2.2 cross-walk and the contrast and carrier audits (§10), the findings register (§11), the rows (§12), the script E15-Q06 verifies against the build (§13) and the written walkthroughs (§14). ### 1.2 Method (honest)

A desk review, clause by clause, of E15-D02 through E15-D09 as merged against `05` §3–§7 and §10, with `14` SCR-011 / 020..029 / 049 / 150 / 153, `15`'s contracts, `11` US-LAY-001..008 and US-SET-002, E09-D09's T2 grammar and `DS` G-T2 and R1–R7 as the binding neighbours (the E13-D05, E29-D05 and E30-D05 method). Every verdict carries `KEY`:line evidence and, where a merged board draws the case, a board number. The tool imports, never copies (R316 BQ-10): E11-D07's key-map engine (`nrm`, `expand_keys`, `load_keymap`, `shipped_bindings`, `load_reserved`, `coactive`, `parse_05_hotkeys`), the E15-D06 audit's `hx`, `ratio`, `grey`, `de`, `sim`, `THEMES` and `BAR` and the E15-D05 and E15-D06 audits' `PAIRS` (their contrast lists), through which `contrast_check.py`'s ratio and `docs/research/_tools/cvd_simulate.py`'s projection are reached. No screen reader was run and no participant was seen; every evaluative sentence that rests on judgement is marked `(heuristic)`; the AT names appear only in the routing sentence of §14 (R316 BQ-8).
