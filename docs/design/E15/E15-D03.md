### 1.4 The T2 rule (stated once; every gated row cites "§1.4")

Per E09-D09 §2.2 and A1: the control keeps its native role and full label; `aria-disabled="true"`, never `disabled`; the CMP-019 `lock` glyph (`decorative=false`, named "Not available") **leads** the label; the label in `color.text.tertiary`, the reason in `color.text.secondary`, never `color.text.disabled` or `opacity.disabled` (`D02`:644); the reason is a registered key's sentence (≤ 60 characters, one sentence, a capability, never data) in a node present at mount and linked by `aria-describedby`; legs checked in the server's order — **view-as first** (`rbac.view_as`), then permission (`rbac.permission`), then account scope (`rbac.account`), then state keys; form **2a** inline beside the control, **2b** (the reason visually hidden and echoed by CMP-016 on hover and focus) only for an icon-only control or a tab-strip segment with no room; while viewing as, SCR-124's banner is **the one notice** and every gated control points at it instead of repeating it; `Enter`, `Space` or a chord on a T2 control changes nothing, sends nothing and re-shows the reason once, politely; a T2 control emits no analytics. **Not T2:** an absence (nothing to save, nothing to revert to, a workspace never stored) is an availability state — `aria-disabled`, its reason as the item's second line or beside the control, **no lock glyph, no CMP-079, no key**; a validity failure (an empty or clashing name) is an inline field error, **never a lock**.

### 1.5 Exits are never gated (A3)

On every frame of this spec, for every role, while viewing as, under the kill switch, a lockout or any state: **Close** (panel and tab), **Move** (header handle, Move panel, Move to ▸), **Discard** (SCR-029's Discard changes, Revert to saved…, a never-stored copy's Discard restored layout…, the restore notice's Dismiss) and **`Esc`** stay T1 and are never drawn with a lock or a reason. Rearranging is client state and T1 for every session (`D02`:640). An exit may be unavailable only by absence (Revert on a never-stored layout), shown by §1.4's availability form, and then another exit stands in for it on the same frame (Discard restored layout…, D-8): no frame is left without a way to drop the working copy.

**The overlap rule (PROPOSED; handed to E15-D04, PENDING).** The band reaches 10 px<!--=BUDGET:h.hitband-reach--> past each side of the gutter: through the neighbour's frame reservation and 8 px<!--=BUDGET:h.hitband-into-content--> into its content. Beside a vertical splitter it covers the header's side inset, which holds no control (`space.3` is wider than the reach, §4.2.1; tool-gated), and the body's edge. Below a horizontal splitter it is the top of the next panel's header row; above it, the bottom of the previous panel's body (a chart's time strip, DOM ladder rows, tape rows). In the overlap: (1) hover, the cursor and a neighbouring chart's crosshair pass through (`E11-D02`:355); the resize cursor shows only over the gutter and the frame reservation. (2) A press on a control (the header handle, a picker, the sync badge, an IconButton) is the control's and never starts a resize; across a control the splitter's press area is the gutter plus both frame reservations, 8 px<!--=BUDGET:h.hitband-control-yield-->, and the full band elsewhere along its length (heuristic: WCAG 2.5.8 is met by the band's length, not by its depth across a control). (3) A press anywhere else in the overlap is held: if it moves `space.1` or more across the splitter line (its resize direction) with the button down, it is a resize and the content beneath receives `pointercancel` and no click; if it is released first, the content beneath receives it as a plain click. (4) **On an armed DOM**, a press in the overlap on a ladder row therefore never trades on press: released without moving, it reaches the row as a click and the DOM's own one-click rules, confirm and preconditions apply unchanged; moved, it resizes and places nothing. A drag that starts on a ladder row (if the DOM's host spec defines one) is not available in the overlap. Whether the DOM's one-click fires on press or on click, and the drag threshold as a dock default, are E15-D04's and the DOM host spec's to confirm (§19.3 PENDING row); until then this rule binds. ### 12.2 Focus order and rules

Inside a panel, `Tab` follows the handle, the heading's pickers, the sync badge, the toolbar (one stop, roving), ⋮, ×, then the body, then the splitter that follows the panel. Focus is never moved by a sync change, a restore, the restore notice or a background event (`D02`:612). Panel focus (the active panel) and keyboard focus are distinct: clicking anywhere in a panel or arriving by `Ctrl+Alt+←/→` makes it the active panel; the active panel keeps its frame fill and bar while keyboard focus moves inside it; controls carry CMP-039's ring.

### 12.3 Regions, names and live regions

Each panel is `role="region"` named per D-5; the tab strip is a `tablist` named "Workspaces"; the toolbar a `toolbar` named "Layout"; the minimised tray a `toolbar` named "Minimised panels". One polite live region (CMP-081's, pre-rendered empty) carries layout changes, restores, focus arrivals and T2 reasons; one assertive region carries disarms and the save failure. Live values in headers (an arm countdown, a feed age) are never announced per tick; the visible countdown is CMP-229's (E30's) and is never announced per tick.

## 13. Performance — normative statements

- **N-1** Focus is paint, never layout: the focus affordance never changes a panel's content box, so focusing a panel never resizes a canvas (D-1, §3.4).
- **N-2** Chrome hover and active feedback is CSS only (`:hover`, `:active`, `:focus-visible`) and never triggers a canvas repaint or a React render of the panel body.
- **N-3** A loading skeleton never fetches: a panel subscribes after it mounts, and it mounts on first visibility (D02 D-14).
- **N-4** A panel mounts within `14`:87's and `15`:828's 150 ms; a layout transition within `15`:837's 200 ms; a four-pane restore reaches interactive within `11`:2774's 3 s; four active order-flow panes hold `11`:2754's ≥ 55 fps; the frame budget is C-14.2's < 16 ms p95 (`CONSTITUTION.md`:1176).
- **N-5** An inactive dock tab, an inactive workspace tab or a panel behind a maximised one unsubscribes its visual topics after 30 s (`14`:518; `D02`:387).
- **N-6** A hidden window or browser tab throttles visual topics to ≥ 1 000 ms and never unsubscribes (`23`:1357).
- **N-7** A layout change never remounts a WebGL context (`14`:518; `D02`:707).
- **N-8** The restored chip, the dirty marker and the toolbar's states re-render only the tab strip, never the dock subtree.
- **N-9** The header's status words update at most once per second in a fixed-width slot, so the header never reflows on a feed change.

## 15. Security

- **Environment disclosure (C-2.11):** the environment shows on the workspace chrome (TopBar, StatusBar) and independently on every order-capable panel by kind (D-4), so a maximised or cropped panel cannot hide it; LIVE is never a colour-only cue (four channels, §7.2); the environment is derived from the account, never stored in a layout (D02 D-7); before it is known no badge is shown.
- **Viewer honesty (C-2.12, C-12.4):** hiding or disabling a control is presentation, never authorisation; the API enforces RBAC (E09); the spec quotes `E09-D09`:410 in §7.1 and draws gated controls as T2, never hidden.
- **User-supplied names:** a workspace name renders as a text node, never markup; truncation is for display only; 1–40 characters are enforced at input (`maxlength` and validation) and by the contract's sanitising (`22` `WorkspaceInput.name`); names never enter analytics.
- **No credentials** in layouts, exports, analytics or frames.
