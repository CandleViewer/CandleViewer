### 3.2 The degenerate case (AC3)

Whether such a drop is valid depends on the moving panel, because the layout re-normalises when it leaves (the tool's `small_chooser` verdicts on the 454×121 Tape of the `small` fixture): the **DOM**, the Tape's column sibling, leaves the Tape the whole 640 px column, so "Above Tape" and "Below Tape" are valid 454×318 drops the pointer's geometry could not offer, while "Left of" and "Right of" are refused ("Can't drop here: DOM would be narrower than its 380 px minimum." at 226 px; on either side the Tape's 232 breaks too, and the mover's minimum is named first, D04-D-3); the **Chart** leaves the Tape at 121 px, so its top and bottom halves are 60 px and every side is refused, the Chart's 168 px height minimum named first (the mover's), though the top, bottom and centre drops also break the Tape's 120. With the current minimums the keyboard-only drop exists only when the mover is the target's own sibling; the rule matters more if a minimum is lowered. A pointer drop on a withdrawn side's would-be position lands on the centre (tab with), and the ghost shows that result, so the pointer never does something the overlay did not show. When both axes withdraw (a panel under 144 px on both sides) the centre is the only zone.

### 3.3 What a drop may refuse, size by size (AC4; the tool's drop table)

A refusal is distinct from a withdrawal (D04-D-3): the zone is there, hatched, with its reason. The tool attempts every zone of every other panel for the moving Positions panel in `dense`: at **1280×800** six of fifteen drops are valid (above and below the Chart or the DOM, and tab with either) and nine are refused by a width minimum — a side split of the Chart halves 522 into 260 (Positions' 300 named first, the mover's; the Chart's 328 breaks too), of the DOM into 191 (Positions' 300 first; the DOM's 380 too), and every zone of the 255 px Tape leaves Positions under 300 — so **at 1280×800 the east zone of every dense panel is a refusal**, which is why the AC2 frame (`SCR-021/zone-right`) is drawn at 1920×1080, where eleven of fifteen drops are valid and the Chart's right zone yields Chart 404 and Positions 404 wide with the DOM and the Tape grown to 920 tall. This corrects E15-D02 P2–P6, which lit all five zones of the Chart at 1280×800 without checking the minimums (D04-C-1). The depth refusal needs a tree already at depth 6 counting the root: the tool's deep fixture refuses one more split (depth 7) as `layout.limit.depth` ("the layout can't be split this deeply"), not as a size; the target's share halves (`sizes` 0.5 / 0.5) on a valid edge drop. The tab-group refusal needs a tabset of 16 leaves, the contract's cap (`22` `LayoutGridTabset`), which no fixture of four panels reaches; frame `SCR-021/invalid-tab-group` draws it with a labelled 16-tab group. A move cannot add a panel, so the 16-panel cap never applies to a move (E15-D02 §5.3).

## 7. Maximise, minimise-to-tray and Pop out at dock level (board 07; D-21, D-22 carried; D04-D-11)

## 8. Panel too small — the compact view (board 08; D-16 carried; D04-D-5)

The threshold is the kind's minimum: width under 328 (chart), 380 (DOM), 232 (tape) or 300 px (Positions, E29-D01 line 161), or height under 120 px (168 for a chart) at compact (D04-D-5; comfortable 160 / 224). A panel at its minimum keeps it while the canvas narrows and its siblings give up width (D-16; `16` §8 rule 2); only a window too small for every minimum brings the compact view, which a splitter can never cause (it stops first, §6). Frame `SCR-021/compact-view` draws the DOM in a 1024×800 window (the `dense` row leaves the DOM 298 wide, under its 380 px minimum): a shortened header — the kind, the symbol, the arm state (`LOCKED`) and ⋮, `color.surface.raised` — then "Panel too small — showing compact view" in `color.text.secondary`, one summary line that leads with the env badge and the sync letter ("LIVE ● · A · 64,011.0 / 64,011.5"), and [Maximise] [Move…] (CMP-001 secondary; Move… opens the chooser of §5). A compact order panel takes no orders ("Maximise to trade"), and entering the compact view requests the disarm of an armed panel, every arm state reading `LOCKED` on the server's answer, announced once: "One-click trading disarmed: DOM too small to trade." (OQ-I's default). The user learns why from the line itself and from the arm state's tooltip and accessible name; nothing is hidden. ## 9. Undo and `Ctrl+Z` ownership (board 08; D04-D-12)

A mis-drop is reverted from the toast or from the menu, never from a chord: the toast (CMP-045 in CMP-212, `role="status"`, bottom-right of the workspace per E40-D02 §4.8 D-20, `elevation.popover`, `color.surface.raised`, text `color.text.primary`) reads "Positions moved below Chart." with the underlined text button **Undo** (`SCR-021/undo-toast`); the panel menu shows "Undo move" as its first item while an undo is available (`SCR-021/undo-menu`). One step: the last layout change (a move, a split, a tabify, a resize, a close, a minimise, a preset applied) is restored whole, including a closed panel's settings. The undo stays available until the next explicit Save, Save as or Revert to saved, which clear it (the ticket's "until the next explicit save"; a preset applied is a layout change, so it is undoable, never a clearing event — D04-D-12); a toast timeout does not — the menu keeps the item (the ticket's first security note: a drop never silently overwrites the saved workspace; it marks the working copy dirty, `*`, and only Save writes, §11). `Ctrl+Z` is `chart.undo` (keymap line 266, "Undo drawing") and stays with the focused panel's own undo stack (E14); this spec proposes no layout chord (RULED Q-6), so E15-D02 §7 row 19 already describes the hotkey map as it stands, and the US-SET-002 conflict reflection is a hand-off row to E15-D15 and E15-S01 (§18). Polite: "Move undone." After a drop, `SCR-021/dirty-save` draws the `*` and Save, a T1 write for an ordinary session; its inset `SCR-021/dirty-save-view-as` draws Save under view-as in the T2 form — the CMP-019 lock glyph before the full label, `aria-disabled`, its reason the SCR-124 banner's `rbac.view_as` node, the one notice (§11).

## 11. Gated controls — the one T2 statement (E09-D09 §2.1, §2.2, §3.1, §9.1, §9.3; `14` §0.5 rule 5; CMP-079; CMP-019; E30-D02 D3)

**The rule, once.** A control the session may not use — gated by a role, a grant, an environment, a session state such as view-as, or a registered state — is **T2, disabled with a visible reason**, never hidden (`14` §0.5 rule 5): it keeps its native role and its full label; it is `aria-disabled="true"`, never the `disabled` attribute, so it stays in the Tab order and a menu item stays arrow-reachable; a CMP-019 `lock` glyph (`decorative=false`, named "Not available") leads the label; its reason sits under or beside it (2a inline), in the DOM at mount and linked by `aria-describedby`; role legs are wrapped in CMP-079 RbacGate; the label is `color.text.tertiary`, the reason `color.text.secondary`, the fill `color.surface.raised` with a `color.border.default` hairline — never `color.text.disabled`, never `opacity.disabled`; `Enter`, `Space` or a bound hotkey change nothing and re-show the reason once, politely (E09-D09 §9.3); the reason is an `rbac.*` key or a registered state key, one sentence of at most 60 characters naming a capability, never data (§3.1); one notice per read-only panel, every gated control pointing at it (§2.2).

**What SCR-021 has.** No direct dock operation is gated: every move, split, tabify, resize, close, minimise, maximise, Pop out, Return and undo changes the client's working copy and is **T1 for every session, view-as included** (E15-D02 §8.1 line 640, "Client state is T1"). **The one T2 control on SCR-021 itself** is a "Move to ▸" outcome that would break a limit (E15-D02 §8.1's carried row "Move to ▸ outcomes and the picker's Place options that break a limit"): a state the client knows before the item is activated (E09-D09 §2.1 step 4), so the item keeps its role and full label inside CMP-041, is `aria-disabled` and arrow-reachable, carries the CMP-019 `lock` before the label and its state key's sentence as the second line (2a) — `layout.limit.min_size` "A panel would be smaller than its minimum size.", `layout.limit.depth` "The layout can't be split this deeply.", `layout.limit.tab_group` "This tab group can't hold another panel." (E15-D02 §8.1's PROPOSED keys, E15 the vocabulary owner, A-24) — and `Enter` changes nothing and re-shows the sentence once, politely (frame `SCR-021/move-to-submenu`: "Left of" and "Right of" the Chart in `dense` at 1280×800). It is a state leg, not a role leg, so CMP-079 does not wrap it (E15-D02 §8.1 "a state-gated one takes its atom's `disabledReason`"). The only write a frame here points at is **Save** after a drop made the layout dirty (`SCR-021/dirty-save`); its legs are E15-D02 §8.1's, cited, not re-decided: **view-as first** — `rbac.view_as` "Read-only while viewing as {user}." while `GET /auth/session/view-as` reports a context — then `rbac.permission` "Your role can't save workspaces." for a session without `workspaces:write`; then a state the client knows. Per A1, every gated dock write takes this form: the inset `SCR-021/dirty-save-view-as` draws Save in E09-D09's T2 anatomy under the SCR-124 view-as banner, the **one notice** whose `rbac.view_as` node every save pointer references (E15-D02 A-26), with `Ctrl+S` sending nothing and re-showing the reason (AN-11); a Viewer's dirty layout shows the same Save T2 with `rbac.permission`; a locked workspace, once a lock exists (E15-D01 OQ-14, not in R1), would take a registered state key, never a screen-written string. Live validation and status indicators keep showing while viewing as; only the write is gated.

**Not T2, each an outcome at the point of action** (E09-D09 §9.1; E15-D02 §8.1 "Not T2", line 645): the hatched invalid zone and its reason (D-18), the chooser's hatched side and the bar's second line (K4), "Minimum size." on a splitter at its stop, "No panel further that way." in move mode — never a lock glyph, never CMP-079, never `aria-disabled` on the zone or the splitter; they are feedback announced once, politely. The difference from the Move to ▸ item above is timing, not severity: the menu item is known to be refused before it is reached, so it is disabled with its reason (T2); a drag or a chooser step learns the refusal at the point of action, so it is an outcome. **Absence, not a lock:** Pop out in the browser is absent with the footnote (AM-1; US-LAY-005's NFR), never a disabled item. **Completed states are outcomes:** the dropped, undone, maximised, minimised and compact frames carry no gate. **Validity look (E30-D02 D3): not applicable** — SCR-021 has no form. **Live accounts:** no frame here submits an order, so no arm (`X-Arm-Token`) is involved; the compact view withdraws one-click trading (OQ-I) as a request the server answers, drawn as the `LOCKED` arm state, E30-D02's state. No frame on the nine dark boards draws a lock glyph except the `move-to-submenu` frame's refused items and the `dirty-save-view-as` inset; the tool's gate is per frame: the text each of those two frame ids owns on a line of §0 and §3–§9 (its parenthesis, else its sentence) carries the lock, the key and `aria-disabled`, and the text any other frame id owns there, or no frame id, a shared line included, carries no lock, CMP-079 or `aria-disabled`.

## 16. Performance (ticket Performance notes; `14` §0.5 rule 7; E15-D02 §10)

1. **The ghost is CSS-transform-only:** one translucent rectangle (`color.surface.overlay` at `opacity.muted`, its outline) moved and resized with `transform`, never a live panel and never a canvas; a drag costs no chart frame.
2. **The drop commits exactly one layout mutation:** P9 and K5 show a single transition from the pre-drop tree to the post-drop tree, no intermediate reflow; the panels re-parent as persistent per-pane hosts (E15-D02 §8 line 611, §10 item 2), so no WebGL context is remounted and focus survives the node move.
3. **Splitter writes are throttled to one per animation frame** and resize both neighbours without remounting them; a drag that recreated a GL context would stall visibly.
4. **The zone overlay is one layer** above the panels, built once per drag from the target panel's rectangle; hit-testing reads the bands, not the DOM.
5. **Budgets** stay the owners': US-LAY-001's ≥55 fps with four order-flow panes and CMP-081's ≤200 ms layout transition (`14` §0.5 rule 7); the values live in `CONSTITUTION.md` §14.
6. **Transitions that exist**, each with its reduced pair (durations and easing are E15-D14's; token names only, `16` §2.6): ghost appear / leave — `motion.duration.fast` fade → instant; zone lit — instant in both; drop commit — instant in both (one mutation); MoveModeBar enter / leave — `fast` fade → instant; toast — `fast` slide+fade → fade only (`16` §7); tray appear / leave — `fast` → instant; maximise / restore — `fast` fade of the hidden panels → instant; splitter live resize — unanimated in both. No animation is load-bearing (`16` §2.6).
