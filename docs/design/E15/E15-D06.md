### 10.3 The one T2 rule (E09-D09)

E09-D09's grammar is applied once here and cited from the two places that show a gated control (§7.3, §8.5). The decision rule is `14` §0.5 rule 5: a control the session may not use is disabled with a visible reason, never hidden. **A gated control keeps its native role and its full label; it is `aria-disabled="true"`, never the `disabled` attribute, so it stays in the Tab order and a menu item stays arrow-reachable (E09-D09 §9.1); its reason node is in the DOM at mount and linked by `aria-describedby`, rendered inline (2a) as the item's second line or the line under the control (E09-D09 §2.2); the CMP-019 `lock` glyph (`decorative=false`, "Not available") leads the label; role legs are wrapped in CMP-079 RbacGate and a state gate takes the atom's `disabledReason` (CMP-041, CMP-001; E09-D09 §8). Hi-fi: the label in `color.text.tertiary`, the reason in `color.text.secondary`, a primary action's fill `color.surface.raised` with a `color.border.default` hairline; never `color.text.disabled`, never `opacity.disabled` on the label, glyph or reason (E09-D09 §2.2, §11). Keys (E09-D09 §9.3): `Enter`, `Space`, a bound hotkey or an arrow onto it change nothing and re-show the reason inline with one polite announcement. Reasons are E09-D09 §3.1's `rbac.*` keys or a registered state key, one sentence of at most 60 characters naming a capability, never data; a new state string is a PROPOSED key with its vocabulary owner named.** On this surface the rule reaches exactly three frames: `SCR-043/link-no-group` and its light twin `SCR-043/light-link-no-group` (a state gate, `scale.link.no_group`, PROPOSED with E11-D03 as owner — E15-D02 §8.1's registered row quoted in §7.3) and `SCR-023/dirty-save-view-as` (E15-D02 §8.1's Save row under the view-as leg, the one notice being the banner's node). Everything else that looks withheld is **not T2**: the account row is a deferred capability (an absence); the conflict notice is a status; the refusal, the DEMO / LIVE account refusal, the disarm, a completed join or leave are outcomes; "No sync" is content. Over-converting any of them to a lock would be a defect. **No validity-disabled control exists** on this surface (E30-D02 D3's look applies nowhere: every control here has a value, and "Use percentage scale" is never invalid), so the `validity-reason` layer never appears on a board. Exits are never gated: leaving a group, unlinking a scale, × and `Esc` stay T1 under view-as, the kill switch, a lockout and every sync state.

## 15. Performance (the ticket's notes; US-LAY-003 NFR `11` :2794; `14` SCR-023 :589)
