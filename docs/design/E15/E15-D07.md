### 1.4 The T2 rule (stated once; every gated row cites "§1.4")

Per E09-D09 §2.2 and RULED A1: the control keeps its native role (a menu item stays `menuitem`, a picker entry stays `option`) and its full label; `aria-disabled="true"`, never `disabled`; the CMP-019 `lock` glyph (`decorative=false`, named "Not available") **leads** the label; the label in `color.text.tertiary`, the reason in `color.text.secondary`, never `color.text.disabled` or `opacity.disabled` (`D02`:644); the reason node is in the DOM at mount; legs are checked in the server's order — **view-as first** (`rbac.view_as`), then permission (`rbac.permission` / `rbac.owner_only`), then a known state; while viewing as, the SCR-124 banner's `rbac.view_as` node is the one notice and every gated control points at it (one notice, many pointers; `D02`:642). `Enter` or `Space` changes nothing, re-shows the reason and announces it once, politely.

**Condition 1 (RULED).** T2 is drawn **only for a key present in merged E15-D02 §8.1**: `rbac.view_as`, `rbac.permission` (`D02`:639, :661), `layout.limit.panels`, `layout.limit.min_size`, `layout.limit.depth`, `layout.limit.tab_group` (`D02`:659–660, :669–673), and `recording.symbol_not_recorded`, which §8.1 carries at `D02`:662 and :677 as "This panel needs a recorded symbol." — honestly a **PROPOSED** state key there, registered by E09-D09 only once E16 accepts (A-24). `rbac.owner_only` "Only the owner can {verb}." is a registered E09-D09 key (`E09-D09`:90) that the ruling lists as lawful; `D02`:494 records that no R1 entry needs it, so it appears here only on the illustrative entry (D-6). `rbac.owner_only` is not in merged E15-D02 §8.1; it is lawful here only because the ruling's condition 1 enumerates it, and it is registered at `E09-D09`:90. Any other key — `flag.panel_off`, `layout.panel.no_settings`, `layout.panel.at_defaults` — takes §1.6's sentence form and is never drawn with a lock; the tool fails a register row that pairs a lock with a key outside this list (§6.3 M-2).

**In the picker** (AC1) the reason is also joined into the option's accessible name, not only its description (D-5).

### 1.5 Exits are never gated (RULED condition 4)

On every frame of this spec, for every role, while viewing as, and in every state: **Close**, **Move panel**, **Move to ▸** (the item itself; a single outcome inside it may be T2), **Undo move** and **`Esc`** stay T1 and are never drawn with a lock or a reason (D02 §8.1 "client state is T1", `D02`:640; D04 §11). In the picker, **Cancel** and **`Esc`** are never gated.

### 1.6 The state-sentence form (not T2: no lock glyph, no `aria-disabled`, no CMP-079)

An item or entry whose reason is a key outside condition 1, or an availability precondition, keeps its role and full label in `color.text.primary`, with **no lock glyph**; the sentence sits beside it (the picker) or as its second line (the menu) in `color.text.secondary`, linked by `aria-describedby` and, in the picker, joined into the accessible name (D-5). It is **not** `aria-disabled`: RULED condition 1 gives these keys "the sentence beside the item, no lock glyph, no `aria-disabled` lock", read here as no `aria-disabled` at all, so the item stays a plain, focusable `menuitem` / `option` and never carries the lock or CMP-079 (precedent: E15-D03 §8.3's availability rows, merged, and D04 §11 "absence, not a lock"). Activation does nothing: `Enter` (in the picker, also Add panel) re-announces the sentence once, politely; the sentence in the accessible name and the description tells a screen-reader user why. This keeps the sentence form distinct from T2 for every user, not only by the lock glyph (gate G-SENTENCE-ARIA reads §8.2 row by row). Each new key is a PROPOSED A-row to E15-D02 §8.1's owner (A-6, A-7, A-8).

**Absence** is a third form: an item a build can never offer (Pop out in the browser) is absent, with a footnote, never a lock and never a key (D-10).

### 3.2 The growth rule (D-2; written into `14` SCR-024 by A-1, applied in this PR)

1. **Where a new kind lands, decided by its data:** one instrument's price or trade data → **Charts** if it is a price-time plot, otherwise **Order flow**; instrument-wide or derived context (open interest, funding, regime, scanners) → **Market context**; the user's accounts, journal, alerts or lists → **Account**; order entry → **Trading**.
2. **Worked placements** for the five screens not offered today, so their epics add an entry and never renegotiate the screen: multi-chart grid → Charts; replay → Charts; speed of tape → Order flow; imbalance tracker → Order flow; scanner → Market context.
3. **Order inside a category:** the R1 order, then new kinds appended in merge order. Search ranking is CMP-213's own and never changes this order outside search.
4. **Categories:** a sixth category needs an amendment to SCR-024; no epic adds one by itself. Search results hide a category with no hits; the category filter and the no-results sentence always name all five.
5. **Size:** the list virtualises above the fit (tool: 21<!--=BUDGET:picker.virtualise-above--> at 1920, 18<!--=BUDGET:picker.virtualise-above.1280--> at 1280) (CMP-213 `LongList`; its 1 000-item test is the ceiling). The fit is derived, not typed: three list heights of compact entries in the dialog in use (heuristic). RULED BQ-5's default text named 50; RULED Q-6 (issuecomment-6395880131) makes the threshold the fit of the dialog in use (DV-3). R1 has 14<!--=COUNT:inventory.offered-->.
6. **What an epic adds:** one inventory row (category by rule 1, description ≤ one line, chips, gating legs and state read, synonyms) and one thumbnail asset; nothing else on SCR-024 changes.

## 7. Role and capability variants (board 05, light twin 10; board 03, light twin 08)

### 7.1 Viewer and view-as (D-13)

The viewer's and the view-as menus are the owner's menu, item for item, every item T1: moving, closing, maximising, minimising, popping out, syncing and editing settings change the working copy, which is client state (`D02`:640; D04 §11). Nothing is hidden and nothing is falsely locked. Each frame carries an inset showing where the gate lands: the workspace's Save, T2 (§1.4) — `rbac.permission` "Your role can't save workspaces." for the viewer; for view-as, the lock with no inline reason and a callout naming the SCR-124 banner's `rbac.view_as` node (one notice, many pointers). A panel that is offered but whose data the session may not read fails safely at mount with SCR-154's treatment and renders no partial data (§15).

Pop out is absent; under the last group, separated by a rule, the footnote reads "Multi-window workspaces need the desktop app." (shipped, `BrowserShellAdapter.ts`:11) and, PROPOSED (C-2, a copy CR to E15-S02), "Maximise (F11) gives a panel the whole workspace." — `type.ui.caption`, `color.text.secondary`, `space.inset.sm` insets, not focusable, referenced by the menu's `aria-describedby` so it is read on open. ### 7.3 A kind with no settings

Settings… and Reset settings… stay in place in the sentence form (§1.6) with "This panel has no settings." (PROPOSED `layout.panel.no_settings`, A-7). No R1 kind lacks settings at the base; the frame uses the Watchlist **as an illustrative case**, labelled so (Q-3).

The owner's and a manager's menus are the fixed set with no T2 item outside the limits; a manager's picker shows the tickets enabled (`orders:write`, `D02`:494) and the owner-only illustrative entry T2.

## 15. Security

- **Presentation only.** The picker and the menu read role, permissions, view-as and flags to decide what to _show_; RBAC is enforced server-side (E09, C-12.4, `14`:85). An offered-but-unauthorised panel fails safely at mount with SCR-154's permission-denied treatment and renders no partial data.
- **No leak in reasons.** Reasons name a capability, never data: "Owner only" is acceptable; naming an account, a user other than the view-as subject, or an admin-only object is not. Owner-only panels are shown; no owner-only data is previewed (thumbnails are static assets).
- **Copy panel link** copies `/w/:workspaceId?focus=:panelId` — workspace and panel identity only, never an account id, key, token or symbol; opening it on another session re-authorises through the normal session and RBAC and never trusts the link. It needs a stable panel id (G-03).
- **Flags.** The picker never calls `GET /api/v1/admin/feature-flags` on open; a non-admin read is G-1.
- **Analytics** carries closed-set values only (§14).
