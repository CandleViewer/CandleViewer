### 2.3 The sweep: four thresholds and the minimum width, computed (RULED BQ-08; C-i)

**Method.** The tool sums, per step, the slot widths of §2.1 from the token values and the glyph advances of the house table — `D03T`:460–466's per-capital em bounds, `EM_SAFE` for every other glyph, `+2 %` at weight 500 and `+5 %` at weight 600, the `EM_HEADROOM` factor — read from E15-D03's tool at the base, never typed here `(heuristic)`; so the figures are **estimates built on measured token values**, which the boards confirm in PR 3 with Penpot's text bounds (G-SWEEP then compares the drawn bar to the table). The horizontal model is the same in both densities — the controls grow in height alone (`size.control.*`), so the two width columns agree by construction and the comfortable column is kept for the stories' sake. **W-0** is the full bar; **W-k** the bar after steps 1 … k; the frame `SCR-027/narrow-step-k` draws the bar at W-k, the narrowest width at which steps 1 … k suffice; **step k fires when the window's inner width drops below W-(k−1)** — step 1 below the full bar, step 2 below the step-1 bar, and so on (the outside dry run, N2: `D02`:526's "narrower, it shortens in this order" read against the W-k definition; a market panel's steps 1 … 3 the same, its step 4 never); **the minimum window width** is the last step's bar (the bar after step 4 for an order panel, after step 3 for a market panel — a market panel has no arm state, so step 4 does not apply, as `D02`:526's `tape-demo` "takes steps 1–3").
