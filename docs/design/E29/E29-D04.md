## 3. The motion spec

This section is the blotter's one motion definition (RULED BQ-9): the rows and cells of SCR-063 and SCR-078 (CMP-114, CMP-115 and CMP-116 on the CMP-049 treegrid) and the SCR-078 drawer. Every host cites a Mo id and restates no recipe; hand-off C.6 carries §3 copy-ready in `16` §7.1's shape for E05-D03 (#3174), the recipe owner. Tokens by name, none added (RULED BQ-12): `motion.duration.{instant,fast,normal}` and `motion.easing.{standard,decelerate,accelerate}` (`PRIM`:450–477). Reduced motion has one source, `data-motion` (§3.6).

### 3.1 Data-row rules

Every §3.2 row this spec specifies runs at most `motion.duration.normal` (200 <!-- audit:motion.duration.normal=200 --> ms), the ticket's cap on a data row (T Scope; D01:125 "E29-D04 caps data-row motion at 200 ms"), and never `motion.duration.slow`; Mo-5, Mo-7, Mo-11 and Mo-13 are their owners' recipes, cited by reference, and none of them is a data row's. Property is `opacity`, a `transform` or none (T Perf): no `height`, `width`, `top` or `box-shadow` animates and no colour tweens on the virtualised list (`14`:1477 "virtualised; handles 500 rows at 60 fps"; E09-D08:214, :215). Nothing moves under the pointer (D01:406): while it rests on the grid, or an editor or a row menu is open, inserts, exits and re-sorts wait behind the "Updates paused" chip, and values still change in place. Rows sit at index positions, so neighbours never animate: an insert, an exit, a re-sort or an expand re-places them at frame 0, and only the joining or leaving row's own opacity changes. Live data never moves focus (`05`:96), a focused row never leaves (D01:408), and a focused control never sits inside a fading element (`docs/design/E05/E05-D02-feedback-overlays.md`:165). No transition runs continuously on a row (`16`:202): the busy glyph of a Close or a Cancel in flight is static (Mo-12), and an exit takes `pointer-events: none` from its first frame (`docs/design/E05/E05-D02-feedback-overlays.md`:169).

### 3.5 The flash: rate limit and the paired delta

Mo-3 is `16` §7.1's value-change emphasis on the blotter's numeric cells (`16`:199; E30-D04:546), which E05-D02 leaves to the consuming cell (`docs/design/E05/E05-D02-data-display.md`:712): an opacity overlay of `color.status.info.subtle` above the cell at peak 0.25 <!-- audit:cited.16.flash-peak-opacity=0.25 -->, decaying over `motion.duration.fast`.
