## 1. Purpose, scope and method

### 1.1 What this spec fixes

SCR-063 is the blotter every trading epic inherits (E30, E31, E32, E34 consume its flatten, reverse and group views, #735 context). This spec turns `14` SCR-063 and the D01 decisions into hi-fi: the five tabs, every column with its contract field, format and alignment, the inline amend with its pending and rejected treatment, the row actions with their confirmations, the toolbar, every catalogued state, the keyboard model (resolving the D01 round-3 carry, D-2), redlines in token names, and a measured contrast table. It also fills the parts SCR-063 does not carry (Purpose, RBAC, Route, Analytics: the entry template at `14` §0.1 asks for them) from their owners: route R-130 `/positions` (`12` §2), RBAC from `10` §7 (permission matrix) and `23` §6.3 (§1.5 below), analytics names from the E29-S01 and E29-S02 bodies in `docs/plan/backlog/E29.json`.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk design under the agent-delivery adaptation (owner decision 2026-09-25): no participants, no sessions, no prototype, so no quote, timing or success rate appears here. Comparisons with TradingView, Bybit's own terminal, Quantower, ATAS and Sierra Chart are general interaction patterns recalled from familiarity, not re-verified against live builds, and are labelled `(heuristic)`. Contrast is measured on the token JSON at this head (§13). Two D01 decisions stay untested hypotheses: the D4 pending treatment and the D6 correction wording (D01 §5: open, needs sessions; §8.1 Probes B and C).

**Where this design is weaker or stronger than the comparables (heuristic).** Weaker: retail terminals commonly let a row's close act in one click once confirmations are switched off, while a Live close here always opens the D01 D7 dialog (one more keystroke, by design, for a money-moving action); coloured P&L digits are the norm in Quantower, ATAS and TradingView, while this spec keeps digits neutral until text-safe tokens exist (D-6, OQ-C), a slower at-a-glance P&L scan; the SL column costs 184 px that Sierra Chart's single-line trade windows spend on more values. Stronger, in the builds I recall (unverified): I have not seen a per-row native-stop state (SL ✓, verifying, NO SL), exits kept enabled with stated reasons through reconciliation and lockouts, or multi-account totals kept apart per Demo and Live.

### 11.2 The carry: why option B (D-2)

The D01 round-3 carry (#735, from PR #3899): with focus and selection apart, "Space on A → arrow to B → `Ctrl+Delete`" cancels A while the focus ring sits on B, with only a toast after the fact. Option A makes selection follow focus on plain arrows (`Ctrl+arrow` moves focus alone); option B confirms whenever the target excludes the focused row. This spec takes **B**:

1. **B holds on every input path.** It catches the plain-arrow case, a `Ctrl+arrow` move, a click after a keyboard selection, a selected row scrolled out of the virtualised window, and a selected row that has closed and lingers (D01 D13 d). A only makes the divergence rarer and leaves `Space` on A, `Ctrl+↓` to B, `Ctrl+Delete` silent.
2. **B keeps the multi-select model the APG recommends.** D01 D9 is the recommended model of the WAI-ARIA APG listbox page (https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/listbox/, read 2026-10-22), "a recommended model that does not require the user to hold a modifier key ... while navigating"; A is its alternative model, where moving focus without `Shift` or `Control` "unselects all selected options except the focused option", so keyboard and screen-reader users hold a modifier to keep a selection.
3. **B adds no key.** A needs `Ctrl+↑/↓` and a toggle key as keymap commands; documenting them on SCR-063's hotkeys line fails `scripts/keymap_lint.py` until `keymap.json` defines them (code, outside this design ticket), and macOS binds Control+arrow to Mission Control and Spaces by default (an external fact, unverified here), so the "focus alone" key may never reach the app on a supported client (`10` §1).

**The rule.** An action's targets are the selected rows when any are selected, otherwise the focused row (D01 D9 target rule). Whenever the targets do not include the focused row, a confirm that lists the targets opens, even for a single cancel that would otherwise go without a question; it adds "The row you are on (ETHUSDT ▲ Buy 1.00 on Sub-2) is not included." A row's own SplitButton and menu act on that row only, never on the selection. Every confirm names its targets (D01 D9).

### 11.3 Focus, selection and target size

Focus: a 2 px `color.focus.ring` outline inset in the focused cell (`border.focus`), ≥3:1 on every row fill (§13); the focused row adds nothing else, so focus and selection never look alike. Selection: the checkbox, the `color.surface.selected` fill, `aria-selected="true"`. Live updates never move focus (`05` §3.4 item 3); a focused row never leaves (D01 D13 d). Targets: compact controls are 24×24 (`size.target.min`; the checkbox's hit area is the whole 24×24 cell), comfortable 32×32 (`size.control.comfortable` and `size.row.comfortable`, both 32; `16` §3 rule 3 and CMP-002 say 40–44 px, gap §15 item 9). No control is smaller: interactive chips take the control height (§12), and the row menu duplicates every SplitButton item.

### 11.4 Announcements and motion (D01 D13)

Polite: the focused row's changed values and the environment totals, at most once per 2 s per field (`05` §6.2); selection count changes; Near crossings. Assertive, once: order filled or rejected, offline and reconnected (or the post-reconnect summary instead), as `05` §6.2 lists them, and, proposed for E29-D05, D01 D13 f's NO SL and Critical crossings and §7's amend rejected. Never per tick: ages, "Last update" counters, the NO SL timer, value flashes. A closed position or a terminal order lingers in place about 5 s reading "Closed +12.40 USDT", "Filled" or "Cancelled", then moves to Closed today or leaves the tab (D13 d). Value flash at most 200 ms and once per 500 ms per cell, the direction carried by sign or glyph; under `prefers-reduced-motion` no flash, no "New" fade (the marker is static for 5 s), rows leave without animation (`05` §7).
