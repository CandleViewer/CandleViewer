## 1. Purpose, scope and method

### 1.1 What this spec fixes

SCR-078 (`14`:1665-1676) is catalogued as "Panel/Drawer" with a six-item checklist and no states, interactions, RBAC or route, while D01 D14 and D02 §6.4 already give the Fills tab its columns, grouping and export. This spec makes SCR-078 the detail of one scope (an order, a trade group or a closed position) and draws it in every state (§3). It designs the OMS account states #822 names: the reconciling and desync variants (`14`:1412, SCR-063 States; SCR-152's per-panel treatment, `14`:2828), the per-account stale lock (US-POS-008 scenario 3, `11`:3584-3586), the correction that shows both values (D01 D6) and the post-reconnect summary (`14`:2839), with the copy deck (§5).

### 1.2 Scope boundary

Out: SCR-063 itself (D02 draws the grid, the account slot's place and the header and row markers, D02 §10.2; this spec owns the banner design and its copy, D02 §1.3) and the connection machinery (E17-D01's feed states and order-entry matrix; the SCR-152 global slot, CMP-076, CMP-077 and their copy, which are E08-D03's, E08-D11's and E17-D02's; this spec writes no transport string). D02's other "copy: D03" strings outside #822's four message groups (mixed environment, placeholders, bulk-dialog words, empty states, Viewer notices) stay D02's drafts unless the owner rules otherwise (OQ-12).

### 1.3 Method (honest)

Desk design under the agent-delivery adaptation: no participants, sessions or prototype. Every repo claim was read at the base; contrast is computed (§7). Comparisons with Bybit's own order history, TradingView, Quantower, Sierra Chart, ATAS and IBKR's trade log are interaction patterns recalled from familiarity, not re-verified against live builds. Two D01 hypotheses stay untested and carry into §14: the D6 correction wording and the D11 account-level copy.

**Weaker and stronger than the comparables (heuristic, recalled, unverified).** Weaker: a docked drawer narrows the workspace where Quantower or Sierra Chart float a trade log in its own window; a market order shows no slippage (G2) where terminals that record an arrival price do; the CSV is client-built and leaves no audit record (G7); the causing rule and the journal link can be absent (G3, G4). Stronger, in the builds I recall: every correction names its source and keeps the old value visible; exits stay enabled with stated reasons through reconciliation and a stale lock; fan-out fills are grouped per account and a Manager's view is narrowed to their legs.

### 3.4 Summary, slippage and the fixture arithmetic (boards 02, 04, 05)

**Formats.** Averages and slippage carry one decimal more than the tick (63,414.02 for a 0.1 tick), so a fraction of a tick is never rounded away; money to two decimals; the split in whole percent summing to 100; the minus sign U+2212. Summary and section totals round the raw sums (Σ of the unrounded fees), so a total can differ by a cent from the sum of its rounded cells.

### 4.3 Reconciling and desync (board 08)

## 8. Keyboard, focus, announcements and motion (board 12)

### 8.1 Focus

Opening moves focus to the heading (`tabindex="-1"`); the region is a `complementary` landmark named by the heading and joins the `Ctrl+\` region cycle (`05`:94).
