## 1. Purpose, scope and method

### 1.1 What this spec fixes

SCR-078 (`14`:1665-1676) is catalogued as "Panel/Drawer" with a six-item checklist and no states, interactions, RBAC or route, while D01 D14 and D02 §6.4 already give the Fills tab its columns, grouping and export. This spec makes SCR-078 the detail of one scope (an order, a trade group or a closed position) and draws it in every state (§3). It designs the OMS account states #822 names: the reconciling and desync variants (`14`:1412, SCR-063 States; SCR-152's per-panel treatment, `14`:2828), the per-account stale lock (US-POS-008 scenario 3, `11`:3584-3586), the correction that shows both values (D01 D6) and the post-reconnect summary (`14`:2839), with the copy deck (§5).

### 1.2 Scope boundary

Out: SCR-063 itself (D02 draws the grid, the account slot's place and the header and row markers, D02 §10.2; this spec owns the banner design and its copy, D02 §1.3) and the connection machinery (E17-D01's feed states and order-entry matrix; the SCR-152 global slot, CMP-076, CMP-077 and their copy, which are E08-D03's, E08-D11's and E17-D02's; this spec writes no transport string). D02's other "copy: D03" strings outside #822's four message groups (mixed environment, placeholders, bulk-dialog words, empty states, Viewer notices) stay D02's drafts unless the owner rules otherwise (OQ-12).

### 1.3 Method (honest)

Desk design under the agent-delivery adaptation: no participants, sessions or prototype. Every repo claim was read at the base; contrast is computed (§7). Comparisons with Bybit's own order history, TradingView, Quantower, Sierra Chart, ATAS and IBKR's trade log are interaction patterns recalled from familiarity, not re-verified against live builds. Two D01 hypotheses stay untested and carry into §14: the D6 correction wording and the D11 account-level copy.

**Weaker and stronger than the comparables (heuristic, recalled, unverified).** Weaker: a docked drawer narrows the workspace where Quantower or Sierra Chart float a trade log in its own window; a market order shows no slippage (G2) where terminals that record an arrival price do; the CSV is client-built and leaves no audit record, which SR-098 requires (G7, C-17); the causing rule and the journal link can be absent (G3, G4). Stronger, in the builds I recall: every correction names its source and keeps the old value visible; exits stay enabled with stated reasons through reconciliation and a stale lock; fan-out fills are grouped per account and a Manager's view is narrowed to their legs.

### 1.4 Fixtures and security

Accounts "Main", "Sub-1", "Sub-2", BTCUSDT (tick 0.1, lot 0.001) and ETHUSDT; fee rates 0.0200 % maker and 0.0550 % taker; ids are stubs ("…a3f2" for an order, "7f3c9a1e…" for an exec id); the rule name "BTC breakout long" is `22`'s example (`22`:14697). No key, account id, balance or real id appears on any board (#822 security note). Fixtures say LIVE to match D02's boards; E29 runs on Bybit demo until R4 (`docs/plan/backlog/E29.json`:31), where the same frames carry the DEMO pill and identical copy.

(36 characters; resolving is the Owner's by D01 D11 and OQ-6, and E09-D09:97 gives such an action `rbac.owner_only` with its verb, "resolve the lock", an amendment to E09-D09 §3.2, §15), rendered 2a with the reason beside it, and while viewing as anyone `rbac.view_as` comes first (§4.2). Its route does not exist (G10, D01 OQ-7), so it is drawn and annotated "pending route" and stays out of the build until the route exists. **Rows.** On a stale lock (failure, fault, cause not shown, no history) rows keep their values, shaded with CMP-078 at account scope (CR-2), each row's description ending "as of {its `updated_at_ms`} UTC". The kill-switch lock is not shaded and adds no "as of": a kill stops reconciliation, not the private stream, so its rows stay live, as D01 D11 draws that row with its header alone (`docs/design/E29/E29-D01.md`:352) and D02 §10.3 adds no shade. The reassurance `acct.reassure.true` appears only when every open position on the account had `native_stop_present: true` in its last-good frame (`23` §15.2; E29-S07), with the oldest of those frames' times; otherwise `acct.reassure.false` names them. Exits stay enabled and are sent at once for every session that may trade the account (§4.2, legs first): cancel, close, close all and reducing an order's quantity always, and tightening a stop except under the stops rule below. None of them waits for the arm (`22` `ArmToken`: cancels, `closePosition`, `closeAllPositions`, quantity-reducing amends, a take-profit change and a stop's tightening are never refused for want of it), while a new-exposure submit keeps E30-D02's arm on top of its account state. Every new-exposure control is T2 `recon.stale` (`recon.killlock` on the kill-switch lock), and SCR-060's Submit with reduce-only on is T2 `recon.reduce`, closing going through Close position (E30-D02's lockout pattern): this is how this spec reads the AC "every order-entry control for it is drawn disabled" (C-3).

**Stops rule (the client's).** From the first `kill_switch` frame that covers the account in this session (`23` §15.9), for the rest of the session, the stop edits and a recorded take-profit's edit and Cancel (a `trading-stop` write, not a cancel, D02 §10.3) of every position that was open while it covered the account are T2 `recon.stops.frozen`, for every role; SL/TP… stays enabled and SCR-064 says why (E32-D01's `sl.frozen`; D02 §10.3). B8's un-freeze has no public form (W9; INV-B8-g, `28`:4508, tracks it in #2157), so the client never lifts this T2 itself. Other stop edits stay enabled, and one the server refuses shows its refusal at the point of action and stays T2 with that reason (E09-D09 T4, then step 4). While the rule holds on any of the account's positions, every line and announcement for that account says "closing and cancelling still work" instead of "closing, cancelling and tightening stops still work"; the kill-switch line and `acct.locked.line` never claim it. A later session cannot know of a stop an earlier session saw frozen, so after a reload a line may claim tightening still works for such a position until W9 or #2157 lands (G17; D01 OQ-7; C-16).

### 4.5 Corrections explained (board 10)

Carried from D01 D6 and D02 §10.2: a row marker with one subtle highlight (static under reduced motion) that stays until the account's line is dismissed (a standing finding's until a run no longer lists it, below); the line lists the run's corrections most severe first, each linking to its row; the old value stays in the row's tooltip (struck through) and description (spelled out) after dismissal. This spec's shape in the one-line slot: `corr.line`, then Review, which expands the slot into the §5.4 lines (capped, §4.3), and Dismiss. Tone: warning when any line needs the trader, info when every line is "Updated from Bybit". After a reconnect-triggered run the line opens with `corr.window`, the private stream's outage interval and the run's trigger, which the digest is asked to carry (G1). B19's `gap_window` is the re-read window, min(last stream message, last successful reconcile) − 5 s to now (`24`:1920, :1935), not the outage, so it is never shown as one, and a periodic sweep shows no window. **Source of the old value:** no frame or REST read carries one (G1). Target: the run's digest from E29-T06 (D01 OQ-6). Meanwhile an `orders` frame with `change: "reconcile"` gives its row "Updated from Bybit" (the frame names no category, D01:373) with the client's own last frame as the old value; a position frame carries no such marker, so a position's line and marker wait for the digest and the account's desync line is the only signal.

**Standing findings and repeats.** `anomalies`, `unpaired`, `sl_tighter` and `sl_restore_refused` repeat in every pass until the Owner resolves them (`24`:1937), so each is keyed on (category, entity: the order, position or fill) and is a **standing** line: raised and announced once when first seen, never again while a run lists it, and gone when a run no longer lists it. Dismiss hides a standing line, never its way back: its row marker stays until a run no longer lists it, and the slot keeps one line, `corr.standing` ("1 open finding on Sub-1 · Review"), capped as in §4.3 and never announced again (board 10), so an unresolved `sl_restore_refused` or unpaired fill always has an entry point. Every other category is empty on a repeat pass (`24`:1937), so a later listing of the same (category, entity) is a new occurrence, keyed with its run (the digest's, G1; meanwhile the frame that carried it): it raises `corr.line`, is announced, and replaces the row's marker, tooltip and `corr.description`, so a second drift on BTCUSDT is shown and heard (test: two `position_drift` events on one symbol, both announced). **For assistive technology** the old value is spelled out: the tooltip's strikethrough and arrow are visual only (`del` and `ins` with visually hidden "was" and "now", the arrow `aria-hidden`), and the row's description is `corr.description` (WCAG 1.3.1).

### 4.6 Post-reconnect summary (board 11)

After a client reconnect, once the private snapshots and `system` are in (E17-D01:226), the SCR-152 `recovered` region (E08-D03:79; E17-D02's B7) carries the summary: title "Reconnected" (E08-D03), body line 1 `summary.body` in the catalogue's form (`14`:2839), line 2 `summary.line2` with the offline window and, when an account line holds corrections, a pointer to it; Dismiss; held ≥ 10 s and until dismissed (E08-D11 D3). Clauses: orders filled, then stops moved by a rule; the account is named only when clauses span several accounts. "1 order filled" opens SCR-078 for that order; several open the Fills tab filtered to the window. **The fills clause** counts `Trade` fills whose `ts_ms` falls in the window, grouped by order: an order reads "filled" only once it reached Filled, otherwise "partly filled"; an ADL or Liquidation fill goes to its account's line in its tone (`summary.adl`: warning, danger for a liquidation), never to the success region; funding and settlement rows are not listed. The source is the server's digest (E29-S07, G1); until it exists only the fills clause can be built (from the re-snapshot and `listExecutions?from=`, by the same rules), so line 2 becomes `summary.interim` and no rule-action clause is ever shown from a guess. When an account's line holds corrections, line 2 points to it and names a repaired stop first, which is how this spec meets E29-S07's "the repaired-stop entry appears in the summary instead" (C-12). With nothing to report the region reads "Reconnected" alone (E08-D03). Its announcement leads the one assertive utterance a reconnect gets (§8.3).

## 8. Keyboard, focus, announcements and motion (board 12)

### 8.1 Focus

Opening moves focus to the heading (`tabindex="-1"`); the region is a `complementary` landmark named by the heading and joins the `Ctrl+\` region cycle (`05`:94). Tab order: heading → Export CSV → Close → the scope line's link → summary links → the notice band's links → the "N new fills" pill → the table (one roving stop that returns to the last focused cell, `05` §3.4 item 2). `Esc` from anywhere in the drawer closes it and returns focus to the invoking control (CMP-209); if that row has left the list, focus goes to the Fills tab's roving stop. Live updates never move focus (`05`:96). The drawer never covers a focused element (`05` §3.4 item 6; D-3). **In the slot** (SCR-063's banner stop, D02 §11.1): Tab reaches Details, Review, Dismiss, "+2 more accounts" and Resolve… in visual order; Details and Review toggle in place (`aria-expanded`), or open the CMP-017 popover with focus on its first line, `Esc` closing it back to its trigger; `Enter` on a Review line moves focus to that row's cell in the grid; after Dismiss focus goes to the next line in the slot, else to the grid's roving stop, never to `body`. Neither the drawer nor the slot has a field, so `Enter` never submits anything here.
