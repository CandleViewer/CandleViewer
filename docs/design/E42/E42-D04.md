## 6. The before/after diff language (SCR-135, SCR-136, SCR-122's change summary, SCR-145's history)

- **Input.** `detail.before`, `detail.after` (an object or null) and `detail.elided` (Y:19663-19679), read with the
  record's `outcome` and shape (the table's first three rows; CG-30). `04:1305` asks writers for field-level diffs;
  these store whole states and requests, so the renderer computes the changed keys. SCR-122's summary takes the inline
  form: a role → `value`, a grant → Added / Removed, a cap → `value` with its unit (D03 UD-3; §20).
- **Two forms.** _Inline_: one line per changed field, "overrides: 1 → 2"; the arrow is `aria-hidden` and the line reads
  "changed from 1 to 2" (compact rows, SCR-122's summary, flag history); request-shape lines read
  `diff.inline_requested`. _Full_: a `<dl>` with one term per changed field (its path) whose description is a nested
  `<dl>` with the visible terms **Before** and **After** (row expansion and SCR-136; `14:2586`; #887 A11y notes).
- **Rules.** Unchanged fields fold into the `diff.unchanged_n` disclosure; "Added", "Removed" and "Changed" are words
  in a status column, never a colour or strike-through (it means delisted, E08-D01:86); a null `before` reads
  `diff.no_before`, and "Created" / "Removed" need a registry map naming the action so (DJ); nested objects keep their
  path ("overrides › 9f1c…"); lists compare as whole values; a value over 120 characters folds behind `diff.full`.

- **Safety rules.** **S-1** A status change is applied in the frame it is known; a transition follows it, never
  precedes or delays it (E09-D08 S1), so no reduced-motion user misses one (#887 Scope). **S-2** Security and money
  states never animate: the tamper and maintenance banners, the live row, a flag's value, the env badge (E11-D06:359,
  :362). **S-3** A focused control is never inside a fading element; nothing delays focus or an announcement (E09-D08
  D2, F-4). **S-4** No cross-fade between allowed and denied, ON and OFF, passed and FAILED (E09-D08 F-8). **S-5** Only
  `opacity` and `transform` animate; heights and widths never do (F-2). **S-6** Nothing loops past 5 s but M14's
  request-bound spinner (E09-D08 D13 asks `16` §7 for that exception), and nothing flashes (`16:172`; `05:208`).
- **Reduce and mid-flight.** Every row's twin is "instant style change plus text — never removal of the signal"
  (E08-D11:160). A mode flip lets a running transition finish on its clock; builders add no `transition: none` reduce
  utility and never re-mount a layer on the `data-motion` flip (the two builder rules of E09-D08 §14).
- **Flash and vestibular.** Zero flashes in every row. The largest movement is the drawer's 480 px (1280) or 640 px
  (2560) over 200 ms, motion-safe only; no parallax, zoom or scroll-linked motion; no smooth scrolling.
- **Coverage.** 23 rows: 9 animate under motion-safe (M1, M2, M5, M6, M12, M14, M15, M19 and M21, inherited and
  unused), each with a reduce twin; of those, M5, M14 and M15 carry information and name their carriers; 13 have no
  motion in either mode and say so; M8 is inherited. Boards 63–65 draw the storyboard, the twins and the flash sheet.
