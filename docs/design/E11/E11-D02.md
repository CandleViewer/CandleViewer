## 1. Purpose, scope and method

### 1.1 What this spec fixes

The chrome every chart surface renders inside: the SCR-030 chassis (header rows, pane stack, gutters, time strip, price gutter, Deep Stats slot, dividers), CMP-188 PriceAxis, CMP-189 TimeAxis, CMP-190 Crosshair with CMP-197 ChartTooltip, CMP-226 BarCountdown, CMP-199 ChartLegend, CMP-198 ChartWatermark / PaneBackground, the multi-pane chrome (splitters, collapsed stubs, pane focus, per-pane labels — feeds E11-S09), the SCR-046 diagnostics overlay's field list (feeds E11-T07), the SCR-030 states, the E11-S12 aggregation indicator, all of it in three themes × three densities. Out of scope, per #308: footprint / profile / heatmap / bubble / Deep Stats **content** (E18–E22), order and position lines (E31), drawing tools (E14), the scale and time context menus and go-to-date (E11-D03), the data table and the empty / error states (E11-D04), settings beyond the Scales section (E15), the motion spec proper (E11-D06).

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk design and heuristic evaluation under the agent-delivery adaptation (owner decision 2026-09-25): no participants, no sessions, no prototype review — the E06 spike is a Node benchmark with modelled GPU cost and no pointer input (E11-D01 Q1-f, O2), so the ticket's "prototype review against the E06 spike build" could not happen and is recorded in §14 as a gap, not as done. Contrast and CVD are computed programmatically (§11), never eyeballed. Frame: Nielsen's ten heuristics plus the trading heuristics of `05` (keyboard-first as speed §1; one hotkey layer §3.1/§3.3; no hover-only or drag-only interaction §3.1 item 6; colour never alone §4; equal-status canvas alternatives §6; reduced motion §7). Reference products are cited only where E11-D01 verified them; nothing new was read from the web for this spec.

### 1.3 Locked inputs (built on, not re-decided)

- **E11-D01 (#3828) D1–D6 and the three rulings of 2026-10-20:** the plain wheel over an axis scales that axis, `Alt+wheel` and `Ctrl+wheel` unbound; a vertical plot drag is inert while autoscale is on; "Lock scale" = autoscale off with the **"Price scale locked" badge on the price axis as the restore control**, announced once; the time-axis cluster `auto` (follow-live; off = **"Paused at hh:mm"**), `fit` (fit the loaded bars), `›` (= latest = `End`); the readout magnet off by default; the data cursor and the pointer crosshair are **one position with two drivers, last input wins**; the Tab order (Q4-i) toolbar → **status chips (Stale, aggregation — one roving stop)** → plot (one roving stop) → handles → price axis → lock badge → time axis → cluster, with "the state badges" among the stops in reading order (D4); the aggregation chip (W2 wording) is the **only chip beside "Stale"**, the E12 corner slot stays LOD-only; the remote crosshair on a linked chart is a dashed vertical only; time synced in a sync group, price never; the left-edge loading band, the kept right margin, the go-to-date marker and pill.
- **E06-D03 (#3912) D10:** the keyboard data cursor is a full-height column ≥2 px in `color.focus.ring` with a 1 px pane-colour halo on both sides (the double ring), a band behind narrow bars, the CMP-039 ring once at the chart boundary; surrogates are sized to their visual targets (`05` §6.1); the 26 §12 rules.
- **E17-D01 (#3905):** feed vocabulary and precedence Disconnected > Stale > Resyncing > Delayed > Live; per-topic thresholds T1 (book 2 s, ticker 5 s, trades 10 s) and T2 = 3× (architect ruling A2 on #249); the chart's stale chip in CMP-239's `Stale Ns` format ("Stale 7s" in E17-D01) and 15 % dim; "warn fast, clear slow".
- **E30-D01 (#3802) §3:** no `color.env.*` pair reaches 7:1 in any theme and five of the eight env text cells fall below AA in dark and high-contrast, so the environment badge's **text is `color.text.primary` on a neutral `color.surface.raised` pill** and the env hue carries the pill's border, icon and the CMP-074 band.
- **E06-D02:** SCR-046's states, layout, vocabulary, the 2 Hz sampling and the closed copy payload; this spec adds context fields (§9) and corrects one chip pair the audit fails (§11, A-6).
- **Plan:** `26` §3.5 (bar-index space, clamp `[0.05, 200]` px/bar), §3.6 (`LOD_PROFILE_M0` frozen: L0 ≥12 px, L1 ≥10 px, L2 ≥6 px, ±15 % asymmetric hysteresis — a legibility floor E06-D01 measured, never traded by a density mode), §3.7 (SDF text, ≤6 text draw calls per pane, ≤2.0 ms), §4 (≤18 draw calls per pane, ≤40 for 3 panes; crosshair labels and order-line labels are DOM), §5 rule 5 (the density triple), §12; `06` §4.3 stage budgets and §4.4 ladder; `05` §3.1.6, §3.4 items 6–8, §4, §6, §7; `16` §2–§7; `14` SCR-030 and its two checklists, SCR-046; `15` §7.

## 3. Decisions at a glance

| D1  | **Three gridline tiers, two of them decorative.** Minor = `color.grid.line`, major = `color.border.default` (measured 1.15 / 1.50 :1 on the canvas in dark), both below 3:1 **by design** — background grid is chrome that must recede (`16` §6 rule 7); the only meaning-carrying lines (session / day boundary, maintenance edges) are 1 px dashed `color.text.tertiary` at ≥5.96:1 in every theme (11.87:1 in high-contrast). ## 4. Gridline hierarchy (D1–D3)

**Why this is the highest-leverage decision.** A grid either recedes and lets price dominate, or it competes with data-ink and the chart reads as noise (#308 Technical notes). `16` §6 rule 7 draws the line precisely: gridlines are structural, so where they **carry meaning** they meet 3:1; purely aesthetic background gridlines are **minimised rather than exempted**. The chassis therefore has exactly two decorative tiers, deliberately faint, and one meaning-carrying tier at text-level contrast. No new hex value is invented: every tier is an existing token, measured in §11.

### 4.2 Which price levels get a major line (D2)

With `tick` the instrument tick size and the label step `s_L` chosen in §5.2 from the ladder `{1, 2, 5} × 10^n × tick`:

1. The **major step `s_M`** is the smallest decimal-round price value — `10^k` or `5 × 10^k` in price units, `k` any integer — that is **≥ 2 × s_L and an integer multiple of s_L**. Majors therefore always coincide with a label and always land on a number a trader calls round.
2. Consequence: majors fall every 2nd label when `s_L` leads with a 5 (50 → 100, 25 → 50, 0.5 → 1), every 5th when it leads with a 1 or a 2 (2 → 10, 0.2 → 1, 20 → 100, 1 → 5).
3. Worked (pane 600 px, compact `G` = 40 px): BTCUSDT tick 0.5, visible 63,900–64,300 → `s_L` = 50 (9 labels, 75 px apart), `s_M` = 100 (majors at 63,900 … 64,300 every 2nd label). ETHUSDT tick 0.01, 3,240.00–3,260.00 → `s_L` = 2 (11 labels), `s_M` = 10 (every 5th). SOLUSDT tick 0.001, 150.200–152.600 → `s_L` = 0.2 (13 labels), `s_M` = 1.0 (every 5th). Dense (`G` = 32) BTCUSDT → `s_L` = 25 (17 labels, 38 px), `s_M` = 50 (every 2nd).
4. Log scale: majors at decades, minors at the 2× and 5× intermediates that clear the collision gap (E11-S06); percent scale: majors at multiples of 1 %, 5 %, 10 % by the same rule over the percent ladder `0.1 / 0.2 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 5 / 10`.
5. Never: "every nth pixel", "every nth label" without the round-number test, or a major line that has no label.

### 4.3 When the crosshair and a gridline coincide (D3)

Two channels, both independent of colour: **pattern** — gridlines are solid, the crosshair is dashed 4 / 4 (CSS px, scaled per §4.4), so a dashed line over a solid line still reads as two objects at any DPR; **luminance** — the crosshair is the brightest hairline in the pane (≥9.3:1 on the canvas) and clears every gridline tier by ≥3.08:1 (measured, worst case the high-contrast major line). No halo on the pointer crosshair: a halo would cost two more line instances per frame for a case the two channels already solve. The **keyboard cursor column** (E06-D03 D10) is the exception and keeps its 1 px pane-colour halo, because it is ≥2 px of solid `color.focus.ring` that must stay distinct from candle bodies it crosses, not from the grid. When the crosshair sits exactly on a boundary line (dashed on dashed), the crosshair's dash phase is offset by 4 px so the two dash trains interleave; the boundary keeps its date label on the axis and the crosshair pill is drawn over it (D5 priority).

### 4.4 Units and device pixels (ORCH-8)

Every width in this spec is in **CSS px** unless marked; the engine draws in device pixels (`26` §3.4 DPI: backing store at `devicePixelRatio`, geometry snapped to device pixels for hairlines). Rules: (1) a **hairline** (grid, boundary and maintenance edges, crosshair, axis rules, the splitter hairline, the last-price and mark lines) is `max(1, floor(dpr))` device px — 1 device px at dpr 1, 1.25 and 1.5, 2 at dpr 2 and 2.5, 3 at dpr 3 — so it is never blurred across two device pixels and never thinner than one; (2) **dash patterns** (`4 / 4`, `6 / 6`, `2 / 6`) are CSS px scaled by the same `floor(dpr)`, so the dash rhythm reads the same on every monitor; (3) 2 px rings and bars are `2 × floor(dpr)` device px; (4) snapping follows E11-S06 (round to the device grid, a half-device-pixel offset for odd widths); (5) on a **DPR change** (a monitor move, OS scaling, browser zoom) the engine receives `resize { width, height, dpr }` (`26` §6) and within **one frame** re-snaps every hairline, re-measures the gutters (LAYOUT dirty), re-scales the SDF text (one 24 px size class, no atlas regeneration) and re-positions the DOM overlay from the next `viewport` message; E11-S10 owns the resize / DPR path and asserts it with the golden image at dpr 1.25.

## 5. Axis decimation (D4, D5)

Specified as a rule so it holds at continuous zoom (#308 Technical notes); E11-S06 implements it and already drafts the same shape (priority-ordered candidates, a 1.5-label-height collision gap, boundary ticks at priority 0). This section fixes the numbers and the drop order E11-S06 leaves open.

### 5.1 Two gaps

- **Collision gap** (hard, every density): no two retained labels may be closer than **1.5 × label height** edge to edge (E11-S06 AC); a label whose box would overlap a placed label is dropped, whatever its priority.
- **Density gap `G`** (soft, picks the ladder step): the ladder step is the **smallest** step whose on-screen spacing is ≥ `G`, with `G` = **96 / 80 / 64 px** centre to centre on the time axis and **48 / 40 / 32 px** on the price axis for comfortable / compact / dense (D6). `G` scales with the density mode and with the text-scale factor (`--cv-text-scale`, §6 rule 6), never with zoom.
- **Cluster exclusion zone** (time axis only): the strip's label area ends **8 px left of the cluster's left edge** — the Paused pill included, so the zone grows while follow is off. A candidate whose box crosses that edge is dropped like a collision, **except a P0 date label, which shifts left** until it clears the edge (keeping its boundary line where it is); the crosshair time pill flips to the left of its anchor when it would intersect the zone (§5.5). The zone is the only place a label moves instead of dropping, and it exists because three 24 px segments plus borders (≈ 74 px) exceed the compact gutter (≈ 48–61 px), so the cluster always extends under the plot's right edge.
- **Tag exclusion zone** (time axis only; mirrors the cluster rule — HPE-R2-3): the strip's label area begins **8 px right of the zone tag's right edge** (`UTC 13:47:05` is ≈ 90 px at 11 px mono, so the area starts ≈ 100 px from the strip's left edge). A candidate whose box crosses that edge is dropped like a collision, **except a P0 date label, which shifts right** until it clears the edge (its boundary line stays where it is). With both zones, no label's visibility depends on where it lands against fixed furniture, so nothing blinks while panning (§5.3's stability rule); E11-S06 implements both edges with one routine.

### 5.2 Ladders

- **Price:** `{1, 2, 5} × 10^n × tickSize` — the ladder is in **tick units**, so at tick 0.5 it yields the price steps 0.5, 1, 2.5, 5, 10, 25, 50 … : 2.5 and 25 are legitimate steps (5 and 50 ticks; TradingView's price scale uses them too) and §4.2's dense BTC case uses 25. What is excluded is E11-S06's 2.5 **multiplier** (`2.5 × 10^n × tickSize`), which at n = 0 is 1.25 at tick 0.5 or 0.025 at tick 0.01 — not a whole number of ticks, so its labels would leave the tick grid; every `{1, 2, 5}` step is a whole number of ticks (ORCH-5, built on F-9). Ideal step = range / ⌊paneHeight / G⌋; pick the first ladder value ≥ ideal. Labels are formatted to the tick's precision (0.5 → one decimal: `64,050.0`), thousands separated, right-aligned in the gutter, `type.num.*` per density.
- **Time** (the 1/2/5 scheme generalised to the calendar's bases): `1 s, 2 s, 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, 30 s, 1 m, 2 m, 5 m, 10 m, 15 m, 30 m, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h, 6 h, 12 h, 1 d, 2 d, 1 w, 1 M, 3 M, 1 y`. Sub-minute steps exist only for intervals below one minute (tick / second bars). Pick the smallest step whose spacing (step ÷ bar interval × px per bar) is ≥ `G`. **Non-time bar types** (`bar_type` tick / volume / range / renko / P&F) have no uniform time per bar: CMP-189's label strategy for them — bar-index ticks with sparse time anchors and the non-time axis affordance of E12-D01's stimulus A — is **E12-D06's** (its density ladder), with E12-D07 for the SCR-045 close-time column; this spec's ladder applies to time bars and to the time anchors of non-time bars (§21).

### 5.5 Price axis: what never moves (D5)

- The **last-price pill** is pinned at the last close and suppresses any tick label whose box comes within one label height of it; it is never covered by anything (SCR-030's "labels never obscure the last price").
- The **crosshair pill** is drawn over tick labels (which hide beneath it) but is **nudged** vertically, by the minimal amount plus a 2 px gap, when it would overlap the last-price pill; the crosshair line stays at its true price. The same rule applies on the time axis between the crosshair time pill and a boundary date label (the date label yields; the boundary line does not). The crosshair time pill **flips to the left of its anchor** when it would intersect the cluster exclusion zone (§5.1); it never sits under the cluster.
- Order and position line labels (E31) obey the same stack: below the last-price pill, above tick labels.
- Autoscale margin: 5 % of the range above and below the visible extremes (`26` §3.5 "configurable margin"; SCR-031 Scales owns the setting) so the pill never sits on the pane edge.
