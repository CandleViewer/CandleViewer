## 6. Editors

### 6.1 Parameter editor (NC-8, outside the menu subtree)

- **Anatomy (§3).** Label and field with its unit; pointer steppers; `Apply`; Recent (the last three committed sizes
  for that symbol and mode, newest first; a click fills the field, never commits); the message line; the estimate line.
  Opening it never moves the rows above; the rows below move down once, and nothing moves while typing.
- **Keys.** Typing; `↑`/`↓` step and `PageUp`/`PageDown` step ten times (tick 100, volume and delta one floor, range and
  Renko 1 tick); `Home`/`End` minimum and maximum (CMP-008); `Enter` validates, then commits or refuses with focus kept;
  `Esc` closes the editor and returns to its row with nothing changed; `←` does the same only with the caret at the
  start and nothing selected, otherwise it moves the caret; `Tab` walks field → Recent → `Apply`, then leaves the popover
  without a commit; `Shift+Tab` from the field returns to the row.
- **Estimate (G-2).** Requested 300 ms after the last keystroke, for a valid value only; while invalid the line is
  empty. It names the window it builds: the loaded window (E11-D03's days-to-load control), cut at the recording start
  and at 31 days. Tick and volume estimates are near-exact, so past 250,000 bars C-19 refuses the commit; a delta
  estimate is a bound and warns (C-20); range and Renko count one bar per trade (`bars/estimate.py:15-16`) and warn only
  if G-2 supplies a tighter bound; `bar_rebuild_too_large` stays the backstop. A failed estimate reads C-18 and the
  commit proceeds (D-2). The estimate is never announced while typing; it is part of the field's description.
- **Renko held specimen (board 05, AC 4).** The one field accepts digits (ticks). Precedence: digits are ticks; `atr:N`
  means ATR(N) of 1m bars, resolved once into ticks at the build start (ADR-0033 R2 (a) and (b)). R1 answers C-12. The
  specimen draws the R2 anatomy greyed and labelled "Held for R2": "atr:14" in the field and the line "ATR(14) of 1m
  bars at the build start ≈ {37} ticks"; it is not a state of the R1 menu.

### 6.2 Custom interval (waits for G-1b)

- "Custom interval…" (`menuitem`, `aria-expanded`) opens a CMP-007 field below it, outside the menu, with C-40's helper.
- Accepted: a whole number, spaces allowed, then `m`, `h` or `H`, or no unit (minutes, TradingView's habit); `1d`,
  `1D` and `24h` are 1D. A capital `M` is refused: elsewhere it reads as months. Range 1m to 24h (1,440m).
- A value equal to a ladder item (5m, 60m, 2h, 1d) selects that item and is never added to Recent; any other commits a
  client-aggregated interval from 1m on the server's grid (E12-S11; D02 §3) and becomes Recent's first chip.
- Messages: C-13 for an empty `Enter`, `0m`, `3x`, `25h`, `1.5h`, `2d` or `M`; C-14 for `s` or `w` units (post-GA).
- **Recent:** at most five, newest first, de-duplicated (a repeat moves to the front); a sixth drops the oldest; the
  row never wraps beyond one line at 400 px. Session-only until G-3 stores it.

### 6.3 Favourites editor (NC-9, in place)

- "Edit favourites…" replaces the menu inside the same popover (no dialog; the chart stays visible); `Esc` or `Cancel`
  returns to the menu and discards; `Save` (CMP-001 primary) writes and returns.
- A `listbox` "Favourite slots" of nine options, "Slot 1, 1 minute" … "Slot 9, empty", each with its Kbd digit; below,
  `Change…`, `Move up`, `Move down`, `Clear` and `Add current ({15m})` (D02 §4's label) act on the selected slot (the
  APG rearrangeable listbox: no control inside an option); `Cancel`, `Save` last.
- Keys: `↑`/`↓` or a digit select a slot; `Enter` = `Change…`; `Alt+↑`/`Alt+↓` move it; `Delete` clears it.
- `Change…` opens the picker: the menu without Favourites, under the banner C-41 "Choose an entry for slot {4}";
  activation assigns instead of committing, and a mode's editor assigns its size; an entry already in another slot
  shows that slot's digit and moves here (C-42). `Esc` returns to the editor.
- Rules: empty slots stay empty and their digit does nothing (the menu says C-42 "No favourite on {3}"); a non-time
  slot keeps its parameter and follows D-2 when it needs a build; the default ladder is D02 §4's (1m 3m 5m 15m 30m 1h
  4h 12h 1D) and E12-D04 renders it unchanged.
- Persistence (G-3): `PATCH /settings` under `settings:write` (`22:8979-8987`, no audit record). While it saves, the
  editor stays and `Save` shows CMP-001's loading state, which is not T2 (E09-D09 §9.1): " …" after its label and
  `aria-busy="true"`, still focusable (never the `disabled` attribute, which would drop focus to `body`), and a second
  press does nothing until `PATCH /settings` answers; then it closes to the menu, or C-41's failure line shows and is
  announced once (§8.4). Before G-3 the item is absent and the default ladder is read-only. A Viewer sees the item in
  CMP-079's T2 form (E09-D09 §2.2, `E09-D09.md:49`, `:76`): still a menu item, `aria-disabled` and focusable, the `lock`
  glyph (CMP-019, `decorative=false`, name "Not available") leading the full label in `color.text.tertiary`, and the
  reason line "Your role can't change settings." (`rbac.permission`: a Manager holds `settings:write`) in
  `color.text.secondary`. A failed save keeps the editor and its edits with C-41's failure line; a refusal for
  permission (E09-D09 T4) turns its controls into the same T2 form with that reason, their fills becoming
  `color.surface.raised`.

### 6.4 About bar modes (CMP-069 inline)

The About item (C-31) expands a panel under the Bar modes header, outside the menu, and moves focus to its heading
(`tabindex="-1"`), so a screen reader reads it; `Esc`, or the item again, collapses it and returns focus to the item.
It holds C-32 and, by state, C-33 or C-34 with C-35 or C-36. No tooltip carries any of it. Activating the route
closes SCR-042 with no commit and opens M-023, which returns focus to the trigger when it closes.

## 7. Placement and overflow (AC 5)

1. **Layer and bounds.** The popover renders in the shell layer, so it may leave the chart panel but never its OS
   window: the main window or a SCR-027 floating window. It keeps 8 px (`space.2`) inside the window.
2. **Anchor.** Top-left 4 px (`space.1`) under the trigger's bottom-left. From SCR-041's "More…" it replaces SCR-041's
   content in place at SCR-041's anchor and keeps its own width; `Esc` returns to SCR-041 with focus on "More…", and a
   commit carries SCR-041's symbol with the chosen interval or mode in one commit (NC-10; E12-D04 draws that side).
3. **Horizontal.** Shift left until the right edge clears the window by 8 px and the price gutter's last-price pill by
   4 px (E11-D02 D10's priority stack; E11-D03 §3.1). Where the window's room left of the pill is narrower than the
   popover, the popover narrows to that room, down to 320 px; with less room than that it may cover the gutter, and
   the price stays readable in the legend and the status row (X-4, §11).
4. **Vertical.** Below while the content fits; else flip above when the space above is taller; else clamp to the
   taller side, maximum height = that space minus 8.
5. **Scroll.** One scroll area for the menu and its panels; group headers stick to its top; the cut edge shows a 1 px
   `color.border.subtle` rule and the scrollbar stays visible while scrollable. On open the checked item (or an open
   editor) scrolls into view; focus movement scrolls with `block: "nearest"`. No group is ever removed or truncated:
   every item stays in the accessibility tree and one `↓` away.
6. **Narrow panel.** No chart-panel minimum width exists on `main`; this spec assumes 320 px (the 1,280 px minimum
   window, `16` §8, split in four) for AC 5's frame (OQ-A, §11). CMP-221 overflows later controls into `⋯` first; the
   symbol and interval buttons never overflow, and if even the interval button cannot fit, `⋯ → Interval…` opens
   SCR-042 at `⋯`.
7. **Text scale and zoom.** At 200 % the 1,280 × 800 window is 640 × 400 CSS px: the width rule holds and the height
   scrolls; at 150 % text, descriptions wrap to two lines; nothing truncates.

### 8.3 Focus

The technique is roving `tabindex`, not `aria-activedescendant`: DOM focus moves to the item, and into the editors
and the panel outside the menu. On open, focus lands on the checked item in its home group (Time, Recent or Bar
modes; a favourite shows checked too). After a commit it returns to the trigger, or to the chart region when `I`
opened the menu (D02 D-11). An estimate, a recording change or a store update never moves focus or the active item
(`05` §3.4 item 3). The ring is never covered by a sticky header (WCAG 2.4.11): scrolling keeps the focused item below
them. The recording route (§6.4) closes the menu with no commit; M-023 then returns focus to the trigger.

### 8.4 Live regions

One polite region in the popover, empty at mount, text swapped (`05` §6.2): an invalid `Enter`'s message or C-19,
once; a T2 item's reason on `Enter`, `Space` or `→`, once (D3-5; E09-D09 §9.3); C-41's failure line, once;
C-42, the recording start included, once. The menu's name and the focused item are announced natively; the estimate is
read on demand from the field's description; a commit's result and a build's start are the chart's announcements
(D02 §7). Nothing in SCR-042 streams.

### 8.5 Motion, high contrast, forced colours

Open and close follow E11-D06 row 39 (opacity, `motion.duration.fast`, `motion.easing.standard`; 0 ms under
`prefers-reduced-motion`); the editor, the panel and the favourites editor appear at 0 ms in both modes; scrolling into
view is instant; no glyph animates (E12-D10 owns motion). In high contrast and `forced-colors: active` every state keeps
a shape or word: ◉/○, the 2 px checked outline, the ring, the `lock` with its reason (⊘ with C-26 for an unservable
slot), ✕ with the 2 px boundary, " …"; panels gain a 1 px border. Every text pair used meets 7:1 in high contrast (§9).

### 8.6 Keyboard-only walk (F-BAR-2 and F-BAR-3; board 17)

1. Chart focused on BTCUSDT 1m. `I` (or `Tab` to the toolbar, roving to "Interval: 1 minute", `Enter`): "Interval and
   bar mode, BTCUSDT, menu", "1 minute, checked".
2. `V` (typeahead): "Volume, 50 BTC per bar, Bars close on traded volume; one big trade can close several. Needs a build:
   about 3.1 million prints, about 30 seconds".
3. `Enter` (the row needs a build, so the editor opens; `→` does the same): "Volume per bar, BTC, spin button, 50".
4. Type `0`: the message line shows C-8; `Enter` keeps focus and speaks it once. Type `75`: C-15, then C-16 after 300 ms.
5. `Enter`: the menu closes; focus on the trigger, "Bar mode: switching to Volume, 75 BTC per bar, showing 1 minute";
   the chart says "Building the chart as Volume 75 BTC bars. Escape cancels." (D02 §7).
6. F-BAR-3: `Esc` (nothing else takes it) cancels; the strip reads D02's cancel notice; reopen and `V`, `→`: the field
   reads 50 with C-22.
7. At any step `Esc` closes with no change and focus on the trigger.
