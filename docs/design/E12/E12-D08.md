- **R-1 Never approximate.** No state ever offers to build non-time bars from klines or estimates; the only exits are the merged ones (`Switch to time bars`, `Go to recorded history`, `Build earlier bars`, `Record this symbol`), and every state reads as a statement of fact, never "error", "unfortunately" or an apology (T Tech; `22`:4188–4190; E16-D05:152; RULED BQ-28).

Two waits name their condition instead: the disconnected card waits for the connection, whose countdown is SCR-152's (E11-D04:367), and a build with neither a total nor an estimate waits on D08-G1's bound (§7.2).
