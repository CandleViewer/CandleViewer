### 1.5 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Never colour alone.** Every type at every tier carries direction by a named non-colour channel (fill, outline, weight, span pattern, position or word); colour is the second channel, never the first (`05`:107; `16`:160); §4.1's known limit is the one disclosed exception (D06-C11). The direction table (§4) is the tool's source for this check.
- **R-2 Never bridged.** No line, area or baseline crosses an absent slot, a coverage hole or a quiet interval; fills close to their base at each break edge (T AC5; D02:38).
- **R-3 No motion.** Nothing in the series layer animates; every state is a static mark plus a word (`05`:202; motion is E12-D10's).
- **R-4 Real prices anchor.** Crosshair readouts, drawings, order lines and the mirror's first values are real OHLCV; derived values come second and say so (T Tech; US-CHART-002).
- **R-5 Survivors only.** No mark below L0 needs a per-bar quantity `26`'s decimation discarded; the two quantities this spec adds to the survivors are routed (hand-off A-11).
- **R-6 Neutral stays neutral.** `color.candle.wick` is never remapped by the CVD-safe palette or the inverted convention (E11-D05:111); convention and palette swap colours only.
- **R-7 No account state.** No position side, PnL or account-derived input reaches series colour, weight or shape, and the series layer offers no order affordance (T Sec).
- **R-8 One rounding rule per quantity** (§2).

## 10. Equi-volume and delta-volume (RULED BQ-14)

### 10.1 Width mapping

- x = volume (equi-volume) or \|delta\| (delta-volume). r = x / max(median x, smallest non-zero x) across the loaded series; it is recomputed when the series loads and when a bar closes, not on pan; if every x is zero, every bar takes the floor.
- w0 = clamp(roundHalfUp(14<!--=GEOM:equi.factor--> × r), 6<!--=GEOM:equi.min-->, 48<!--=GEOM:equi.cap-->) px.
- Zoom scale k = (s − 1<!--=GEOM:equi.gap-->) / mean(w0), recomputed with the median; bars sit at cumulative x with a 1<!--=GEOM:equi.gap--> px gap, so the mean pitch stays s.
- Wicks take §3.1's formula on min(w, 35<!--=GEOM:body.cap-->), w the bar's drawn width k × w0, so they stay 1<!--=GEOM:wick.up--> px up / 2<!--=GEOM:wick.down--> px down at every width and a wide bar keeps the weight channel.

### 10.2 The cap and the suspended mapping

- **Cap:** a bar with 14<!--=GEOM:equi.factor--> × r > 48<!--=GEOM:equi.cap--> is drawn at the cap with a 5<!--=GEOM:notch.w--> × 3<!--=GEOM:notch.h--> px notch cut into the top edge of the body (when the bar is ≥ 7<!--=GEOM:notch.min-w--> px wide and the body ≥ 5<!--=GEOM:notch.min-body-h--> px tall). The mirror appends ", width capped (N × median)", N to one decimal.
- **Below L0 mean width** the mapping is suspended: equal slots of pitch s with the standard ladder (§5.5); the chip's disclosure text adds "· width mapping off" (§6.1).

### 10.3 Delta

The delta-volume price body follows candle direction; the delta sign lives in the signed sub-pane, which takes the volume sub-pane's slot (§12) in this mode: zero line a hairline in `color.candle.wick`; positive bars rise, negative bars fall, each with the price bar's x and width; signed labels ("+1.2", "−0.034") in `color.axis.text` only at L0 and only where the slot is ≥ 24<!--=GEOM:delta.label-slot--> px, dropped on collision — the only series text, slot-gated (hand-off §E). Only trade-built bars carry delta; kline backfill has none, and the earliest bar with delta is E12-D08's partial-history boundary.
