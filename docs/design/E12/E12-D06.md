## 1. Units, DPR and rounding

| Rule | Definition |
|---|---|
| Bar spacing | `s` = slot pitch in logical (CSS) px, the engine's `barSpacing` (26 §3.5, clamped to 0.05–200) |
| Units | **Every px value in this file is logical**; the renderer multiplies by `devicePixelRatio`. At non-integer DPR a width is max(1, round_half_up(w × DPR)) device px and left edges snap to whole device px (26 §3.4). The 2560×1440 frame is the 1280×720 layout at DPR 2 |
| Rounding | round_half_up(x) = floor(x + 0.5). Renderer and golden tests evaluate D1 as floor((7·s + 5) / 10) in float64: the same value, exact for integer and half-integer s (`floor(s*0.7 + 0.5)` gives 31 at s = 45, not 32) |
| Body (D1) | body = clamp(round_half_up(0.7·s), 1, 35). If body ≥ 4 and even: body + 1 when body + 1 ≤ s − 1, else body − 1. So bodies ≥ 4 px are odd |
| Weight (D2) | wick_up = max(1, round_half_up(body / 24)) = max(1, floor((body + 12) / 24)); wick_down = 2 · wick_up. That is 1 px up / 2 px down for every body ≤ 35 |
| Placement | Body left edge xL = round_half_up(xc − body / 2), xc = slot centre; centre column = xL + floor((body − 1) / 2). A 1 px wick sits on the centre column, a 2 px wick on the centre column plus the column to its right. A 2 px horizontal stroke covers the price row and the row below |
| Caps | Wicks, stems, ticks and spans are pixel-snapped rectangles (butt caps) |

| s | round_half_up(0.7·s) | body | gap s − body | wick up / down | OHLC tick per side |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.55 (L0 floor, §2) | 2 | 2 | 0.55 | 1 / 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 / 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 / 2 | 2 |
| 5 | 4 | 3 (5 > s − 1) | 2 | 1 / 2 | 2 |
| 6 | 4 | 5 | 1 | 1 / 2 | 2 |
| 8 | 6 | 7 | 1 | 1 / 2 | 3 |
| 12 | 8 | 9 | 3 | 1 / 2 | 4 |
| 24 | 17 | 17 | 7 | 1 / 2 | 8 |
| 48 | 34 | 35 | 13 | 1 / 2 | 17 |
| 50 to 200 | 35 to 140 | 35 (cap) | 15 to 165 | 1 / 2 | 17 |

## 2. Density ladder and LOD

Levels and thresholds come from 26 §3.6, which is normative for the engine, with its ±15 % dead band:

| Level | Nominal | Moves coarser at | Moves finer at | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | s ≥ 3 | s < 2.55 | — | full geometry (§3) |
| L1 | 1.5 ≤ s < 3 | s < 1.275 | s ≥ 3.45 | L1 mark, per bar |
| L2 | s < 1.5 | — | s ≥ 1.725 | L2 mark, per px column |

The renderer draws the level the engine reports (`FrameStats.lodCandles`, 26 §8), including the frame-budget
step-down (26 §3.2, §11), and never recomputes it from s. **Why the ticket's 8 / 4 / 2 / 1 px bands are replaced:**
26 §3.6 defines three candle levels at 3 and 1.5 px, benchmark B2 (26 §13) gates flicker on them, and the decimator
emits exactly the L2 quantities, so marks changing at 8, 4, 2 and 1 px would need levels the engine does not have. The
five bands stay as the ladder's sample steps, each drawn with its level's mark; aggregation starts at s < 1.5, which
covers the acceptance criterion's "below one pixel" case.

| Ticket band | Level | Ladder step (sample s) | What draws |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 8 px | L0 | 1 (s = 12) | body 9, wicks 1 / 2 px |
| 4–8 px | L0 | 2 (s = 6) | body 5 (D1 forces 4 to 5), wicks 1 / 2 px |
| 2–4 px | L0 at s ≥ 3, L1 below | 3 (s = 3) | body 2, wicks 1 / 2 px; the 2 px up body is outlined top and bottom only |
| 1–2 px | L1 at s ≥ 1.5, L2 below | 4 (s = 1.5) | L1 mark |
| < 1 px | L2, from s < 1.5 (card "< 1.5 px") | 5 (s = 0.6) | L2 mark, chip `L2 agg 1.7 bars/px` |

**L1 mark (D4).** Per bar, two 1 px parts on the centre column: a high–low column in the bar's tint (42 % of its
direction colour, §8), and an open–close span in the direction colour: down solid; up dotted 1 on / 1 off (phase
anchored at the span's top row, so the first row always draws); unchanged (O = C) a 1 px `color.candle.wick` dot. No
wicks or bodies at L1. **L2 mark (D4).** The same two parts per 1 px logical column (DPR device px wide), built only
from 26 §3.6's surviving quantities (min-low, max-high, first-open, last-close, count): tint column min-low to
max-high; span first-open to last-close, solid if last-close < first-open, dotted if greater, a dot if equal; count 0
draws nothing. Column c takes the bars with c ≤ xc < c + 1; decimation never aggregates across a gap (§6). L1 and L2
share one mark, so crossing from L1 to L2 changes density, not meaning.

**LOD indicator chip (D10).**

| State | Chip text |
|---|---|
| L0 | hidden |
| L1 | `L1 bodies only` |
| L2 | `L2 agg n bars/px`, n = 1 / s rounded half up to one decimal (s = 0.6 gives `1.7`, s = 1.4 gives `0.7`, s = 0.05 gives `20.0`) |
| Equi- or delta-volume below L0 | append ` · width mapping off at this zoom` (D6) |
| Forming bar visible, L1 or L2 | append ` · last bar forming` (D5) |

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Order | Suffixes follow the table, e.g. `L2 agg 4.2 bars/px · width mapping off at this zoom · last bar forming`. Line, area and baseline have no L1 (§3), so their chip is hidden until L2 |
| Placement | Last item of the price pane's legend row (top-left DOM pane chrome, 26 §3.1), 8 px after the previous item. Rebuilding and partial-history banners use the separate top-of-pane slot, so they never hide it (E12-D01 findings, `docs/research/findings/E12-bar-modes.md` §4) |
| Typography | `type.num.sm` (JetBrains Mono 11 px) in `color.axis.text` on `color.surface.raised`, 1 px `color.border.default` border, 20 px tall, `space.inset.sm` (8 px) side padding, `radius.control`. Contrast 5.62:1 dark, 5.92:1 light |
| ARIA | Plain DOM text with an id; no role and no live region, because the level is ambient state and changes are not announced (E12-D01 findings §8). CMP-180's rolling `aria-label` appends the same string, built from the chip's text or via `aria-labelledby`, so the two cannot drift |
| SCR-046 | Field "Series LOD" shows the same string, or `L0 full detail` while the chip is hidden; source `FrameStats.lodCandles` and s |

## 3. Geometry per chart type

**Component cross-check (test plan).** CMP-181 `chartType` covers `candle`, `bar` (OHLC), `line`, `equi-volume`,
`delta-volume`; CMP-222 `value` adds area, baseline, Heikin-Ashi and hollow candles. CMP-181 `barMode` covers `time`,
`range`, `volume`, `tick`, `renko`, `pointfigure`; delta bars (`bar_type` `delta`, 23 §6) are offered by CMP-220 but
missing from it. Every type here is expressible by some component prop; widening CMP-181 goes to E12-D09 (§13). The
table defines L0; L1 and L2 follow §2 and §4.

| Type | L0 geometry | Weights | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Candle | Body = D1 width, open to close. Wick high to low on the centre column, drawn first, under the body. Up body: 1 px outline with tint inside; down body: solid | wick 1 px up / 2 px down (D2) | `color.candle.up` / `.down`; tint computed (§8) |
| Hollow candle | Candle geometry. Fill by close vs open (hollow = 1 px outline, interior unpainted). Colour and wick weight by close vs previous close (D3, §4) | as candle | as candle |

| Equi-volume | Candle geometry; width per bar from volume (below) | D2 on the bar width | as candle |
| Delta-volume | Candle geometry for price direction; width per bar from abs delta; signed delta sub-pane (below) | as equi-volume | as candle |
| Range, tick, volume, delta bars | Candle geometry on equal slots (non-time modes, below) | as candle | as candle |
| Renko | Bricks of D1 width on equal slots; height = brick size on the y scale; no wick | 1 px outline | up / down |
| Point & Figure | X column (up): two 1.5 px diagonals per box; O column (down): one 1.5 px ring per box (below) | 1.5 px | up / down |

| Rule | Definition |
|---|---|
| Up outline | 1 px on all four sides when the body is ≥ 3 px wide; a 2 px up body is outlined top and bottom only, so its tint stays visible |
| Base value | B = close of the first visible bar (recomputed when the visible range changes) or a user-set price (chart setting, E12-D05) |
| Line types | No L1 (there is no body to keep): L0 down to the L1→L2 threshold (1.5 px, same ±15 %), then L2, one vertex per px column at its last-close (an L2 survivor) |
| Doji (O = C, H > L) | Candle family: D1 width × 2 px body (1 px when s < 8) on the price row, 1 px wick, all `color.candle.wick`, never up or down colour; mirror word "unchanged". OHLC: 1 px stem and ticks in `color.candle.wick`. Hollow: the 2 px body keeps its D3 previous-close colour and wick weight |
| Zero-range (O = H = L = C) | max(body, 3) × 2 px in `color.candle.wick`; at L1/L2 a 1 px dot of the same colour, kept by the L2 min/max. Twice the 1 px grid hairline and neutral, so never read as grid or artefact |

**Equi-volume and delta-volume (D6, D7).** x is volume (equi-volume) or abs delta (delta-volume).

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ratio | r_i = x_i / m, m = max(median x, smallest non-zero x) over the loaded series, recomputed on load and at each bar close, never on pan. The floor only guards a zero median; if every x is 0, all bars take the 6 px floor |
| Base width (D6) | w0_i = clamp(round_half_up(14 · r_i), 6, 48); capped when 14 · r_i > 48 |
| Zoom | s stays the mean slot pitch (bar + 1 px gap). Scale k = (s − 1) / mean(w0) over the loaded series, recomputed with m. Edges e_i = e_0 + k · W_i + i, W_i = sum of w0 before bar i; bar i covers [round_half_up(e_i), round_half_up(e_{i+1}) − 1), so its width w_i (minimum 1) is followed by exactly 1 px of gap. At k = 1 (s = mean(w0) + 1), w_i = w0_i; the frames are drawn at k = 1 |
| Wicks | D2 on w_i: bars 36–48 px wide get 2 px up / 4 px down |
| Cap | Drawn at the cap width with a notch: an unpainted 5 × 3 px rectangle centred on the body's top edge (when w_i ≥ 7 and the body ≥ 4 px tall). Mirror appends `, width capped at 48 px (volume N x median)` (delta-volume `delta size N x median`), N = r_i to one decimal |
| Below L0 | D6's "mean width < 3 px" is s < 3 with §2 hysteresis: the mapping is suspended, bars take equal slots of pitch s with the L1/L2 marks, and the chip appends ` · width mapping off at this zoom`. The mean pitch is s either way, so the window does not jump |
| Axis | Non-time label rule below; note "X axis is volume-weighted, not time" or "X axis is delta-weighted, not time" |
| Delta data | Only bars built from trades carry delta (kline backfill has none); the earliest bar with delta is the series' left edge, marked as E12-D08's partial-history edge |
| Delta sub-pane (D7) | Takes D11's sub-pane slot (height, separator) and replaces the volume sub-pane in this mode. Signed d = buy − sell per bar at the price bar's x and width. Zero line 1 px `color.candle.wick`; y range [min(0, min d), max(0, max d)] over visible bars plus 16 px headroom at each end; positive bars rise (42 % tint, 1 px `color.candle.up` outline), negative bars hang (solid `color.candle.down`). Gutter: `0` at the zero line, unit (e.g. `delta BTC`) at the top |
| Delta labels | `+x.x` / `-x.x`: sign plus one decimal (`+1.2`), two significant digits below 0.1 (`-0.034`), K/M/B with one decimal above 1,000 (`+1.2K`); `type.num.xs` `color.axis.text`, 2 px beyond the bar end. L0 only, only when the bar's slot is ≥ 24 px, dropped on collision (§10); an omitted label's value is still in the mirror (§7) |
| Delta at L1 / L2 | L1: each bar a 1 px column, position carries the sign. L2: per px column a 1 px column from min(0, min d) to max(0, max d) in `color.candle.wick` |

**Non-time bar modes, P&F and the volume sub-pane.**

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Slots | Range, tick, volume and delta bars, Renko and P&F use equal slots of pitch s whatever the trade rate. Nothing is drawn for the time between bars, only data gaps (§6). Range-bar price jumps (24 §3.3 `gap_before`, an over-width bar) are not gaps. Renko and P&F keep their own geometry whatever the chart type; any other chart type draws its geometry on the bar mode's slots |
| Time axis | The series' contribution to CMP-189: a label marks a slot boundary with one UTC hh:mm:ss time (equi- and delta-volume on time bars use the interval's own format): open time at the bar's left boundary (range, tick, volume, delta bars; equi- and delta-volume), close time at the brick's right boundary (Renko), first-box time at the column's left boundary (P&F). Thinned left to right: a label is placed only if its centre is ≥ 64 px from the previous one |
| Axis note | Persistent, under the axis, left-aligned, `type.num.sm` `color.axis.text`: "X axis is bar index, not time" (range, tick, volume, delta bars), "X axis is brick index, not time" (Renko), "X axis is column index, not time" (P&F) |
| P&F boxes (D4) | barMode `pointfigure`. Column width = slot s; box height h = box size on the y scale. Per box a glyph rectangle body(s) × h, centred in the slot (the D1 gap separates columns), stacked first box to last. X (up): two 1.5 px corner-to-corner diagonals, `color.candle.up`; O (down): a 1.5 px ellipse ring inscribed in the rectangle, `color.candle.down`. Anti-aliased, not pixel-snapped (1.5 px is deliberately fractional) |
| P&F floor | Glyphs need body(s) ≥ 5 (s ≥ 6) and h ≥ 6. Below that at L0 each column is one body over its boxes with the candle encoding (X outlined with tint, O solid); at L1/L2 the L1/L2 mark (X dotted, O solid) |

Hollow candle (D3): fill = close vs open; colour and wick weight = close vs previous close.

| | close ≥ previous close | close < previous close |
|---|---|---|
| close ≥ open | hollow, up colour, 1 px wick | hollow, down colour, 2 px wick |
| close < open | solid, up colour, 1 px wick | solid, down colour, 2 px wick |

Without colour, fill × wick weight still gives four distinct marks. The first loaded bar has no previous close and uses
close vs open for both channels; below L0 hollow candles draw the standard L1/L2 mark. **Known limits:** a body under
3 px tall has no room for the tint and an L1/L2 span under 3 px cannot show the dots, so such a bar with no wick beyond
it carries direction only by colour and the mirror word (its price moved less than 3 px). The CVD-safe palette and the
inverted convention (§8) change hues only; no shape rule changes.

## 5. Forming bar (D5)

The last bar of a `bars` frame may carry `confirm: false` and must not be treated as closed (23 §6).

| Case | Treatment |
|---|---|
| L0 | Normal direction encoding (no tint change) plus a 1 px dashed outline, 2 on / 2 off, `color.candle.wick`, on the 1 px ring just outside the mark, so it also shows on solid down bodies. The mark is the body (candle family; equi- and delta-volume; range, tick, volume, delta bars), the open-to-close tick box (OHLC), the brick (Renko) or the column's box stack (P&F). Line, area, baseline: the segment into the forming close is dashed 2 on / 2 off in the line colour; the disc stays |
| CMP-226 chip | `forming mm:ss`, only for time intervals ≥ 1 m and while the countdown setting is on (SCR-031, E12-D05). In the price-axis gutter at the last close, directly under the last-price tag (above it when the tag is within 20 px of the pane bottom), not in a pane corner. CMP-226 owns its style, 1 Hz update and `aria-live="off"`; h:mm:ss when an hour or more remains |
| Interval < 1 m | Quiet rule: no chip, only the static outline and the mirror suffix. On a 1 s chart the treatment's only change per second is a 1 px dashed ring stepping one slot right: no motion, no canvas text, under 05 §7's three-flashes-per-second limit |
| Non-time modes | No chip here; CMP-226's non-time behaviour is E12-D09's decision |
| L1 / L2 | No outline; while the forming bar is in view the LOD chip appends ` · last bar forming` |
| Mirror | The accessible name ends `, forming (not closed)` |
| On close | The outline moves to the new last bar in the same frame; nothing animates (E12-D10 owns any transition and its reduced-motion form must keep this state). An amended confirmed bar (23 §8.2) is redrawn in place without an outline |

## 6. Gaps (D8)

A gap is a window with no data inside an otherwise loaded series. It is drawn as a gap and never interpolated
(US-CHART-011).

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Detection (time modes) | Consecutive bars with t_{i+1} − t_i > interval (open times). The gap spans k = (t_{i+1} − t_i) / interval − 1 missing windows, from t_i + interval to t_{i+1}: the builder's `gap_before` case (24 §3.3, an empty interval emits no bar). The missing slots keep their k · s px on the axis |
| Band (time modes) | Hatch of 45° 1 px lines in `color.border.default`, 10 px period; a 1 px dashed vertical (3 on / 3 off, `color.candle.wick`) at each edge |
| Label (time modes) | `no data hh:mm–hh:mm` (`type.num.sm`, `color.axis.text`; hh:mm:ss below 1 m; date added when the range crosses a UTC day), centred in the band, 8 px below the plot top. If wider than the band, it goes above the band instead: on a `color.surface.raised` plate at the plot's top edge, centred on the band and drawn over neighbouring bars, with a 1 px `color.axis.text` leader down into the band. Labels never overlap: on collision the later one is dropped; its band, edges and mirror stop remain. A gap under 3 px wide at the current zoom draws only one dashed vertical |
| Series | Line, area and baseline end at the last close before the gap and restart after it; fills close per segment; the base line stops at the band edges. Nothing the series draws crosses a gap |
| Non-time modes | No band, because x is not time: a 1 px dashed vertical (3 on / 3 off) on the slot boundary between the two bars, and `gap hh:mm–hh:mm` in the time-axis gutter centred on it (thinned with axis labels, with priority over them). Source: a data-coverage gap between the two bars (recorder gap list, 24 §13.3; replay coverage gaps, 26 §10) |
| vs E12-D08 | The partial-history edge is the series' left boundary where older history is not loaded or not available: one solid 1 px vertical plus `history starts hh:mm`, never hatched, data on one side only. A gap always has data on both sides |
| Not a gap | A WS sequence gap (resync, stale badge, 26 §11) or a rebuild; only data still missing after resync is drawn. Each gap is one arrow-key stop (§7) |

## 7. Accessibility

**DOM mirror text (CMP-181, D9).** Numbers use the instrument's price and quantity precision with the locale's
separators. `<UTC time>` is the bar open time: hh:mm UTC, hh:mm:ss UTC below 1 m, the date at 1 D and above; the date
is prefixed whenever it differs from that of the previously announced bar. Direction is one word: "up", "down" or
"unchanged". N is the 1-based bar index in the loaded series.

| Type | Accessible name of the focused bar |
|---|---|
| Candle, OHLC, equi-volume | `<UTC time>, open O, high H, low L, close C, volume V, up` |
| Hollow candle | as candle; the word is close vs previous close, the one relation the numbers do not give |
| Heikin-Ashi | `<UTC time>, open O, high H, low L, close C, volume V, Heikin-Ashi close X (derived, up)`: real OHLCV first, X rounded to tick size, the word is the drawn HA direction |
| Line, area | `<UTC time>, close C` |
| Baseline | `<UTC time>, close C, above base B by D` (`below base`, or `at base B` when C = B) |
| Delta-volume | candle text + `, delta +D (buy B, sell S)`, buy = (V + D) / 2, sell = (V − D) / 2: buy 3.84 and sell 2.63 give `delta +1.21` |
| Range, tick, volume, delta bars | `bar N, opened <UTC time>, open O, high H, low L, close C, volume V, up` |
| Renko | `bar N, closed <UTC time>, open O, close C, up` |
| Point & Figure | `column N, X, from P1 to P2, boxes K` (`O` for O columns; P1 first box, P2 last) |
| Doji / zero-range | candle text with `unchanged` / `<UTC time>, no range, price P, volume V` |
| Gap stop | `gap, K bars missing, hh:mm to hh:mm UTC`; non-time `gap, no data hh:mm:ss to hh:mm:ss UTC` |

Suffixes come last, in order: the cap note (§3), then `, forming (not closed)`. **Why Heikin-Ashi announces real
prices first (D9):** crosshair readouts, drawings and order lines anchor to real time and price (US-CHART-002), so the
numbers a user hears must equal what the axis shows and what could be traded. HA is a smoothing aid; its close is
derived and must never be mistaken for a tradable price, so it comes second and is labelled "derived".

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Keyboard | CMP-181, 26 §3.11: ← / → one bar (a gap is one stop); Shift+← / → ten bars; Home / End the first / last bar of the visible range. The mirror is windowed to the visible range (05 §6.1) and never aggregated: at L1/L2 an arrow still steps one real bar (SCR-045's aggregated rows are E12-D07's) |
| Announcements | A focus move reads the bar's text once, via focus or the polite live region (26 §12: at most one per 250 ms, last state always announced), never both. Changes to a focused forming bar and bar closes are not pushed; the current text is read on the next move or the screen reader's read-focus command. LOD changes are not announced (§2) |
| Chart label | CMP-180's rolling `aria-label` appends the LOD chip string while the chip is visible; nothing else from this spec |
| Reduced motion | Nothing here animates, flashes or depends on timing; with `prefers-reduced-motion` (05 §7) or the engine's motion flags off every state is identical. CMP-226's 1 Hz text change is not motion |
| Forming state | In the accessible name, never only visual (§5) |

## 9. Contrast and CVD method

WCAG 2.x relative luminance (the `docs/design/_tools/contrast_check.py` helpers) on token hex values. Chart ink and
meaningful lines need ≥ 3:1, text ≥ 4.5:1 (05 §5).

| Pair | Dark: on #0B0E11 / on canvas #11151A | Light: on canvas #F6F7F9 / on #FFFFFF |
|---|---|---|
| `color.candle.up` | 7.87 / 7.45 | 5.03 / 5.39 |
| `color.candle.down` | 4.94 / 4.68 | 4.85 / 5.20 |
| `color.candle.wick` (neutral marks) | 6.29 / 5.96 | 6.36 / 6.81 |
| Line stroke (proposed values) | 7.45 / 7.05 | 6.30 / 6.75 |
| `color.axis.text` on `color.surface.raised` (chip text) | 5.62 | 5.92 |
| Up vs down | 1.59 (luminance 0.377 vs 0.218) | 1.04 |
| Up tint vs canvas; down vs up tint | 2.30; 2.04 | 1.84; 2.64 |

All ink reaches 3:1. Up vs down is 1.59:1 dark and 1.04:1 light: in greyscale the light up and down colours are the
same grey, so §4's shape channels carry direction. The tint is a fill; the 1 px outline carries the edge at full
contrast. Grid (1.15:1) and `color.border.default` (1.50:1: hatch, separator, chip border) are decorative; their
meaning is always also carried by a ≥ 3:1 line or by text. **Light plot ground drawn in the round-2 light frames:
TBD-by-artist.** The token is #F6F7F9 (numbers above); round 1 drew #F6F8FA, where values differ by ≤ 0.04 (up 5.06,
down 4.88, wick 6.40).

**CVD.** `docs/design/_tools/cvd_sim.py`: greyscale = Rec. 601 luma; deuteranopia = Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes
(2009) 100 % matrix; protanopia and tritanopia = the Machado 2009 matrices, added to the script by this PR (tooling
change noted in the PR). Produced: per-frame greyscale and deuteranopia for candle L0 dark and light, candle 2560,
OHLC, hollow (regenerated for D3), delta-volume, Renko, range and tick; the greyscale and deuteranopia sheets for HA,
line, area, baseline, equi-volume, gap, forming, doji/zero-range, density ladder, P&F, the LOD close-up and line light;
and the protan/tritan sheet for candle L0 dark and light. Round 1 (candle family, pre-redraw) observed up and down
separable by fill (tint vs solid) and outline, with the 1 px vs 2 px wick visible but faint at 1×. The orchestrator
records the observed round-2 results here after viewing the sheets. Coverage note: the test plan asks for protanopia
and tritanopia of every type at three densities; this round covers candle L0 (§13).

## 10. Renderability and performance

Bounds: SCR-030 ≥ 58 fps at 2560×1440 with 100k bars; 26 §4 draws candles in 2 instanced draw calls (per-instance x,
openY, closeY, highY, lowY, colourIdx) and targets ≤ 18 draw calls per pane; 26 §7 budgets 28 B per bar.

| Rule | Draw approach | Per-bar cost, flag |
|---|---|---|
| Bodies and wicks, L0 | Candle program: wick batch then body batch, one instanced quad per bar each; widths and weights are uniforms from s; direction, doji, forming and capped are flag bits packed with `colourIdx` | 2 quads, 2 draw calls, as 26 §4 |
| Up outline and tint | Fragment shader paints the outer 1 px ring in the up colour and the inside in the tint; tints are opaque palette entries computed on theme change | Per-bar alpha blending replaced by opaque colour |
| OHLC | One instanced draw with a 3-quad template (stem + two ticks) | 12 vertices per bar vs 8: flagged for the Architect's vertex budget |
| L1 mark | The same two batches: tint column in the wick batch, span in the body batch; dotted span = `mod(row − top, 2)` | 2 quads, as L0 (26 §3.6 note, §13) |
| L2 mark | Decimator output, one instance pair per logical px column | Constant in bar count (≤ plot width) |
| Forming outline | One extra instance in the body batch, dashed from the perimeter coordinate | 1 per series |
| Cap notch | Flag bit; the body fragment shader leaves 5 × 3 px unpainted | 0 extra |
| Equi- / delta-volume widths | Per-instance W_i (f32, exact integers) and w0_i (u8); k, e_0 and the first visible bar's W_0 and i_0 are uniforms, and the vertex shader computes edges relative to them (as 26 §3.3 does for time), so zoom changes uniforms only (TRANSFORM, 26 §3.1) | +5 B per bar vs 28 B: flagged. A change of m at bar close rewrites W from the first changed bar (≤ 0.5 MB at 100k bars, ≤ 0.5 MB/s at 1 s intervals): flagged; skip when no w0 changes |
| Delta labels | SDF glyph runs (26 §3.7) in the pane's text batch; L0 only, slot ≥ 24 px, collision-thinned; otherwise omitted, value kept in the mirror | Per-bar text, flagged and bounded: ≤ plot width / 24 labels, 1 draw call |
| Volume sub-pane | Instanced quads (`bar` program), 1 draw call; per-column max at L1/L2 | Max per column is not in 26 §3.6's surviving set: flagged |
| Delta sub-pane, L2 | Per-column min and max of delta | Not in the surviving set: flagged |
| Line, area, baseline | `line` program instanced segments (26 §4), 1 draw call; area and baseline fills one blended triangle strip per segment, baseline colour split per fragment against a uniform y(B) | Blending is per pane, not per bar, bounded by fill area. Close spikes inside one L2 column are dropped: flagged (keeping them needs per-column min/max close) |
| Dashed lines | Distance-along-line varying with a pattern LUT (26 §3.10) | — |
| Gap band | One quad per gap, hatch in the fragment shader; labels as collision-thinned glyph runs | Per gap; a quiet 1 s chart can have many: instanced, labels bounded |
| P&F glyphs | One instanced quad per box, analytic SDF X or ring in the fragment shader, 1 draw call | Instances = visible boxes (≤ plot area / (s · h), glyphs only ≥ 6 px): flagged |
| Chips, axis notes | DOM pane chrome (26 §3.1) | 0 GPU |

The series adds 2 draw calls per pane (wicks, bodies), plus 1 text call when delta or gap labels exist; no rule adds a
draw call per bar. One L2 column per logical px halves the instance count at DPR 2.

## 11. Security and privacy

- **No order affordance:** nothing in the series layer places, amends, arms or cancels an order; marks, chips and
  mirror nodes carry no order action. Chart trading is the separate armed surface (CMP-229).
- **No account-derived colouring:** series colour, fill, weight and outline come only from market data (price
  direction, volume, delta) and the theme. Position side, P&L, order state or any other account attribute must never
  change the series, so a screenshot of it reveals nothing about an account.
- No analytics events originate in the series layer, and engineering must not instrument it per frame.

**Clarifications added in this rewrite, beyond D1–D12, for the orchestrator to confirm:**
- **Equi-/delta-volume zoom:** k = (s − 1) / mean(w0) scales the D6 widths. Without it, absolute 6–48 px widths can
  never meet D6's "mean width < 3 px". At k = 1 the widths are exactly D6's.
- **Zero-median guard:** m = max(median, smallest non-zero value).
- **Wide equi-/delta-volume bars:** bars 36–48 px wide get 2 / 4 px wicks from D2's formula, where D4's wording
  says 1 / 2 px.
- **Line types:** they have no L1, and their forming segment is dashed.

- **Hollow candles:** below L0 they use the standard mark.
- **Volume columns:** direction is real close vs real open.
- **Non-time labels:** the label sits on the bar's left boundary (Renko: the right boundary).
- **Gaps:**

  - labels are collision-thinned;
