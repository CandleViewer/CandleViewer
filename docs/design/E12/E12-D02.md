
The toolbar, both axes, the drawings layer and the trading-overlay layer stay mounted through every E12 state;
only the series data swaps, in one frame (`setData`, 26 §9). Values in every sketch are illustrative. This is a requirement for engineering (E12-S11),
not an aesthetic: it is what makes ≤200 ms possible and what prevents the flash of an empty chart. The strip
(S) overlays the top edge of the price area and never reflows the plot; the legend shifts down one row while it
shows. Order and position lines belong to another epic, stay live through every E12 state and are never shed
(06 §4.4). No control on any E12 surface places an order.

**Semantics (CMP-220 contract, E12-D01 §8).** The trigger is a menu button (`aria-haspopup="menu"`) named
"Interval: 15 minutes" or "Bar mode: Volume, 50 BTC per bar". The menu (`role="menu"`) holds three `role="group"`
sections separated by `role="separator"`: "Favourites", "Time", and "Bar modes: the x-axis is not time". Entries
are `menuitemradio` with `aria-checked` on the active one. A mode's one-line description (E12-D01 §3), its current
parameter and any "needs recorded prints" reason are its accessible description, never visual-only text. A menu
may hold only items, groups and separators, so each parameter editor is rendered inside the same popover but
outside the menu subtree: a `role="group"` named "Volume per bar" holding a `spinbutton` whose label carries the
unit ("Volume per bar, BTC") and whose message is linked by `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`.

**Keyboard path.** With the chart focused: `Alt+I` (proposed, OQ-4) or `Tab` to the toolbar, roving to the
interval button, `Enter`. In the menu: `↓`/`↑` move across all groups, `Home`/`End` jump, `1..9` choose a
favourite, `Enter`/`Space` commit an entry with its shown parameter, `→` opens the parameter editor (focus in the
field, value selected); `Esc` closes without change and returns focus to the trigger, and `Tab` closes without
change and moves on, as the ARIA menu pattern does. In the editor: type, or `↑`/`↓` to step, `PageUp`/`PageDown`
for ten steps; `Enter` commits mode and parameter and closes the menu; an invalid `Enter` keeps focus in the field
and announces the message once; `Esc` or `←` returns to the mode row. Committing returns focus to the trigger; a build that starts then follows D-11.

**Parameter fields.** Units from the 22 `/market/bars` table; bounds from `bars/limits.py` and `bars/spec.py`.
Defaults on first use are E12-D01 §3's designed values; afterwards the last value used for that mode.

| Mode   | Field label (API unit)                    | Bound the client checks                            | Message shown below the field                                            |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tick   | Trades per bar                            | 100 to 1 000 000                                   | "Enter 100 to 1 000 000 trades."                                         |
| Volume | Volume per bar, in the base asset (`BTC`) | ≥10 lot steps; whole numbers in the shipped parser | "At least 1 BTC per bar (10 lot steps, whole numbers)."                  |
| Range  | Range per bar, ticks                      | 2 to 100 000                                       | "Enter 2 to 100 000 ticks."                                              |
| Delta  | Signed volume per bar, base asset (`BTC`) | as Volume                                          | as Volume, with "signed volume"                                          |
| Renko  | Brick size, ticks                         | 2 to 100 000; `atr:` refused                       | "ATR bricks are not available yet. Enter a brick size in ticks."         |
| Custom | Interval, number plus `s`/`m`/`h`/`d`/`w` | the served ladder until OQ-1                       | "Use a number and a unit: s, m, h, d or w." / "7m is not available yet." |

**Favourites.** The default ladder is time-only and ascending so a higher digit means a longer bar: `1` 1m, `2` 3m,
`3` 5m, `4` 15m, `5` 30m, `6` 1h, `7` 4h, `8` 1D, `9` 1W (proposed, confirm in E12-D04). Any slot may hold a
non-time entry such as `volume:50`, because `PaneIntervalCode` spells every bar type. The editor: `↑`/`↓` move
between slots, `Enter` assigns from the menu groups, `Alt+↑`/`Alt+↓` reorder, `Del` clears, "Add current" fills the
focused slot; `Enter` saves and `Esc` discards.

**States.** Symbol with no recorded prints: non-time rows and (after OQ-1) sub-minute rows are disabled with "Needs
recorded prints: none yet". Shallow recording keeps them enabled and F-BAR-5 marks the boundary. Flags off: rows
absent, never offered and then failed (E12-S12).

yields to an open popover or an in-progress drawing (the keymap's existing `Esc`), innermost first.

**RBAC.** Every role changes symbols, intervals, modes and chart types for its own view. `Record this symbol` is
an owner CTA (M-023, owner only per `12-sitemap.md`); managers and viewers see an explanatory sentence. The
diagnostics link (SCR-046) is owner-only; others see the copyable correlation id. No E12 surface places an order.

## 12. Heuristic evaluation (heuristic)

Confidence basis: a walkthrough of the flows against the merged contracts, ADR-0033 and the shipped `bars/` code.
Nothing was observed with users. Sev is the severity of what remains open (3 = major, 2 = minor); "—" means
the design resolves it.

| Heuristic                      | Finding                                                                                                                                                                    | Sev | Resolution                                |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- | ----------------------------------------- |
| H1 Visibility of system status | Without G-2 the strip cannot show a true percentage; an indeterminate wait of up to 10 s reads as a hang.                                                                  | 3   | Elapsed time plus D-5 phases; OQ-2        |
| H2 Match with the real world   | US-CHART-003 speaks of "contracts"; the API unit is base-asset volume. E12-D01 §3 labels delta in ticks; the API says signed volume.                                       | 2   | API units (D-3); OQ-9                     |
| H3 User control and freedom    | Cancel and `Esc` restore at once; `Esc` in SCR-041 restores both values; a finished build is undone by choosing the previous setting, which is cached.                     | —   | D-1, D-6                                  |
| H4 Consistency and standards   | 05 §3.2, 17 §7 and the generated keymap disagree on several chart keys; ARIA menus forbid text fields inside them.                                                         | 2   | Editor outside the menu (§4); OQ-4        |
| H5 Error prevention            | Bounds checked before any request; unservable modes hidden; unrecorded windows known in advance from `/market/data-coverage`.                                              | —   | D-3, D-4                                  |
| H6 Recognition over recall     | Digits shown on favourites; current parameter and unit on every mode row; recent values in the editor.                                                                     | —   | §4                                        |
| H7 Flexibility and efficiency  | No typed interval entry on the chart: digits are the `1..9` presets (typing "15" would switch twice) and letters such as `F` are taken, so a custom value takes two steps. | 2   | `Alt+I` then the custom field; OQ-1, OQ-4 |
| H8 Minimalist design           | One strip message at a time; one parameter field in SCR-031 instead of four.                                                                                               | —   | D-7                                       |
| H9 Recover from errors         | Every failure keeps the chart and names cause and next step; SCR-048 carries a copyable correlation id.                                                                    | —   | D-1, §8                                   |
| H10 Help and documentation     | Mode descriptions in CMP-069; "Learn more" on SCR-047; copy owned downstream.                                                                                              | —   | E12-D08, E16-D05                          |

| Trading heuristic                         | Finding                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T1 Sub-250 ms perception loop (10 §10)    | The pending mark acknowledges a switch within one frame; the held series keeps streaming during a build, so the market read never freezes for up to 10 s.      |
| T5 Honest uncertainty                     | Where order flow begins, whether bars are aggregated, and whether the x-axis is time are stated in text, never inferred (C-2.14).                              |
| T9 Equal-status alternatives              | Each state has a description, data-cursor or SCR-045 expression; nothing is canvas-only.                                                                       |
| Latency and staleness                     | A stalled feed marks the forming bar stale in text (E12-S10); the countdown runs from the offset-corrected exchange clock.                                     |
| Error prevention for money-moving actions | No order control on any E12 surface; order and position lines stay live through every state (06 §4.4).                                                         |
| Keyboard-first and hotkey conflicts       | Every flow completes without a pointer; new bindings wait on the keymap linter (OQ-4).                                                                         |
| Reconnect continuity                      | A disconnect cancels the build it started (ADR-0033); the previous series stays, frozen with its last-good time, and the strip offers a retry after reconnect. |
| Multi-monitor and DPI                     | LOD buckets follow device pixels, so moving a window to another monitor can change the chip's level; it changes silently, with no announcement.                |
| Owner vs manager RBAC                     | The record CTA is owner-only in the UI, but managers hold `recording:write` on the server (OQ-5).                                                              |
| Demo vs Live                              | Market data and bar series are the same in both; every frame keeps the header's environment badge.                                                             |

**Comparison** (public sources and the repo's feature matrix, `docs/research/20-feature-matrix.md` §A):

| Product            | Bar types and entry, as documented                                                                                                                                                      | Where CandleViewer is weaker                                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| TradingView        | Typed interval entry on a focused chart, seconds included ("5S"), per third-party guides [1]; seconds, range, tick and P&F gated by plan, no volume bars (matrix rows 18–24)            | No seconds or custom intervals until G-1; no typed entry                       |
| Sierra Chart       | Bar period types: days-minutes-seconds (to milliseconds), volume, trades, delta volume, six range definitions, reversal, four Renko variants, P&F, all from its local intraday file [2] | Far fewer variants: one range definition, fixed Renko, no reversal or P&F      |

| Quantower          | Aggregations: time, tick, range, Renko, volume, Heikin-Ashi, P&F, line break, Kagi, reversal [4] (search summary, medium confidence)                                                    | Fewer types; HA is a chart type here, so HA bars cannot carry footprint (D-10) |
| Exchange terminals | Bybit's web UI has no footprint or order-flow panes (`10-personas.md` §1)                                                                                                               | —                                                                              |

and the tape (row 152), which E12 does not cover.
