### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity (RULED BQ-06).** **Blocking:** no compliant path in the merged text for a `05` clause or a WCAG 2.2 A or AA criterion; a state carried by colour alone with no text or glyph carrier; an announcement with no string and no politeness; **a value announced per tick, or an announcing row with no stated maximum rate**; a pointer-only or hover-only interaction (`05`:35); a G-T2 miss on a gated control; a focus destination unstated in R369's precise sense (no merged text **and** no `05` rule yields it); **a fact the canvas shows that no table row and no mirror text carries** (`T a11y`'s P0 case; `05`:233's parity task). A destination that `05`:96 yields, or a restoration after an unmount, is a **Minor** that makes it explicit (R374). **Major:** an at-risk clause; a sub-AA pair whose words or glyph carry the state, with the DS change request named; a T2 over- or under-conversion; a key with no registry row at its story's Ready gate. **Minor:** wording, cadence, order, a destination made explicit. **Info:** a row recorded for a consumer.
- **R-2 Findings and their closing (RULED BQ-06).** An F row names the merged sentence, the replacement, one owner (the spec and its area: `E12-D0n · area/chart-engine`, or an input's owner such as `E06-D03 · area/chart-engine`; never a person or a lane) and one target: **E12-D12 (#313)**, which lifts the finding's version into the hand-off, plus the consuming story; for E12-D08 the target may also be its own open PR. Every F row carries a **`G-T2`** cell (`T2 · 2a` / `2b` / `sentence` · `validity` · `availability` · `n/a`), a **`Focus`** cell (`kept · <element>` · `moves to · <element>` · `restored to · <element>` · `returns to · <trigger>` · `n/a`) and a **`Rate`** cell (`never` · `once per <episode | entry | change | press | action>` · `≤ N per <s> per <region | key>` · `n/a`); the tool fails a row with any of the three empty. **The hold:** a Blocking F row stamps E12-D12 (#313) "cannot be Done until fixed or an exception is recorded on #312" and the consuming story's Ready gate. **Closed parents are never reopened** (D03, D04, D05, D06 and D07 are closed); `T`'s "reopen" wording is C-k. **Accepted risk** (`T AC5`): a row with an owner (the spec's area), a reason and the consumer that carries it; the "follow-up ticket" is a comment on that consumer under the filing freeze, and the decision record is the orchestrator's under the delegation (the CDO substitution, `T Adapt`). No row is silently closed.

### 5.3 Heikin-Ashi, stated once (`T AC4`; RULED BQ-08)

**Real OHLCV is read first.** On a Heikin-Ashi chart the mirror's full summary is the real open, high, low, close and volume, then "Heikin-Ashi close 64,206 (derived), up"; the short form is the real close, "14:12, 64,232" (`D06`:351, :359; `15`:2291). **Why:** the price axis, the crosshair readout, drawings and order lines use real prices, so the numbers a user hears equal the numbers a user would trade (`D06`:167, :359). **The other values are reachable in SCR-045:** `HA close` and `HA dir` in the auto tier, and `HA open`, `HA high` and `HA low` in the extended tier, one step away in the column picker; every HA cell's accessible name ends "(derived)" (`D07`:195, :199). On a non-time mode Heikin-Ashi is not drawn: the decision callout offers "Switch to time bars" and the mirror reads the mode's own rows (`D06`:167; `15`:2851). The same rule stands in the table: real values lead, the HA columns follow, labelled derived (`D07`:199). One finding: F-02 (Minor).

### 7.2 The combined rule (PROPOSED default 1)

`11`:1035 caps live-region announcements at two per second and names the cursor. Read for the band: **the chart panel's polite writes — the cursor region (RG-04) and the panel region (RG-05) together — stay at or below two in any one second.** The shell pair (RG-06) and the table's region (RG-10) keep their own numbers (BG-09, BG-10). Without the rule a held cursor key (2 per s) plus one build start puts three writes in one second: that is F-01.

### 7.3 The collision order (`T AC3`)

When two writes would fall inside one second, in this order: (1) an assertive row is never coalesced or dropped (`05`:148; R369's rider) — none comes from the band; (2) a user-action confirmation (a switch result, a build's start, a cancel, a completion, an overrun, a refusal) is never dropped and is serialised at least 500 ms after the write before it; (3) a state entry or exit (the LOD chip, a correction) waits behind class 2 and is dropped only by a newer write of the same fact; (4) the cursor's short or full form: **latest wins, the earlier one is dropped, the trailing flush is kept.** **So the one dropped is always the older cursor text.** A bar close writes nothing (Q07-36), so the worst case of `T AC3` is the held cursor plus a one-shot, and the rule trims the cursor, not the one-shot.

### 7.4 The worst case, simulated by the tool

The tool runs 60 s of: a bar close every 1 s, a cursor key held throughout, a build started at 10 s, overrun at 30 s and cancelled at 45 s, an LOD entry at 20 s and exit at 40 s. It asserts: the maximum writes in any 1 s window across RG-04 and RG-05 = 2 <!-- audit:sim.max-per-1s=2 --> (≤ 2); every dropped write is class 4 (0 <!-- audit:sim.dropped-non-class4=0 --> non-class-4 drops); every class-2 write is spoken (3 <!-- audit:sim.class2-spoken=3 --> of 3 <!-- audit:sim.class2-total=3 -->); the trailing flush after release is spoken (kept <!-- audit:sim.flush-kept=kept -->).

The Announcements panel of SCR-117 lowers any class and never raises one above this table (`05`:149; `14`:2448). The opt-in 10 s line is a setting there whose key is unnamed (G-11; `D09` DR-3).
