## 1. Purpose, method, locked inputs and the rules stated once

### 1.1 Purpose

Four moments change what is under the trader (`T` Context): (a) the interval or bar-mode switch, (b) the bar close, (c) a rebuild's start, progress, completion and cancel, (d) an LOD threshold crossed while zooming. For each, this spec fixes the property animated (if any), the duration and easing tokens, the reduced-motion equivalent and its carrier, the per-frame budget and the drop rule.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

A desk spec from merged public text, every cite re-read at the base. Nothing was run; every evaluative sentence is `(heuristic)`. The default is less (`T` Technical notes): a transition is kept only where the static design cannot carry the information, and here it always can.

### 1.3 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 The loop's clock.** Any canvas motion lives in the render loop, integrates real `dt` and never a frame count, so a dropped frame never changes the outcome (`26`:130); no CSS transition ever touches canvas content (`T` Technical notes).
- **R-2 The retained frame is a state rule, not an animation.** On a switch the previous series stays drawn, labelled as the previous setting, until the new series paints in one frame (`D02`:25 D-1; `26`:738; `14`:1049). Engineering never implements a fade from blank (`T` Technical notes).
- **R-3 Interruptible.** A pan, a zoom, a key that moves the view, a second switch or a motion-mode change to `reduce` ends anything in flight where it is (`E11D06`:81 D5).
- **R-4 No information in motion.** Every moment's end state carries its meaning in text or a static mark, identical in both motion modes (MR-3, `16`:208; `E05D03`:118–120).
- **R-5 Dropped, never queued.** A transition that would start in a frame already over budget is skipped and its end state applied in that frame; it is never queued, stretched or replayed, and the end state is identical whether it played or not (§8).
- **R-6 Safety messages first.** An error, a stale-data indicator or a rebuild failure appears in the frame it is known, in both motion modes, and no transition delays or covers it (`T` Security; `E11D06`:91 D15). The two security rules, R-6 and R-7, are stated here once and cited elsewhere (BQ-19).
- **R-7 No account state in motion.** No transition indicates order or position state; motion carries no data and no authority (`T` Security; `E11D06`:96 D20).

## 3. Moment (a): the interval or bar-mode switch (M-01)

### 3.1 The decision

The switch is a series replacement in one frame in both modes, never a transition (`E11D06`:348–351; `D02`:25 D-1). The previous series stays drawn and labelled as the previous setting until the new series paints (R-2); the pill names the target with a pending mark, and after D02's strip threshold the strip says both (`D02`:25). A locked price scale keeps its range through the swap; an auto scale re-fits on the first new frame, with D-15's armed-trading exception (`D02`:39). The new mode is announced once, politely, by D11's scenario 6, never during a transition (`D11`:380–390).

### 3.2 The rejected variant and its budget

A crossfade of the full series needs both renderings in one frame, an extra series pass, and blends up and down bodies into false candles (`E11D06`:90, :99). Against the switch budgets it has no room: a `motion.duration.normal` fade spends 200<!--=BUD:fade.ms--> ms of the 200<!--=BUD:switch.ms--> ms cached-switch budget (`11`:975), 0<!--=BUD:switch.room--> ms left, and is twice the 100<!--=BUD:type.ms--> ms chart-type budget (`11`:915). Per `T` AC, the variant is replaced by the instant swap with the previous frame retained; its measured cost is a §12 item (O-1), recorded here as rejected by arithmetic, not by measurement.

## 4. Moment (b): the bar close (M-05; D12-G2)

### 4.1 The decision and the threshold

The close is a one-frame change in both modes: the forming ring leaves the closed bar and is drawn on the new forming bar in the same frame, the countdown reads "closed" and rolls to the next bar instantly (`E11D06`:214), and SCR-045's row for the bar changes its state word (`D06`:264). **Stated numerically (`T` AC):** 0<!--=BUD:close.ms--> ms at every bar close, time and non-time bars; the ticket's figure, 1<!--=BUD:close.min.s--> s, is its shortest interval and is covered by the same 0 (`E11D06`:317–319), so no transition exists to overlap the next close. At a 1 s interval the ring moves once per second, a change of position and not a flash (D11's count, `D11`:710).

### 4.2 The rejected variants

(b) A `motion.duration.fast` opacity settle on the closed bar on intervals of a minute and longer: it would spend 100<!--=DUR:fast--> ms, 10<!--=BUD:close.fast.pct--> % of a 1 s close, and carries nothing the ring, the word "closed" and the table row do not; rejected `(heuristic)`. (c) A bound of `min(fast, interval / 10)`: the same cost at 1 s and no information; rejected. Neither was measured (O-2).

### 4.3 What carries the close without motion

The accessible name of the focused bar and the SCR-045 table row carry it in text (`T` a11y; E12-D06 §13, E12-D07); focus never moves on a close (`D13`:263 MO-3).

## 5. Moment (c): rebuild progress, completion and cancel (M-06, M-07, M-08)

### 5.1 The phases (M-06, confirmed)

D08 §7 stands as merged: nothing but the pill's pending mark until the strip's threshold; strip text after it; the bar only with D08-G1's total, from D08's determinate threshold; updates at 2<!--=BUD:progress.hz--> Hz, inside `T`'s ≤ 4 Hz (`D08`:229–248). Phase changes are cuts; the glyph is static (`D08`:248).

### 5.2 The fill between two updates (M-07; D12-G2)

CMP-023's catalogued recipe applies: `transform: scaleX` per sample over `motion.duration.normal` with the easing `motion.easing.standard`, steps under reduce (`15`:288, `16` §7.2's progress row, D08 §7.2), kept PROPOSED to its owner E42-D04 (§13); the sibling row is E47-D08's I-38 (`E47D08`:178, if #5444 merges as is). The arithmetic: at 2 Hz a sample arrives every 500<!--=BUD:progress.period.ms--> ms; the 200<!--=DUR:normal--> ms tween ends 300<!--=BUD:progress.slack.ms--> ms before the next, so tweens never overlap or queue. It lives in the DOM strip, not the canvas: 0<!--=DUR:instant--> ms of engine frame time (§8). The "{n} %" text and `aria-valuetext` carry the value in both modes, so the reduce steps lose nothing.

### 5.3 Completion, cancel and the restore (M-08, confirmed)

The new series paints in one frame (`D02`:25 D-1). On cancel the previous series is already on screen, so the restore is immediate, with no refetch and no empty frame (`D08`:258); the pill and the field revert in the same frame and the outcome notice appears as a cut (`E11D06`:91 D15). Completion never moves focus (`D08`:260–262). Announcements: start, completion, cancel and refusal once each, politely, progress only on demand (`D08`:292–297; `D11`:410–416).

## 6. Moment (d): the LOD crossing while zooming (M-02)

### 6.1 The decision

As invisible as possible in geometry, as explicit as possible in the indicator (`T` Technical notes): the marks change in one frame in both modes, no crossfade (`E11D06`:90 D14); the aggregation indicator's words appear, change and leave instantly and are announced politely once on entry and once on exit, never per change (`D06`:254). The ±15 % dead band is the anti-flicker mechanism (`26`:244): with the candle tiers at 3 and 1.5 px of bar spacing, a tier demotes below 2.55<!--=BUD:lod.l0.down--> px and promotes above 3.45<!--=BUD:lod.l0.up--> px, and 1.275<!--=BUD:lod.l1.down--> / 1.725<!--=BUD:lod.l1.up--> px for the lower tier, so a slow zoom across a threshold steps once.

### 6.2 Inertia meeting the LOD change (coordinated with E11-D06)

No inertia parameter is set here; every one is E11-D06's (`E11D06`:77–81, D1–D5, PROPOSED there). The interaction only: during a coast the LOD tier is evaluated per frame against the dead band and applied as a cut, so a decelerating coast crosses each tier once; a motion-mode change to `reduce` stops the coast where it is (D5), and the keyboard's discrete steps cross tiers one step at a time (`E11D06`:100 D24).

## 7. Reduced motion (each moment; `05` §7)

**Which source wins.** The in-app override (SCR-117 → Reduced motion: system / on / off, and `Ctrl+Shift+M`) wins over the OS `prefers-reduced-motion` in both directions; `system` defers to the OS (`16`:218; `E05D03`:206–220); the engine reads the resolved `motion` from `candleviewer:themechange` (`16`:169) and, under `reduce`, stops inertia and any canvas flash (`E05D03`:247–251). **The two reduce forms** (BQ-09, read as E05-D03's "tiers"): remove, the end state at frame 0 (M-01 … M-06, M-08: already cuts, identical); substitute a static carrier (M-07: the fill steps, the text carries) (`E05D03`:81–94). **Canvas calm** (E47-D08 §2's third tier, `disable_canvas_animation` on with motion `safe`; `E47D08`:130, if #5444 merges as is): every moment is identical there, because none animates on the canvas. **The walk** (`T` AC, a reviewer with animation disabled): bar close, rebuild completion and LOD change each remain detectable through §2's Carrier column; nothing is discoverable only by having seen an animation (R-4). Auto-moving content keeps `05` §7's pause rule (any auto-moving content over 5 s has a pause control).

## 8. Budgets and the drop rule (R-5; `T` AC "dropped under load")

The frame budget is 16.7<!--=BUD:frame.ms--> ms at 60 fps (`26`:101–135); SCR-030's floor of 58 fps at 2560 × 1440 with 100 k bars allows 17.24<!--=BUD:fps58.ms--> ms (`14`:742). Every canvas moment adds 0<!--=DUR:instant--> ms, because none animates (§2); the one tween, M-07, is a compositor transform in the DOM strip, 0<!--=DUR:instant--> ms of engine time, its main-thread cost a §12 measurement (O-3). **Per moment:** 0<!--=DUR:instant--> ms added by motion; the replace and LOD frames' own cost is the `06` / `26` bench's, not this spec's (`E11D06`:100 D24). **The drop rule:** under the `06` §4.4 degradation ladder (`06`:86–100) and E11-D06's rung 0 (`E11D06`:99 D23, PROPOSED there), a transition that would start in an over-budget frame is skipped and its end state applied (R-5); the strip's M-07 tween retargets to the newest sample and never queues (a DOM tween is not engine-scheduled; D12 §9 sheds nothing); with 100 000 bars at budget, a bar-mode switch is already a cut, so the end state is identical whether or not anything played (`T` AC). Rebuild progress updates at 2 Hz, never per frame (§5.1).

## 9. Flashes and vestibular safety

This spec states only its own counts; the flash-rate budget is E47-D08's (#1307), read at #5444's head `307dffee24`<!--=SHA:E47D08--> and cited by id, if #5444 merges as is (R6): FR-1, the minimum interval between two starts of one cell (`E47D08`:189), and FR-3, the combined starts in one region in any half-open one-second window (`E47D08`:191); not duplicated (BQ-10). Flash starts per moment: 0<!--=BUD:flash.starts--> (M-01 … M-08 are cuts or one tween per sample, none a luminance flash); the ring's move at a 1 s close is 1 per second, a position change (`D11`:710). No large-area parallax, no scale, no zoom animation; MR-1 … MR-3 stand as merged (`16`:208).

## 10. Announcements, focus, security and observability

No transition moves focus or makes an announcement per frame; an announcement is made at the start and never waits for a transition (BQ-13; `E11D06`:83 D7; `D08`:292–297; `D11`:380–416). R-6 and R-7 carry `T` Security. Motion emits no analytics; dropped-transition counts, if instrumented at all, belong in the diagnostics overlay beside dropped frames (`14`:1119, SCR-046), never in product analytics (`T` Observability).
