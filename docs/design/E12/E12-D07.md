### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 One series.** Rows are the loaded series the canvas draws, read from the same store; the table issues no fetch of its own (`14`:1066; D04E11:260).
- **R-2 No column the canvas series lacks.** Every cell is a field of `22`'s `Bar` or `23`'s bars item, a value derived from those fields with its formula stated, or "—" (T Tech; RULED BQ-5).
- **R-3 Words carry every state.** A row state is a word in the `state` cell and in the row's accessible name, never a colour, weight or glyph alone (D04E11:183).
- **R-4 Keyed by `(generation, index)`.** Rows, the focused row and the selection are keyed by `(generation, index)`, never by time (`22`:4190–4191; `23`:2866).
- **R-5 Market data only.** No account, position, order or key value appears in a cell, a copy or an analytics property (T Sec; D04E11:127).
- **R-6 Never a silent blank.** A value that is absent reads "—" with its reason in the cell's accessible name; it never reads `0`.

## 2. The row header and row identity (RULED BQ-3, BQ-4)

### 2.1 Time bars

As merged: the `<th scope="row">` time cell, `hh:mm` at intervals of a minute or more, `mm:ss` below, the full UTC date-time in its accessible name (D04E11:179). No change.

### 2.2 Non-time bars

- **Visible.** The row header reads the open time to the millisecond and the bar's position: `10:22:11.412 bar 3 of 5` (D02:64; D06:355). The date is the day rule's and the caption's, as on time bars (D04E11:179). The cell reserves 25 characters, enough for two-digit counts; a longer count widens the column for the loaded series, never a single row.
- **Position.** "bar k of n" counts the bars of one generation that share one open time (`22`:4189–4190), in served order; a bar alone at its open time reads `bar 1 of 1`. It is never a raw `index`: `index` restarts in every generation (`22`:18837), and index-only labels are rejected (D02:32).
- **Never across a seam.** The count restarts after every seam row (§7.1): two bars with one open time in two generations are each `bar 1 of 1`.
- **Accessible name.** "bar 3 of 5, opened 2026-10-20 10:22:11.412 UTC, closed 10:22:14.020 UTC after 0:02.6"; on the forming row "bar 1 of 1, opened 2026-10-20 10:22:11.412 UTC, forming, not closed yet"; when `close_time` is not sent, "close time not sent" (D07-G4). The close clause keeps T AC2 true at every width, including when the `close time` and `duration` columns are hidden (§5).
- **Order.** Rows follow the served key `(t, generation, index)` ascending (`22`:4187–4188); the caption carries "Bar order · not time" (D02:32; §7.4).
- **Identity.** Rows are keyed `(generation, index)` (R-4), so an amended bar replaces its row in place (`23`:2893–2897) and a repeated open time never merges two rows.
