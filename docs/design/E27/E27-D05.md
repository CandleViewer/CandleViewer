## 1. Purpose, method, locked inputs and the rules stated once

### 1.1 The review's job

To give the accounts and key-vault surfaces an accessibility verdict per `05` clause and WCAG 2.2 criterion before E27-D06 lifts them into the handoff pack: the catalogue requirements per screen, the focus maps, the keyboard flows, the T2 table, the live-region plan, the carrier and contrast tables, the finding register and the screen-reader script. It edits no merged text: a defect in a merged design is an F row, a plan-text change is an A row (§15.4), and a disagreement between merged texts is a C row. `git diff` on E27-D02, E27-D03 and E27-D04 is empty.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

A desk review, clause by clause, of E27-D03 (all sections, read at the base) and E27-D04 §2–§4 (at its pin) against `05` §3–§8, with `14`'s per-screen A11y lines and checklists, `15`'s component contracts, `23`'s throttles and E09-D09's T2 grammar as the binding neighbours (the E30-D05 method, `E30-D05`:72–74). Every verdict carries `file`:line evidence and, where a merged board draws the case, a board number. The tool imports, never copies: the E27-D03 audit's pair list, theme resolver, `ratio`, `luma601` and `matrix`, its `cvd` module's `simulate`, `delta_e76`, `srgb_to_xyz` and `xyz_to_lab`, and the E27-D04 audit's board count; it runs the E27-D03 audit once to count that spec's drift. There is no prototype (`T` §Test asks for one "where feasible"): the keyboard walk is written as numbered sequences on the merged model, and the built walk is E27-Q04's. No screen reader was run and no participant was seen; every evaluative sentence that rests on judgement is marked `(heuristic)`.

### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity** (BQ-4; E30-D05's R-1 adapted, `E30-D05`:72). **Blocking:** a clause or a WCAG 2.2 A / AA criterion with no compliant path in the merged designs, a state carried by colour alone, a G-T2 miss that hides the reason, or a destructive step not perceivable non-visually. **Major:** an at-risk clause on a destructive or key-custody step, a T2 leg order or conversion miss (`DS`:17 makes a G-T2 miss Major in a spec). **Minor:** wording, cadence or order, or an at-risk clause whose interim path the merged designs already give. **Info:** recorded for a consumer.
- **R-2 Findings and their closing** (BQ-4). An F row names the merged sentence, the replacement, one owner (a spec or a component and its area, never an agent) and one target: E27-D06 (#819), which lifts the finding's version into the pack, plus the consuming story (#895–#899; Info rows: E27-D06 only). **AC 6:** E27-D06 (#819) is not signed off while a Blocking row stands; none stands at this head. A changed design is re-reviewed by re-running this tool's F gate against E27-D06's pack, not by a new review.
- **R-3 Verdicts** (`E30-D05`:74). `compliant` · `at risk` · `non-compliant` · `out of scope — <owner>` · `not yet decidable — <owner>`. A `non-compliant` clause carries a Blocking F row; an `at risk` clause carries a Major or Minor F row or names its interim path.
- **R-4 R5 is the arm rule** (`DS`:74), written so to keep it apart from `T`'s risk class "R5 Scope". No E27 control is a C-2.6 protective write (`D03`:1871), so nothing protective is greyed anywhere in this epic; SCR-079's Change leverage… on a LIVE account needs the arm, its `arm.locked` leg consumed from E30-D02.
- **R-5 One motion definition.** E27-D04 §2–§4 is the epic's motion owner; every motion row here cites its M row or §4 line.
- **R-6 No data in a string.** Example strings use placeholders (`{label}`, `{env}`, `{user}`, `{key_last4}`); the tool fails a quoted example that carries a fixture label or a decimal amount (G11).

## 8. The destructive confirmations and the write-only secret

### 8.1 SCR-129 — what a non-visual user hears before the press

`T` §Security asks that the consequences list, the typed-label requirement and the irreversibility all be announced. As merged: the dialog is an `alertdialog`, labelled by its title and **described by the first consequence sentence** (`D03`:1451; `D03`:847; `D03`:1980); the list is read in order when browsed (`D03`:1453); the typed requirement is the field's label (`D03`:1455); "what can't be undone" is one item of the list. A screen reader therefore announces, on open, the title, the role and one sentence; the rest is reachable but not announced. That fails the Security note's "all announced" `(heuristic)` and is F-01 (Major, R-1: at risk on a destructive step, the list still perceivable). **The replacement:** `aria-describedby` points at the whole restatement group — the list and its "can't be undone" item. Disable and Delete open while the summary loads (`D03`:1980; KS-D-01), and a description filled after focus has landed is not re-read `(heuristic)`, so the loaded announcement (LR-15) speaks the count, the irreversibility sentence where the action has one, and the typed requirement: "{n} consequences listed. {irreversibility sentence} Type {label} to confirm." SR-17 is the loading-to-ready criterion; SR-09 covers a summary already loaded on open. **The confirm's form:** E27-D03 keeps the confirm out of the DOM until the summary loads, then focusable `aria-disabled` in the validity look with its hint (`D03`:1458; `D03`:1945; E27-D03's C11 at `D03`:2710); the shipped CMP-044 instead disables it natively for a loading summary and an unmatched typed field (`15`:520; `15`:525). E27-D03's form is the bar (BQ-9): a native `disabled` confirm is skipped by `Tab`, so the hint under it is never met by a keyboard user `(heuristic)`. F-05 (Minor: E09-D09 lets a validity control be natively disabled, so the shipped form is legal; the design's form is the better path) with A-05 to `15`.

### 8.2 The write-only secret

`14` asks for a "value is write-only" description on SCR-126 (`14`:2666) and, on SCR-127, a label that says so (`14`:2680). Verified on the three hosts: SCR-126's key field carries the help "value is write-only" and the secret CP-1's sentence, in the DOM at mount through `aria-describedby` (`D03`:1192–1193); SCR-016 step 3 consumes the same fields (`D03`:1244); SCR-127 stage 2 carries the same two descriptions on "New API key" and "New API secret" (`D03`:1294). E27-D03's write-only variant of CMP-201 puts **no show control in the DOM** (`D03`:702), so a screen-reader user is never left hunting for one, and CMP-034 is permanently masked with one description node per page (`D03`:657; `D03`:182). Compliant for SCR-126 and SCR-016; at risk for SCR-127, whose `14` line asks for the label (F-03). **The drift:** `15` still documents CMP-034 with a show toggle and a timer (`15`:401; `15`:403; `15`:406; `15`:410) and CMP-201 with a default show toggle and no write-only variant (`15`:2632; `15`:2634); E27-D03's Appendix A rows never landed (`D03`:3018). A story built from `15` as it stands would draw a show control on a secret field: C-02, C-03, and G-02 to E27-D06.

### 8.3 The one server-driven focus move (E27-D04 C-4)

`05`:96 lets only a user action or an explicit modal alert move focus. E27-D04 announces SCR-127's stage 4 → 5 change politely and then moves focus to stage 5's heading (`D04`:163; `D04`:192; `D03`:791; `D03`:1344). **Verdict: compliant inside the open modal.** Stage 4's focused content is removed by the change; leaving focus where it was would drop it to `body` (WCAG 2.4.3), so the move is the only compliant path, and it happens inside a modal the user opened. The wording of `05` does not name this case, so C-01 records it and A-01 gives `05`'s owner the copy-ready words "focused content replaced inside an open modal". The danger states never move focus (`D04`:167) — kept. The order of the two voices is a separate, judged point: a focus move announced right after a polite message can cut that message on some screen readers `(heuristic)`; F-04 makes the heading's region carry the sentence so one voice speaks.
