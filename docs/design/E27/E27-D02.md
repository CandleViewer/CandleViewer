## 1. Purpose, scope, method and locked inputs

### 1.1 What this spec fixes

The low-fidelity structure of the five E27 admin surfaces (SCR-125 accounts list, SCR-126 add / edit, SCR-127 key
rotation, SCR-128 key health and secrets policy, SCR-129 disable / delete and its revoke dialog), the trading-side
SCR-079 account drawer, and the account step of SCR-016 with the SCR-019 checklist card (the memo's OQ-7 ruling hands
those two here). The failure states are the point of this epic (ticket Context), so each is a frame of its own at the
happy path's fidelity (AC2): a key with withdrawal permission, an allowlist that does not match, a stale balance, a
replacement key that fails, a delete blocked by open exposure. Every frame names its data source, its RBAC
requirement, its audit or analytics event (§7), its focus order, landmarks, live regions and dialog role (§5, §9) and,
where the ticket names one, its perceived-performance target. Out of scope (ticket): visual design, colour and type
(E27-D03), motion (E27-D04), design-system contributions (E27-D03), Storybook, profiles (E28) and the order ticket
(E30) — consumed where these screens touch them, never redrawn.

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk wireframes and a heuristic evaluation under the adaptations (owner decision 2026-09-25): no engineer, security
engineer, QA or CDO took part; the "design critique with engineering present" is replaced by citing a contract line
for every state (an impossible state is caught by the source column of §7), the keyboard-path dry run by §9.2 on
paper, and each `14` checklist by §12. Every judgement is `(heuristic)`. Sources read for this text: `14` §0 and the
eight entries; `15` (every CMP cited, §11); `11` §2 (US-ACCT-001..010); `22` (the `/exchange-accounts…` family, `/me`,
`/auth/step-up`, `/admin/security/summary`, `/admin/capacity`, `/onboarding/checklist`, the schemas named in §7); `23`
§6.2, §9.5, §15.2, §15.4, §15.9; `24` §14, §15.2, §15.3, §15.5; `21` §3.1.3, §3.2; ADR-0009, ADR-0010; `CON`
C-2.6..C-2.12, C-12.2, C-12.4, C-12.12; the memo and its protocol; E42-D02, E09-D09, E30-D02 §14, E39-D02 C29; the
registry `ACT`, `SU`, `ASU`, `SYS`, `REV` and `MX`.
