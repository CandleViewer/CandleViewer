## 16. The design-QA procedure (for later epics; RULED BQ-25)

- **PS-1 Inputs.** The epic's states matrix, its copy deck, its composition map, its focus map and script, its motion table, the token JSON, the a11y gate's route list; each lifted by content at the base, never retyped.
- **PS-2 Base.** One `main` commit; every cite pinned to its words; unmerged parents named only, each a delta row.
- **PS-3 Observation.** Source, the merged suites and stories; nothing reaches an exchange, a testnet or an external host; what needs a live session, a deployed build or a speaking reader is owner-run and named.
- **PS-4 The seven steps** in `T Test`'s order, each with its method and its count.
- **PS-5 Rows.** One finding, one id; a known record cited, not restated; the cells: class, severity, clause, owner (spec and area), target, G-T2, focus, rate, sentence with its cite, reason.
- **PS-6 Classes.** Implementation defect, accepted divergence, specification gap; nothing unclassified at close.
- **PS-7 Severity.** Blocking (a frozen string, a focus-map regression, a task not completable by keyboard, a secret shown out of place), Major, Minor, Info.
- **PS-8 Decision.** The orchestrator decides each divergence at review; an accepted row edits `14` in the same PR and goes to `15` as an A-row; the originating design ticket gets a comment and stays closed.
- **PS-9 Routing.** Comments on open owners; the register for the rest; an issue only for a Blocking or security row, by id, class and clause.
- **PS-10 Stop conditions.** A frozen string differs, a secret is shown out of place, or a cite cannot be read at the base: the row is written at once and the run continues; nothing is fixed in the QA PR.
