## 1. Purpose, method, locked inputs and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this review fixes

The accessibility contract of the auth band as one sequence: the verdict per `05` clause, the WCAG 2.2 cross-walk, the focus-continuity map, the script, the assertions, the time limits and the measured colour. It edits no merged text: a defect in a merged design is an F-row routed to its owner, a plan-text change is an A-row (§C), and a disagreement between merged texts is a C-row (§17.1).

### 1.2 Method (honest)

A desk review under T Adapt. **Pass 1, structured:** clause by clause (§3, §4), every verdict with `file`:line evidence, every judgement `(heuristic)`. **Pass 2, exploratory:** a fresh-context reader of T Test, given only the merged frames, §6 and §13, wrote its rows `(heuristic; agent pass, no participant)`, measuring contrast from the frames' pixels; it read the pass-1 sections afterwards, only to de-duplicate and regrade. Each of its rows is folded below into an F-, C-, Q- or G-row or a fix in this spec, or closed with its reason (BQ-7). **The script's dry run:** a second fresh-context reader walked §13 against the frames and the bound strings, a desk dry run with no screen reader; each step it could not pass or fail without interpretation is rewritten in §13.2 or §13.3, or carried as a Q- or G-row (AC5), as the second table below says; the writer's own desk walk is in §19.1. The tool resolves every bound token in dark, light and high contrast through `packages/ui/tokens`, measures every pair by the WCAG 2.x formula, measures every boxed target on the frames drawn at one CSS pixel per image pixel (D03:52; D06:11) from their pixels, simulates protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia with CVD's constants (gated against that file) and greyscale by luminance, and checks every cite at the base and by content at `origin/main`. No participant, session, timing or quote exists.

### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity (RULED BQ-9).** **Blocker:** a task in the band cannot be completed with the keyboard and a screen reader only (`05`:238), or any path where a user with a disability could be unable to authenticate, however rare (T AC6). **Major:** completable, but a `05` or WCAG 2.2 AA clause fails; `03`:326's floor for an AA violation is raised to Major because the band has no other way in (T Context). **Minor:** wording, consistency, or an AA nuance that blocks nothing. **Info:** a `(heuristic)` observation with no clause.
- **R-2 Routing and the hold (RULED BQ-9).** An F-row names one owner — the spec and its area, never an agent — and its target, the spec's follow-up revision PR (no new ticket, no reopen), or E09-D07 (#190) for a component contract; the consumer that builds meanwhile; and a close state. At merge one comment on each closed owning issue lists its F-rows (`16`:575's change-request comment) and says that a Blocker holds E09-D11's design QA (#291) and E09-D12's pack rows (#194) for that surface until the follow-up lands and this review re-reviews it; the line `re-sync needed: <what changed>` goes on each open engineering ticket that builds the surface.
- **R-3 Verdicts.** `compliant` · `at risk` (an F-row, a G-row where the contract lacks what the design draws, or a C-row with its A-row where a ruling decided against the clause) · `non-compliant` (a Blocking or Major F-row) · `N/A` (its reason in the cell). A verdict never depends on an unapplied A-row (BQ-32 rider).
- **R-4 The copy ladder (RULED BQ-17).** One string per announcement: (1) a later merged text that decides the string; (2) the surface's spec or frame; (3) `14`; (4) D02's wireframes. A ruling of record decides above the ladder: SCR-001's label and verb (#194 BQ-13). Built strings are evidence for E09-D11, never a source.
- **R-5 The busy rule (RULED BQ-14).** The focused field becomes `readonly`, never `disabled`; the submit carries `aria-disabled="true"` and `aria-busy="true"` and stays focusable; `aria-busy` sits on the form; focus does not move; where a dialog's controls must all be inert, focus rests on the dialog (S03:83).
- **R-6 The never-list (RULED BQ-20).** §11.2 binds every live region, alert, name, description, title and script line.
- **R-7 Carried causes (RULED BQ-12).** A cause that crosses a route is spoken once, on the destination, by focus landing on its alert or reason line; the source never speaks it before the cut, and the destination never repeats it on a re-render.
- **R-8 Restoration.** A row whose trigger disables or unmounts the focused element names where focus goes in the same frame; a restoration after an unmount is not a move (D09:250).

### 13.1 Configurations and the pass rule

- **Configurations** (`05`:219–224; C-1): NVDA on Windows with the Electron shell and with Chromium for the fast gate (one Chromium engine, one expected column); VoiceOver on macOS with the Electron shell; JAWS with the Electron shell runs the smoke subset against the same strings: `05`:220 names `05`'s own smoke script (§8.2 item 3's tasks, `05`:230–233), not this script's steps, so the auth band's JAWS smoke set is taken here as the first step of each probe set, SR-1, SR-8 and SR-17 (A, C and B; RES:249) `(heuristic)`. - **Pass rule:** a step passes when every scripted string is heard once, in order, focus is where its map row says, and nothing on the never-list is heard. The screen reader's own role words and verbosity are not scripted (the app's strings are verbatim, the reader's phrasing is not; C-32).
