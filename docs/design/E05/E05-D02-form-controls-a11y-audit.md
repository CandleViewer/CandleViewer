# E05-D02 — Accessibility pre-review, slice 3: form controls

**Ticket:** E05-D02 (#161) · companion to `E05-D02-form-controls.md` (slice 3) · required by
`docs/plan/16-design-system-brief.md` §12 (accessibility items) and `docs/plan/05-accessibility-standard.md` §3
(keyboard, focus, target size), §4 (colour independence), §5 (contrast), §6.2 (live regions), §7 (reduced motion) and
§11 (design-system requirements).
**Status:** pre-review. The ticket's Definition of Done asks for an Accessibility role pre-review here; **the formal
accessibility and CVD sign-off is E05-D04's**, and this document does not replace it.

Covers the 11 components of slice 3 (CMP-007, 008, 009, 034, 040, 047, 048, 049, 054, 065 and 079) as specified in
`E05-D02-form-controls.md` §7. The slice is text-first: nothing is drawn yet, and the boards PR builds and exports the
sections on Penpot page `10 Components — Atoms`. The CMP-039 ring and the CMP-002, CMP-005 and CMP-014 that slice-3
components nest are reviewed in slice 1's audit (`E05-D02-a11y-audit.md`).

**Method.** Every ratio below was computed for this review from `packages/ui/tokens` as #3925 left them (`a0132de01`,
their last change), each theme layered as `packages/ui/tokens/tests/verify_tokens.py` layers it (its loader, imported),
with `contrast_ratio()` from `docs/design/_tools/contrast_check.py` (WCAG 2.x relative luminance) and ΔE76 from
`docs/research/_tools/cvd_simulate.py`; values are rounded half up to two places. A dimmed state blends the colour at
`opacity.disabled` (0.45) over its surface. "The host surfaces" are `color.surface.canvas`, `raised` and `overlay`; "the
well" is `color.surface.app` (spec §6 G4). The keyboard and name/role rows summarise the full tables in
`E05-D02-form-controls.md` §7. "§6 Gn" points at that document's owner decisions; "conventions n" at its §3.

## 5. CVD (colour-vision deficiency)

Not reviewed in this pre-review: nothing is drawn yet, and the formal CVD review is E05-D04's (`05` §4.2, brief §13.3
item 3). ΔE76 spot checks with `docs/research/_tools/cvd_simulate.py` (base, then protanopia / deuteranopia /
tritanopia); structurally, no slice-3 state depends on hue alone:

- **Invalid against rest** (`status.danger.default` vs `text.tertiary` boundaries): 69.8 (32.3 / 46.8 / 87.0) dark,
  51.2 (25.2 / 36.3 / 63.7) light, 74.8 (45.9 / 51.6 / 90.9) high contrast — and the 2 px width, the glyph and the
  words carry it anyway.
- **Error against warning glyphs** (`status.danger.strong` vs `status.warning.strong`): 14.7 (12.5 / 14.0 / 11.2) in dark
  and high contrast, **0.0 in light** (identical, slice 1 A6): the glyph shapes and the words tell them apart.
- **Selected tint** (`action.selected`) vs canvas: 27.4 (28.6 / 29.9 / 16.0) dark, 11.3 (11.1 / 12.3 / 8.7) light; vs
  a hovered row 25.4 dark, 8.7 light (5.6 under tritanopia). The checked box and the outline carry selection.
- **Selected outline vs focus ring** (`border.selected` vs `focus.ring`): 16.1 dark, 12.6 light, 58.5 high contrast —
  slice 1 A7's near-identical blue rings, kept apart by the 2 px gap.

## 6. Findings for the owner and E05-D04

1. The field boundary binds the text token `color.text.tertiary` (5.20:1 or more): a semantic `color.border.input`
   alias is the token follow-up (§6 G1, slice 1 A3, E42-D03 DS-3, E30-D04 CR-1).
2. The invalid boundary `color.status.danger.default` falls to 2.43–2.92:1 on dark `surface.hover` and
   `surface.selected` (2.87:1 high-contrast selected): fields are not placed there (§6 G2).
3. A checked box on a `surface.selected` row is 2.58:1 in dark: selected rows take `color.action.selected` (3.12:1,
   thin) and E11-D04's rows follow when next revised (§6 G3).
4. Light error and warning glyphs share one amber (slice 1 A6); the error look keeps a red boundary (§6 G2).
5. Three merged specs fill the field well three ways; `color.surface.app` is recommended (§6 G4).
6. Field errors are polite and once; CMP-027's `role="alert"` stays the host's summary, and an in-panel load error
   needs CMP-027's polite form in slice 4 (§6 G17).
7. `aria-errormessage` is dropped so a message is read once (§6 G16, heuristic).
8. CMP-034's auto-hide cannot meet WCAG 2.2.1's 20 s "extend" option; an accessibility setting that lengthens it is
   proposed (§6 G20), and its security rules S1–S7 go to E05-X02 (§6 G19).
9. A placeholder is never a name: CMP-054 gains a mandatory `label` (§6 G13), and CMP-040 routes `aria-labelledby` to
   the controls `<label for>` cannot name, closing slice 1 C5 (§6 G10).
10. A `legend` is not in the heading list: CMP-065's legend wraps a heading (§6 G14).
11. Out-of-range days are inactive (2.81:1 light at 0.45) and the caption names the range in words.
12. A T2 field's lock leads its value inside the well, and its label keeps `color.text.secondary` (8.14:1 or more on
    the host surfaces), as E30-D04 §2.5 (2) draws the order ticket's fields (spec conventions 24, §5.2); four merged
    specs lead the field's label with the lock instead, in `color.text.tertiary` (5.20:1 or more): an owner decision
    (spec §6 G23).
13. An option the form's own rules make unavailable keeps its reason and stays arrow-reachable (E30-D04 §17's validity
    look); its dimmed label (2.81–4.14:1) is reported, not gated, and its reason line carries the state (spec
    conventions 26).
14. CMP-008's step buttons stay out of the Tab order (`tabindex="-1"`, the APG Spin Button pattern), where E30-D02 §13
    stops on the price field's pair: an owner decision (spec §6 G22).
