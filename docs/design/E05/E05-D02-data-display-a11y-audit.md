`docs/plan/16-design-system-brief.md` §12 (accessibility items) and `docs/plan/05-accessibility-standard.md` §3

**Status:** pre-review. The ticket's Definition of Done asks for an Accessibility role pre-review here; **the formal
accessibility and CVD sign-off is E05-D04's**, and this document does not replace it.

Covers the 12 components of slice 2 (CMP-011, 012, 013, 018, 020, 021, 024, 025, 030, 032, 033 and 037) as specified
in `E05-D02-data-display.md` §6 and drawn on Penpot page `10 Components — Atoms`. Their board exports are PENDING

**Method.** Every ratio below was computed for this review from the token files in `packages/ui/tokens` as #3925
left them (`a0132de01`, their last change; dark, light and high contrast, layered as `verify_tokens.py` layers

## 5. CVD (colour-vision deficiency)

Not reviewed in this pre-review: the PR adds no CVD exports, and the formal CVD review is E05-D04's (05 §4.2, brief
§13.3 item 3). The specs ran dE76 spot checks with `docs/research/_tools/cvd_simulate.py`, and this review re-ran the
pairs below with the same tool. Structurally, no slice-2 state depends on hue alone:

- **Status tones** (CMP-011, CMP-012) are told apart by glyph shape and word. Light warning and danger glyphs and
  outlines (`.strong`) are identical (dE76 0 for every vision type, slice 1 A6), while their dots are 56.6 apart
  (34.5 under tritanopia) and their chip fills 18.8 (9.5); dark info and success dots are 11.9 apart under
  tritanopia.
- **Direction** (CMP-020, CMP-024) is carried by the + or − sign and by the slope. The buy and sell strokes are only
  8.3 (dark), 16.1 (light) and 5.8 (high contrast) apart under deuteranopia.
- **Estimated** (CMP-011) is the dashed outline and the literal word.
- **Confidence** (CMP-032) is a shape difference, filled disc against ring (also 19.6–66.6 apart in every
  simulation), and the label names the level.
- **Legend swatches** (CMP-037): the label names what each swatch keys, and direction tokens add ▲ or ▼. The heatmap
  `bid.3` and `ask.3` swatches are only 4.8–5.0 apart under deuteranopia, so a deuteranope relies on the label there
  (§5 E26: E05-D04 decides whether heatmap legends also carry the glyph).
- **Avatars** (CMP-013, CMP-025) carry no colour yet; a future palette is CVD-reviewed before it ships (§5 E2).

## 6. Findings for the owner and E05-D04

1. `colorBySign` text fails 4.5:1 in 14 tier pairs (dark sell from raised to selected; dark and high-contrast zero on
   selected; light buy and sell on overlay, hover, selected and sunken): text-grade tokens in the next E05 token
   pass (§5 E1). Until they land, the brief §12 contrast item stays open.
2. Status dots fall below 3:1 off app and canvas (light warning 2.46–2.999, light info 2.86, dark danger 2.43–2.92,
   high-contrast danger 2.87 on selected). The spec places dots on app and canvas only, always beside a glyph and a
   word (§5 E11); the identical light warning and danger glyph colours (`.strong`) are slice 1 A6.
3. Light filled confidence dots are 2.86:1 on selected and sunken (§4); the label names the level (§5 E17).
4. Dividers (`color.border.subtle`) are 1.00–1.43:1, and invisible on light overlays and on hover in both themes:
   slice 1 A4.
5. The legend swatch outline is 1.57–1.91:1; it only marks the zero stop (§5 E16).
6. The heatmap bid and ask swatches nearly merge under deuteranopia (dE76 4.8–5.0): §5 E26, for E05-D04.
7. The removable chip's focus ring touches its label (§5 E7); the link ring's radius is §5 E8.
8. The names folded into +N reach assistive technology only, and the overlap hides each covered avatar's second
   initial (§5 E23, E6).
9. An abbreviated value must not name a span with `aria-label`; the design uses visually hidden text (§5 E15).
10. A labelled divider is not a heading: forms that need section navigation add their own (§5 E24).
11. The estimated chip's link to its methodology, and the rule that it is never dimmed, wait for §5 E19.
12. CopyButton shows no visible text on copy and has no failure state (§5 E20).
