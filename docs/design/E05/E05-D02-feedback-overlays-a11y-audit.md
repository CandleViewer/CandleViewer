# E05-D02 — Accessibility pre-review, slice 4: feedback and overlays

**Ticket:** E05-D02 (#161) · companion to `E05-D02-feedback-overlays.md` (slice 4) · required by
`docs/plan/16-design-system-brief.md` §12 (accessibility items) and `docs/plan/05-accessibility-standard.md` §3
(keyboard, focus, target size), §4 (colour independence), §5 (contrast), §6.2 (live regions), §7 (reduced motion) and
§11 (design-system requirements).
**Status:** pre-review. The ticket's Definition of Done asks for an Accessibility role pre-review here; **the formal
accessibility and CVD sign-off is E05-D04's**, and this document does not replace it.

Covers the 17 components of slice 4 (CMP-015, 016, 017, 023, 026, 027, 028, 029, 038, 041, 043, 044, 045, 046, 089, 090
and 091) as specified in `E05-D02-feedback-overlays.md` §7. The slice is text-first: nothing is drawn yet, and the
boards PR builds and exports the sections on Penpot page `10 Components — Atoms`. The CMP-039 ring and the CMP-001,
CMP-002, CMP-005, CMP-006 and CMP-014 these components nest are reviewed in slice 1's audit (`E05-D02-a11y-audit.md`);
the CMP-007 and CMP-040 fields and the CMP-079 RbacGate wrapper in slice 3's (`E05-D02-form-controls-a11y-audit.md`).

**Method.** Every ratio below was computed for this review from `packages/ui/tokens` as #3925 left them (`a0132de01`,
their last change), each theme layered as `packages/ui/tokens/tests/verify_tokens.py` layers it (its loader, imported),
with `contrast_ratio()` from `docs/design/_tools/contrast_check.py` (WCAG 2.x relative luminance) and ΔE76 from
`docs/research/_tools/cvd_simulate.py`; values are rounded half up to two places. A blend is the colour at its opacity
over the surface, mixed per sRGB channel as `verify_tokens.py`'s `over()` does: the scrim is black at `opacity.scrim`
(0.6), a plain-disabled item `opacity.disabled` (0.45). "The host surfaces" are `color.surface.canvas`, `raised` and
`overlay`. The keyboard and name/role rows summarise the full tables in `E05-D02-feedback-overlays.md` §7. "§6 Hn"
points at that document's owner decisions; "conventions n" at its §3. In §4–§6 a colour token drops its `color.` prefix
(`text.primary` is `color.text.primary`) except `color.border.*`, which keeps it because `border.*` names the
border-width primitives.

item 3). ΔE76 spot checks (base, then protanopia / deuteranopia / tritanopia); every tone carries a glyph and a word:

  contrast**: the tone's word and the value text carry it.
- **Success against warning** (`.strong`): 57.6 (15.7 / 31.1 / 133.6) dark, 65.0 (17.4 / 27.2 / 136.2) light.
- **Focus ring against a focused item** (`focus.ring` vs `surface.hover`): 65.9 or more in every theme and simulation.

## 6. Findings for the owner and E05-D04

1. The dialog card is 1.25:1 against the scrimmed canvas in dark; its content and the trap carry it, and the scrim needs
   a colour token (§6 H1, E09-D08 A-2).
2. Red danger text fails 4.5:1 on the dark overlay (3.17:1): danger menu items keep `text.primary` and a `.strong` glyph
   (§6 H16); E14-D03's `status.danger.default` glyph falls to 2.92:1 on a focused dark item.
3. Light `status.warning.default` is 2.76:1 on the overlay and the progress track: toasts keep `.default` edges with
   words and glyphs (§6 H6); the progress warning fill binds `.strong` (§6 H4).
4. The dark danger progress fill is 2.92:1 on its track: a `color.track` token is the follow-up (§6 H4).
5. E39-D03's hold ring is 1.90:1 (dark) and 2.25:1 (high contrast) on its track: its percentage text is mandatory.
6. Light `surface.hover` equals the overlay, so skeletons on an overlay take `surface.selected` (§6 H3).
7. The tooltip's echo mode is `aria-hidden`, so a T2 reason or a name is never read twice (§6 H10).
8. A toast never hosts its own live region; CMP-212's pre-rendered regions speak (§6 H28); a toast with an action or a
   `danger` tone never times out (§6 H26); Undo never delays an order cancel (§6 H27).
9. `Esc` always cancels a dialog before its send (05 §3.4 item 4), so NoEscClose goes (§6 H22); the TopBar and the panic
   cluster stay outside the inert page (§6 H23); toasts render above the scrim but stay inert, announced through
