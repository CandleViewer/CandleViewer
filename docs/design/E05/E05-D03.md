## 3. The reduce substitutes: one mechanism

- **One rule.** Every reduce cell in §2 is `none`. The mechanism is the universal reduce block under
  `:root[data-motion="reduce"]` (`theme.css`:55-66), and the OS fallback applies only while `data-motion` is absent
  (`theme.css`:68-80). There is no scoped exemption and no per-recipe reduce CSS (R162 BQ-05, BQ-12). A component's
  own reduce rule may only select its static end state, as `spinner.css`:48-50 does with `animation: none`; it never
  adds motion.
- **The spinner (row 7).** No rotation: a static glyph with its in-flight label. The pulse at `15`:188 cannot survive
  the universal block (E09-D08 C-1, `E09-D08`:411) and is retired by A-11.
- **The skeleton (row 6).** The static two-tone fill from the first frame (`15`:196).
- **The value flash (row 3).** No flash and no momentary text tint; the tinted glyph and the sign carry the direction
  (§5).
- **A mode flip mid-transition.** The running transition completes on its clock; nothing re-mounts on the
  `data-motion` flip (`16`:230-231).

## 4. The rules as checkable statements

Each statement has an id; the tool asserts §2 against it (G3-RULES) and #3174's tests implement it.

- **MR-1 (the gate; WCAG 2.3.1, `05`:208).** "no element starts more than 3 flashes in any 1 000 ms window". The window
  is half-open, from a start t up to but not including t + 1 000 ms. #3174's unit test drives it with a fake clock.
  The arithmetic: at 500 ms a cell starts at most 2 flashes in any window. The ticket's "one flash per 333 ms" (T
  Technical notes) is not a safe minimum: 333 ms admits 4 starts (at 0, 333, 666 and 999 ms), so an interval must be
  at least 334 ms to hold 3 (C-6). HS-1's 500 ms is the setting.
- **HS-1 (the hot-surface design rule; E08-D11 D15, `E08-D11`:72).** At least 500 ms between flash starts per cell
  (≤ 2 per second); at most 20 emphasised cells per frame, the focused symbol and open positions first; the latest tick
  wins and nothing queues; a cell that has changed at ≥ 1 Hz for 10 s stops emphasising until it is quiet. Values,
  arrows and signs always update; only the emphasis is dropped.
- **MR-2 (WCAG 2.2.2; `16`:178, :229).** "an animation whose `animation-iteration-count` × `animation-duration` exceeds
  5 000 ms, or is `infinite`, ships a pause control or stops itself by 5 s; the only exception is a request- or
  job-bound busy indicator with its in-flight label". Row 6 runs 1 200 ms four times, 4 800 ms in all, and passes; row 7
  is the exception (E09-D08 A-10 and C-17); every other row is one-shot.
- **MR-3 (`16`:104; `05`:205-206).** "every row names a non-motion carrier (text, glyph, sign, ARIA state) present in
  the reduce end state; the reduce snapshot's accessibility tree equals the safe end state's". Motion is never the
  sole carrier of meaning: §2's last-but-one column names the carrier on every row.

One-shot rows start only on a state change, a user action or a server answer; E10-D04 §11 (`E10-D04`:247-254) records
such changes as not flashes, and the tool applies MR-1's count to the repeating rows (3, 6 and 7).

**What the numbers say.**

- **Hue carries the direction; nothing inverts.** Every up peak is green (144.9° to 154.0°) and every down peak red
  (346.3° to 354.8°), in every theme; an up flash never takes the down hue.
- **Lightness carries none.** In dark and hc the peak lightens the cell; in light it darkens it. Up against down is
  1.02 to 1.19, so without hue the two peaks look alike: the glyph and the sign are required in safe mode as well
  (`05` §4).
- **The digits never take the tint.** `color.text.primary` holds at least 9.07 over every peak; the direction colour as
  text over its own peak measures 3.15 to 5.71 and meets the text bar in one row of twelve (dark, up, canvas).
- **WCAG 2.3.1.** Light is a general flash (|ΔL| ≥ 0.1 with the darker state below 0.80); dark and hc are not. No peak is
  a saturated red (the red share stays below 0.8 in every row), as E08-D11 found (`E08-D11`:228).

**Safe mode.** The E08-D11 overlay; the digits in `color.text.primary`; the arrow glyph and the sign in the direction
colour carry the direction (Q-1 records the sign's text contrast). **Reduce mode.** No flash and no momentary text tint;
the tinted glyph (non-text, 3:1: at least 4.41 on either surface) and the sign carry the direction. This meets AC 2
("a colour tint and a glyph") through the glyph's tint, and supersedes `16`:182's "colour-only momentary text tint"
(A-5).

**BQ-09, RULED as A23.** The light-theme flash is **accepted as designed**: at ≤ 2 starts per second per cell with the fast-market stop it passes WCAG 2.3.1 by rate (and 2.3.2's three-per-second bar), the emphasised area is small and capped, and reduce mode removes it entirely; the light alpha stays 0.25 (PROPOSED within E08-D11's 0.15–0.35 as a _tuning_, not a gate) for the owner's later prototype run.
The tool asserts the rate cap and the stop on row 3 (G3-RULES).

## 6. The Reduce Motion override

### 6.1 The inputs

Four inputs exist: the OS query `prefers-reduced-motion`; the account tri-state `accessibility.reduced_motion`
(`system`, `on` or `off`; SCR-117, `AccessibilitySettingsScreen.tsx`:82-84); its boolean twin
`appearance.reduce_motion`; and the local override in `localStorage["cv.theme.v1"]` (`store.ts`:22). The shipped fold
(`themeFromSettings.ts`:85-89) is documented as it is: a tri-state `on` or `off` wins; otherwise a twin `true` reads as
`on`; otherwise the result is `system`.

### 6.2 The rule

The latest explicit choice wins over the OS in both directions: SCR-117 and `Ctrl+Shift+M` write the one store
(`E08-D11`:160, `16`:139). `system` defers to the OS (`store.ts`:172). The account value is the durable record and
`cv.theme.v1` its pre-paint cache; on sign-in the account value replaces the cache.

### 6.3 The precedence table (4 × 3)

Columns: the account tri-state. Rows: the twin and the OS query. Cells: the resolved `data-motion`. Twin `false` and an
absent twin resolve alike and share a row; the tool checks every cell against the shipped test's 18 cases
(`a11y-preferences-theme.test.tsx`:393-410; G6-PRECEDENCE).

| Twin, OS                            | `system` | `on`     | `off`  |
| ----------------------------------- | -------- | -------- | ------ |
| twin true, OS no-preference         | `reduce` | `reduce` | `safe` |
| twin true, OS reduce                | `reduce` | `reduce` | `safe` |
| twin false/absent, OS no-preference | `safe`   | `reduce` | `safe` |
| twin false/absent, OS reduce        | `reduce` | `reduce` | `safe` |

AC 4's case is the third row's `on` cell: the OS says no-preference, the user turns Reduce Motion on, and reduced
motion applies. The `off` column is the other direction: an explicit `off` keeps motion under an OS `reduce`.

### 6.4 `data-motion` and persistence

`data-motion` keeps the shipped two values, `safe` and `reduce`; the override vocabulary stays `system`, `on` and `off`
in the store and the account (`16`:139). Components read the attribute only; the OS query appears only in the fallback
before the runtime writes the attribute (`theme.css`:68-80); G-1 lists the four components that still read the query.
A flip never re-mounts anything, and a running transition completes on its clock (`16`:230-231).

### 6.5 The shortcut `Ctrl+Shift+M`

- **Behaviour.** It toggles the resolved mode to an explicit choice: from `system` it writes the opposite of what the
  OS resolves, so `on` or `off`. It persists like the SCR-117 choice and announces once, politely, "Reduced motion on"
  or "Reduced motion off". The way back to `system` is SCR-117 only.
- **Scope.** Global, and it runs inside dialogs and drawers (`E08-D06`:402); suppressed while
  `data-keymap-context="Locked"` is mounted (`KeymapHost.tsx`:71).
- **Conflicts.** None in the shipped keymap or its reserved-keys list, and no Electron accelerator. Outside the app,
  Chromium binds the chord to its profile menu and Firefox to Responsive Design Mode; whether `preventDefault` holds on
  the reference browser is G-3, routed to E10-S02 (#305). The Electron shell is unaffected.
- **Wording.** `05`:78 calls the binding a "Session override" while every entry point writes the persisted store: C-2.

### 6.6 Where the setting lives

Settings → Accessibility (SCR-117, `14`:2406-2410) → Reduced motion (system / on / off), with no Appearance duplicate
(A-8 re-words `16`:186). Flash-on-update has no control of its own here: it follows `data-motion`, as the shipped
`flashOnTick` flag does (`model.ts`:60). SCR-117's own "disable flash-on-update" option (`14`:2410) is H-1.

### 6.7 The chart engine (stated here, designed by E11)

Under `motion: "reduce"` in the `candleviewer:themechange` event (`16`:141) the engine stops inertia, heatmap trail
decay and any canvas flash (US-SET-007, `11`:5180-5182). The last-price pill never flashes in either mode
(`E11-D06`:255), and E25-D03's M-14 uses no flash (`E25-D03`:367). The engine's design is E11's.
