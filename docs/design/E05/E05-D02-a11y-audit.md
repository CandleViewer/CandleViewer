# E05-D02 — Accessibility pre-review, batch 1: actions and selection

**Ticket:** E05-D02 (#161) · companion to `E05-D02.md` (batch 1 of 4) · required by
`docs/plan/16-design-system-brief.md` §12 (accessibility items) and `docs/plan/05-accessibility-standard.md` §3
(keyboard, focus, target size), §4 (colour independence), §7 (reduced motion) and §11 (design-system requirements).
**Status:** pre-review. The ticket's Definition of Done asks for an Accessibility role pre-review here; **the formal
accessibility and CVD sign-off is E05-D04's**, and this document does not replace it.

Covers the 11 components of batch 1 (CMP-001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 010, 014, 019 with the 47-glyph icon set,
031 and 039) as specified in `E05-D02.md` §8 and drawn on Penpot page `10 Components — Atoms`.

**Method.** Contrast ratios are the component specs' measurements on the shipped tokens in `packages/ui/tokens`
(dark, light and high contrast; WCAG 2.x relative luminance). Every figure was recomputed from the token files for
this review and matches; the few pairs a spec covers only as "passes" are filled in from the same recompute. The
keyboard and name/role rows summarise the full tables in `E05-D02.md` §8. "§6 Xn" points at the owner decisions in
`E05-D02.md`.

## 5. CVD (colour-vision deficiency)

Not simulated in this pre-review: the PR adds no CVD exports, and the formal CVD review is E05-D04's (05 §4.2, brief
§13.3 item 3). Structurally, no batch-1 state depends on hue alone:

- **Buy and sell** (CMP-001) keep their words in every state, loading included; the ▲/▼ glyph of 05 §4.1 waits for the
  custom trading glyphs (§6 D12).
- **Selection and checked state** are shapes or positions: the `check` and `minus` glyphs (CMP-005, CMP-031), the radio
  dot (CMP-006), the switch knob (CMP-004), the slider thumb (CMP-010), the filled, bold selected segment (CMP-003) and
  the filled pressed box (CMP-002).
- **Gating** adds the `locked` glyph and a reason; **critical** adds the `warning` glyph and a bold label (CMP-004).
- **Status** contexts pair the registry glyph with the status word (CMP-019).
- **Colour swatches** (CMP-031) differ by hue by design; their names (`aria-label`, the proposed name tooltip) are the
  non-colour channel.

## 6. Findings for the owner and E05-D04

1. Five label-on-fill pairs below 4.5:1 (CMP-001; CMP-002 shares the fills): **#3896** (§6 A1). Until it lands, the
   brief §12 contrast item stays open.
2. Light `color.drawing.selected` is below 3:1 on raised (2.999) and overlay (2.76): a palette token PR (§6 A5).
3. Light `color.status.success.strong` is 2.78:1 on sunken and selected, and danger and warning glyphs share one amber:
   the token follow-up (§6 A6). The status word keeps the meaning.
4. The focus ring over chart data ink is 1.02–1.51:1: the halo proposal (§6 D11).
5. Non-text outlines bind the text token `color.text.tertiary`, and the dark knob on the off track has a thin 3.08:1
   margin: a control-outline token (§6 A3).
6. High contrast draws the focus ring in the primary fill's colour; the 2 px gap keeps WCAG 1.4.11 (§6 A7).
7. The spinner's reduced-motion trough dips to 2.18–2.73:1 and its loop needs the 5 s cap (§6 D9).
8. Icon-only buttons show mouse users no name: the proposed name tooltip (§6 D10).
9. Arrow keys check SegmentedControl options, which would write to the exchange in CMP-128/129: the consumers confirm
   (§6 D5).
10. The critical toggle's confirm-gate variant is not decided yet (§6 D3), and no thick focus ring is defined (§6 A9).
11. A slider on `color.surface.selected` falls to 2.58:1 in dark: a usage rule for screens.
12. Build gaps (`E05-D02.md` §5.3): CMP-006's disabled-with-reason variant comes with its rework (drawn, not yet
    verified), and five sections still show the reason as a caption until the CMP-016 Tooltip is placed.
