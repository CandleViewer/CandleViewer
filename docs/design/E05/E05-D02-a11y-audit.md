**Ticket:** E05-D02 (#161) · companion to `E05-D02.md` (slice 1 of 5) · required by
`docs/plan/16-design-system-brief.md` §12 (accessibility items) and `docs/plan/05-accessibility-standard.md` §3
(keyboard, focus, target size), §4 (colour independence), §7 (reduced motion) and §11 (design-system requirements).
**Status:** pre-review. The ticket's Definition of Done asks for an Accessibility role pre-review here; **the formal
accessibility and CVD sign-off is E05-D04's**, and this document does not replace it.

Covers the 11 components of slice 1 (CMP-001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 010, 014, 019 with the 47-glyph icon set,

are PENDING (text-first merge, 2026-10-23); the boards PR completes slice 1 (`E05-D02.md` §8).

**Method.** Contrast ratios are the component specs' measurements on the shipped tokens in `packages/ui/tokens`
(dark, light and high contrast; WCAG 2.x relative luminance). Every figure was recomputed from the token files for
this review and matches; the few pairs a spec covers only as "passes" are filled in from the same recompute. The
keyboard and name/role rows summarise the full tables in `E05-D02.md` §8. "§6 Xn" points at the owner decisions in

## 4. Contrast pairs each component introduces

Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG 1.4.3); glyphs, fills, outlines and focus indicators need 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). Body labels on the
surfaces use `color.text.primary`, which `verify_tokens.py` proves at ≥4.5:1 on app and canvas; this review's
recompute covers the other five tiers (lowest on `color.surface.selected`: 10.17:1 dark, 12.82:1 light, 11.51:1 high
contrast), so they are not repeated. **Bold** marks a failure; the five label-on-fill failures of the first round were
fixed by **#3925** (#3896). Plain disabled controls (the whole root at `opacity.disabled`) are not measured: they rely
on the exemption WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11 give inactive components (05 has none of its own), and they are never used for
an RBAC, environment or risk denial, which is always the gated state measured here (`14-screens-catalogue.md` §0.5.5).

## 5. CVD (colour-vision deficiency)

No CVD simulation is reviewed here: the CVD renders of CMP-001 and CMP-004 come with the boards PR (`E05-D02.md`
§8), and the formal CVD review is E05-D04's (05 §4.2, brief §13.3 item 3). Structurally, no slice-1 state depends on
hue alone:

- **Buy and sell** (CMP-001) keep their words and a direction arrow (`arrow-up` / `arrow-down`) in every state,
  loading and gated included (§6 D16).
- **Selection and checked state** are shapes or positions: the `check` and `minus` glyphs (CMP-005, CMP-031), the radio
  dot (CMP-006), the switch knob (CMP-004), the slider thumb (CMP-010), the outlined, bold selected segment (CMP-003)
  and the outlined pressed box (CMP-002).
- **Gating** dims only the shape, keeps the label and the value full strength, and adds the trailing `locked` glyph (a
  corner badge on icon-only controls) and a reason (§6 D13); **critical** adds the `warning` glyph and a bold label
  (CMP-004, proposal §6 D4, held).
- **Status** contexts pair the registry glyph with the status word (CMP-019).
- **Colour swatches** (CMP-031) differ by hue by design; their names (`aria-label`, the proposed name tooltip) are the
  non-colour channel.

## 6. Findings for the owner and E05-D04

1. Five label-on-fill pairs were below 4.5:1 (CMP-001; CMP-002 shares the fills): fixed by **#3925** (#3896, §6 A1); a
   CI matrix now holds every label at 4.5:1.
2. Light `color.drawing.selected` is below 3:1 on raised (2.999) and overlay (2.76): a palette token PR (§6 A5).
3. Light `color.status.success.strong` is 2.78:1 on sunken and selected, and danger and warning glyphs share one amber:
   the token follow-up (§6 A6). The status word keeps the meaning.
4. The focus ring over chart data ink is 1.02–1.51:1: the halo proposal (§6 D11).
5. Non-text outlines bind the text token `color.text.tertiary`, and the dark knob on the off track has a thin 3.08:1
   margin: a control-outline token (§6 A3).
6. High contrast's focus ring is white since #3925 (§6 A7). In dark and light the ring is 1.36–1.42:1 from the
   selected outline of a pressed toggle or selected segment; the 2 px gap keeps WCAG 1.4.11, and a registry check is
   proposed.
7. A spinner bound to a request has no time cap and a slow path (an order submit never offers Cancel), a status spinner
   keeps brief §7's 5 s limit, and the reduced-motion pulse never dims below 3:1: an amendment to brief §7 (§6 D15).
8. Icon-only buttons show mouse users no name: the proposed name tooltip (§6 D10).
9. Arrow keys check SegmentedControl options, which would write to the exchange in CMP-128/129: the consumers confirm
   (§6 D5).
10. The critical toggle's confirm-gate variant is not decided yet (§6 D3), and no thick focus ring is defined (§6 A9).
11. A slider on `color.surface.selected` falls to 2.58:1 in dark: a usage rule for screens.
12. Build gaps (`E05-D02.md` §5.3): only CMP-002's icon nesting, scheduled with §6 B6.
13. In light, `color.status.danger.default` was the warning amber; #3925 made it a dark red (10.31:1 under the label);
    the light danger and warning `.strong` glyph colours stay identical (§6 A6).
14. The gated state is readable: labels, values and locks pass at full strength on dimmed shapes (§4, §6 D13).
