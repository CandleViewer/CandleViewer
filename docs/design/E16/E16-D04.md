### 1.4 The degradation ladder and the AC 2 sentence

The ladder is the server's (`20` :904–908; `24` §13.4.1 :3833–3840): at 80 % of the recording budget (or a runway ≤ 7 days) a warning; at 90 % new auto-record starts are refused and retention accelerates; at 95 % (or less than 5 % of the volume free) the write gate closes and symbols without an open position are dropped as gaps — open positions keep writing (`24` :3854: INV-B11-a outranks ADR-0015's "recording halts"; E16-D02 :496). Nothing on the ladder touches order placement (`24` :3845 "Order placement never reads the budget"). The design states the order in text on every level ≥ 1 frame (AC 2): **"Recording pauses before trading is affected."** (`storage.degradation`, a `new` key, §14), followed by the detail line "At 90 % of the recording budget auto-record pauses; at 95 % recording stops for symbols without an open position; orders and positions are never gated by the disk." The tool reads the sentence on every frame whose producer is `level` ≥ 1 and fails an inverted order (§12 R-5).
