**Projection formula** (shown verbatim on the card, with the live numbers substituted):
`days until auto-record pauses = (90 % × cap − used) ÷ daily growth`. With the board figures:
`(180 GB − 41.2 GB) ÷ 1.45 GB/day ≈ 96 days`. When growth is ≤ 0 or unknown (`projected_full_at` = `null`) the
headline reads "Steady" and the formula line reads "Usage is not growing, so there is no projection." Below an hour of
history it reads "Too early to project: less than 1 h of history."

### 4.2 CMP-177 RecorderStatusRow (shipped spec)

**Contract change (this ticket).** The catalogue's `recording: boolean` + `onToggleRecording` cannot express what
the recorder does: it lingers, refuses, gates, degrades and errors (E16-D01 F4), and "stop" is limited (decision 3).
The row therefore takes a `state` enum and explicit actions. The catalogue entry is updated in this PR, and the
handoff checklist is `docs/design/E16/E16-D02-handoff.md` (`16-design-system-brief.md` §11.4, §13.3 item 8).

| `refusal`             | `"symbol_cap" \| "disk_pressure" \| null`                                                                                        | only with `state` = refused                                              |
| `startedAt`           | `string \| null`                                                                                                                 | UTC                                                                      |
| `stoppedAt`, `keptUntil` | `string \| null`                                                                                                              | stopped rows                                                             |

## 5. Auto-record disclosure and review (decision 2: A′, silent)

Auto-record stays silent: no toast, no prompt. Four elements carry discoverability instead.

### 5.1 The reason badge and its "Why?" popover

Every row shows its reason as a CMP-011 Tag: "Manual", "Auto · chart open", "Auto · position open", "Auto · rule",
"Auto · alert". Auto badges are followed by a CMP-002 IconButton "Why is SOLUSDT recorded?" (info glyph) that opens a
CMP-017 Popover:

> **Recorded automatically**
> SOLUSDT's chart has been open since 10:13 UTC; recording started after 60 s, at 10:14. It stops 30 min after the
> last SOLUSDT chart closes. Data is kept for 30 days.

### 5.3 One-click stop, within today's limits (decision 3)

The ticket asks for a one-click stop on a `chart_open` row (AC3, from E16-D01). Today's backend cannot honour it while
the chart is open: `DELETE` is refused with 409 while a trigger is active, and a forced stop re-arms within a tick
(E16-D01 §2). So the row offers "Stop" as a **disabled-with-reason** control: it is focusable, it names the trigger
and it says how to stop ("Close the chart; recording stops 30 min later"). Once the chart closes, the row is
`lingering` and offers "Keep recording". Nothing in the design promises an undo or a suppression; a suppress primitive
is a `post-ga` candidate (decision 3).

## 9. Motion and live updates

### 9.1 1 Hz updates never move the layout

- The `recorder` topic updates at 1 Hz. Values change **in place**: no row animation, no flash, no colour pulse,
  no re-sort while the user is on the page (a sort applies on the next explicit sort or page load).
- Numeric cells use tabular figures (`type.num.sm`) in fixed-width, right-aligned columns (§1), so a value going from
  "9.9 GB" to "10.0 GB" never shifts a neighbour.
- A row entering or leaving the list appears or disappears without a transition; focus never jumps (if the focused row
  leaves, focus moves to the next row and the live region says which symbol left).

### 9.2 The only animated element: the disk meter

| Property                        | motion-safe                                                                      | motion-reduce (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`) |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Disk meter fill width (CMP-023) | tween to the new value over `motion.duration.normal` (200 ms), `motion.easing.decelerate`; at most once per update | jump to the new value, no tween                  |
| Purge progress bar              | same tween                                                                       | jump                                             |
| Starting spinner (CMP-014)      | rotation per CMP-014                                                             | static glyph, the word "Starting" carries it     |
| Everything else                 | none animated                                                                    | none animated                                    |

### 9.3 Live region

One polite live region (`role="status"`) holds the summary line. It announces **changes of state only, at most once
every 10 s**, coalesced to the latest: "ETHUSDT degraded", "Disk 81 % full, auto-record pauses in about 9 days",
"SOLUSDT stopped recording". Numbers that tick (rates, sizes, lag) are never announced. Banners for an incident or
for disk level 3 are announced once as `role="alert"`; they never repeat while the condition holds.

### 10.4 Computed contrast (WCAG 2.x, resolved tokens)

Method: `packages/ui/tokens` at this PR's base, themes as in `themes.json` (dark = primitives + semantic-dark; light
adds semantic-light; high contrast adds semantic-hc), same formula as `docs/design/_tools/contrast_check.py`. Text
pairs need 4.5:1; non-text parts (icons, the meter fill and outline, the focus ring) need 3:1.

| Use                                         | Foreground                     | Background                    | Dark    | Light   | High contrast | Need |

| Since, rate, legend, formula line            | `color.text.secondary`         | `color.surface.raised`        | 8.81    | 8.98    | 14.35         | 4.5  |
| Updated time                                 | `color.text.tertiary`          | `color.surface.canvas`        | 5.96    | 6.36    | 11.87         | 4.5  |

| Warning banner                               | `color.text.primary`           | `color.status.warning.subtle` | 9.86    | 15.62   | 11.16         | 4.5  |

| Links ("View gaps", "Open recorder")         | `color.text.link`              | `color.surface.raised`        | 6.66    | 5.86    | 6.66          | 4.5  |

**Findings, applied in the boards:**

1. **State words are never drawn in a status colour.** `color.status.*.strong` as text reaches only 3.39–4.11:1 on
   `color.surface.raised` in the light theme, so every state word uses `color.text.primary` and the tone moves to the
   icon, which needs only 3:1 (passes in all themes). This also keeps colour from being the only signal.
2. **Meter fills use the `.strong` tones for warning and danger.** The `.default` tones read 2.46:1 against the track
   in the light theme; `.strong` reads 3.37:1.
3. **The meter track gets a 1 px `color.text.tertiary` outline.** `color.surface.sunken` against the card is 1.21–1.22:1
   and `color.border.strong` 1.46–1.80:1, so without the outline the 100 % end of the bar is invisible. The numeric
   text "41.2 GB of 200 GB (21 %)" stays next to the meter in every state.
4. Disabled "Stop" uses `color.text.disabled` (2.86–3.74:1). It is exempt as a disabled control, and its reason is
   available as text (tooltip and `aria-describedby`), so no information is lost.

CVD: no meaning in SCR-140 depends on hue alone (each state is a word plus a distinct icon shape: dot, triangle,
octagon, info circle), so the dichromacy simulation of the running and disk-critical boards is attached as a check
of legibility, not of hue coding (§"Penpot boards").

| H7  | Flexibility and efficiency                  | Owners with 20 symbols need to find the largest consumer fast.                                                                                             | Sort by Size and Rate; "· N % of budget" on NearDiskBudget rows; banner actions that filter to the right control.       |

| H9  | Help users recover from errors              | A refused auto-start is silence today (F4).                                                                                                                | "Not recording: 20-symbol limit reached" / "disk nearly full" rows and chips, each with a next step.                    |
| H10 | Help and documentation                      | The projection is opaque.                                                                                                                                  | The formula is printed with the live numbers.                                                                           |
| T1  | Trading: safety state is never colour-only  | Disk levels are traditionally colour bands.                                                                                                                | Each level is a word, a number with units and an icon; ticks at 80/90/95 % are labelled in the legend.                  |
| T2  | Trading: no surprise side effects           | Browsing ten symbols must not fill the disk (P2b).                                                                                                         | The 60 s dwell is stated in the default statement; auto rows show their start time and cost.                            |
| T3  | Trading: open positions are sacred          | Stopping a position's recording would hide evidence (INV-B11-a).                                                                                           | Stop is disabled with the reason on position rows; level 3 keeps position rows recording, and the banner says so.       |

## 15. Deviations and open questions

- **D-1 (contract gap, for the amendment PR):** no response carries the policy values the copy must read
  (`autostart_delay_s`, `autostop_grace_s`, `max_symbols`, `disk_cap_bytes`, the 80/90/95 thresholds). Proposed: a
  read-only `policy` object on `RecordingStatus` (REST) and the `recorder` WS frame. Until then the client would have
  to hard-code values, which decision 1 forbids. Raised with virtarix-worker-2 on the amendment thread.
- **D-2 (CMP-177 contract):** `recording: boolean` and `onToggleRecording` are replaced by `state` and explicit
  actions (§4.2). The catalogue entry is updated here; the handoff checklist covers the change.
- **D-3 (audit vocabulary):** the catalogue says `recorder.started|stopped|…`, the ticket `recorder.start|stop|…`.
  The design depends on neither; the server's audit catalogue decides.
- **D-4 (themes):** the boards are drawn in the dark theme (default). Light and high-contrast are covered by the
  computed contrast in §10.4 rather than duplicate boards: switching the token theme in the shared Penpot file
  changes every agent's view at once.
- **D-5 (Viewer):** SCR-140 is an owner/admin route, so a Viewer never sees it; the Viewer case is drawn only as the
  REC chip's "restricted" state (§5.4).
- **D-6 (Restart feed, Silent):** taken from US-REC-008 (restart a feed; silent-failure alarm). "Silent" is derived
  client-side from `last_event_at`; if the server adds an explicit state it replaces the derivation.
- **D-7 (catalogue sample data):** the catalogue wireframe's "projection 60 d: 118 GB" is replaced by the state-first
  headline and the formula (E16-D01 §3).
