### 9d. Accessibility notes for E35-D04

SCR-085 is `role="alertdialog"` described by the restatement (E35-D04); focus opens on **Cancel** (as SCR-072),
follows §6's order, is trapped and returns to the trigger (A12; E35-S08); `Esc` always closes it (A12; SC-12): before
Arm nothing is armed; a pending arm continues on the server and resolves in the SCR-080 row ("Arming…", then "Armed ·
DEMO"; CA-20). Pending and progress are text, never a spinner alone, with no motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`
(A9). `Enter` on SCR-085's radios never arms (SCR-006 submits on `Enter`, E09-D06 §5); "Arm on DEMO" is a plain button
naming the act and ignoring activation for 400 ms (as SCR-076). The radios move with arrow keys; a disabled LIVE stays
focusable with its reason (14 §0.5 item 5); DEMO/LIVE is text plus icon, never colour (A1, A6), at the ≥7:1 target
(A7). SCR-006's fields have visible labels and format hints. Nothing extends a challenge in place; its time is said
politely at 60 s and 10 s (E09-D06 §6), and re-sending the arm issues a fresh 5-minute one: WCAG 2.2.1's Essential
exception (Level A), not Extend (a simple action, the same limit, at least ten times), as extending a request-bound
step-up would defeat its purpose (DJ; E35-D04 confirms with `05`'s owner). A refusal is assertive once with its
reason (A8); "Armed on DEMO" is polite; a rule's first fire in an episode is assertive (`05` §6.2), but a trail can
fire each minute (`cooldown_ms` 1000, `max_fires_per_hour` 60), so later ones coalesce into a polite "{rule}: {n} more
fires", against `05` §6.2's never-coalesced rule: E35-D04 settles it with `05`'s owner as an Announcements-panel
choice. Pause and resume are polite once per episode (A11), riding on E17-D01's assertive Read-only entry (private
feed down over 10 s), so no outage is said twice. Engine down is assertive once plus a banner (A10); progress is
polite and throttled. State chips pass deuteranopia, greyscale and high-contrast checks (`05` §4 item 2, §5 item 6);
SCR-086 orders with Move up / Move down, announcing "{rule}, 1st of 2" (`05` §3.4); SCR-084 states gaps in text.
