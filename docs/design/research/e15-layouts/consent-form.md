# E15-D01 — Consent form: layout and workspace research session

Read aloud and signed before any recording starts (discussion guide §2). The participant keeps a copy; the signed form
is kept on the private research drive, apart from the notes, and is the only document that links a name to a session id.

**What the session is.** Up to about 65 minutes: a 45-minute interview over your own trading screen, then about 20
minutes with six printed sync pictures and ten preset cards. It is about how you arrange, link and switch your trading
screens. It tests the design, not you, and it touches no order and no account.

**What is recorded.** Your screen and your voice during the interview, the note-taker's written notes, your answers to
the six sync pictures and your card sort. Nothing else: no camera and no keystroke log.

**Before we start.** Please close any API-key, key-management, funding, wallet or transfer screen, and any account,
balance or position view the session does not need. If one appears anyway, the facilitator says "Pausing the recording"
and stops at once, and that part is cut before the recording is stored.

**Where it is kept.** On the private research drive, readable only by the research team: the owner and the named
facilitator and note-taker. Never in the code repository, the design tool, the issue tracker or a chat. A second
analysis pass may use an AI tool on de-identified notes only, never on the recording.

**How long it is kept.** The closest rule is `docs/plan/04-security-program.md` §15.3 (Personal data: collect only what
is needed and use it only for its purpose). It sets no retention period for research recordings, so until the owner
sets one (findings OQ-2), recordings are deleted when the owner approves the findings addendum that reports them (on
issue #205), and the deletion date is written into the findings. Notes stay on the drive; only redacted results reach
the findings.

**Your rights.** You may stop at any time, skip any question, ask for more time or ask us to pause the recording, without
giving a reason. You may also withdraw afterwards: your recording and notes are then deleted and nothing you said is used.

**Your pseudonym.** You are one of P1 to P6 in every note, sheet and report. No artefact carries your real name, an
account id, any part of a key or a real position size; quotes are edited to remove them before anyone else reads them.
