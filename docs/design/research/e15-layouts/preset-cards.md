# E15-D01 — Preset card deck and card sort: SCR-022's ten presets

- **Use:** Part 2 of a session, about 8 minutes, after the sync stimuli ([`discussion-guide.md`](discussion-guide.md));
  results go on the sheet below and into [`findings.md`](findings.md) §2.2, whose ranking is the pre-registered prediction.
- **Source:** the ten presets of `docs/plan/14-screens-catalogue.md` SCR-022's Type line, named and described exactly as
  there. The catalogue describes none of the five grids, so their lines come from the shipped
  `services/api/candleviewer/api/workspaces_presets.py`; no source describes 3+1, so its line is a marked designer's
  line. SCR-022's checklist names five other presets, which are not cards (report §2.2).
- **Material:** text-only cards (the sort tests words, not thumbnails) at 16 px or more, numbered on the back; shuffle
  before each session and note the order.

## Instructions (read verbatim)

1. **Open sort:** "These are ten ready-made screen arrangements. Put them into groups that make sense to you, as many or
   as few as you like, and give each group a name." Record the groups and their names verbatim.
2. **Ranking:** "Now put all ten in the order you would use them, most used first. Put aside any you would never use."
   Record the order and the cards set aside.
3. **Gaps:** "Is anything missing that you would want ready-made?" Record verbatim.
