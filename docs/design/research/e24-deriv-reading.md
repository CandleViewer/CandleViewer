### 3.4 The cascade (SCR-054's 500 prints/s for 10 s)

5,000 prints · 40 client buckets of 250 ms · 125 prints per bucket · Bybit at most 20 pushes in those 10 s (one per 500 ms) · 2 client buckets per push · the `liquidations` WS topic throttle 500 ms (`ws/topics.py`). Every print is in one of those pushes — batching is a cadence, not a loss (F15, C13).
