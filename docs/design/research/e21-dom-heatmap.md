## 10. Owner session protocol (ready to run; nothing below has been run)

### 10.1 Participants, consent, data

Three sessions of 45 min: S1 the Owner (P1); S2 an intraday manager (P2a); S3 a swing manager (P2b), who also runs T6 **keyboard-only**. The moderator is never the participant: S2 and S3 are moderated by the owner; S1 is moderated by a second person the owner names, or by an agent reading this protocol aloud. If neither is available S1 runs self-moderated from the script, and the sheet records "self-run" as a limitation of that session. The moderator reads: _We record the screen and audio for internal design use; you may stop at any time and have your recording deleted; the recording stays on the owner's machine and is deleted when the findings are accepted; only anonymised notes appear in the findings._ The harness is a replay of the recorder capture (below) with synthetic account state: no Bybit connection of any environment, no key of any kind, no live backend. The ticket's security note ("the harness runs against the demo environment with synthetic account state only") is met by the stricter replay; notes carry initials, never names.

**Harness precondition (G4).** A recorder-captured BTCUSDT volatile window at depths 50, 200 and 500, declared on the sheet by capture date and window; until it exists the sessions do not run. The paper prototype for T6 is SCR-050's ASCII (`14`:1195–1209) and the `05` §6.3 ladder table (`05`:181–189).
