- **Triangulation rule.** A recommendation needs the same finding from ≥2 participants; single-source findings are reported as single-source and

  single-source and provisional** until a second participant per AT type confirms them. An S3 or S4 finding on Q4–Q6 is first-class (#239 AC2),
  not an appendix.
- **Screener (S3, S4).** Daily use of their screen reader or magnification for at least a year; enough trading-chart familiarity to attempt T5
  and T6 (knows what a bar's delta and a point of control are, or is sent a one-paragraph glossary with the consent form). Recorded in the
  context block, never used to exclude a recruited participant after the fact.
- **Pilot.** One pilot run of §3–§5 before S2-a, with a non-participant where one is available (a colleague who will not be recruited), else with

## 4. Gesture-feel block (S1, S2; S4 for T2–T3 only) — reference machine, interactive prototype (P-B)

- Runs only when P-A and P-B hold; otherwise it is deferred and recorded "not run". Start with the frame counter visible for one minute so a
  frame-rate problem is seen before a feel finding is written. Momentum on (`26` §3.11); optional, one participant per session type: repeat
  once with OS "reduce motion" on (`26` §12) — record whether the instant snap felt worse, better or unnoticed.
- S1 and S2: both a wheel mouse and a trackpad are connected; T1 is done once with each (the second device is optional if time is short), and a
  trackpad zoom gesture is tried once so the `ctrlKey` collision (report Q1-b) is observed on the actual machine: did page zoom fire, did the chart
  zoom price, did both? Then, with the prototype's armed state shown: zoom price with the plain wheel over the price axis (report Q1-k — the only price-zoom wheel since the 2026-10-20 ruling dropped `Alt+wheel`);
  then, as a hazard probe only, hold `Alt`, wheel over the price axis, move onto the plot and click without releasing `Alt` — record whether anything
  other than the market order's own arm-gated path reacted (report Q1-b: the prototype binds nothing to `Alt+wheel`, so any reaction is a slip the
  design must never reintroduce). **Electron check (only when the prototype runs in the shell build):** tap `Alt` alone and
  release, then press `→` — did the menu bar take focus, did the arrow leave the chart? Repeat as hold `Alt`, wheel over the price axis, release `Alt`,
  press `→` (report O13: the condition for the shipped `Alt` chords and the `Alt+G` candidate — `Alt+wheel` itself is dropped). S4 does T2–T3 here with their own input device.
- Nothing is explained before a task. The moderator never names a gesture; if the participant asks "does the wheel zoom?" the answer is
  "try what you would normally do".

## 6. Analysis and approval — two stages

**Stage 1 (now; nothing waits for the sessions).** The owner reads the report's §Decisions at a glance and comments `approved` on #239 (or rework
notes naming D-/Q-ids) by 2026-10-22. The orchestrator then posts the §10 text per consuming issue, each marked "(heuristic)", so E11-D02/D03/D06
can proceed in Sprint 04 (report AC3, AC4; via E11-D09 #331).

**Stage 2 (after all sessions).**

1. Thematic coding of the notes per task; a theme needs ≥2 participants to become a recommendation (#239 Test plan); AT-specific items follow the
   §2 single-source rule.
2. For each Q1–Q6: confirm, amend or overturn the report's D1–D6; **every finding that contradicts a planned behaviour is flagged with the
   doc:section it contradicts and added to the report's §Contradictions table** — the contradictions are the valuable part (#239 Analysis).
3. S3/S4 findings are appended to the report's §Findings Q4–Q6 as first-class results and settle, as single-source provisional decisions unless a
   second AT participant confirms them: `role` (Q4-e), the live-region cadence (Q4-d), the ColumnPicker default at 200 % (Q5), the chip wording and
   announcement (Q6-b). The report's AC2 row changes from "not met" to met only when both sessions ran.
4. The owner comments `approved` again on #239 for the updated decisions; they are re-posted on the consuming issues with a link to the filled §7.
   Recordings and notes stay in the private drive; only the filled §7 sheet and the redacted screenshots are linked.

**Chart interaction principles (board 3; one page):**

1. The wheel zooms time over the plot and scales whichever axis it is over — price over the price axis, bar spacing over the time axis — with no modifier at all; the horizontal delta pans; nothing in the plot is drag-only.
2. Every state the chart enters by gesture or on its own — locked scale, paused at the live edge, merged bars — is shown in text and is reversible without dragging.
3. Arrow keys move a cursor over the data, not the view; the view follows the cursor. The cursor and the crosshair are one thing; the last input moves it.
4. The crosshair addresses price levels; snapping to open/high/low/close is a choice, never the default.
5. The data table is a place to work, not a copy to read: the same bars, actions on every row, the primary columns fit at 200 %.
6. Aggregation is disclosed with its ratio and its guarantee; it is never phrased as confidence or staleness.
7. A trading modifier is never also a free navigation gesture at the same pointer position; where they would share a key the navigation binding yields (`Alt+wheel` dropped), and never a timer.
8. Linked charts share time, never price: a neighbour's crosshair is a dashed line at the same moment, and your scale, your lock and your readout stay your own.
9. Every jump has a way back: a jump lands the target in the centre, marks it, says "Paused at …", and `End` returns to live.
10. TradingView habits are matched where cheap (`Shift+wheel` pans, double-click resets) and every divergence is named (arrows move a cursor, `Ctrl+↑/↓` unbound).
