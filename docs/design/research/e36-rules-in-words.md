## 8. Session protocol (five 45-minute sessions; ready to run; nothing below has been run) and the owner walkthrough checklist

### 8.1 Framing, participants, consent, data

**Opening script (read verbatim):** "One thing in this design is fixed and is not what we are testing: every position carries a stop-loss held by the exchange, and a rule can move that stop closer but never remove it; moving it away is an owner-only, audited exception that managers cannot use, and is not what we are testing. We want to learn how you would say your own rules, how you would sort the things a rule can look at, and whether a written rule means to you what it means to the engine." (C-2.6; `20` P4; CMP-158.) **Participants:** the owner (P1) and the account managers who hold `rules:author` (P2; `10` §6 "who enters it"); five sessions of 45 minutes; one session with a keyboard-only / screen-reader participant or, failing that, the accessibility specialist as proxy (§8.4). **Data:** demo environment and synthetic accounts only; no real account ids, balances, API keys or open positions on screen; recordings and transcripts are **internal-confidential** (`20` §13.4 Confidential), stored in the owner's private storage, never in the repo, account identifiers redacted before any analysis pass; an AI pass, if any, works only on de-identified notes (the E30-D01 protocol's rules, reused; G8). **Analysis:** affinity map by the owner plus one independent pass; anything backed by a single participant is a **signal**, not a finding; the owner's `approved` comment on #872 replaces CDO sign-off.

### 8.2 Corpus capture (12 min; RQ1, RQ3)

"Tell me the last five rules you ran by hand this month — exactly as you would say them to a colleague." Record verbatim. Then, for two of them: "Say it as a rule the terminal could run: when … if … then …" Record the order of clauses, the words used for the stop's direction ("tighten", "trail", "never loosen", "move up") and whether the holder of the stop is mentioned. Then the probe: "When you say break-even, do you mean your entry or entry plus fees?" — record the basis named and the word used for it (C23, Q10). Then show the read-back of §2 for the nearest template and ask "Is this what you said?" — record agreement, and every word the participant changes. **Scores H1** (clause order and the ratchet said aloud) and **H3** (does any rule need more than one "either … or" level — if yes, record it verbatim as a graph-only candidate).

### 8.3 Card sort (12 min; RQ2) and comprehension test (10 min; RQ4, RQ5)

**Deck A (32 cards):** the 23 shipped ids behind SCR-081's 24 surfaced names (`orderbook_imbalance` has none; `book_pressure` is added beside `dom_imbalance_ratio` as its second candidate), plus the 8 estimated ids not already among them (`delta_divergence`, `absorption`, `exhaustion`, `trade_cluster`, `iceberg_executed_volume`, `queue_position_estimate`, `hurst`, `adl_risk_proxy`); each card shows the `display_name`, the `description` and the unit — never the id, and no badge (the sort tests topical grouping; the badge is asked afterwards). **Deck B (optional second pass, 105 cards):** the full vocabulary. **Procedure:** open sort ("put together what belongs together, name each pile"), then closed sort against the two candidate sets side by side — CMP-157's `price | position | orderflow | risk | detector` and R2's seven — then "where would you expect to see that a card is a guess rather than exchange data: on the card, on its pile, or both?" **Scoring:** a category is **confirmed** when ≥4/5 participants place ≥80 % of its cards together; "detector" / "risk" are **refuted** when fewer than 3/5 form such a pile; a split of `orderflow` is adopted when ≥4/5 separate the profile cards (Q1). **Comprehension test:** S1, S2, S3 shown one at a time, order counterbalanced, each for as long as the participant wants; "In your own words: what does this rule do, when would it act, and what would it change?" A response is **correct** when it names (a) the trigger, (b) every condition with its grouping (S2's "either … or" inside "and"), (c) the action with its direction (break-even / tighten; never "move" alone for S2) and, for S1 and S3, the break-even basis the participant restates — entry, or entry plus fees — scored against the sentence's "(your average entry)" and (d) the limit (once per position / every 60 s); any miss is incorrect. **Pass:** ≥4/5 correct per summary; otherwise G1–G13 are revised (the word that failed is recorded) and the next three summaries are retested before D02 draws the header. Then, for S3 only: "What did '(estimated)' tell you?" — record whether it is read as precision ("roughly") or as provenance ("a guess from public data"), the E25-D01 Q4 question; and "Where else would you expect to be told?" — scores **H5** against §5.5.
