## Decisions at a glance

Plain statements of the six heuristic decisions; evidence in §Findings, paste-ready form in the protocol §10, owner calls in §Open questions. Board 3 carries this list.

1. **D1 Pan/zoom:** the wheel over the plot zooms time around the bar under the pointer; **over the price axis the plain wheel zooms price and over the time axis it compresses bar spacing** (Q1-k — the only price-zoom wheel: `Alt+wheel` is dropped and no modifier+wheel is bound, ruled 2026-10-20 (orchestrator, owner delegation) — one path per action, the TradingView/Bookmap precedent, the Electron menu-bar hazard); Shift+wheel and a horizontal wheel delta pan; double-click resets the axis you click, and the plot only when no drawing, order line or handle is under the pointer (Q1-d); zoom stops hard at the engine's bar-spacing clamp; no drag-region zoom in v1. At the edges: older bars page in behind a text loading band and the view never jumps; the right-hand margin is kept while the chart follows live. Charts in a sync group share their time range, never their price range (Q1-h, Q1-i).
2. **D2 Autoscale:** dragging the price axis, the price-zoom wheel or keyboard Up/Down switches autoscale off — a vertical plot drag does not while autoscale is on (ruled 2026-10-20 (orchestrator, owner delegation)) — and shows a text badge "Price scale locked" on the price axis that is itself the way back, announced once — never silently, never a prompt, never a hidden preference; the lock is forgotten on reload and is never shared through a sync group. The time-axis cluster reads `auto` (follow live; its off state is the badge "Paused at hh:mm"), `fit` (fit every loaded bar) and `>` (latest bar, = `End`); a go-to-date jump lands the target bar in the centre, marks it, and `End` brings the chart back (Q2-c, Q2-f).
3. **D3 Crosshair:** the readout crosshair snaps to the bar horizontally and is free vertically by default; snapping to open/high/low/close is an option; the drawing magnet is a separate E14 decision. A linked chart shows a remote crosshair as a dashed vertical line only — no horizontal line, no readout, no magnet — and the keyboard cursor broadcasts exactly as the pointer does (Q3-c).
4. **D4 Keyboard:** arrow keys move a data cursor, not the view; the view follows when the cursor reaches an edge; the vocabulary is bar, price level, extreme, page; End returns to live. The cursor and the pointer crosshair are one object with two drivers, the last input wins; the plot is one tab stop (roving tabindex), the price axis, the time axis and the state badges are the next stops in reading order (Q4-i).
5. **D5 Data table:** a working surface, not a read-only copy: bar rows that expand to price levels, actions on every row, seven primary columns that fit the 1280 px minimum viewport at 200 % zoom (CVD rejoins the primary set when the table panel is wider than about 1 400 px at 200 %), the rest one click away.
6. **D6 Aggregation:** a text chip states that columns merge bars, the ratio, what stays exact and how to get back, whenever more than one bar shares a column; it is never worded as a confidence or staleness claim.

Owner calls with dates: O2 prototypes (2026-10-22), O3 — ruled 2026-10-20 (orchestrator, owner delegation): the plain wheel over an axis scales that axis and `Alt+wheel` is dropped, so no owner call remains; O4 footprint readout unit (2026-10-20), O5 column volume, L1 wicks and whether load can ever merge candles (2026-11-06), O13 name the shell ticket that removes the Electron application menu — now for the shipped `Alt` chords and the `Alt+G` candidate only (2026-10-22).

## Method note

- **Desk research only.** No participants were recruited, no sessions run, no one interviewed; the moderated sessions, task timings and AT sessions remain for the owner with the companion protocol. **No quote bank: nothing below is an observation of a user.**
- **Frame:** Nielsen's 10 plus the trading-specific heuristics in `05` — keyboard-first as speed (§1), single hotkey layer (§3.1), no hover-/drag-only interaction (§3.1 item 6), colour never alone (§4), equal-status canvas alternatives (§6), reduced motion (§7).
- **Evidence, cited per finding:** (a) plan text (`26` §3.11/§12, `05`, `11`, `14`, `15`, `E11.json`, the shipped `apps/web/src/keymap/keymap.json`, `docs/generated/default-keymap.md`); (b) corpus 01, 02, 04, 05, 07, 09, 20 and `docs/research/findings/E12-bar-modes.md`; (c) public pages opened 2026-10-19 (TradingView, Lightweight Charts, DeepCharts, Bookmap, W3C, MDN, Highcharts, accessible-chart literature), cited by URL at first use; (d) the prior draft (branch `design/e11-d01-chart-nav-research`, 2026-10-14), reused only where re-verified — the list of its claims retired by the audit is in the PR description, not here.
- **State of the product:** no chart UI is shipped (`packages/chart-engine/src` is scaffold; no chart feature in `apps/web/src/features`), so every CandleViewer statement is about _planned_ behaviour; shipped are the keymap — one chart-navigation binding, `chart.zoom_price` = Shift+wheel; the other nine Chart-context bindings are footprint, interval, order and drawing commands (`default-keymap.md` Chart rows) — and the SCR-117 accessibility settings screen. **No interactive surface exists:** the E06 spike is a throwaway Node bench scene with modelled GPU cost and no GPU context (`docs/plan/spikes/E06-K02.md` summary and "Why modelled GPU cost"; `packages/chart-engine/bench/scenes/README.md`; its only "wheel" is a scripted sweep in `bench/driver.mjs`), no wheel or pointer handler exists in `packages/chart-engine` or `apps/web/src`, and E06-T01 #220 / E06-T02 #221 are open. T1–T6 wait on the prototype decision (O2).
- **Confidence labels:** every finding is "(heuristic)" plus a basis — **high**: two or more independent CandleViewer sources agree and public prior art concurs, _or_ a contradiction verified directly in each source (the grade is then confidence that it exists; the recommended side is graded on its own); **medium**: one CandleViewer source, plan sources of one lineage, public sources only, or split prior art; **low**: a single third-party page or an inference. Nothing is marked "observed".
- **Competitor coverage:** TradingView from its own pages — the Advanced Charts Shortcuts and Featuresets pages (the embeddable library's documentation and defaults, re-read 2026-10-19; not necessarily tradingview.com's behaviour) and the Supercharts hotkey list (https://www.tradingview.com/support/shortcuts/, "Chart" group, 43 rows, accordion expanded in a browser on 2026-10-19 — script-rendered, so the URL alone shows no rows); the Help Center "Hotkeys" article (https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000623399-hotkeys) covers desktop-app windows only; corpus 01 §6.4 rows are third-party (TradingCode.net) — low. ## Findings: navigation, scale and crosshair (Q1–Q3)

### Q1 — Pan/zoom gesture bindings and muscle memory (→ E11-S04; decision D1)

**Planned.** `26` §3.11 gesture table: drag plot = pan time (and price if unlocked) with momentum; wheel = zoom time at cursor; `Shift+wheel` = horizontal

**Findings.**

- (heuristic, medium) Prior art splits, and much of it is silence: documented are LWC's default magnet-to-close and Bookmap's lines at the pointer; TV documents a magnet for drawing points only; DeepCharts documents none (free is inferred from silence). - (heuristic, medium) **Contradiction Q3-b — two magnets.** Readout: E11-S07 weak = 8 px, no default. - (heuristic, medium — plan sources checked directly; prior art: TV syncs the crosshair but documents no remote appearance) **Q3-c — the remote crosshair and who broadcasts.** `26` §3.11 says the crosshair "broadcasts to the sync group", CMP-190 is "synced across all panes + linked charts", US-LAY-003 says the crosshair in the other charts moves "at the same timestamp", and E11-S07 publishes "the crosshair position … once per frame" — none says what a linked chart draws, nor whether the keyboard cursor broadcasts. Decisions: (1) **A remote crosshair is a dashed vertical line only** at the synced time — no horizontal line (price is not synced, Q1-h, and the neighbour's price means something else on another symbol), no readout, no tooltip, no axis price pill, and no magnet applied remotely (the magnet is the origin chart's reading of its own OHLC); the time pill may be drawn on the remote time axis because time is what is shared. This keeps the local chart readable and matches what the plan actually shares — a timestamp. (2) **The keyboard data cursor broadcasts exactly like the pointer:** cursor and crosshair are one `position` (Q4-i), so a keyboard step in chart A moves the dashed line in chart B on the same frame, which is what "focusing should mirror hovering" (Chartability) requires and what keeps the accessible path equal-status (CMP-190 A11y).
