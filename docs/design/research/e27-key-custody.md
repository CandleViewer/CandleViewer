## 5. Persona walkthroughs and findings (all `(heuristic)`)

**W1 — P4 adds `sub_001` and its key** (scenario 1, `10:202`; SCR-125 → SCR-126, or SCR-016 step 3 on a fresh
install). The owner registers the account shell — label, UID, kind, environment (`22:16318-16324`); "API keys are

with only the allow-listed rights — `ContractTrade` `Order` and `Position`, and at most `Derivatives`
`DerivativesTrade` (`22:2644-2654`) — and its allowlist set there to the egress IP (`ADR9:16`).
Back in CandleViewer the owner pastes key and secret into write-only fields and presses one action, behind the `keys`
step-up (`SU:521`), that verifies and stores (`22:2636`). The catalogue draws another machine: "IP whitelist …
[ + add ]", "[ Test connection ]", then "[ Save (step-up) ]" (`14:2449-2453`).

**W2 — P4 rotates on schedule** (scenario 2, `10:203`; SCR-128 → SCR-127). The signal is the T−14 d warning
(`rotation_state: warn`, `22:16453-16457`; `11:403`) and the admin strip's "Key for sub_002 expires in 12 d → Rotate"
(`E42-D02:704`). The owner creates the new key at Bybit with the same rights and allowlist, pastes it, confirms with

## 7. Compromise first response (RQ5; PROPOSED, `(heuristic)`; C-12.12 stands)

**Why this order** `(heuristic)`. Nothing in CandleViewer stops someone who holds the key from trading at Bybit
(`22:2787-2791`), and an actor who can sign at the egress IP needs no fund right to move value (KV-E11, `TM:298`). Only
step 2 stops it, so it comes at once, as C-12.12 requires, and waits on nothing; the freeze before it takes a moment and
no code, and stops CandleViewer's own trading on the account. The revoke here can be refused — `leave_positions` while a
position lacks its native stop, `flatten_first` while the account is still not flat (`22:14133-14142`) — so a revoke
tried before step 2 never holds it up: a refused revoke sends the owner straight to the Bybit delete. After step 2 an
account with no other signable key reads as dead (`auth_error`), and the revoke leaves its positions at Bybit, each with
whatever native stop it still carries, the stop-loss refusal lifted (`22:14160-14173`): CandleViewer can then neither
read them nor re-assert a missing stop (`22:14154-14156`; `22:14166-14168`), so the compromise card sends the owner to
check each position's stop-loss in Bybit's dashboard. A key deleted at Bybit and still stored here fails every check
from its next one, hourly or on the next auth failure (`24:4669`; `RB:53`): order entry latches off for every account
until the key is revoked here and a restart passes the check (`23:4259-4263`; `RB:30-31`), and the key's account is
disarmed, which no restart undoes (`RB:90-92`). That read-only state is C-12.12's containment ("set read-only mode",
`CON:817`), not a cost. Step 3 follows step 2 at once: a revoked key leaves the stored-key check (`RB:6-7`), and a latch
that came first can end only after the revoke. The replacement waits for step 4: an actor who can sign at the egress IP
is in the process, a dependency, the host or a coerced Owner (KV-E11, `TM:298`), and IR-01 revokes every session before
any credential (`04:1403-1404`). Step 4 holds the audit too, as C-12.12 lists it (`CON:824-825`): it shows whether the
leaked key placed orders with no CandleViewer intent (SR-070, `04:826`), the mark of such an actor, and a new key held
the same way would be in that actor's reach.

**Flatten first: the owner's choice, never the drawn default (OQ-16).** A flatten through CandleViewer — the freeze's,
or the revoke's `flatten_first` — reads and closes the exposure through the account's signable keys (`22:14146-14150`),
so waiting for one before step 2 keeps the leaked key valid at Bybit until the flatten ends. Whether the owner may take
that route is queued for the owner as item 24 on #2701 (an amendment of C-12.12, C-16.1; #732 comment 6364262239). The
drawn path never waits on a flatten; a frame that offers one says, before the confirm, that the key stays valid at Bybit
until the flatten ends, and a refused or unfinished flatten sends the owner to the Bybit delete at once.

**Where the path ends.** With step 5, the replacement, which comes only once step 4's three steps are done
(`CON:824-825`). These screens draw no control for step 4: the compromise card names C-12.12's three steps and hands
them to E27-T05's compromise runbook (#900), as C18 asks, which also asks it to say how the audit reads the window's
order history at Bybit while the account has no signable key (IR-01 pulls it after its new keys are in, `04:1404-1406`).
Only then does the account trade again: the owner releases the freeze, a rung-4 `killswitch` step-up with no grace
(`E42-D01:880`), and re-arms the rules by hand, since the freeze and the revoke disarm them (`22:6341-6344`; `22:14133`;
`11:423`), they "stay disarmed until re-armed by hand" (`10:217`), and an import re-arms nothing (`22:2636-2716`). Where
a failed check disarmed the account first, the path also needs a restart whose check passes and the account's trading
