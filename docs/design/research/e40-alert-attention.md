### 6.1 What a `once` alert does when its firing is snoozed, muted or in quiet hours (as built, and the PROPOSED rule)

As built: `gate()` runs `snoozed` and `muted` before `trigger_mode` (`GATE_ORDER` in `alerts/gating.py`), and only `_fire` disarms a `once` alert, so a `once` alert whose firing is stopped by snooze or mute **stays armed** and fires on the next evaluation that passes the gates; the stopped firing is **not recorded** — a non-storm stop increments `cv_alert_suppressed_total{reason}` and writes no `alert_deliveries` row (`alerts/evaluator.py`), which US-ALRT-006 sc. 2 ("its alerts are recorded but produce no sound or notification") will not allow once symbol mute has storage (G5) — G16. Quiet hours have no gate at all in `alerts/` (the setting is untyped, G4). PROPOSED rule for E40-T05, TradingView's precedent (§3): a `once` alert stays armed through snooze, mute and quiet hours and disarms on its first **delivered** firing; a muted or quiet-hours firing is recorded as a `suppressed` row with `suppressed_reason` `muted` / `quiet_hours` (SR-3's pattern), shown "Muted · recorded" (F2, Part B NC-7); a snoozed firing is not recorded (US-ALRT-006 sc. 1: "it does not fire until the snooze expires").

## 8. The 2 s budget (protocol item)

SCR-092 budgets "≤2 s from condition met to visible toast"; US-ALRT-001 says "an in-app toast fires within 1 second" and "end-to-end latency ≤1 s" (C3). The question is **perceived adequacy**: does a 2 s delay change what the trader does? Heuristic prediction (H5): for a price-cross used as an entry trigger at BTC speed, 2 s is a move the trader wanted to be in; for threshold and swing alerts it is irrelevant. P-7 (§9) shows the same recorded burst with the toast delayed 1 s and 2 s and asks the participant to act; the owner's answer confirms the 2 s bar or escalates it to the Architect (ticket Performance note). Until then the design input is: budget 2 s (SCR-092), target 1 s (US-ALRT-001), the toast carries the fire time so a late toast is read as late, not as now (SCR-092 "names the alert, symbol, metric value and time").
