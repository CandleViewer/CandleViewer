## Method note (agent-delivery adaptation, owner decision 2026-09-25)

The ticket calls for 3–4 moderated sessions on an instrumented prototype. Delivery is a solo agent with one human owner, so — as `e16-recorder-mental-model.md` and `e30-d01-session-protocol.md` did — the sessions are replaced by (1) the heuristic evaluation of §2 against the shipped contracts and code, (2) arithmetic from the tool over the repo's fixtures (§3), and (3) the protocol of §8 and the checklist of §9 for the owner. **The repo holds no representative real tape** (§3.0): the only recorded prints are 46 BTCUSDT prints over 9.3 s; the corpus the big-trade golden calls "recorded" is assembled from documented shapes at a fixed 30 s cadence; the conformance tapes are synthetic. So no density, threshold or percentile number below is market evidence; the rates 50 / 500 / 2 000 come from US-BIG-001 and the ticket. Reading-rate figures cite Brysbaert (2019) or are marked `(heuristic)`.

## 8. Session protocol (ready for the owner; nothing below has been run)

### 8.1 Participants, consent, data

Four sessions of ≤45 min: **S1** the Owner (P1), **S2** an intraday manager (P2a), **S3** a swing manager (P2b) **keyboard-only** (mouse disconnected), **S4** the Owner or a manager with **NVDA** active (or VoiceOver), default verbosity.
