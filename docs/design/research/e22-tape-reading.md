## Method note (agent-delivery adaptation, owner decision 2026-09-25)

The ticket calls for 3–4 moderated sessions on an instrumented prototype. Delivery is a solo agent with one human owner, so — as `e16-recorder-mental-model.md` and `e30-d01-session-protocol.md` did — the sessions are replaced by (1) the heuristic evaluation of §2 against the shipped contracts and code, (2) arithmetic from the tool over the repo's fixtures (§3), and (3) the protocol of §8 and the checklist of §9 for the owner. **The repo holds no representative real tape** (§3.0): the only recorded prints are 46 BTCUSDT prints over 9.3 s; the corpus the big-trade golden calls "recorded" is assembled from documented shapes at a fixed 30 s cadence; the conformance tapes are synthetic. ### 3.5 Bubble radius over the drawn range (tool `worked.bubble`; r 3 px at the class's big threshold → 24 px at its tier-2 threshold = 4× big; area-linear PROPOSED)

Only qualifying prints are drawn (US-BIG-003), so the floor is the tier-1 threshold and the cap the tier-2 threshold (a client-side 4× multiple of the engine's one threshold, G8); above the cap the radius stays 24 px and the bubble carries its notional label (F14). With the cap at 4× big for every class the radius depends only on the multiple of the threshold, so one table serves majors, mid-caps and a seeded new listing (the seed = the newlist tape's p99, 1 732 USDT, on the nearest 1-2-5 step — synthetic, shape only):

| Multiple of the big threshold | Majors USDT (250k → 1M) | Mid-caps USDT (50k fallback → 200k) | New listing USDT (seed 2 000 → 8 000) | Linear | Area-linear (`sqrt`) |  Log |
| ----------------------------: | ----------------------: | ----------------------------------: | ------------------------------------: | -----: | -------------------: | ---: |
|              1× (floor = big) |                 250 000 |                              50 000 |                                 2 000 |    3.0 |                  3.0 |  3.0 |
|                          1.5× |                 375 000 |                              75 000 |                                 3 000 |    6.5 |                 10.2 |  9.1 |
|                            2× |                 500 000 |                             100 000 |                                 4 000 |   10.0 |                 14.1 | 13.5 |
|                            3× |                 750 000 |                             150 000 |                                 6 000 |   17.0 |                 19.7 | 19.6 |
|             4× (cap = tier 2) |               1 000 000 |                             200 000 |                                 8 000 |   24.0 |                 24.0 | 24.0 |

Largest gap between the laws: area-linear vs log 1.1 px (at 1.5×), linear vs area-linear 4.1 px (at 2×); cap-to-floor ink 64× under every law — the clamp, not the law, sets the dynamic range; area-linear adds 594 px² per 1× step, and a true "area ∝ notional" would put the floor at r 12 px (F14). The sell mark is a filled square of the same area as the buy disc (F16): side = r·√π (1.772 r), 5.3 px at the floor and 42.5 px at the cap (17.7 px at the r 10 px glyph threshold); a square on the diameter would carry 27 % more area (4/π). The **shape floor** `(heuristic)`: the shape tells the side only from ≈r 5 px (side 8.9 px; 1.085× the threshold — at 1.1× the law gives r 5.3 px; tool `shape_floor`): at the floor a 5.3 px square and a 6 px disc read alike, and on the dense tape 14.1 % of the flagged prints sit below r 5 px (6.0 % below r 4 px, 49.4 % below the glyph threshold; synthetic, shape only) — F16, T5; raising r_min is E22-D03's. Three densities for board 05 and the protocol: thresholds at p99 / p95 / p80 of the window's notional (1 % / 5 % / 20 % of prints flagged — the last is the cap's edge, F19).
