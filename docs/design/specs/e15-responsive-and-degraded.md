## 1. What this document is

The normative rules a builder reads to make the docked workspace usable on a narrow or short window and on a device that draws slowly, and to make a restoring workspace show what is ready. It states each rule once with its reason. It does not cover the non-E15 causes of SCR-153 (exchange feed, recorder, rule engine: E08, E16, E35 own them and share CMP-092's vocabulary), the measurement code (E15-K01, #371), tablet or phone layouts (out of the product), or motion (E15-D14).

## 3. The tabbed collapse (BQ-06; A41)

1. **One group.** Under 1280 px wide every leaf of the stored tree is shown as one tab of a single CMP-251 DockTabGroup filling the dock area, in the tree's reading order (row before column, start before end); the members of an existing tab group stay adjacent in their order. A floating window (SCR-027) is untouched. The stored layout never changes: nothing is saved, the layout is not marked dirty, and widening shows the arrangement exactly as it was.
2. **The active tab** is the panel focused at the moment of collapse; at a launch that starts narrow, the layout's last focused panel, else the first in reading order. Widening restores the arrangement with focus on the same panel; a maximised panel's state returns with it.
3. **Reaching every panel.** The tab row is CMP-251's tablist (arrows, `Home`, `End`, automatic activation); `Ctrl+\` and `F6` still move between the chrome regions; the panel picker is unchanged. A hidden tab mounts on activation (E15-D02 D-14) and keeps E15-D03 N-5's 30 s unsubscription.
4. **Overflow.** When the tabs do not fit, labels shorten from "Chart · BTCUSDT 1m" to the kind's glyph plus the symbol (the word kept in the accessible name); if they still do not fit, a "{n} more panels" button at the row's end opens a menu of the rest, each entry with its pending conditions (§5). A horizontally scrolling tab row is not used: a tab out of view carries no count.
5. **What the transform costs.** No WebGL context is created or lost by the collapse or the restore (E15-D03 N-7); a hidden panel is the same mounted panel behind a tab.

## 5. Hidden tabs: nothing fails in the background (BQ-08; A42)

1. **A tab's name carries its panel's pending conditions** after its region name, in a fixed order — an error, then disconnected or stale, then unread alerts ("Positions, 1 alert"). The visible tab shows the registry glyph and the word for an error and a CMP-012 count joined to the name; never colour alone. The overflow button sums its tabs ("3 more panels, 1 with an error").
2. **Account-level events are not panel-bound.** A fill, a rejection, a rule trigger and an automatic flatten keep their global surfaces and `05` §6.2's assertive announcements whether or not their panel is visible.
3. **Panel-local states mark the tab.** A restored panel known to fail (a delisted symbol) is marked from the layout document and the instrument metadata before it mounts.
4. **An armed order-capable panel hidden by the collapse** (A42): its tab carries the environment badge (CMP-075) and the arm word; the client asks the server to disarm it on hide and shows the server's answer — the disarm authority is the server's (C-2.21, C-12.4), and the design never arms or disarms by itself; the disarm is announced once, in A30's form: "One-click trading disarmed: {Kind} hidden in a tab."; trading hotkeys act only on the visible, focused panel and never reach a hidden tab. Re-arming is the user's act on the visible panel, as anywhere else.

## 6. The reduced profile (BQ-10, BQ-12)

### 6.1 The trigger (PROPOSED until E15-K01, #371, calibrates; then an A-row)

The p95 frame time over a rolling 60 s window above 18.2 ms — US-LAY-001's ≥ 55 fps with four order-flow panes, above C-14.2's < 16 ms p95 so a device on budget is never told it is slow. Sampled only while the window is visible and focused; starting 10 s after a boot, a restore or a layout change (E15-K01's warm-up); a single frame over 200 ms is dropped as an outlier (E15-K01's GC case). Detection reads frame time only, never the user agent (`11` US-LAY-008).

### 6.2 The proposal: a non-modal CMP-092 row, never a dialog

A modal would take focus from an order panel and could catch a held key, so the proposal is an `info` row in the shared banner slot (E15-S08's non-blocking suggestion). It names the measurement with its window and its target (§10 CP-1), the three changes with this device's values before → after (CP-3), and offers [Use reduced profile] [Details] [Not now]; "Don't ask again on this device" sits in Details (CMP-069 `inline`).

### 6.3 What it sheds — three items, nothing else

| Sheds                   | From → to                                                                                          |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| heatmap depth           | one tier lower, from E15-K01's config object                                                       |
| panels you aren't using | their update rate becomes 200 ms; the focused panel, order entry, positions and orders keep theirs |
| new panels              | capped at E15-K01's `recommendedMaxPanes`; existing panels stay                                    |

Existing panels are never closed, hidden or minimised by the profile: above the cap the proposal says "You have 6 panels; all stay. Adding a 7th asks first." (E15-S01's confirmation quotes the reduced cap). The engine's own LOD step-down (`06` §4.4) and US-DOM-005's automatic depth shedding are separate mechanisms with their own disclosure, never this row.

### 6.4 Never shed

The reduced profile may never shed, and no variant may hide or slow:

- the environment badge (CMP-074 / CMP-075)
- the arm state
- positions and orders, and their update rate
- every error state
- the Safety cluster (CMP-211)
- the SCR-152 region
- every SCR-153 row
- the protection states (the native stop)
- the stale and disconnected marks
- alerts and fills
- the focus affordance
- the announcement rates of `05` §6.2

Trading remains possible or is explicitly blocked, never ambiguous (SCR-153).

### 6.5 Hysteresis and the declined path

The profile never changes without a click. Accepted: it stays until the user restores it; no automatic restore offer. Not now: no proposal again this session, even if the condition returns. Don't ask again: none on this device until the user resets it in Display quality on this device (§7). A new session re-arms with a fresh window. Declined: the full profile continues and the measured cost stays visible in §7's control.

## 7. Manual controls and device scope (BQ-11)

One device-scoped state, three places: (1) the banner row's action while a row shows; (2) the workspace toolbar's ⋯ menu, "Display quality on this device ▸ Full · Reduced", with "Measured: 23.8 ms per frame (p95, last minute)" as its caption and "Ask again about display quality" when the proposal was silenced, plus two command-palette entries, "Reduce display quality" and "Restore full display quality" (no new chord); (3) SCR-118 Data & performance — E46-D02's screen, a hand-off row with this copy, never drawn here. **Disclosure:** the desktop app "Remembered for this device. Your other devices keep their own setting."; the browser "Remembered in this browser on this device." (browser storage is per browser profile and is cleared with the site's data). **A preference, never a permission:** the server never reads it, it changes no risk limit, the panel cap is a client display limit only, and it is stored locally without a user id; it holds no credential. A reduce or a restore applies within one frame of the click, and the measured cost shown is the last window's, refreshed while the menu is open.

## 8. SCR-153's two E15 rows (BQ-09)

Two CMP-092 `info` rows — the GPU never blocks trading — in the shared banner slot below the SCR-152 region, after the shipped `gpuFallback` row in the routing order. **Variants:** the suggestion (with its Details); reduced active; full by choice (shown once after a restore, then the control lives in §7); exit (the row leaves; SR-45's "Full quality restored."). ## 9. SCR-150 boot and workspace restore (BQ-14, BQ-15)

**Two clocks.** SCR-150 stays the full-screen boot with E15-D02 §5.14's one stage list — Starting the app · Signing in · Opening your workspace · Connecting to market data — its slow line at 5 s and its actions; the boot's budget is ≤ 2.5 s to the interactive shell. The restore begins when the shell is up: the dock frame with real headers and skeleton bodies (E15-D02 D-14). US-LAY-002's ≤ 3 s runs from the dock frame's first paint to interactive.

**The fill order** (subscriptions and first snapshots): shell → private account state (orders, positions, wallet: they feed the environment, arm and position displays every header shows) → the focused panel → the remaining charts → the order-flow panels → the auxiliary panels. Private state goes first because it is small and safety-bearing, and it is `23` §9.3's order; the focused panel stays first among panels, so the thing being looked at is usable first. Under the collapse only the visible tab mounts; a hidden tab mounts on activation.

**Interactive** means a panel's header controls work and its first snapshot is painted, or its own error state shows; the workspace is interactive when every visible panel is (under the collapse, the active tab).

**Past 3 s** each waiting body says in words what it waits for (CMP-038, CP-19), one line in the restore-notice row lists what is still loading and what is ready (CP-18), and the shell, the menus and the ready panels stay usable. The skeleton's shimmer stops at 5 s (CMP-015), so no motion stands in for progress; there is no spinner over the canvas; after 10 s a waiting panel offers [Retry] in its body, isolated from the others (PROPOSED). Nothing implies the app has frozen.

**One pane fails.** E15-D03's `error-delisted` state, unchanged (CP-21); every other panel is fully interactive.

**Announcements (polite):** SR-27 per completed stage; the slow line once at 3 s; at interactive one string that also carries the failures, so the restore speaks once (CP-22), within E17-D02 §11's shared budget. `workspace.restore_completed {ms, paneCount, failedPanes}` fires at that moment.

## 10. The copy deck (BQ-16)

Show the measurement with its window and its target, in milliseconds at p95, never a verdict about the machine (an average hides the slow frames the p95 counts). Every degraded string says what still works. No alarm words on an info row; never "Trading is off" where cancel and close remain. `{…}` are values from the probe or the layout; the figures shown are fixtures. E15-D02's strings are kept word for word where they meet the rules (SR-45, SR-46, `D02`:544–548); a change is named in the row.

## 11. Contrast under reduced settings (BQ-13)

A lower heatmap depth lights fewer rows, so more of each panel shows the ramp's zero stop and the canvas behind it; it adds no colour. The tool therefore measures every chrome element drawn over panel content against the darkest and the lightest of the hosted backgrounds — the five surfaces and the eleven `color.heatmap.*` stops — in dark, light and high contrast (E15-D03 §11.2's lo / hi method, the ratios through `contrast_check.py`); the chips, rows and callouts carry their own fill, so their text is measured on that fill. Text 4.5:1, non-text 3:1. A failure revises the chrome treatment, never the bar; none fails.
