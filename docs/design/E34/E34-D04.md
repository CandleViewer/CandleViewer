### 1.3 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 Severity** (BQ-3; the E28-D05 mapping, RQ-3). **Critical:** a task a keyboard-plus-screen-reader user cannot complete (`05`:282), or a safety gate weakened for such a user. **High:** completable, but no compliant path for a WCAG 2.2 A or AA criterion or a binding `05` rule, or a state carried by colour alone. **Medium:** an at-risk criterion, or a keyboard, name or focus behaviour left unstated or stated two ways. **Low:** wording, cadence or a reported stretch. **Info:** recorded, no fix. A G-T2 miss lands at Medium at least, a Recurring-item miss at Low at least (`DS`:15). The ticket's "blocker" (AC 3) is a Critical or High row.
- **R-2 Closing** (BQ-3). An F row names its criterion, its line, the fix, one owner and its A-rows. **Sign-off needs every Critical and High A-row applied**; a Medium or Low row may be logged instead with its owner and a date, by 2027-01-14 (the end of Sprint 16, before E34-D05's sprint). The hand-off (E34-D05, #1043) holds until the Critical and High rows are applied. Info rows carry no fix.
- **R-3 Verdicts.** `compliant` · `at risk` (an F row) · `non-compliant` (a High or Critical F row) · `out of scope — <owner>`.
- **R-4 Request rows** (`DS`:79). RQ-1 and RQ-2 are read at their pushed heads and quoted by section id. Each A-row's Old is found exactly once in its target at that head and under the named section; the A-rows are applied by the two specs' owner in their PR 1 before they merge (BQ-2), at the local heads §1.4 pins by content, and §13.1's Landed column says where each landed: its New once and its Old gone there, or "fix-forward slice after merge" with its Old still once. ## 3. Table semantics (T §Scope)

- **SCR-061 preview and outcome.** RQ-1 §7 names a `<table>` with the account as row header and, in the same paragraph, a grid with one Tab stop and arrows by cell. A body row holds controls (the include box, the row menu, "Why this size", the override fields), so the table is CMP-049's grid mode (`15`:584), `role="grid"`, the account `th scope="row"` and each column a `columnheader`. **Verdict:** compliant once read as the grid; RQ-1's "`<table>`" is the static case CMP-049 reserves for rows without controls (C-1).
- **SCR-062 treegrid.** CMP-049 `treegrid` (RQ-2 §2.1): one header row for both levels, so every leg cell has its column header and its row header (the account), `aria-level`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-posinset` / `aria-setsize` from the model. **Verdict:** compliant. **Sort:** `aria-sort` sits on the `columnheader`, the sort button inside it; RQ-2 §2.1 puts it on the button (F-11), and a sort must keep each group's legs under it (F-11).
- **Nested leg tables in confirms** (CMP-044 `legs`): a static table, `th scope="row"` the account, read in the dialog's scroll region (`15`:521). **Verdict:** compliant.
