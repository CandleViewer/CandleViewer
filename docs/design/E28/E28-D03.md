### 10.2 Focus and targets

- **Focus:** every interactive element (rows, matrix cells (read-only: §6.1), links, chips, fields, buttons, the readiness row's expander) shows the 2 px `color.focus.ring` (`border.focus`) outside a 2 px gap. The measured pairs are P17 and P18.

### 10.3 Responsive and text scaling (D-14)

- **Width:** desktop only (`16` §8): 1280 × 800 is the minimum viewport. The admin content panel caps at 1280 and centres above that.
- **Matrix:** the symbol column and both header rows are sticky; when the grid is wider than the viewport or at 200 % text it scrolls horizontally (`16` :144 rule 4). 1280 × 800 is the minimum viewport (`16` :270), and no new breakpoint is added. No cell shrinks below 24 × 24.

Each record lists its fields in column order, except that SCR-061 puts the SL cell and the status word first so the stop and the verdict are never pushed below the fold.

### 10.5 Performance at design time (D-13)

The matrix, the SCR-062 grid and the preview use flat fills, 1 px outlines and glyphs only. They use no per-cell shadow, gradient, blur, opacity animation or per-row transition, which keeps SCR-133 at ≤ 600 ms over about 400 symbols, SCR-062 under 4 ms scripting per frame at 50 × 5, and the preview at ≤ 300 ms (`14` SCR-133, SCR-062, SCR-061 Performance).
