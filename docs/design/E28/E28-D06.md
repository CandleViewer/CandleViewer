## 18. The tool

`docs/design/_tools/e28_d06_handoff_audit.py`, stdlib only and offline, run as `python3 -I`. Two stages, chosen by `--stage deck|full` (default `full`; never by which files exist): `deck` reads the deck alone; `full` reads the pack, the hand-off, the deck and the pins file. `deck` reads the deck and the pins (PR 1a). `--check` exits 1 on any FAIL and names the gates a stage does not run; `--self-test` applies one mutation per gate in memory and expects exactly that gate to FAIL; `--json` prints the counts; `--repin-ruled` rewrites the pins file for the stage's cites. Its gates:

- **G-STAGE:** every file the stage needs is present.
- **G-DECK:** the header first, then rows with every field and no other; unique keys; known kinds, statuses, renders; a `frozen` value FZ-n or null; every owner a ticket key; each cited line holds the row's `quote` or `text`.
- **G-STATUS:** a `merged` row cites `22`, `14` or a merged parent; a `RULED` row cites D02; a D04 or D05 row is PROPOSED and names its request row; a D02 row from its §10 "(4) new" keeps PROPOSED.
- **G-CODES:** each of `T`'s eleven codes has a row whose `code` or `rule` is it, or is its header alias (`22`'s served code).
- **G-FROZEN:** FZ-1 … FZ-7 each on at least one row, in order, the freeze PROPOSED in the header.
- **G-COMP:** every CMP on the six `14` Components lines of these surfaces is in §3.2 or named as not used.
- **G-BIND:** every `22` and `MX` cite of §9 and §4 holds its field or route on its line.
- **G-BUDGET** and **G-AHEAD:** every BUD, DATE and SPRINT anchor computed from its source.
- **G-EVENT:** §11's `ACT` cites hold their action names.
- **G-HIGH:** §7.3 lists exactly D05's five Highs, each with an owner and R-6.
- **G-HANDOFF:** hand-off §G has one `16` §11.4 block per changed component, every template line in order, each `[x]` with evidence or `[ ]` with an `N/A —` or `PENDING —` reason.
- **G-AROW:** each hand-off §C anchor line is the entry's last pointer line at the base.
- **G-FILLED:** no placeholder word anywhere in the three texts.
- **G-CITE:** every cite in range, on a non-blank line, equal to the pins file's hash (at the deck stage, the deck's cites).
- **G-RULED:** §6 carries the joint stale-preview sentence verbatim (by its sha256, the same in #840 and #841); §7.2 cites E47-D04 as the focus owner; §7.3 dates every High and says a 1b merge is not the owner's approval; no pre-stage wording about an unmerged parent.
- **G-HYGIENE:** no closing keyword, local path, URL or host, agent name or deny word in the texts, the tool or the pins.
- **G-SCOPE:** the diff touches only this ticket's files (three at the deck stage, five at the full stage).
