## 1. Purpose, method, locked inputs and the rules stated once

### 1.1 What this spec settles

One treatment for every canvas surface, so the remediation stories build one model and not six (T Context): which surfaces exist and what each still lacks, what a focusable unit is and what it is called, how big the accessibility tree may get, which table each surface has and how that table stays live, what the canvas node says, and how QA proves that the accessible layers carry everything the pixels show. It re-decides nothing the locked inputs fix; where merged texts disagree a C-row says which reading it takes (§16.1).

### 1.2 Method (honest)

Desk work at the base under the agent-delivery adaptation: no participant, prototype or screen-reader session exists, and every judgement is heuristic. The tool reads the register's screen surfaces and its E47-D06 rows, `05`'s three fixed column sets, the figures of §4 on their cited lines, and checks every cite at the base or at its pinned head. The NVDA and JAWS walkthrough of a coded prototype is a written session protocol for the owner (hand-off §H), not evidence.

- **R-7 Stale is never live.** When the focused unit leaves its surface's visible range, the table says so in a banner with a keyboard refresh (§6.5); under a stale or disconnected feed every layer carries the feed-state clause (D04E11:264).
- **R-8 Equal status.** The `View │ Table` switch sits in every inventory panel header, never in settings, for every role (D04E11:135; `11`:1155).
- **R-9 Words are E47-D07's.** This file names only that a change is announced and its label (§13); policy, wording and verbosity are E47-D07's (#1306; T OOS).
- **R-10 Gated stays.** A gated control in any layer is T2 (§10's paragraph); nothing in the accessible layers is `inert` or merely dimmed.
