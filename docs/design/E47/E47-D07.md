### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 One pair, by default.** A taxonomy event is written into the shell's one pair (ANN:2–4) unless §2 names it local: polite and assertive, pre-rendered empty at load, owned by the app shell; no component owns a second region for a pair event (`05`:154; D17:249). The named local exceptions (§2): the replies at the control, T-9 and T-10; the panel and control statuses, T-26, T-45, T-46 and T-51 … T-53; the `echo-polite` rows, T-47 and T-48 (§6.3); and T-17, whose `alertdialog` speaks it on focus while the pair does not repeat it (§9; R-6).
- **R-2 Text swapped, never recreated.** The two containers live for the session and are never destroyed or re-mounted; a message is a child node inside its stable container, and a later message replaces it (ANN:86–96; `05`:154).
- **R-3 Assertive is never throttled, merged or cut.** An assertive event is queued by rank (§5.1) and spoken alone and in full, once; nothing ambient delays, merges with or replaces it (`05`:152; T AC2).
- **R-4 Latest wins, never appended.** An ambient field is written as the latest value at most once per cadence, as one composite per cadence in a fixed order; a value already said is not said again (PW:88, :109–111; `05`:151).
- **R-5 One announcement per entity state change.** Bursts of one entity collapse to its latest state (§5.2); distinct events never merge.
- **R-6 No echo across channels.** An event spoken assertively is not repeated by the polite channel as a state change (T Scope).
- **R-7 Focus stays.** An announcement never moves focus or changes the user's input; the auto-flatten `alertdialog` is the one exception (`05`:96; §9).
- **R-8 Nothing is lost by timing.** Every assertive event writes a CMP-060 log entry (2.2.1; `15`:690, :692), the fallback of record when a write overruns the reader (§5.1). The attention set, the fixed safety group of §3.2 (T-11, T-13, T-15, T-17, T-22; T Scope names the kill switch and the auto-flatten), also shows a persistent CMP-045 toast (`15`:531); any other toast follows `15`:531's defaults and is not the record (BQ-9). The event is still spoken once (R-6): its toast or surface is the pair's writer, except for T-17, whose dialog speaks. A local reply (T-9, T-10) stays at its control until the next submit and writes the CMP-060 entry only.
- **R-9 Closed words.** Templates take only the closed slots of §7.1; reasons come from the closed map of §7.3, never upstream text; secret-bearing values are never a slot (T Sec).
- **R-10 Scope.** An announcement is made only from a frame the session may read (`23`:927); it names the account when more than one account is in scope.
- **R-11 A delta is a G-row.** This PR changes no `apps/web` file; every difference from the shipped code is routed to its owner (BQ-0).

## 2. Regions and their hygiene (BQ-1; R-1, R-2)

**The pair.** `ShellLayout` renders `ShellAnnouncer` once (ANN:4): two visually hidden containers, `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-live="assertive"`, both `aria-atomic="true"`, exempt from modal inertness so they speak under a CMP-043 dialog (ANN:86–91). They are in the DOM from the first paint, empty, and stay for the session (R-2). ## 5. The assertive channel: order and no loss (BQ-4, BQ-5; T AC2, T AC4; R-3)

### 5.1 The queue

Each assertive event enters one queue with its rank (§4): **1** the auto-flatten; **2** the other safety rows (kill switch, native-SL failure, lockout, unwind incomplete); **3** execution (fills, rejects, unknown, trade groups, rule actions); **4** connectivity and environment. The queue is drained once per frame, so events of one frame are ordered by rank, then by arrival. The head is written alone and in full; the next write waits for the head's speech estimate, `max(1.5 s, characters ÷ 15 per second)` (PROPOSED, no owner figure; D07-Q3), and a message being spoken is never cut: "auto-flatten first" holds among the events pending together (D07-C12). T-17's write is its dialog taking focus (§9): the reader's focus speech is the queue's item, and the next write waits for its estimate. The estimate is a guess at the reader's rate: a slower rate, a braille display or a reader that pauses on punctuation can overrun it, and the page cannot observe an overrun ("replaced while speaking"), so the drop counter cannot count it; the log entry is the fallback of record (R-8), and the session protocol listens at 100 % and 60 % of the reader's default rate (hand-off §I; D07-Q3). Assertive writes bypass E17-D02's 5 s region budget, which is ambient-only (D17:249, D07-C14). Each assertive write drops the ambient values waiting for the cadence (PW:116–120): the next composite carries fresh values. Nothing is ever dropped from the queue; the drop counter must stay 0 (§12). The shipped announcer keeps one message and a later write replaces it (ANN:23–24, :38–46): the queue is D07-G1, a G-row to E10-S01 (#304).

### 5.2 Bursts of one entity (R-5)

One announcement per entity state change, never per wire frame (`23`:858 sends every state, unthrottled). Collapsing partial fills is a stated deviation from that per-state delivery, for partial fills only: they are not among `05`:152's six, which are never coalesced (BQ-5; D07-C11). the partial fills of one order inside one cadence collapse to its cumulative state, "Partially filled. Limit buy 0.3 of 0.5 BTCUSDT, average 64,000.0." (W-2); a later partial waits out the cadence after the last one spoken and, while it waits, a newer partial of the same order replaces it in place; "Filled" (W-1) is always spoken and supersedes a waiting partial of its order. A trade group speaks one sentence per group state, naming every failed account (W-10), and each leg reject in full (W-3 with the account). Flapping is spoken once (D17:265). Two distinct events, even of one symbol, never merge.
