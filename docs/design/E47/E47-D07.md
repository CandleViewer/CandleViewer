### 1.4 Rules stated once (every later row cites "R-n")

- **R-1 One pair.** Every taxonomy event is written into the shell's one pair (ANN:2–4): polite and assertive, pre-rendered empty at load, owned by the app shell. No component owns a second region for them (`05`:154; D17:249).
- **R-2 Text swapped, never recreated.** The two containers live for the session and are never destroyed or re-mounted; a message is a child node inside its stable container, and a later message replaces it (ANN:86–96; `05`:154).
- **R-3 Assertive is never throttled, merged or cut.** An assertive event is queued by rank (§5.1) and spoken alone and in full, once; nothing ambient delays, merges with or replaces it (`05`:152; T AC2).
- **R-4 Latest wins, never appended.** An ambient field is written as the latest value at most once per cadence, as one composite per cadence in a fixed order; a value already said is not said again (PW:88, :109–111; `05`:151).
- **R-5 One announcement per entity state change.** Bursts of one entity collapse to its latest state (§5.2); distinct events never merge.
- **R-6 No echo across channels.** An event spoken assertively is not repeated by the polite channel as a state change (T Scope).
- **R-7 Focus stays.** An announcement never moves focus or changes the user's input; the auto-flatten `alertdialog` is the one exception (`05`:96; §9).
- **R-8 Nothing is lost by timing.** Every assertive event also writes a CMP-045 toast and a CMP-060 log entry (2.2.1; `15`:533, :690).
- **R-9 Closed words.** Templates take only the closed slots of §7.1; reasons come from the closed map of §7.3, never upstream text; secret-bearing values are never a slot (T Sec).
- **R-10 Scope.** An announcement is made only from a frame the session may read (`23`:927); it names the account when more than one account is in scope.
- **R-11 A delta is a G-row.** This PR changes no `apps/web` file; every difference from the shipped code is routed to its owner (BQ-0).

## 2. Regions and their hygiene (BQ-1; R-1, R-2)

**The pair.** `ShellLayout` renders `ShellAnnouncer` once (ANN:4): two visually hidden containers, `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-live="assertive"`, both `aria-atomic="true"`, exempt from modal inertness so they speak under a CMP-043 dialog (ANN:86–91). They are in the DOM from the first paint, empty, and stay for the session (R-2). The per-message child `<p>` inside the stable container (ANN:41, :93) conforms to `05`:154: the container, not the child, is the region, and a fresh child makes the same words in a later episode speak again (a reading, PROPOSED; D07-A3).

**Writers.** Every surface writes through `announce` or the ambient writer; none renders a live region of its own for a taxonomy event: CMP-212 ToastHost and CMP-045 Toast write the toast's sentence into the pair (D07-A6, D07-A7, D07-A8; D07-C10; the build is D07-G4, E40-S02 #1130), CMP-077 ReconnectOverlay writes the connection rows (ANN:7–10), CMP-117 RiskLockoutBanner writes the safety rows, the ambient writer writes the fields (PW:2–4).

**Local regions (the exceptions).** Two kinds stay local, each a container pre-rendered empty with its control and only its text swapped: (1) the reply to the user's own action at the control that took it: a form error, a refused submit, `role="alert"` once (§11; D07-A3); (2) E17-D02's panel statuses, the panel's `role="status"` (D17:257). A local region never carries a taxonomy event the pair already speaks (R-6).

## 5. The assertive channel: order and no loss (BQ-4, BQ-5; T AC2, T AC4; R-3)

### 5.1 The queue

Each assertive event enters one queue with its rank (§4): **1** the auto-flatten; **2** the other safety rows (kill switch, native-SL failure, lockout, unwind incomplete); **3** execution (fills, rejects, unknown, trade groups, rule actions); **4** connectivity and environment. The queue is drained once per frame, so events of one frame are ordered by rank, then by arrival. The head is written alone and in full; the next write waits for the head's speech estimate, `max(1.5 s, characters ÷ 15 per second)` (PROPOSED, D07-Q3), and a message being spoken is never cut: "auto-flatten first" holds among the events pending together (D07-C12). Assertive writes bypass E17-D02's 5 s region budget (D17:249, D07-C14). Each assertive write drops the ambient values waiting for the cadence (PW:116–120): the next composite carries fresh values. Nothing is ever dropped from the queue; the drop counter must stay 0 (§12). The shipped announcer keeps one message and a later write replaces it (ANN:38–46): the queue is D07-G1.

### 5.2 Bursts of one entity (R-5)

One announcement per entity state change, never per wire frame (`23`:858 sends every state): the partial fills of one order inside one cadence collapse to its cumulative state, "Partially filled. Limit buy 0.3 of 0.5 BTCUSDT, average 64,000.0." (W-2); a later partial waits out the cadence after the last one spoken and, while it waits, a newer partial of the same order replaces it in place; "Filled" (W-1) is always spoken and supersedes a waiting partial of its order. A trade group speaks one sentence per group state, naming every failed account (W-10), and each leg reject in full (W-3 with the account). Flapping is spoken once (D17:265). Two distinct events, even of one symbol, never merge.

## 6. The polite channel and verbosity (BQ-6, BQ-7; T AC3)

### 6.1 Ambient fields (R-4, R-6)

Only the active symbol's fields: bid and ask (T-30), mark (T-31), its position's unrealised P&L (T-32), CVD when a CVD panel is shown (T-33), then the tape summary (T-35) and the big-trade line (T-36) by verbosity (§6.4). One shared writer per symbol (PW:6–7) holds the latest value per field and writes **one composite per cadence** in that fixed order, joined as the writer joins them (PW:88; the joiner is E47-D06's, PW:87); a field whose value equals what was last said is skipped (PW:109–111). Nothing is appended and nothing queues: the 10 Hz stream of a minute yields at most 28<!--=SIM:fast.max-per-field--> writes per field at the 2 s cadence (§10.2). A position size change from the user's own fill is never spoken by the polite channel (T-34; R-6). "Connection quality" is not a field: its state entries are T-25 and T-26, and the latency number is silent (D17:262; D07-C5).

### 6.2 Discrete polite statuses

`status-polite` rows are spoken once per state change at most once per 5 s per region (D17:249); the ambient writer waits out that budget after another writer's message (PW:95–98). A countdown speaks at its marks and at its end only (T-45, T-46): a lockout or grace countdown at 5 minutes and 1 minute left and once at the end (PROPOSED marks); the digits are `aria-live="off"` (D17:262; §11).

### 6.3 Echoes of the user's own keys

Outside the cadence and owned by the control that has focus: cursor and adjustment echoes use E06-D03 D5 unchanged (D03E06:105; D09:208); focus moves after live data are announced by label at most once a second (D04:123). `11`:1035's "≤2/s" reads as D5's cursor rule (D07-A12; CAT:400, :459).
