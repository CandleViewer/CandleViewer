## 0. Read first: what this run is and is not

- **Client portion only.** Backend latency is out of scope (E29 owns the 50 ms; the span `exchange.place_order`
  is where it is measured). The submit posts to a loopback stub. **Budget #4 end to end on Bybit demo
  (`06` row 4) is PENDING E29-K01 (#843)** (section 8).
- **The ticket is a stand-in.** SCR-060 and `packages/order-ticket-core` are not on `main`. The reducer is a
  pure stand-in for `size()` + `validate()` (scaled-bigint parsing, as Decimal parsing would be). The real
  parts are the keymap path (`KeymapHost`, dispatcher, registry), the `<Chart>` binding over the engine and
  the Electron shell. The spike page does not boot the SCR-010 shell.
- **The single-key buy is a stand-in.** `B` is `ticket.side_buy` (sets the side, not arm-gated), so no
  single-key arm-gated "buy market" exists. `Ctrl+Enter` (`ticket.submit`) is measured as is; the extra key
  exists only on the spike branch.
- **Every figure below is "dev host, not reference (OL-1)".** The decision rests on the _relative_ comparison
  between placements. The demo leg (budget #4 end to end) is PENDING E29-K01 (#843).
- **Both client legs have results.** The in-process leg (section 6.1) isolates the reducer and the keymap dispatch;
  the Electron leg (section 6.2) adds the trusted input pipeline, React commit, the next frame, the replayed WS load
  and the chart engine in both RunModes. Under xvfb with no GPU the page had no WebGL context (`gl: "none"`), so the
  engine drew nothing in either RunMode: the RunMode comparison covers the engine's non-GL main-thread work only.
- **The host is below the reference class** (section 2). A budget met here is conservative evidence; a budget
  missed here is inconclusive (the E15-K01 evidence rule) and goes to a reference-machine owner leg.

## 1. Method

1. Pin `main` (`136142433`) and the fixture capture; fix the seed. Section 4 is written before any run.
2. The harness builds the web app with `CV_SPIKE_E30_K01=1` and drives Electron through Playwright
   `_electron` against the loopback stub (`apps/desktop/e2e/perf/`), egress guard on, name resolution
   failing for every non-loopback host. Headless Chromium is an optional cross-check.
3. The spike page mounts the real `KeymapHost` (armed through `setArmPort`, confirm skipped: BQ 6), the real
   `<Chart>` (engine in `worker` and in `inline` RunMode), a stand-in ticket and a WS client. The recorded
   BTCUSDT capture is replayed through the stub's gateway at a rate scaled to the 350 KiB/s of `23` section
   16.3 (the scale is in the results: BQ 8).
4. Matrix: 3 reducer placements x 2 RunModes x 2 runs; per cell 1 000 samples per trigger after 50 warm-up
   samples, paced at 20 Hz. Each run is a fresh Electron profile.
5. Time is attributed on the worst cell of each budget with Electron `contentTracing` (`CV_K01_TRACE=1`):
   reconciliation, layout, reducer.
6. The decision table (section 6) is filled from the results, then the acceptance scenarios are applied
   (section 7).

### 4.1 Finding: budget #4 has no client share (proposals for the later `06` amendment PR)

`06` section 5.2 gives the backend <= 50 ms and, in the same row, a demo REST round trip of <= 250 ms. Together that
is the whole 300 ms, so the client share is 0 ms, before the 20 to 50 ms Tailscale RTT that `06` section 3.1 counts
inside #4. E30's own Performance notes ask for no more than 20 ms between gesture and request, and US-ORD-006 allows
50 ms. These cannot all hold. **PROPOSED, not applied here:**

- **`06` section 5.2 amendment (wording).** Replace the single "p95 < 300 ms on demo" line with a split: "Budget #4
  is measured at three points. Client: gesture to `fetch()` <= 50 ms p95 (US-ORD-006, US-DOM-007; measured by the E30-K01
  harness). Backend-added: <= 50 ms p95 (`exchange.place_order` span). Exchange: Bybit demo REST round trip,
  recorded, not budgeted by CandleViewer. The end-to-end figure is p95 < 300 ms on demo **measured from the request
  leaving the client** (so the client share is budgeted separately and Tailscale RTT is reported, not summed in), and
  p95 < 500 ms on live as before." The exact numbers of the exchange leg wait for the E29-K01 demo run (section 8).
- **`14` SCR-060 (same PR).** Replace "click to ack <= 500 ms p95" with a reference to `06` budget #4, so the screen
  catalogue stops holding a second figure.
- **`32` risk-register row (text, number taken at the amending PR).** `RSK-NEW · Budget #4's arithmetic leaves no
client share, so a passing client can still miss the end-to-end gate.` Taxonomy: R2 render performance. Trigger:
  gesture to `fetch()` p95 above 20 ms while the backend and exchange legs sit at their budgets. Likelihood L3,
  impact I3 (score 9, to be confirmed by the register's owner). Mitigation: the `06` split above; the K01 harness
  becomes the E30-Q03 gate. Owner: the architect. Review: when the E29-K01 demo run lands.

largest spreads are on the gesture rows (store / worker click 4.8 against 15.5 ms; sync / worker stand-in key 14.2

between other lanes' suites (section 2.1), so the spread measures the shared host as much as the page. Section 6.1's
proposal (apply the 15 % rule only where the p95 is at least 10 % of its budget) would decide the sync and store
end-A rows; it is not applied here.

**Reading.**

- **Recompute end A (the reducer).** Sync and store: 0.3 to 0.5 ms p95, p99 at most 1.3 ms, in both RunModes:
  at least 16 times under 8 ms. The worker placement: 3.7 to 6.2 ms p95 and 12.6 to 37.4 ms p99 (its end A is the
  reply arriving, so it carries the round trip under main-thread and host load). The clone itself stays at or under
  one 0.1 ms clock step (`cellMeta[].cloneCostMs`: p50 0, p95 0.1), so the hop is scheduling, as in section 6.1, and
  it uses 46 to 78 % of the 8 ms at p95 and exceeds it at p99.
- **Recompute end B (to the first frame after the commit).** Every cell sits at p50 15.4 to 16.0 ms, one 60 Hz
  frame, whatever the placement: the definition waits for the next `requestAnimationFrame`, so it is floored by the
  frame cadence (the smoke run's observation, section 8, now confirmed on 36 000 samples). p95 is 18.7 to 26.7 ms

## 7. Decision (PROVISIONAL until ADR-0046 is ratified; in-process and Electron evidence)

Applying the acceptance scenarios to section 6:

- **Recompute p95 above 8 ms in the sync placement?** On end A, no: 0.01 ms in-process and 0.3 to 0.4 ms in Electron.
  On end B, yes, 19.6 to 22.6 ms, but for every placement alike, at a p50 of one 60 Hz frame (section 6.2). The
  scenario's mandate ("B or C") does not separate the placements on this evidence: the miss is the end-B definition
  (the first `requestAnimationFrame` after the commit) and the host (below-class, so INCONCLUSIVE, never FAIL), not
  the reducer. SCR-060's "every input recalculates risk/fee/margin in <= 8 ms (pure client-side)" (`14-screens-catalogue.md`:1476 on `main`) is a
  recompute figure, which end A meets by a wide margin. **Proposal for the ruling, not applied:** gate the 8 ms on end
  A (or on the commit), and report end B against "within the next frame" (16.7 ms at 60 Hz) rather than 8 ms.
- **No placement meets 50 ms gesture to `fetch()`?** Every placement meets it: p95 2.4 to 10.4 ms in all 18 gesture
  cells (3 placements x 2 RunModes x click, `Ctrl+Enter`, stand-in key), also inside E30-D06 section 1.3's 20 ms client dispatch. No `32` row and no
  scope question are forced by the numbers. The tail (p99 up to 79.1 ms on this shared host) is recorded for E30-Q03.
- **Placement: the Electron numbers confirm option B.**
  - Against C (worker): the hop costs 3.7 to 6.2 ms p95 on end A in Electron (sync and store 0.3 to 0.5), its p99 of
    12.6 to 37.4 ms exceeds 8 ms, one run's p95 read 8.3 ms (worker / worker, price key), and its end B is the
    highest (22.6 to 26.7 ms p95 in the worker RunMode). A worker would make the reducer slower and buy nothing.
  - B against A: end A is the same (0.3 to 0.5 ms p95). On end B, store has the lowest p95 in both RunModes (19.3 to
    19.7 and 18.7 to 19.1 ms, against sync's 20.9 to 22.6 and 19.6 to 20.8) and the steadiest runs. The gap (1 to 3 ms)
    is within this host's noise, so it is not proof that B is faster; it shows B costs nothing, and B keeps the
    reducer free of React in `packages/order-ticket-core`.
  - **Recommendation: option B**, the pure reducer called imperatively from the handler into a store outside React,
    the submit imperative from the handler through one transport call that does not use `apiFetch`'s retry.
- **ADR-0046's reopen clause.** The ADR reopens the worker if end B exceeds 8 ms p95. It does, literally, but the
  worker placement's own end B is the worst of the three, so reopening C cannot fix it. The ADR carries an evidence
  update that says so; its status stays Proposed and the reading of the clause is for the ratification.
- What this run cannot say: the inline RunMode with real GL (`gl: "none"` here), the sandboxed renderer, and
  reference-class numbers (section 2.1).

## 9. What E30-T01 needs from this finding (PROVISIONAL until ADR-0046 is ratified)

- **Reducer placement:** option B (section 7): a pure reducer in `packages/order-ticket-core`, called from the
  handler into a store outside React. No worker, so no message boundary or snapshot-ownership question. Electron confirms it (section 7): the
  worker hop costs 3.7 to 6.2 ms p95 on end A in the renderer (0.06 to 0.7 ms in-process) for a reducer that costs
  0.3 to 0.5 ms there, and does not lower end B.
- **Where the metrics are measured:** `cv_ticket_recompute_ms` from the input event's `timeStamp` to the reducer
  returning (end A) and to the first `requestAnimationFrame` after the commit (end B); `cv_ticket_click_to_ack_ms`
  needs the E29 ack and stays pending E29-K01. The gesture-to-`fetch()` mark is the instant before the single
  transport call.
- **CI gate baseline** ("<= 8 ms p95, +20 % regression"): anchor it to the in-process reducer harness for end A
  (stable, display-free, about 0.01 ms: a +20 % gate is meaningless at that scale, so gate the 8 ms absolute). The Electron
  end A is 0.3 to 0.5 ms p95 at a 0.1 ms clock step, so +20 % is one step there too: the same absolute gate. End B
  cannot carry an 8 ms gate while it ends at the next frame (p50 one 60 Hz frame on every cell, section 6.2); gate it,
  if at all, against a frame (16.7 ms) on a named host class, pending the ruling proposed in section 7. The gesture
  to `fetch()` baseline on this host is 2.4 to 10.4 ms p95 against 50 ms (and E30-D06 section 1.3's 20 ms).
- **Submit dispatch path:** imperative from the handler; `apiFetch`'s CSRF retry stays off the measured path (a
  retry would add a second `fetch()`); whether production keeps it is T01's call, with the retry counted in the
  metric.
- **What the harness offers E30-Q03 (#1155):** the Electron driver, the stub (`POST /api/v1/orders`, the recorded-WS
  replay), the in-process leg, the mark names and the results schema.
