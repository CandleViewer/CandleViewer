## Read first: what this run is and is not

**Every number in §4 is SYNTHETIC.** The repo holds no account-consistent recording: every private Bybit fixture is a
single row transcribed from the docs (`packages/fixtures/bybit/README.md:106`), and the order-path recording is E29-K01

The model is partly circular: "closed-PnL" is our model of what the venue reports (assumptions in §3). The Gherkin
"Given the 3-day comparison harness has run against a demo account" is **not met by this PR**: the demo leg (one demo

## 1. Question and decision criteria (stated before the run, from #880)

Which source is authoritative for the daily-loss cap's figure; is unrealised PnL counted continuously or sampled; what
are the day-boundary semantics; what happens in a provisional-fee window; what cadence fits the rate budget.

1. **Accuracy:** #880's wording is "within 0.5 % of `GET /v5/position/closed-pnl` over the window". The criterion this
   finding **applies** restates it twice; both restatements are open to the coordinator's ruling on the BQ list:
   - **Restatement (i), the base (BQ-1 default):** 0.5 % **of the cap**, not of the day's figure: 0.5 % of the seeded
     250 USDT (`21:4312`) = **1.25 USDT**. The tables also give each divergence as a percentage of the cap.
   - **Restatement (ii), the reference (this finding):** closed-PnL **settled and dated by the closing fill's exchange
     time** ("settled": every record of the day's closes, all posting lags elapsed), not the records whose
     `updatedTime` falls in the window as the venue reports them. Reason: the venue dates a record by `updatedTime`
     (A5), which moves a close across a boundary when its record posts after it (§4.1); the cap must date a loss by
     the fill. The as-reported row stays in §4.1 as the honest answer to the ticket's wording: **against closed-PnL as
     reported, the hybrid does not meet 0.5 %** (193.53 USDT, 7 hours over), and no source does.
2. **No REST on the hot path:** the evaluator has ≤ 5 ms p95 (#881); it reads cached or streamed state only.
3. **Deterministic under replay:** the same events give the same figure (rule-runtime determinism, `docs/plan/30-release-roadmap.md` §7.3 item 6).

checks the backfilled-loss case and checks the offset-change rule for four offsets. Seed 880, 3 days, two accounts (A,
B), BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT. Money is `Decimal`.

- **Trail.** Account A on BTCUSDT is scripted: a position carried over the first boundary; a short opened at D1 09:00
  into a rising mark; a partial close at 13:59 whose opening-fee attribution is provisional for 90 s; the rest closed
  **at a loss** inside a 20 min reconnect gap (backfilled over REST, so no `execPnl`) whose record posts late, at
  16:15; a short-to-long flip; a close at 23:59:20 whose record posts at 00:00:50; an ADL close; and a close at 22:59:30
  just before the shifted boundary. The other three account-symbol pairs are seeded random round trips with 1-3
  partial closes. Funding settles every 8 h (BTC) and 4 h (ETH), the cadences of the funding-history fixtures. Account
  B receives a 1,000 USDT transfer. An offset change to +60 min is requested on D1 at 12:00; under the §5.6 rule the key
  in progress runs on to the first +60 boundary at or after its old end, so the keys are K0 `[0 h, 24 h)`, K1
  `[24 h, 71 h)` (47 h, the transitional key) and K2 `[71 h, 72 h)` (the window ends at 72 h).
- **Assumptions.** A1: a record's `closedPnl` = price PnL − the opening fee prorated to the closed size − the closing
  fee + the funding accrued since the open, prorated. A2: the record posts 0-90 s after the closing fill, **except** the
  backfilled close's record, which posts 2 h 10 min late on purpose so that the unconfirmed state of §5.5 is observed
  at two hour-ends. A3: the record is rounded to 4 dp, fees to 8 dp. A4: maker 0.02 %, taker 0.055 % (illustrative;
  the product reads real rates). A5 and A6: §2.1. A7: the position projection's average entry equals the venue's, so
  a backfilled close's price PnL is exact; the pessimistic margin comes only from the fee and funding bounds.
- **Sources, per account and day key, at each hour end** (72 × 2 rows in `hours`):
  - **local (from the stored rows):** Σ (`closed_pnl` − `exec_fee`) over the day's fills by exec time; a hypothetical
    aggregation, nothing on `main` computes it. No funding; the backfilled fill contributes its fee but no PnL; opening
    fees count when paid.
  - **closed_pnl (as `DayRiskFigures` reads it):** Σ records whose `updatedTime` is in the day and ≤ now (A5).
  - **wallet:** equity now − equity at the day start (cash, transfers and unrealised).
  - **hybrid (the decision, §5):** per close, the stream's own record figure dated by the closing fill's exec time.
    Until the record posts: a provisional close books the **opening fee at the taker-rate bound** and no received
    funding (§2.1: the closing `execFee` is on the push; the opening-fee and funding attribution are what lag; this
    run varies the opening fee, not the closing fee); a backfilled close books its projection figure with the same
    bounds and credits no gain. The venue record then replaces the figure; it can only tighten a confirmed figure.
  - **settled:** every record of the day's closes, dated by exec time, all lags elapsed (the reference of §1 (ii)).
- **Cause attribution** is an exact ladder from each source to closed-PnL, one step per named cause, and the steps sum
  to the divergence (asserted): `backfill_null_execpnl`, `funding_missing`, `attribution` (opening fee and funding

  posted yet), plus `transfer`, `unrealised` (wallet), `provisional_fee` and `unconfirmed_backfill` (hybrid). An
  episode is a run of hours in one day key with |divergence| > 1.25 USDT; its named cause is the largest step.

## 5. Decision: a hybrid (no single source meets all three criteria)

| Criterion                         | local (from the stored rows)           | closed_pnl (REST)   | wallet equity                 | hybrid                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 accuracy ≤ 1.25 (§1 (i) + (ii)) | fails (backfill, funding, attribution) | is the reference    | fails (unrealised, transfers) | passes (0.0002 outside provisional hours; by construction, §4.1) |
| 1 as #880 words it (as reported)  | fails                                  | is the reference    | fails                         | fails (193.53: dating and posting lag)                           |
| 2 no REST on hot path             | passes                                 | fails               | fails (poll)                  | passes                                                           |
| 3 deterministic replay            | passes                                 | fails (lag, paging) | fails                         | passes (stream events only)                                      |

1. **Realised:** a stream-fed, in-memory counter per (environment, account, paper) and day key, idempotent by exec id
   (SR-E39-11). Each close is booked at its **record figure**, computed locally from the stream: price PnL (`execPnl`),
   minus the closing fee, minus the opening fee prorated from the opening fills' `execFee`, plus the funding rows
   accrued since the open, prorated. This tracks the venue's attribution, so it is not a cash ledger.
2. **Unrealised, continuous:** the blocking figure is `realised_today + min(0, net uPnL)`, where net uPnL = mark-to-market
   minus the opening fees and the **paid** funding of the still-open positions (the costs closed-PnL will attribute at
   the close). Received funding is **not** credited before the close realises it (`min(0, funding)` per position): if
   A1 is wrong, the realised leg is corrected by reconciliation but the unrealised leg never is. Recomputed on every
   position event and on the 5 s tick; no REST. **Mark source and age:** the cached public mark price, if it is at most
   `risk_state_max_age_seconds` (10 s, #881) old; otherwise the `markPrice` of the private `position` push the adapter
   already stores (`oms/positions.py:456`), under the same 10 s bound; if neither is fresh for any open position's
   symbol, or neither has arrived, the account answers **`risk_state_unavailable`** (new opening orders refused).
   `sys.stale_feed_block` (`24` §11) covers the book and trades, not the mark, so it does not stand in for this.
3. **Carried positions, pessimistic (BQ-3 (a)):** the whole net uPnL of a carried position counts every day it is open;
   the close lands the whole record on the close day, as the venue does. A `risk_eval` boundary snapshot is written as
   evidence. Cost in this run: 0 extra breach-hours.
4. **Reconciliation off the hot path, tighten only:** every 30 s in the existing reconcile cycle
   (`reconcile_job.py:104-106`), on the poll lane, never the stop and cancel reserve (C-12.7), incremental from the last
   record's `updatedTime` (A5: every page is read, order not relied on). Each venue record is matched to its local close
   and replaces the local figure only if it shows more loss; a divergence over the threshold logs the account and writes
   an audit row when it tightens. A record matched to a **provisional or unconfirmed** close _confirms_ it: it replaces
   the bound in either direction (the venue is the truth; the lockout stays sticky, §6). Proposed rule until follow-up 2
   keeps `orderId`: a match may tighten any close, but releases a bound only when it is the sole candidate and no
   sibling close is awaiting a record; otherwise the §6 timeout releases it.
   - **Matching key.** By the record's `orderId` once follow-up 2 keeps it. Until then the adapter drops it
     (`trading_parse.py:417-418`), and the key is (symbol, closing side, closed qty, average exit price at the
     instrument tick) against a local close whose last fill's exec time is within 120 s before the record's
     `updatedTime` (A2's 90 s plus margin). Zero candidates or more than one: the record is **unmatched**.
   - **An unmatched record is booked in full**, dated by its `updatedTime` (the only time it has), never dropped: it
     is a tightening (SR-E39-11). Where it duplicates a close the counter already holds (an ambiguous match), the day
     counts that loss twice until follow-up 2 lands; that is the safe side, it is logged, and its audit row names it.
   - **Rate budget.** One `closed-pnl` request per account per 30 s (one page of 100; a second page only when more than
     100 records posted in 30 s) = 0.033 requests/s per UID against the documented 50/s per UID (0.07 %); 64 accounts
     on one host = 2.1 requests/s against the 120/s per IP (1.8 %). This answers US-POS-008's "within the rate budget"
     in numbers; the cold-start read (§5.8) adds ⌈records/100⌉ `closed-pnl` pages and ⌈fills/100⌉ `execution/list`
     pages per account, once.
5. **Backfilled fills:** a fill backfilled over REST (no `execPnl`) is booked from the position projection's average
   entry, with the opening fee at the taker-rate bound, no received funding, and **no gain credited**, and is marked
   **unconfirmed** until a record covers it. While any close is unconfirmed the `RiskSummary` shows it and a flatten
   waits for confirmation (or for the §6 record timeout). A gap not yet backfilled is stale state:
   `risk_state_unavailable`, fail closed, with no timeout. §4.1 shows the case: a backfilled loss blocks on the hybrid
   and not on the stored rows.
6. **Day boundary:** one key per deployment from `day_boundary_utc_offset_minutes` (default 0 = UTC, so `24` §11 holds by
   default); fixed minutes, no time-zone names, no daylight-saving logic; no per-account offset. Fills are dated by
   exchange `execTime`, never receive time; a venue record is dated by its closing fill, **not** by `updatedTime`
   (§4.1: dating by `updatedTime` moved 153.85 USDT across the K1/K2 boundary). The rollover runs on the
   exchange-synchronised clock. **An offset change never shortens the key in progress:** that key keeps its start and
   runs to the first boundary of the new offset that is at or after its old end, so it lasts 24 h to under 48 h (§4.4:
   36, 25, 47 and 34 h for −720, −60, +60 and +840); whole days on the new grid follow. It never moves an open lockout
   (`21:4329`, SR-E39-09); a lockout set in the key in progress lasts to that key's end.
7. **Cadence:** every execution and position event, plus the 5 s tick from cached marks; REST only in the 30 s
   reconcile. A private stream older than `risk_state_max_age_seconds` (10 s, #881) answers `risk_state_unavailable`;
   so does a stale mark (§5.2). `DayRiskFigures`' per-entry closed-PnL read is retired when E39-T01 serves
   `realised_loss_today` (`24:2878`).
8. **Cold start and restart (RK-D02):** on process start, and whenever the counter for an (environment, account,
   paper) is lost, it is **rebuilt**, bounded to the current day key: (a) from that key's `executions` rows ordered by
   `ts_exec` (idempotent by exec id; a NULL `closed_pnl` row is booked as a backfilled close, §5.5), **and** (b) from a
   full `closed-pnl` read of the key (every page; A5; if A5's demo check shows `startTime` filters on `createdTime`
   rather than `updatedTime`, widen `startTime` to the oldest open position's `createdTime`, or the 7-day maximum, so
   carried positions' records are read), matched as in §5.4, with unmatched records booked in full. Until
   both complete, `RiskGate.check` answers **`risk_state_unavailable`** for that account: new opening orders are
   refused, protective orders pass. A failed or slow rebuild stays fail closed; there is no timeout that admits orders
   on a partial counter. Until follow-up 1 stores `Funding` rows, the rebuilt funding term comes from the records only.

The figure is sync-enforced (C-2.21): the counter publishes plain figures, no interpreter is queried on this path
(C-2.20), and B20 records the lockout.

## 6. The fee-lag worst case (Gherkin "a close whose fees settle 90 seconds later")

**Lock on the pessimistic bound; flatten only on settled confirmation.** Per §2.1 the closing `execFee` is on the push;
what lags is the venue's attribution of the opening fee and the funding. For the window, the close is booked with the
opening fee at the taker rate × the opening value and no received funding (this run exercises the opening-fee bound;
the bound covers a missing closing fee the same way). The taker rate is the larger of the cached `fee-rate` and the
opening fills' pushed `feeRate`; a tier change inside the cache's TTL can make the cached rate not a bound for one TTL,
so a provisional close also queues an off-path `fee-rate` re-read in the next reconcile cycle. New opening orders are
blocked at once if the bound breaches; the lockout is sticky. No automatic flatten happens until the settled figure
confirms the breach (AC-RK-28: a provisional fee plus one bad mark must not flatten; also two consecutive marks ≥ 5 s
apart for the unrealised leg). When the record posts, the counter holds the exact figure again, which may be smaller;
an engaged lockout is not released by that (SR-E39-09/-11), only by the window end or an override.

**Record timeout (A6).** A missing record must not defer a flatten for ever (an ADL close may post none). If no record
covers a close after 10 reconcile cycles (5 min), the stream's own figure counts as settled for the flatten decision:
`execPnl` plus the pushed `execFee`, the opening fee and funding from the stored rows (or, for a backfilled close, the
projection figure with `execution/list`'s `execFee`), with no bound, except that the bound's funding term (paid funding
booked, received not credited) stays until follow-up 1 stores `Funding` rows. The timeout figure is for the flatten
decision only; the engaged lockout stays sticky. The timeout is logged and audited; a record that
posts later still tightens. A gap whose backfill never completes is not covered by this timeout (§5.5).

Production has only the record's `pnl` after confirmation, where the harness takes `min(exact, pnl)`; the difference is
at most one Q4 rounding (0.00005 USDT), appears only after the record posts, and changes no 4 dp figure above.

Why: "a bad day cannot become a disaster". Deferring lets a trader open new risk for up to 90 s on an unknown figure;
locking provisionally on the _point_ estimate can under-count; the bound never under-counts, and its cost is small (here
the bound exceeded the settled figure by at most 1.84 USDT, 0.74 % of the cap, at one hour-end). Flattening on the
bound would turn a fee estimate into a forced market exit, which is the disaster on the other side.

## 7. Production metric

`cv_risk_pnl_divergence_usd{environment,source}`, `source` ∈ {`closed_pnl`, `wallet`}, the maximum over accounts of
|hybrid − source| measured on settled records only (records older than 90 s, dated by exec time). Account ids stay out
of labels (the #908 ruling); the per-account value goes in a structured log (ids and codes only, C-12.6). Alert above
max(the worst settled divergence measured on the demo leg, 1.25 USDT = 0.5 % of the cap). Here the settled worst case is
0.0002 USDT (by construction, §4.1), so the floor applies until the demo leg runs. #880 and #881 name the labels `{account,source}`; that is
stale.

## 8. ADR draft section (for E39-T03)

The number is assigned by the coordinator: **ADR-00NN (number assigned by the coordinator)**. ADR-0016 is the
statechart runtime (`docs/plan/27-adrs/README.md:24`); #880 and #957 still say "ADR-0016".

> **ADR-00NN — Daily-loss figure: source and day boundary.** Status: proposed (E39-K01).
>
> _Context._ The cap's figure has three candidate sources that disagree by fee and funding attribution, posting lag,
> day dating, backfilled fills and transfers (E39-K01 §4). The evaluator has ≤ 5 ms p95 and must be replay-deterministic.
> Accuracy is judged against closed-PnL settled and dated by exec time, within 0.5 % of the cap (two restatements of
> #880's criterion, E39-K01 §1, open to the BQ ruling).
>
> _Decision._ The figure is `realised_today + min(0, net uPnL)`. Realised is a stream-fed in-memory counter booking each
> close at its locally computed closed-PnL figure (fees and funding prorated), dated by the closing fill's exchange
> time. A 30 s off-path reconciliation against `GET /v5/position/closed-pnl` may only tighten a close's figure; a
> record with no matching local close is booked in full. On process start the counter is rebuilt from the day key's
> `executions` and a full closed-PnL read, and the account is `risk_state_unavailable` until both complete. Backfilled
> fills are booked pessimistically (no gain credited) and unconfirmed until a record covers them. Provisional fees are
> booked at the taker-rate bound: they block at once and never flatten; a close with no record after 5 min counts its
> own stream figure as settled. Net uPnL credits no received funding and uses a mark at most 10 s old (public ticker,
> else the private position push); with neither, the account is `risk_state_unavailable`. The day key is the
> deployment's fixed-minute offset, default UTC; an offset change never shortens the key in progress, which runs to the
> first new-offset boundary at or after its old end (24-48 h), and never moves an open lockout. Wallet equity is not a
> source.
>
> _Consequences._ `24` §11's `sys.daily_loss_lockout` reads "until the next day boundary (policy offset, default UTC)" on
> the blocking figure. The figure can differ from the venue's closed-PnL window by records the venue dates on the next
> day; the divergence metric measures settled records only. Funding rows must be stored. Carried positions count
> pessimistically, which can lock earlier than a day-mark-to-market rule; an offset change can make one day key up to
> 47 h long; until `orderId` is kept, an ambiguous match can count one loss twice. Every one of these errs toward
> blocking.
