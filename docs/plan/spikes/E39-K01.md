## Read first: what this run is and is not

**Every number in §4 is SYNTHETIC.** The repo holds no account-consistent recording: every private Bybit fixture is a
single row transcribed from the docs (`packages/fixtures/bybit/README.md:106`), and the order-path recording is E29-K01

emits **domain-level numbers** (fills, funding settlements, transfers, closed-PnL records), never Bybit JSON, keys or
account ids. It validates the decision rule and the cause taxonomy; it is not evidence of Bybit's behaviour.

The model is partly circular: "closed-PnL" is our model of what the venue reports (assumptions in §3). The Gherkin
"Given the 3-day comparison harness has run against a demo account" is **not met by this PR**: the demo leg (one demo
account, 3 days, through the E27 key vault) is owner-run, pending #843, and is the In Test item. Its table is appended

**Where the synthetic trail lives (BQ-8).** Only under `docs/plan/spikes/E39-K01-harness/`: never in

## 1. Question and decision criteria (stated before the run, from #880)

Which source is authoritative for the daily-loss cap's figure; is unrealised PnL counted continuously or sampled; what
are the day-boundary semantics; what happens in a provisional-fee window; what cadence fits the rate budget.

1. **Accuracy, the pass/fail rule (BQ-1 ruling):** a source passes when its divergence from
   `GET /v5/position/closed-pnl` over the window is within **0.5 % of closed-PnL over that window**: the 3-day run,
   per account (§4.1a). Beside it, each divergence is given in USD and as a percentage of the cap, as the Gherkin asks:
   the seeded 250 USDT (`21:4312`), so 0.5 % of the cap = 1.25 USDT. Two finer readings are reported because their
   verdicts differ from the rule's; each difference is marked for the **owner's decision** in ADR-0047 (§8):
   - **per day key:** the same rule, with each key's closed-PnL as the denominator;
   - **per hour, against closed-PnL settled and dated by the closing fill's exchange time** ("settled": every record of
     the day's closes, all posting lags elapsed), under both denominators. Reason for this reference: the venue dates a
     record by `updatedTime` (A5), which moves a close across a boundary when its record posts after it (§4.1); the cap
     must date a loss by the fill. Against closed-PnL as reported, per hour, no source meets either denominator (the
     hybrid: 193.53 USDT, 7 hours over).
2. **No REST on the hot path:** the evaluator has ≤ 5 ms p95 (#881); it reads cached or streamed state only.
3. **Deterministic under replay:** the same events give the same figure (rule-runtime determinism, `docs/plan/30-release-roadmap.md` §7.3 item 6).

## 3. The harness (throwaway)

`python daily_pnl.py` writes `E39-K01-results.json`; `--check` builds twice in-process and compares bytes; `--latency`
runs the micro-benchmark. `test_daily_pnl.py` (8 tests, no network) pins the committed table byte-for-byte to the seed,
checks the backfilled-loss case, the offset-change rule for four offsets, criterion 1 over the window (the stored rows
fail, the hybrid passes) and that the bounds never lock later than day-mark-to-market or the settled figure. Seed
880, 3 days, two accounts (A, B), BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT. Money is `Decimal`.

- **Trail.** Account A on BTCUSDT is scripted: a position carried over the first boundary; a short opened at D1 09:00
  into a rising mark; a partial close at 13:59 whose opening-fee attribution is provisional for 90 s; the rest closed
  **at a loss** inside a 20 min reconnect gap (backfilled over REST, so no `execPnl`) whose record posts late, at
  16:15; a short-to-long flip; a close at 23:59:20 whose record posts at 00:00:50; an ADL close; and a close at 22:59:30
  just before the shifted boundary. The other three account-symbol pairs are seeded random round trips with 1-3
  partial closes. Funding settles every 8 h (BTC) and 4 h (ETH), the cadences of the funding-history fixtures. Account
  B receives a 1,000 USDT transfer. An offset change to +60 min is requested on D1 at 12:00; under the §5.6 rule the key
  in progress runs on to the first +60 boundary at or after its old end, so the keys are K0 `[0 h, 24 h)`, K1
  `[24 h, 71 h)` (47 h, the transitional key) and K2 `[71 h, 72 h)` (the window ends at 72 h).
- **Assumptions.** A1: a record's `closedPnl` = price PnL − the opening fee prorated to the closed size − the closing
  fee + the funding accrued since the open, prorated. A2: the record posts 0-90 s after the closing fill, **except** the
  backfilled close's record, which posts 2 h 10 min late on purpose so that the unconfirmed state of §5.5 is observed
  at two hour-ends. A3: the record is rounded to 4 dp, fees to 8 dp. A4: maker 0.02 %, taker 0.055 % (illustrative;
  the product reads real rates). A5 and A6: §2.1. A7: the position projection's average entry equals the venue's, so
  a backfilled close's price PnL is exact; the pessimistic margin comes only from the fee and funding bounds. A8: a
  transaction-log row is visible at its `transactionTime` (no posting lag modelled).
- **Sources, per account and day key, at each hour end** (72 × 2 rows in `hours`); (a) to (d) are the BQ list's
  letters, and transaction-log is added by the BQ-1 ruling:
  - **(a) local (from the stored rows):** Σ (`closed_pnl` − `exec_fee`) over the day's fills by exec time; a hypothetical
    aggregation, nothing on `main` computes it. No funding; the backfilled fill contributes its fee but no PnL; opening
    fees count when paid.
  - **(b) closed_pnl (as `DayRiskFigures` reads it):** Σ records whose `updatedTime` is in the day and ≤ now (A5).
  - **(c) wallet:** equity now − equity at the day start (cash, transfers and unrealised).
  - **transaction-log (a settled source for funding and fees):** Σ `change` over the `TRADE` and `SETTLEMENT` rows by
    `transactionTime` (= exec time), transfers excluded by type: price PnL (`cashFlow` = `execPnl`, §2.1) minus fees as
    paid plus funding as settled. REST rows carry `cashFlow`, so a backfilled fill is complete here.
  - **(d) hybrid (the decision, §5):** per close, the stream's own record figure dated by the closing fill's exec time.
    Until the record posts: a provisional close books the **opening fee at the taker-rate bound** and no received
    funding (§2.1: the closing `execFee` is on the push; the opening-fee and funding attribution are what lag; this
    run varies the opening fee, not the closing fee); a backfilled close books its projection figure with the same
    bounds and credits no gain. The venue record then replaces the figure; it can only tighten a confirmed figure.
  - **settled:** every record of the day's closes, dated by exec time, all lags elapsed (the per-hour reference of §1).
- **Cause attribution** is an exact ladder from each source to closed-PnL, one step per named cause, and the steps sum
  to the divergence (asserted): `backfill_null_execpnl`, `funding_missing`, `attribution` (opening fee and funding
  booked when paid, not at the close), `rounding`, `boundary` (exec-time day ≠ `updatedTime` day), `fee_lag` (record not
  posted yet), plus `transfer`, `unrealised` (wallet), `provisional_fee` and `unconfirmed_backfill` (hybrid);
  transaction-log takes the ladder from its `attribution` step. An
  episode is a run of hours in one day key with |divergence| > 1.25 USDT; its named cause is the largest step.
- **Not covered by this run** (each has its specified behaviour in §5 or §6; none is evidence):

  | Case                                                       | Specified behaviour                                                                                                    |
  | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | A process restart mid-day (cold start)                     | §5.8: rebuild from the day key's `executions` and a full closed-pnl read; `risk_state_unavailable` until both complete |
  | Duplicate exec ids (a replayed push, a backfill overlap)   | §5.1: the counter is idempotent by exec id (SR-E39-11); a second sight is dropped, never booked twice                  |
  | A `BustTrade` (liquidation) close                          | Booked like any close from its push; its record matched and tightening as in §5.4                                      |
  | An ADL close (`AdlTrade` on the push, no such record type) | Booked from its push; the record-timeout rule of §6 confirms it if no record posts (A6)                                |
  | A WS gap whose backfill never completes                    | §5.5: stale state, `risk_state_unavailable` for the account until the backfill completes; no timeout releases it       |
  | A flip at the boundary                                     | The closing part is dated by the fill's exec time like any close; the opening part carries over (§5.3)                 |
  | A projection average entry that differs from the venue's   | Not modelled (A7); the record replaces the figure when it posts; until then the bound covers fees and funding only     |
  | A stale or missing mark price                              | §5.2: fall back to the private `position` push's mark, else `risk_state_unavailable`                                   |

Transaction-log is compared as a settled source for funding and fees (BQ-1 ruling; RSK-050 names it), not adopted as a
day-figure source: it is a REST read, it dates fees and funding when paid rather than at the close, and its rows can
lag in volatility with no stated bound (A8). The demo leg compares it with the closed-PnL records per position (§4.1b).

1. **Realised:** a stream-fed, in-memory counter per (environment, account, paper) and day key, idempotent by exec id
   (SR-E39-11). Each close is booked at its **record figure**, computed locally from the stream: price PnL (`execPnl`),
   minus the closing fee, minus the opening fee prorated from the opening fills' `execFee`, plus the funding rows
   accrued since the open, prorated. This tracks the venue's attribution, so it is not a cash ledger.
2. **Unrealised, continuous:** the blocking figure is `realised_today + min(0, net uPnL)`, where net uPnL = mark-to-market
   minus the opening fees and the **paid** funding of the still-open positions (the costs closed-PnL will attribute at
   the close). **A recorded deviation (BQ-2 ruling):** US-RULE-012's NFR is the plain sum, realised plus unrealised
   (`11:3892`); this figure is stricter, because an unrealised gain never offsets a realised loss. E39-T03 aligns
   `24:3856`, `24:1293` and the story's NFR wording in the same change, and D-4's "realised" reading (`TM:320-323`) is
   confirmed on #2701 before ADR-0047 is final. Received funding is **not** credited before the close realises it
   (`min(0, funding)` per position): if A1 is wrong, the realised leg is corrected by reconciliation but the unrealised leg never is. Recomputed on every
   position event and on the 5 s tick; no REST. **Mark source and age:** the cached public mark price, if it is at most
   `risk_state_max_age_seconds` (10 s, #881) old; otherwise the `markPrice` of the private `position` push the adapter
   already stores (`oms/positions.py:456`), under the same 10 s bound; if neither is fresh for any open position's
   symbol, or neither has arrived, the account answers **`risk_state_unavailable`** (new opening orders refused).
   `sys.stale_feed_block` (`24` §11) covers the book and trades, not the mark, so it does not stand in for this.
3. **Carried positions, pessimistic (BQ-3 (a)):** the whole net uPnL of a carried position counts every day it is open;
   the close lands the whole record on the close day, as the venue does. A `risk_eval` boundary snapshot is written as
   evidence. Cost in this run: 0 locked account-hours over (b), and no carried-loser re-lock, because the trail
   carries no loser (§4.6, which also specifies that case). Choosing (b) later is a `risk_policy` field and so a
   contract change made contract-first (`21` `risk_policy`, `22`), never a change to this spike (BQ-3 ruling).
4. **Reconciliation off the hot path, tighten only:** every 30 s in the existing reconcile cycle
   (`reconcile_job.py:104-106`), on the poll lane, never the stop and cancel reserve (C-12.7), incremental from the last
   record's `updatedTime` (A5: every page is read, order not relied on). Each venue record is matched to its local close
   and replaces the local figure only if it shows more loss; a tightening writes an audit row (proposed action
   `risk.pnl_tightened`, §7), and a divergence over the threshold is logged with ids and codes only (C-12.6). A record
   matched to a **provisional or unconfirmed** close _confirms_ it and replaces the bound in either direction (the venue
   is the truth), under SR-E39-11 as written (BQ-1 ruling): **an open lockout is never released by settlement**, and a
   settled figure that lowers a provisional or unconfirmed bound is recorded with an audit row (proposed action
   `risk.pnl_bound_settled`, §7), never silently. Proposed rule until follow-up 2
   keeps `orderId`: a match may tighten any close, but releases a bound only when it is the sole candidate and no
   sibling close is awaiting a record; otherwise the §6 timeout releases it.
   - **Matching key.** By the record's `orderId` once follow-up 2 keeps it. Until then the adapter drops it
     (`trading_parse.py:417-418`), and the key is (symbol, closing side, closed qty, average exit price at the

     `updatedTime` (A2's 90 s plus margin). Zero candidates or more than one: the record is **unmatched**.
   - **An unmatched record is booked in full**, dated by its `updatedTime` (the only time it has), never dropped: it
     is a tightening (SR-E39-11). Where it duplicates a close the counter already holds (an ambiguous match), the day

   - **Rate budget.** One `closed-pnl` request per account per 30 s (one page of 100; a second page only when more than
     100 records posted in 30 s) = 0.033 requests/s per UID against the documented 50/s per UID (0.07 %); 64 accounts
     on one host = 2.1 requests/s against the 120/s per IP (1.8 %). This answers US-POS-008's "within the rate budget"

## 6. The fee-lag worst case (Gherkin "a close whose fees settle 90 seconds later")

**Lock on the pessimistic bound; flatten only on settled confirmation.** Per §2.1 the closing `execFee` is on the push;
what lags is the venue's attribution of the opening fee and the funding. For the window, the close is booked with the
opening fee at the taker rate × the opening value and no received funding (this run exercises the opening-fee bound;
the bound covers a missing closing fee the same way). The taker rate is the larger of the cached `fee-rate` and the
opening fills' pushed `feeRate`; a tier change inside the cache's TTL can make the cached rate not a bound for one TTL,
so a provisional close also queues an off-path `fee-rate` re-read in the next reconcile cycle. New opening orders are
blocked at once if the bound breaches; the lockout is sticky. An automatic flatten (`breach_behaviour = flatten`) acts
only on a **confirmed realised breach**, or on a breach that holds at **two consecutive marks at least 5 s apart**
(BQ-2 ruling; AC-RK-28: a provisional fee plus one bad mark must not flatten). When the record posts, the counter holds
the exact figure again, which may be smaller; that lowering is audited (`risk.pnl_bound_settled`, §7), and an engaged
lockout is not released by it (SR-E39-09/-11), only by the window end or an override. How often the bound locked an
account the settled figure would not have (RK-D05's cost): 0 account-hours in this run (§4.6).

**Record timeout (A6).** A missing record must not defer a flatten for ever (an ADL close may post none). If no record
covers a close after 10 reconcile cycles (5 min), the stream's own figure counts as settled for the flatten decision:
`execPnl` plus the pushed `execFee`, the opening fee and funding from the stored rows (or, for a backfilled close, the
projection figure with `execution/list`'s `execFee`), with no bound, except that the bound's funding term (paid funding
booked, received not credited) stays until follow-up 1 stores `Funding` rows. The timeout figure is for the flatten
decision only; the engaged lockout stays sticky. The timeout is logged and audited (`risk.pnl_bound_settled`, reason
`record_timeout`); a record that
posts later still tightens. A gap whose backfill never completes is not covered by this timeout (§5.5).

Production has only the record's `pnl` after confirmation, where the harness takes `min(exact, pnl)`; the difference is
at most one Q4 rounding (0.00005 USDT), appears only after the record posts, and changes no 4 dp figure above.

Why: "a bad day cannot become a disaster". Deferring lets a trader open new risk for up to 90 s on an unknown figure;
locking provisionally on the _point_ estimate can under-count; the bound never under-counts, and its cost is small (here
the bound exceeded the settled figure by at most 1.84 USDT, 0.74 % of the cap, at one hour-end). Flattening on the
bound would turn a fee estimate into a forced market exit, which is the disaster on the other side.

> **ADR-0047 — Daily-loss figure: source and day boundary.** Status: proposed (E39-K01).
>
> _Context._ The cap's figure has four candidate sources (and transaction-log, compared) that disagree by fee and
> funding attribution, posting lag, day dating, backfilled fills and transfers (E39-K01 §4). The evaluator has ≤ 5 ms

>

> a stream-fed in-memory counter booking each close at its locally computed closed-PnL figure (fees and funding

> the first new-offset boundary at or after its old end (24-48 h), and never moves an open lockout; the loss cap and
> US-POS-009's "today" share that key. Carried positions count pessimistically. Wallet equity is not a source; `GET
/v5/account/transaction-log` is a settled comparator for funding and fees, not a source.
>
> _Owner decisions open._ (1) Criterion 1's finer readings: the rule passes the hybrid; per day key it fails on dating
