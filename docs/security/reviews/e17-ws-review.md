## 4. Residual risks

1. **The server's transport limits are configured** (#3890: `ws_max_size` 260 KiB, deflate per encoding,
   `limit_concurrency` 256, a 1 024-message bound, handshake and keep-alive timeouts; SR-191), with a
   request-header deadline and size bound, a fragment cap, a budget for every inbound frame and a drain bound
   on a close. WS-D11 to WS-D14 (the model) and WG8 (04 §5.13) are proposed residual Low, and RSK-063 no longer
   lists the limits as open: the wording is the QA-requested re-rate of 20 Oct, the ratings this PR's proposal
   for the owner's sign-off (§5). The five items that the PR of #2176 lists as outside its scope are owned by
   the model (the Security engineer rates them with these rows). WS-D14's residual is the rate at which
   connections open, which the gateway does not bound (23 §16.2): it is the network layer's (tailnet-only
   reachability, SR-045 and SR-047), and the Low holds only while that stays true (model §4, §10).
2. **The outbound budget is off by default.** `CV_WS_BACKPRESSURE_ENABLED` stays off until the §8.6 gates
   pass, and its removal (#2900) is deferred past GA (the issue, like #3146's, was closed as not planned on
   2026-10-19). With it off, the control in force is the 1 024-frame
   push bound, which counts frames, not bytes; there is no coalescing, send interval or byte budget. WS-D01
   and WS-D08 stay contingent on the flag and on the staged load run.
3. **E17-R-05 is open** (triaged privately). It needs an owner decision
   before the E17 gate.
4. **Frames already handed to the transport when a grant is withdrawn are still delivered**, ahead of
   `permission_change`: what sits in the transport's write buffer and the kernel's send buffer. The model's
   §5 states this window (this PR); it has not been quantified.
5. **The idle lock does not end a socket** (E17-R-27). A session that idle-locks keeps receiving private
   topics until its access token expires, at most one access-token lifetime (12 min). This is a
   deliberate E09-S03 decision, now recorded in the model's §10. Revisit trigger: any increase of the
   access-token lifetime, or reachability beyond the tailnet.
6. **Ownership of a replay stream is E26's** (WS-E03). Nothing publishes replay frames in this tree yet.
7. **Producer-side scoping belongs to the producer epics** (SR-E28-21, E17-R-17): `rules` to E35-S06,
   `trade_groups` to E28-T05 and E34-T01. Latent: no such producer publishes to the hub.
8. **The kill-switch producer is not wired** (WS-E04, WS-E06, E17-R-18; #956, E39-S03). Order entry is
   enforced by the OMS, never by the WS mirror (C-2.21).
9. **View-as sessions** (E17-R-26, #3085) are described in 23 §9.6 and not built. No context can exist,
   so no socket can carry the wrong scope. The gateway half has to be reconciled with the identity guard
   (WS-S04) when it lands; the model's §10 now says so.
10. **The upstream book-gap guard has no production caller** (WS-T07, E17-R-22). Latent until the
    book-delta producer (#604) lands.
11. **Audit row volume for authentication failures** (E17-R-19) is a question that spans REST and WS,
    owned by #3009 and E09 (U39).
12. **Smaller latent items** are recorded as findings: web-client validation memory (E17-R-20) and
    snapshot sharing identity (E17-R-24).
13. **Accepted residuals are unchanged:** WS-I04 and WS-R04, accepted by the owner (#1778, review date
    2027-01-15); since E09-X02 F-8 (#298) WS-I04's `git_sha` is in `auth_ok`, and `connection_id`,
    `server_version` and `request_id` stay pre-auth. WS audit is best-effort; a row that is not
    confirmed written is counted.
14. **The unauthenticated-window argument holds only for tailnet-only reachability** (model §4 and §10).
    The controls that provide that reachability (SR-045, SR-047) were not executed here (§3).
15. **Not covered by this pass:** E09 session internals, E08 upstream connection security, E26 replay
    routing, the rule IR parser and exchange-message decoders, and the staged measurements in §3.
