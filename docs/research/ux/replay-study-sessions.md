## Method note (agent-delivery adaptation, owner decision 2026-09-25)

The ticket calls for structured sessions with the owner and the two account managers, a 2-second screenshot test and one keyboard-only session. Delivery is a solo agent with one human owner, so — as `e16-recorder-mental-model.md` and `e30-d01-session-protocol.md` did — the sessions are replaced by (1) the heuristic evaluation of §3 against the catalogue, the contracts and the shipped replay code, (2) the teardown of §2 from public documentation fetched 2026-10-21 (no exchange host contacted; a claim not backed by a fetched page is labelled "(heuristic, unsourced observation)"), and (3) the protocol of §6 and the checklist of §6.7 for the owner. Severity uses Nielsen's scale: **S4** catastrophe (a trader can act on stale prices or lose a protection), **S3** major, **S2** minor, **S1** cosmetic. A default that differs from a contract or from the shipped code is recorded as a contradiction (§7.1), never silently.

### 2.2 What CandleViewer copies and what it must avoid (heuristic, each with its source)

1. **Copy:** a status line that says what to do next and how far the segment has played (ATAS [AT-1]) → the transport carries a one-line state ("Seeking to 13:52:04…", "Gap 13:52–13:55 — recorder was offline", "End of range"); H1.
2. **Copy:** "Go to real time is not an exit" vs "Jump to real-time chart" ending replay (ATAS [AT-1] vs TradingView [TV-1]) → CandleViewer's `R` must say which it is: "Leave replay and return to live data", confirmed (SCR-097), with what happens to simulated positions (F6); H5.
3. **Copy:** the first-available-day jump (TradingView [TV-2]) and the automatic jump to the first available bar → US-RPL-001's "earliest available timestamp offered with the reason" and US-RPL-007's "jump to earliest"; H9.
4. **Copy:** one shared clock across charts (TradingView "All charts", Sierra "All Charts in Chartbook" [TV-1, SC-1]) and the lesson that per-chart empty-period skipping breaks sync (Sierra [SC-1]) → the gap policy is a session property, never per pane (F9); H4.
5. **Copy:** stepwise speed keys (Bookmap `.` / `,` [BM-2]) → next / previous preset keys, missing from the keymap today (F14, G2); H7.
6. **Avoid:** trading executed on real-time data while the chart replays (TradingView [TV-1]) — the exact "act on stale prices" hazard this ticket names; CandleViewer's fills come from the paper matcher against the replayed book (`24` §12, §13.7) and nothing can reach an exchange (F23, F24); H5.
7. **Avoid:** idealised fills (ATAS "immediately in full" [AT-1]; Sierra's bid/ask sizes always 1 [SC-1]) → the queue-position, latency and partial-fill model of `24` §12.1 stays, and SCR-099 states its assumptions (F25); H2.
8. **Avoid:** an undocumented replay indicator (Sierra [SC-1]) and a symbol-only indicator (TradingView [TV-1]) → word + glyph + date + frame on every pane (F2, F4); H1.
9. **Avoid:** a free numeric speed field as the primary control (Sierra, up to 100000 [SC-1]) → presets anchored to budgets, a custom value within the API range as the secondary path (§4); H6.
10. **Avoid:** empty windows during replay (ATAS [AT-1]) and a mandatory restart (Bookmap [BM-1]) → the terminal's panels stay, fed by the replay clock (SCR-097), with the per-pane opt-out of §3.6; H3, H7.

### 3.4 Replay-vs-live signalling (heuristic; PROPOSED; D06 validates)

**Starting point.** E11-D02 §10 already specifies the per-pane treatment of a replaying chart: a **4 px `color.replay.border` frame** (CR-4, a proposed token: `palette.purple.400` dark, `palette.purple.600` light) inside the pane-stack boundary, a **"REPLAY hh:mm:ss" chip** (`color.text.primary` on `color.surface.raised`, 2 px border in the replay token, a replay glyph, the clock at 1 Hz) at the right end of the status row, the **virtual-now** dashed line with the **future dimmed** (`26` §10 item 1), and the watermark gaining "· replay". E11-D06 D20 makes the frame and the chip safety elements: they change in one frame, never cross-fade, in both motion modes. `23` §11 requires "a visually distinct chrome so it can never be mistaken for live". This memo decides how that per-pane treatment scales to the **page** (every dockable panel, the DOM, the tape, the ticket) and what the non-colour carriers are.

### 3.5 Recorder emptiness as the normal case (heuristic)

Replay depth equals recorder depth (`14` §0.5.3). The shipped facts make partial and empty windows the **first-weeks default**, not an edge case: recording starts only on chart-open, a manual add or an open position (ADR-0015; E16-D01 F4), `retention_days` defaults to 30, `GET /market/data-coverage` is capped server-side to the last 90 days (`24` §13.3.1, #2216), covered intervals merge across ≤1000 ms, a window with no covered interval is refused with `no_data_recorded` (422), and a partly covered window is accepted with every uncovered span seeded into `coverage_gaps` (INV-B12-d) and a `ReplayGap` marker placed before the events of its instant. The engine's per-session `GapPolicy` is `skip` or `stop` — exactly SCR-097's "[Skip gap] [Stop here]".

| Track class | What the data says                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Visual (PROPOSED)                                           | Text carrier (mandatory)                                                                                              | Where                                    |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Recorded    | a `DataCoverage.streams[].intervals[]` row (tier questdb or parquet)                                                                                                                                                         | solid track                                                 | `aria-valuetext` "13:38:04 — recorded"; the tier is a tooltip detail, never a colour                                  | CMP-170 track, CMP-172 timeline          |

Three design rules follow. **Per stream:** coverage differs per stream (a window can hold trades and no book — US-RPL-003's "not recorded" DOM while price replays), so the scrubber's single track shows the **worst** of the session's selected streams and SCR-098's coverage timeline shows the per-stream lanes (F11, Q5). **Boundary first:** the history-start marker is drawn before anything else so a window that begins before it is corrected at once (US-RPL-001 scenario 3; E11-D03 D15 does the same in the go-to-date dialog). **Copy:** replay reuses E16-D05's cause-first structure; the "History starts <ts> — recording began then" stem is logged there as a blocking misread (E16-D01 §4) and is not re-litigated here (C11).

### 3.6 Live panes beside replay (US-RPL-005; heuristic)

The precedents split three ways: ATAS empties every non-selected window [AT-1], Bookmap cannot show live at all [BM-1], Sierra lets other chartbooks' windows stay live [SC-1], and TradingView keeps the trading panel and quotes on real-time data during replay [TV-1] — the last is the hazard. **PROPOSED default: no live pane** (SCR-097's `[ ] keep live panes` unchecked); **opt-in per pane** (US-RPL-005's scenario), persisted with the session (US-RPL-009 restores the pane configuration); **order-entry-adjacent panes are replay-locked while paper fills are on** — the ticket, the DOM ladder, the positions grid — because a real-time book beside a ticket that fills against the replayed book is the confusion the ticket exists to prevent (F27, Q6). An opted-out pane shows **no** replay frame or chip, its normal env badge and its feed chip. The remaining problem is lexical: "live" means the environment (`14` §0.5.1), the healthy feed (E17-D01's chip vocabulary) and, in US-RPL-005, "not replaying" — F1 asks the owner to settle the word for the opted-out pane (Q3); until then the memo proposes the chip's accessible name "Real-time — not replay".

### 3.7 Paper trading in replay (US-RPL-006; heuristic)

**PROPOSED: the real ticket (SCR-060 as E30-D02 specifies it) in a distinct state, not a separate surface.** Rehearsal is the point (US-RPL-006 "rehearse execution"), so the keys (E30-D01 SC-1), the arm model and the bracket must be the ones the trader uses live; a second ticket would double the design and implementation cost and train the wrong hands. The distinct state has five carriers: the pane sits inside the replay frame with its chip; the submit label carries "simulated" (SCR-097 checklist "paper-trading controls drawn with 'simulated' labelling"); the arm pill's second line reads "REPLAY · simulated fills"; the env badge reads DEMO (the paper account's environment); and the session ends in SCR-099 with "simulated" in the heading. Above 25× the ticket is **disabled with the reason** (SCR-097 validation) — and that rule is UI-only today (F7, G1). The isolation is structural, not chrome: private topics cannot be replayed (`23` §11), the `replay` execution mode routes to the local `PaperMatcher` (`24` §13.7) whose fills carry `is_paper: true` on the normal private topics, `orderLinkId` generation raises for `replay` (`24` §8), and the E26 threat model's Critical row RP-E01 names the one gap — no OMS ticket owns the explicit refusal of a replay-origin order (`04` E26 section; gaps filed as #2668–#2677). The design assumes that refusal exists server-side (A6) and never relies on the badge.

### 3.8 Scrub latency honesty (heuristic)

The budgets disagree (C2): SCR-097 "seek ≤1 s to any point within the loaded window", US-RPL-004 "≤2 s", `24` §13.7 "≤2 s for any point in a recorded day", `30` §6.3 exit 8 "within 1 s for a 200-depth symbol", and the ticket's "1 s p95 / 2 s ceiling". ADR-0035 (proposed) measured 8.6 s p95 at the 300 s snapshot interval as first written and **0.41 s p95** with 60 s snapshots and collapsed deltas (synthetic 200-depth, laptop), and the control endpoint says a seek "is never cheaper than a snapshot interval (60 s)". Nielsen's limits [NN-1] frame the perception: 0.1 s "reacting instantaneously", 1 s "flow of thought … uninterrupted", 10 s "keeping the user's attention". The design therefore has **three rungs**, each with a text carrier (F17, F18):

| Elapsed       | What the trader sees                                                                                                                                                         | Why                                                                                                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| drag, <0.1 s  | the thumb's time readout moves with the pointer (local); the panes keep their last frame; one `seek` is sent on release, never per pixel                                     | instant feedback without a round trip; the control endpoint is rate-limited                            |
| 0.1 s – 1 s   | the transport's state line "Seeking to 13:52:04…"; the chip's clock shows the target; nothing animates                                                                       | the flow stays uninterrupted; eased bars would mark instants that never happened (E11-D06 D20's logic) |
| 1 s – 2 s     | the same line gains the cause and the elapsed time: "Rebuilding the book from the 13:51:00 snapshot · 1.4 s"                                                                 | the budget's p95 is 1 s; past it the trader must know why                                              |
| >2 s (breach) | the cause line stays, the controls stay live, `Esc` / Pause cancels the seek (B12: `CANCEL` from `buffering` returns to `paused`, cursor unchanged); at 10 s a cancel button | the ceiling; never a spinner alone; never a frozen transport                                           |

Every pane flips **together**: a seek produces one `snap` with reason `replay_seek` per subscribed topic (`23` §11), so the design must not stagger panes. The observed tolerance is measured in T3 (§6.3) and is not claimed.

## 4. The canonical speed-preset list (DECIDED here; write-back done)

**The lists in the sources (seven, not two):**

**Decision: `0.5× · 1× · 2× · 4× · 10× · 25× · 100×`, plus a custom value accepted within the API's 0.1–100 range.** Rationale: (1) every preset above 1× anchors to a stated budget — 4× is the raw-tick sustain target, 25× the 1-minute sustain target **and** the paper-fill boundary (SCR-097), 100× the hard playback budget (`06` #15); 10× is the scan speed between them; (2) 1× is the honesty speed of "would I have seen it live?" and 0.5× the slow-motion for a fill, with tick stepping below that; (3) the list is the SCR-097 wireframe's and agrees with US-RPL-004 on six of seven values; (4) seven buttons fit the transport row at compact density beside the custom field (F21); (5) the API is continuous, so **no backend change** is needed and the DB CHECK already admits every preset and the custom range; (6) the incumbents offer either an unbounded number (Sierra [SC-1]) or an undocumented list (TradingView [TV-1], Bookmap [BM-2]) — a budget-anchored list is the better professional default (H6). **Rejected:** CMP-171's 5× and 50× (no anchor; 50× sits inside the paper-disabled zone and adds an eighth button), US-RPL-004's 50× for the same reason and because 25× must be reachable exactly, the checklist's 0.25× and `30`'s 0.25×–20× and 20× (the API floor is 0.1 and the custom value reaches it; 20× is `06`'s load-test setting, not a user speed). The custom field is the secondary path, clamped and validated to 0.1–100 with the reason shown (CMP-171 `presets` prop stays; `value` may be off-preset).

**Write-back in this PR:** `15` CMP-171's purpose line now names the canonical list and the custom range; `14` SCR-097's checklist phrase "speed 0.25x-50x" now reads the canonical presets. The SCR-097 wireframe already showed the list. `11` US-RPL-004, `20` C13 and `30` §6 are owned elsewhere and are reconciled through E26-T07 (§9.1, C1).

### 6.4 The 2-second "is this live?" screenshot test (AC scenario 3; open)
