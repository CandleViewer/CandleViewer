## Method note (agent-delivery adaptation, owner decision 2026-09-25)

The ticket calls for 4–6 moderated sessions with the owner and available order-flow traders, a clickable static prototype of SCR-030 at three zoom levels and reference screenshots. Delivery is a solo agent with one human owner, so — as `e16-recorder-mental-model.md`, `e30-d01-session-protocol.md` and E26-D01 did — the sessions are replaced by (1) the heuristic evaluation of §4 against the catalogue, the contracts and the shipped code, (2) the teardown of §2 from public documentation fetched 2026-10-21 (no exchange host contacted; a claim not backed by a fetched page is labelled "(heuristic, unsourced observation)"), (3) the worked geometry of §3 over the plan's own scenes, and (4) the protocol of §8 and the checklist of §8.6 for the owner. Severity uses Nielsen's scale: **S4** catastrophe (a side or a size can be misread and acted on), **S3** major, **S2** minor, **S1** cosmetic. A default that differs from a contract or from the shipped code is recorded as a contradiction (§7.1), never silently. **Four things upstream are settled and are not re-decided here:** the legibility floor is frozen as `LOD_PROFILE_M0` (E06-D01 §2); the DOM mirror does not mirror every cell (E06-D03 F15); imbalance is exact and the three supported input sources are exact (`24` §7.2, cross-spec ruling 2, E25-D01 F18); the vocabulary is `24` §4.1's (`bid_volume` = traded at the bid = aggressor sell; the shipped `color.footprint.bid` / `ask` token pair, which inverts it, is C18). A pull against any of them appears below as a contradiction for the owner, never as a recommendation.

### 2.2 What CandleViewer copies and what it must avoid (heuristic, each with its source)

1. **Copy:** bid (sell) left, ask (buy) right in a two-number cell — TradingView's default "Buy and Sell" [TV-1], Quantower's Imbalance layout [QT-2], Sierra's "Bid Vol x Ask Vol" [SC-1] → R2; the aligned layout the three documented defaults agree on (the half-level tilt is item 12's alternative).
2. **Copy:** a one-number compact mode that is the signed delta — NinjaTrader's Delta style [NT-1], ATAS "Delta" [AT-1] → `26` §3.6's L1 "one number (delta)" has precedent; R1.
3. **Copy:** a **width** gate for text (ATAS "Width to Show Text" [AT-1]) beside the frozen height ladder — §3.2 shows width binds first on wide scenes → R3, K01 question 1; and a text-hide threshold **the user may raise** (Exocharts "Min. cluster height to show text" — "Raise this value if the text looks cramped" [EX-1]; ATAS "Width to Show Text" [AT-1]), kept **clamped at the frozen floor** so it can never go below the measured legibility (E06-D01 §2) → R3, D12.
4. **Copy:** a zero-denominator rule (Sierra "Zeros as Ones" [SC-1], ATAS "Ignore Zero Values" [AT-1], Volumetrica "Include Zero on Imbalance" [VT-1], Exocharts "Imbalance ignore zeros" [EX-1]) → `24` §4.2's `zero_policy` stands; the tooltip names when the floor substituted (R2).
5. **Copy:** stacked zones projected forward until price trades through them (TradingView [TV-1]; Quantower's "support or resistance" reading [QT-3]) → US-FP-008's persistent zone and CMP-132's crossed state; R4's bracket.
6. **Avoid:** text that shrinks continuously with zoom (NinjaTrader [NT-1], Sierra's automatic font [SC-1], Volumetrica's dynamic size [VT-1]) — E06-D01 §2 measured 10 px as failing (83 %) for two numbers; the ladder steps, never scales (E11-D06: one frame, no crossfade).
7. **Avoid:** abbreviated numbers inside a cell (Sierra "10.0K" [SC-1], Volumetrica "K" [VT-1], Exocharts "Short numbers" — "1,250,000 is drawn as 1.25M" [EX-1]) — E06-D01 §4: full, never compact; K01 question 2's mitigation (b) would reopen this (C11).
8. **Avoid:** imbalance by text colour alone (NinjaTrader's magenta text, Sierra's text coloring, Exocharts' text-only marking when "Draw box behind Imbalances" is off [EX-1]) — `05` §4 item 3 and E06-D01 §3: the outline is the channel, colour reinforces.
9. **Avoid:** a side derived from tick direction (TradingView "from the direction of intrabar candles") — Bybit gives the taker side (`24` §2.1, E22-D01 F2); US-FP-002 scenario 2 documents it in the info popover.
10. **Avoid:** the whole layer flipping to candles when zoomed out (ATAS [AT-2]) — `26` §3.6's L2 keeps colour and the imbalance carriers; the degradation is announced (E06-D03 D7).
11. **Note:** no vendor documents a per-cell context menu or a hover readout beyond the chart values — the SCR-030 items have no precedent either way; R5 is heuristic and §8.3 T4's card-sort decides.
12. **Note:** the one alternative to the aligned two-column level is a half-level **tilt** that puts the diagonally compared pair side by side (Jigsaw daytradr's "Tilt Mode" [JG-1]; unsourced here) — not the default (R2, D2): the level must stay aligned with the price axis, the SCR-045 level rows and the mirror, and a tilt breaks E11-D04 D15's parity; it is listed so the owner's sessions can ask for it.

## 8. Session protocol (4–6 sessions; ready for the owner; nothing below has been run) and owner checklist

### 8.1 Participants, consent, data

- **Participants:** the owner (P1, tagged on the sheet because the owner also approves the memo) and the available order-flow traders — comparable traders, each tagged P1-like; one session where the participant also authors a rule or an alert from a cell (the M1 mode); at least one session with a screen reader or at 200 % zoom (§8.5). - **Consent and data (ticket security note):** sessions recorded with consent; recordings may carry faces and voices (PII) and live only on the access-controlled research drive, never in the repo; names are redacted in any write-up (participant ids P-1 … P-6); raw recordings are deleted after synthesis per the research data policy. Every stimulus is synthetic or demo data; no real key, account id or balance appears.
- **Opening script (read verbatim):** "You will see footprint cells at several sizes. We are testing whether the cells tell you what traded where, how small they can get before you stop trusting the numbers, and what you would do next — not whether you like footprints."
- **Analysis:** two independent affinity passes reconciled; anything backed by one participant is a **signal**, not a finding; the per-participant stop heights and arm preferences are reported as a list, **never as a mean**; results are appended to this memo as a dated addendum.
