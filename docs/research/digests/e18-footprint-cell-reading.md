## Method note (agent-delivery adaptation, owner decision 2026-09-25)

The ticket calls for 4–6 moderated sessions with the owner and available order-flow traders, a clickable static prototype of SCR-030 at three zoom levels and reference screenshots. Delivery is a solo agent with one human owner, so — as `e16-recorder-mental-model.md`, `e30-d01-session-protocol.md` and E26-D01 did — the sessions are replaced by (1) the heuristic evaluation of §4 against the catalogue, the contracts and the shipped code, (2) the teardown of §2 from public documentation fetched 2026-10-21 (no exchange host contacted; a claim not backed by a fetched page is labelled "(heuristic, unsourced observation)"), (3) the worked geometry of §3 over the plan's own scenes, and (4) the protocol of §8 and the checklist of §8.6 for the owner. Severity uses Nielsen's scale: **S4** catastrophe (a side or a size can be misread and acted on), **S3** major, **S2** minor, **S1** cosmetic. A default that differs from a contract or from the shipped code is recorded as a contradiction (§7.1), never silently. **Four things upstream are settled and are not re-decided here:** the legibility floor is frozen as `LOD_PROFILE_M0` (E06-D01 §2); the DOM mirror does not mirror every cell (E06-D03 F15); imbalance is exact and the three supported input sources are exact (`24` §7.2, cross-spec ruling 2, E25-D01 F18); the vocabulary is `24` §4.1's (`bid_volume` = traded at the bid = aggressor sell). A pull against any of them appears below as a contradiction for the owner, never as a recommendation.

## 8. Session protocol (4–6 sessions; ready for the owner; nothing below has been run) and owner checklist

### 8.1 Participants, consent, data

- **Participants:** the owner (P1, tagged on the sheet because the owner also approves the memo) and the available order-flow traders — comparable traders, each tagged P1-like; one session where the participant also authors a rule or an alert from a cell (the M1 mode); at least one session with a screen reader or at 200 % zoom (§8.5). Record self-reported colour-vision deficiency, assistive technology and the text scale the participant normally runs.
- **Consent and data (ticket security note):** sessions recorded with consent; recordings may carry faces and voices (PII) and live only on the access-controlled research drive, never in the repo; names are redacted in any write-up (participant ids P-1 … P-6); raw recordings are deleted after synthesis per the research data policy. Every stimulus is synthetic or demo data; no real key, account id or balance appears.
- **Opening script (read verbatim):** "You will see footprint cells at several sizes. We are testing whether the cells tell you what traded where, how small they can get before you stop trusting the numbers, and what you would do next — not whether you like footprints."
- **Analysis:** two independent affinity passes reconciled; anything backed by one participant is a **signal**, not a finding; the per-participant stop heights and arm preferences are reported as a list, **never as a mean**; results are appended to this memo as a dated addendum.

### 8.2 Stimuli (static PNGs; three zoom levels; the rungs; the arms)

- **Data:** a recorded BTCUSDT 5m symbol-day once one exists (E16; exported by E18-T05) rendered through E18-S01's engine or an offline renderer at the compact density, text scale 1; until then frames from `bench/fixtures/footprint.mjs` (seeded) with the word **SYNTHETIC** printed on the image — never the 360-print fixture presented as market data (§3.0).
- **Three zoom levels** (one PNG set each, 2560 × 1440 and 1280 × 800): **Z1** 24 visible bars (two numbers fit: `bid_ask` at L0), **Z2** 60 bars (the US-FP-001 NFR scene: one number fits, two do not), **Z3** 100 bars (E18-K01's B3 scene).
- **The legibility rungs:** the same bar rendered at cell heights **4, 6, 8, 10, 10.2 and 12 px** at text scale 1, compact (the frozen table's own boundaries plus the ticket's four); the **125 % arm** repeats 7.5, 12.5 and 15 px. The participant reads the bid and the ask of three marked cells aloud at each rung, descending; the stop height is the first rung at which any digit is misread or the participant declines to read.
- **The imbalance arms:** the same Z1 frame in **arm A** per-cell outline only, **arm B** zone bracket only, **arm C** both (the proposed default), shown in a rotated order; one isolated imbalance and one stack of 3 are in every frame.
- **The reference screenshots:** the public documentation pages of §2.3 opened live (no copies stored); the participant names which product's layout they read fastest and why.
- **The menu card-sort:** five cards — Focus imbalance, Create alert at this level, Copy cell stats, Replay from this bar, Add note — ranked for three situations: an isolated imbalance, a stack, a plain cell.
